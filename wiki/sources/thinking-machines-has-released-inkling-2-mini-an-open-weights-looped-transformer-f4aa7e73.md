## Excerpts

> Thinking Machines has released Inkling 2 Mini, an open weights looped transformer. It scores 20 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, above the median of 10 for comparably-sized open weights models. Inkling 2 Mini is a 26B total parameter, 5.4B active parameter mixture-of-experts model, released under an Apache 2.0 license with text and image input and a 1M token context window. It is small enough to run on a MacBook or DGX Spark, and sits on the Pareto frontier for intelligence versus size. Inkling 2 Mini matches Qwen3.8 27B (xhigh) on the Intelligence Index with roughly 5x fewer active parameters, but is verbose, generating 161M tokens per Index run versus a median of 85.7M, at about $0.59 and 9.0 minutes per task.
