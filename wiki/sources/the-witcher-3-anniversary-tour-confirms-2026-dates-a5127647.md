## Excerpts

> The upcoming European tour will begin in Dublin on October 21, 2026, before travelling to more than 25 cities, including Manchester, Berlin, Paris, Vienna, Milan, and Stockholm. The tour features a 14-piece ensemble orchestra performing music from the game and expansions. It includes Percival Schuttenbach, the Polish folk metal band that co-composed much of the soundtrack. Music arrangements are supervised by co-composer Marcin Przybyłowicz. The tour celebrates the 10th anniversary of The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt.
