## Excerpts

> MercurySteam, the developer of Metroid Dread and Blades of Fire, announced layoffs on May 12, 2026. However, the number of affected workers was not stated.

> The studio acknowledged this as something common in the industry but described it as a difficult and painful situation, expressing their deepest respect, gratitude, and support for the people affected.

> The layoffs come after studio management was accused of harassment and forced crunch last fall.
