## Excerpts

> OpenAI is launching a significantly more capable and compute-efficient memory architecture built on dreaming. With dreaming, memories are automatically updated as time passes, allowing ChatGPT to revise its memory from "You're going to Singapore in July" to "You went to Singapore in July 2026" when the trip ends.

> OpenAI reports factual recall rising from 41.5% (2024) to 82.8% (2026) on its own internal eval, with preference and time-sensitive scores in the low-to-mid 70s.

> The rollout began on June 4, 2026, with ChatGPT Plus and Pro subscribers in the United States getting first access. OpenAI says expansion to additional countries and to Free and Go users will follow over the coming weeks.
