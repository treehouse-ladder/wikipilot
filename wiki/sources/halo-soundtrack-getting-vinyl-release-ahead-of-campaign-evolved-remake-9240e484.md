## Excerpts

> The soundtrack for the first three Halo games is getting a vinyl release ahead of the Campaign Evolved remake. The new Halo soundtrack release comes on special Spartan Green vinyl, inspired by Master Chief's iconic MJOLNIR-powered assault armour.

> The box set features 83 tracks from the first three Halo titles, specially remastered for vinyl and pressed onto eight heavyweight LPs. The soundtracks are available individually, or as a special 8LP boxset featuring artwork by Halo 3's lead concept artist Isaac Hannaford.

> The original music was composed by Martin O'Donnell and Michael Salvatori. Halo: Combat Evolved includes two LPs for $42, while Halo 2 and Halo 3 feature three LPs each and cost $62 apiece.
