## Excerpts

> Vidu Q4 Preview is now available on the Vidu platform (platform.vidu.com), priced starting at $0.045 per second of video at 540P, scaling up to $0.39 per second at 4K resolution. A 30% promotional discount applies to image-to-video and reference-to-video generation through November 30, 2026.

> The model supports image-to-video and reference-to-video generation with up to 15 image references and up to 3 audio references per request. Text-only prompt-to-video generation is not available in this preview release.

> Vidu Q4 Preview is a public preview of Shengshu Technology's next-generation flagship video model. The full Q4 release date has not been announced.
