## Excerpts

> Kalshi traders priced approximately a 68% probability that access to Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 would be restored before July 1, 2026, while Polymarket listed a roughly 71% chance by the same date.

> The government still believes Fable 5 can be jailbroken to unlock the cybersecurity reach of its sibling system, Mythos 5. The company disputes that reading, arguing the weakness is narrow and common across rival models.

> The export control directive issued June 12, 2026 required Anthropic to suspend access to Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 for any foreign national; because API access cannot reliably filter users by nationality, Anthropic disabled both models for all customers worldwide.
