## Excerpts

> Every composer discusses the potential threat that AI-generated music poses, as AI tracks are already flooding streaming platforms and making it harder for actual bands to be heard.

> According to two-time Grammy-winning composer Austin Wintory, the threat of lawsuits helps protect against AI replacement, as there are a lot of legal issues around the ability to copyright generated music, and every contract he's signed in recent years includes a declaration that none of his work was created using AI.

> American-Canadian composer Lena Raine agrees with these concerns, noting that her score for Celeste led to major label opportunities like working with Sony on Sackboy, but she later pulled back from similar offers and focused on indie projects that offered more space for experimentation and self-expression.
