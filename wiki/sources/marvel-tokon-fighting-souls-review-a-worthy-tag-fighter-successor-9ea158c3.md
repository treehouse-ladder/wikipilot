## Excerpts

> Marvel Tōkon: Fighting Souls, the latest flashy fighter from Guilty Gear developer Arc System Works, is a 4v4 tag fighter that features, at launch, a roster of 20 heroes and villains from across the Marvel multiverse.

> The result is a wonderful fighting system and flashy new look that quickly establishes its own identity, one that is worthy of standing with the giants of the past—although the PC version isn't as super as the heroes in the game.

> Tōkon is filled to the brim with a blend of Arc System Works' best-in-its-class anime excess and the recognizable, iconic heroes from the comics.
