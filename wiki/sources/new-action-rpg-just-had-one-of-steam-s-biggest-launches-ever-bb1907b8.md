# New Action RPG Just Had One of Steam's Biggest Launches Ever

Source: Kotaku, 2026-10-30

## Excerpts

> Phantom Blade Zero rocketed onto Steam with over 700,000 concurrent players, cementing its spot as the 15th most-played game at launch ever on Valve's storefront.

> The game peaked at 723,876 concurrent players on Steam a few hours into launch — a record-tier debut that still sits well below Black Myth: Wukong's second-biggest-ever launch peak of 2,415,714, making Phantom Blade Zero the second major Chinese AAA action breakout of this cycle.

> S-Game's new AAA action title launched October 29, 2026 on PC and PS5 to strong concurrent player numbers, placing among the 15 most-played games at launch in Steam history.
