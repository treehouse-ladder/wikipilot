## Excerpts

> The French union STJV announced its 'Summer Greve Fest' strike on June 22, calling for anyone employed by a video game company across the entire video game sector to strike on June 25.

> STJV requested that every video game worker, including those in game engines, marketing services, streaming, merchandise, esports, online content creation, and teaching positions, simply not show up to work on June 25.

> A month after their national strike on May 27, the situation worsened with more than 1,000 jobs threatened or already destroyed. STJV said an 'exceptional disastrous situation' requires an equally exceptional response and explained the French video game industry is currently in a critical condition.

> STJV announced that a picket line will be held outside Quantic Dream's head office in Paris on the same day as the planned Summer Greve Fest strikes.
