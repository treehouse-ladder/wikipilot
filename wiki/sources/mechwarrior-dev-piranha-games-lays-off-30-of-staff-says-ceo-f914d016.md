## Excerpts

> CEO Russ Bullock of MechWarrior developer Piranha Games confirmed that the studio has laid off roughly 30% of its employees. The layoffs first came to light on LinkedIn, where multiple employees reported being let go as part of an 'economic restructuring' at the company.

> 17 employees were let go in this round of layoffs, but the MechWarrior 5: Mercenaries DLC announced in March was still slated for launch in May. The studio is also working on another MechWarrior product for release in the fall.

> This is the second round of layoffs at Piranha in just over a year - parent company Enad Global 7 laid off 38 Piranha employees in January 2025, just a few months after the release of MechWarrior 5: Clans.
