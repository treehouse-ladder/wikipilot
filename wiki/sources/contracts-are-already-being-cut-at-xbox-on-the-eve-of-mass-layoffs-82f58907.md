## Excerpts

> Xbox is cutting contracts with external vendors as part of broader cost-saving operations, ahead of an anticipated wave of mass layoffs of its full-time, internal employees, as well as plans to spin-off various first-party game studios. One source told Kotaku that some contractors who were involved in supporting various aspects of Xbox's big summer showcase in June have already been informed that their roles were cut from next year's fiscal budget. The company's fiscal year ends today, June 30, with mass layoffs impacting full-time staff expected to roll out in the weeks ahead.
