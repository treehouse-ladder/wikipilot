## Excerpts

> Unreal Engine 5.6 is now available with MetaHumans that can be fully authored directly within the engine. MetaHuman Creator now offers the ability to create a near infinite range of plausible body shapes, and with significant expansion of MetaHuman's database of real-world scan data for faces and bodies, you can create a more varied range of higher-fidelity characters. The MetaHuman Creator web application discontinuation will happen over the course of 2026, giving users time to migrate their MetaHumans to the integrated engine version.
