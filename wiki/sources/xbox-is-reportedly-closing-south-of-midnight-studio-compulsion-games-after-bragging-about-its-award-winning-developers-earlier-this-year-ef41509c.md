## Excerpts

> Xbox is reportedly closing South of Midnight studio Compulsion Games. The planned closure would affect more than 100 employees and arrives the same day Craig Duncan, head of Xbox Game Studios, announced his resignation - making June 15, 2026, among the most turbulent single days in Xbox's recent history.

> Just months earlier, in April 2026, Xbox's new leadership congratulated Compulsion on its Peabody Award, with one executive naming South of Midnight as an example of the quality Xbox would supposedly be pursuing.
