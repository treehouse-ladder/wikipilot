## Excerpts

> I gave the exact same prompt to Codex Desktop running GPT-5.6 Sol Ultra - the mode where Sol makes aggressive use of sub-agents. It produced a much better game! GPT-5.6 Sol has you in a museum, rescuing your two other raccoon crewmates in order to stack on top of each other and bust the golden sardine out of its case.

> The version produced from the one-shot prompt had a bug where each raccoon had an eyeball enlarged to the size of a giant sphere floating over their head. Despite reviewing screenshots during development Codex failed to spot and correct this bug.
