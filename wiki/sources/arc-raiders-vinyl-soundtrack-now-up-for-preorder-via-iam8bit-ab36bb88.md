## Excerpts

> iam8bit have opened up preorders for the vinyl soundtrack to extraction shooter ARC Raiders. The release will be a 2LP available in two vinyl color variants - an exclusive 'split spectrum' green and blue split + yellow and red split colored vinyl, and a standard variant on black vinyl.

> It will be housed in a die-cut wide-spined single-pocket jacket with artwork by Robert Sammelin. It is priced at $43 and is scheduled to release in Q4 2026.
