## Excerpts

> Netflix is laying off around 1,000 employees, roughly 6% of its workforce, in its biggest round of job cuts in years, announced on October 14, 2026. The product, tech, legal and human resources teams are the most impacted.

> The company's games division will also be impacted by the wider organizational changes, though games was not named among the most affected teams and Netflix has not disclosed how many games staff will be affected.

> The cuts come after Netflix separately closed games studios Night School and Moonloot and cut internal game teams earlier in 2026, as the streaming giant makes organizational changes to match its priorities.
