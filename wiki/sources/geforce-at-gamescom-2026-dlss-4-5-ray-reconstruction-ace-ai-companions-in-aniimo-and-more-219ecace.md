> NVIDIA ACE is coming to Aniimo, allowing players to speak naturally with AI companions, teach them new skills, and coordinate in-game actions while discovering distinct personalities.

> NVIDIA ACE will come to Aniimo in early 2027. The AI companions run locally on RTX GPUs for natural voice and text interactions as well as adaptive behavior.

> In Aniimo, the AI functionality will be limited to the player's home area and restricted to selected activities, including crafting, mining and resource gathering.
