## Excerpts

> AI-generated music now accounts for more than 50% of daily uploads on streaming services.

> Up to 85% of all streams on AI-generated music are detected as fraudulent, according to Deezer's analysis.

> Entire albums published by game development studios use AI-composed music, with some even publishing AI-generated OSTs for games that don't exist.

> Deezer has implemented a detection algorithm that automatically labels AI-generated tracks to exclude them from monetization.
