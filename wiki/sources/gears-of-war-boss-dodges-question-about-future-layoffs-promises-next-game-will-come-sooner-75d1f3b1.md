## Excerpts

> Studio head Mike Crump dodged questions about layoffs in a BBC interview, framing the goal as simply making good games people enjoy and saying the team needs to focus on the things it can control.

> Crump said E-Day provides a 'great foundation' for future games and promised the next Gears game won't take seven years to arrive like this one did.

> The Coalition has mostly dodged Xbox's layoffs, though the game's story director Juan Vaca and others were laid off last month among over 260 employees cut on September 22. Matt Booty said Microsoft is only three-quarters of the way done with its cuts, and Blizzard is expected to face cuts next.
