## Excerpts

> During a talk at GDC Festival of Gaming in February 2026, Outersloth's communications director Victoria Tran explained that roughly half of all pitches are immediately rejected, equating to around 1,700 rejected pitches. Outersloth has rejected every single generative AI pitch that has been sent its way.

> Innersloth announced Outersloth in 2024 as an indie game fund designed to support indie developers financially, not provide publishing services. The fund appears to have a clear stance against generative AI-based projects, making it notable in an industry where AI adoption is becoming more prevalent.
