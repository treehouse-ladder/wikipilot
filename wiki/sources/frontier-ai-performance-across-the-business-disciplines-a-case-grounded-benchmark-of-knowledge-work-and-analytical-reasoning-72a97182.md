## Excerpts

> BusinessCaseBench evaluates open-ended analytical knowledge work using 238 business school cases paired with instructor solutions, yielding 615 questions across eighteen disciplines from Strategy and Finance to Leadership & Organizational Behavior.

> The benchmark evaluated three frontier models—GPT-5.4, Claude Sonnet 4.6, and Gemini 3 Flash Preview—on all 615 BusinessCaseBench questions using both Standard scoring and Complete Answer scoring metrics.

> A cross-model oracle that takes the best score among the three frontier models reaches 92.8%, which is 4.5 percentage points above the leading single model.

> Fewer than 7% of questions defeat every frontier model, indicating that the required knowledge is distributed across the frontier rather than absent from it, and that complete and thorough responses to highly open-ended analytical tasks remain the main challenge and opportunity for human-AI collaboration.

> Open-ended advisory and opportunity-identification activities (e.g., identifying organizational opportunities, advising on financial matters) rank among the hardest tasks; structured analytical and explanatory activities approach ceiling performance.
