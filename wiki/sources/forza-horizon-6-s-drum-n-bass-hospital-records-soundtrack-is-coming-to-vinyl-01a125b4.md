## Excerpts

> Forza Horizon 6's fan-favourite drum 'n' bass Hospital Records radio soundtrack is getting a vinyl release. The 24 tracks will be available via a 3LP gatefold pink vinyl, priced at £26.99, due for release on 7th August 2026.

> The album captures the long-running partnership between Hospital Records and the Forza Horizon series, gathering 24 tracks from across the label's drum & bass spectrum, hosted in-game by Chris Goss and Dynamite MC, with contributions from artists including Metrik, Fred V, S.P.Y and Logistics.
