## Excerpts

> A new Nintendo Direct livestream has been announced for June 9, and you can tune in at 7 AM PT / 10 AM ET to watch it. Nintendo says the livestream will run for roughly 50 minutes, and it'll highlight upcoming Nintendo Switch 2 and Nintendo Switch games. After the broadcast ends, there'll be a new Nintendo Treehouse livestream immediately after, showcasing gameplay of select titles featured in the Direct. Nintendo has held a June Direct showcase every year since 2012, and it's not breaking that run in 2026.
