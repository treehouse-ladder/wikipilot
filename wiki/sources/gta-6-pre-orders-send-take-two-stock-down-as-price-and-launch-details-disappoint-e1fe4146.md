## Excerpts

> Take-Two stock fell nearly 3% this week after Rockstar Games officially opened Grand Theft Auto VI pre-orders on June 25, triggering a classic sell-the-news reaction from short-term traders.

> Rockstar confirmed GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026, with a standard edition priced at $79.99. That figure disappointed bulls who had speculated the title could command between $90 and $100. Another minor reason for the dip could be the lack of a disc in physical copies of the game.

> Despite the stock dip, Wall Street's long-term outlook for TTWO remains firmly positive. Bank of America analyst Omar Dessouky maintained a Buy rating with a price target of $368.
