## Excerpts

> With their full consent and active collaboration, EA is using AI voice technology to expand on the 50+ recording sessions they have already completed.

> The claims first surfaced on the Chirpin Zebras podcast, as reported by IGN and Game Developer, where commentator John Buccigross described hearing a playback of his AI-generated voice. He noted the system made mistakes — such as saying "chairs" where it should have said "seats" — and that the fix has to be entered manually.

> It is unclear whether EA is using the tech in other aspects of the game as well.
