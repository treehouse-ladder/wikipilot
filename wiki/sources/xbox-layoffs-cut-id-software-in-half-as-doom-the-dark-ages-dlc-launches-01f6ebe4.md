## Excerpts

> DOOM: The Dark Ages Revelations, the first major DLC for the bombastic shooter, launched on July 7, 2026 across Xbox Series consoles, PC, and PS5. On July 6th, Xbox laid off roughly half of id Software, a day before the expansion launched — roughly 95 employees, including senior staff, were part of that reduction.

> Despite the timing of the layoffs, the DLC itself received positive reviews. Revelations provides 10-15 hours of new content, with scope equal to both of Doom Eternal's campaign expansions combined, and a new Chain Spear weapon that adds another layer to aerial combat.

> A senior staff member who spent twenty years at id Software criticized Xbox for deeming half of his team useless.
