## Excerpts

> Gamescom 2026 kicked off with a major surprise in the form of Nodusfall, a multiplayer souls-like from developer Hoyoverse, the studio behind free-to-play gacha games like Genshin Impact and Zenless Zone Zero.

> Players take on the roles of Weavers, heroes who must band together to battle 'mythic beings' in a world where 'divine authority has crumbled and order has collapsed.' The game will feature cooperative play in which groups of four coordinate to take down powerful monsters.

> Nodusfall is being made in Unreal Engine 5.

> The trailer wasn't shy about its inspirations, namely 2022's Elden Ring. Multiple shots in the trailer look like they could have been ripped straight out of From Software's GOTY open-world RPG, including a boss enemy that looks nearly identical to Elden Ring's infamous Malenia.
