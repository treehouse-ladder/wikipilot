## Excerpts

> KO_OP, the developer of Goodbye Volcano High and Young Suns, has laid off 13 employees. In a post on Bluesky, the worker-run indie studio explained the decision was made to ensure its survival amid a financing drought.

> The layoffs included team leads and elected executives, and the studio cited a radically changed games funding landscape, saying it could not secure funding for new projects while finishing current work.

> The decision was made democratically, and profits from Young Suns will still be shared with impacted members. If KO_OP manages to secure funding in the coming months, it has pledged to rehire as many people as possible.
