## Excerpts

> Alex Rehberg's soundtrack for Rust was digitally released in 2022 and Laced Records have confirmed a vinyl release on August 6.

> The 3LP set features 29 specially remastered tracks and sleeve artwork created in collaboration with Facepunch Studio designer Lewis Ainslie.

> Fans can pick up standard black vinyl, or a special Laced Record Exclusive Edition that features grey and black splatter-effect discs influenced by the game's industrial landscape. Fans can pre-order the Rust soundtrack now for $62.

> Laced Records is bringing the complete Rust video game soundtrack over to vinyl, set to be pressed and shipped out in January 2027.
