## Excerpts

> AA-Robotics v0.1 is a research preview for testing how language models understand, plan, and act in the physical world. Each model controls a 6-axis robot arm (5 joints and a gripper) and is given a simple objective.

> GPT-6 Astra ranked first with a score of 69, followed by GPT-6.1 Sol, Gemini 4 Argon, Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Fable 5.1. Astra averaged 30 minutes and $16.36 per task.

> On simpler tasks such as pressing a red button, the top models scored full marks. On harder tasks such as tidying blocks into a box, Astra earned only partial credit while GPT-6.1 Sol scored full marks. The results cover the first eight tasks.
