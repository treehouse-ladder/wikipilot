## Excerpts

> 007 First Light managed to sell 1.5 million units in its first 24 hours, as shared by IO Interactive who both developed and published the game.

> The game was initially available on May 26 for those who preordered it digitally, with the full launch following one day later.

> It's the fastest-selling game in IO Interactive's history, with the company having previously released the rebooted Hitman trilogy.
