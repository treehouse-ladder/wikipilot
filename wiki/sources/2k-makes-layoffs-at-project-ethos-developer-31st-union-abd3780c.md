## Excerpts

> An undisclosed number of employees were laid off at 31st Union, the 2K-owned studio working on the upcoming free-to-play shooter Project Ethos, on May 8, 2026.

> Studio head Ben Brinkman sent out an internal memo praising the game's 'renewed direction and vision,' before stating that the team's 'success is dependent on our ability to work more quickly and nimbly.'

> Project Ethos is becoming a 'skill-based PvP roguelike' as part of the 'renewed direction' that was announced in 2025. This represents a significant pivot from the game's original concept as an extraction shooter.
