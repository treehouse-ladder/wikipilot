## Excerpts

> HY-Motion 1.0 is Tencent's open-weight text-to-3D human motion generation model. It ships in two variants: a 1.0B parameter full model and a 0.46B parameter distilled variant for lighter inference budgets.

> The model generates human motion directly from text descriptions and exports to SMPL-H and FBX formats, making its output directly importable into Unity and Unreal Engine character animation rigs without requiring intermediate retargeting steps.

> HY-Motion is released as open weights on HuggingFace under a permissive license, positioning it as a freely deployable alternative to Tencent's commercial MoreFun animation tools previously presented at GDC 2026.

> The model handles a range of motion types including locomotion, gestures, and interaction sequences, with the text conditioning enabling style control (slow walk, aggressive sprint, nervous fidget) that would previously require separate motion-capture recordings or hand-keyed animation.
