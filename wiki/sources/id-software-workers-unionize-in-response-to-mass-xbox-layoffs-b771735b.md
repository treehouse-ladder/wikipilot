## Excerpts

> The overwhelming majority of workers at Doom studio id Software – 165 of around 185 total employees – announced that they're forming a wall-to-wall union in conjunction with Communications Workers of America (CWA).

> Fear of studio closures, mass layoffs, and oppressive implementation of generative AI fueled workers to take collective action.

> CWA Local 6215 members have set three targets in their contract negotiations with Microsoft: the remote work policy, benefits like childcare, and guardrails for the use of AI technology.
