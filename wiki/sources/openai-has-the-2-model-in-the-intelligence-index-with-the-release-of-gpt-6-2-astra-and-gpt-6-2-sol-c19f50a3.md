## Excerpts

> With the release of GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol, OpenAI now has the #2 model in the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, trailing only Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2.

> GPT-6.2 Astra (max) scores 62 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, 5 points above GPT-6 Astra (57), behind Claude Fable 5.2 (max, 65).

> On the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index (Codex harness, max effort), GPT-6.2 Astra is flat with GPT-6 Astra, and GPT-6.2 Sol loses 3 points relative to GPT-6.1 Sol.

> Several effort levels of GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol sit on the Cost per Task Pareto frontier.
