## Excerpts

> Claude Opus 5 is our most intelligent model, delivering frontier performance for agentic coding, computer use, and complex reasoning at the same price as Claude Opus 4.8: $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output tokens.

> Opus 5 introduces a new effort setting, ranging from low to high plus a new max tier, that lets you balance intelligence against speed and token cost. Thinking is on by default on Opus 5, with the effort parameter controlling how much the model thinks.

> Fast mode runs Opus 5 approximately 2.5x faster at twice the price ($10 input / $50 output per million tokens). Opus 5 comes close to the frontier intelligence of the larger Claude Fable 5 while running at Opus speed and Opus pricing.

> On Frontier-Bench, our evaluation of whether a model can build working software from engineering drawings, Opus 5 more than doubles its predecessor's score and surpasses every competing model, including Claude Fable 5. Fable 5 remains our recommendation for the most advanced multi-day autonomous agent work.
