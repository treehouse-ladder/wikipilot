## Excerpts

> PhysForge is a decoupled two-stage framework supported by PhysDB, a large-scale dataset of 150,000 assets with four-tier physical annotations. In the first stage, a VLM acts as a 'physical architect' to plan a 'Hierarchical Physical Blueprint' defining material, functional, and kinematic constraints. In the second stage, a physics-grounded diffusion model realizes this blueprint by synthesizing high-fidelity geometry alongside precise kinematic parameters via a novel KineVoxel Injection (KVI) mechanism. Experiments demonstrate that PhysForge produces functionally plausible, simulation-ready assets, providing a robust data engine for interactive 3D content and embodied agents.
