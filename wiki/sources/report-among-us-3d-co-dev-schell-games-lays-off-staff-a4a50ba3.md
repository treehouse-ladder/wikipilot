## Excerpts

> Schell Games had their first ever layoffs, with employees from multiple departments affected. Workers from the art, design, visual effects, and marketing departments were impacted. Most of the laid-off employees have August 2026 as the end date of their work experience at Schell Games.

> Marketing director Dwayne Waite Jr. confirmed that the reported layoffs took place on Friday and affected the marketing team.

> One company manager expressed sympathy for affected colleagues, noting that 'The industry (and beyond) right now is hemorrhaging'.
