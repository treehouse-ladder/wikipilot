## Excerpts

> Today we're introducing major updates to Codex: Sites, a way to instantly publish interactive apps and dashboards Codex builds for you; Annotations, an in-place editing tool that lets you point at exactly what needs to change; and six role-specific plugins covering data analytics, creative production, sales, product design, equity investing, and investment banking.

> More than 5 million people now use Codex every week. Non-developers — including analysts, marketers, operators, designers, researchers, investors, and bankers — make up about 20% of overall Codex users and are growing more than 3x as fast as developers.

> The six plugins ship with 62 popular business applications (Snowflake, Figma, Salesforce, and others) and 110 automated skills out of the box.

> With Annotations, you point to the exact part you want to refine and tell Codex what needs to change. Codex focuses the update on the part you selected, so you can refine your work without starting over or reworking the parts you already like.
