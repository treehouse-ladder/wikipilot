## Excerpts

> Halo Studios has released an extended six-hour version of Halo: Campaign Evolved's 'Main Menu Theme' ahead of the game's launch next month.

> Campaign Evolved will also feature a remastered version of the original soundtrack, which combined sweeping orchestral cues with marching military snares, prog-rock percussion, and Gregorian Monk chants.

> The original score was composed by Martin O'Donnell and Michael Salvatori. Rather than creating an entirely new soundtrack for the 2026 remake, Halo Studios is remastering the 2001 choirs, sweeping orchestral arrangements, synthetic ambience, and marching beats.
