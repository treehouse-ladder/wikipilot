## Excerpts

> Walt Disney Records has released 'The Soul of Racing (Main Theme)' from the Star Wars: Galactic Racer Original Video Game Soundtrack. The track features music composed by Gordy Haab, whose credits include Star Wars: Zero Company, Star Wars Jedi: Survivor, and Indiana Jones and the Great Circle.

> The full OST will be available for pre-order starting today, debuting worldwide on October 6 at the time of the game's official release. The track is now available on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, YouTube Music and other digital platforms.

> For 'The Soul of Racing' we wanted to enhance the pure adrenaline and excitement of racing. Something to capture the competitive spirit, all while underpinning the emotional weight of it all. The goal was to musically highlight the thrill of pushing yourself and your racer right to the edge - so that every second feels exhilarating!
