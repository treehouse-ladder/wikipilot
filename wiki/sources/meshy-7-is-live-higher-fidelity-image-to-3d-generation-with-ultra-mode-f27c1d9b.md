## Excerpts

> Meshy-7 is the newest 3D generation model, with higher-fidelity geometry than Meshy 6. Meshy 7 introduces the ultra_mode parameter, which enables Ultra generation for even finer surface detail. For the Multi-Image to 3D API, Meshy 7 is Meshy's multi-view generation model, which conditions on all of your input views together and drives texturing from them. For the Retexture API, Meshy-7 is the newest texturing model, with sharper surface detail and cleaner PBR materials than Meshy 6.
