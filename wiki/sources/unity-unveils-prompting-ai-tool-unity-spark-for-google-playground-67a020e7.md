## Excerpts

> Unity goes on to describe Spark as beginning game development with a text prompt to 'eventually' grow into full game experiences, skipping the step of coding.

> Developers interested in experimenting with Playground can also take advantage of Unity Spark, a new, separately-announced tool from Unity built to work with Playground.

> Unity itself has expressed a need for stronger guard rails on generative AI within its own tools after an employee used Unity Muse to generate an image of Disney's Mickey Mouse on an official live stream, prompting copyright concerns.

> Recent surveys indicate that creatives within the game industry are worried and cynical about generative AI for game development and its effect on the quality of games.
