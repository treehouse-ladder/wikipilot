## Excerpts

> Crazy Taxi: World Tour was announced during the Xbox Summer Showcase at SGF 2026, and a Steam page listing that disclosed generative AI use stirred up controversy. Generative AI was used to support development of background assets for the game, though assets generated were still subject to review by the development team. No AI was used in reference to the performers in the game. Series creator and lead producer Kenji Kanno explained that generative AI was used as a reference, where artists would generate ideas and look at generated images before drawing the actual thing themselves, with all creators and programming handled by humans.
