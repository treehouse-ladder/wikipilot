## Excerpts

> Forza Horizon 6's digital recreation of the country is easily the franchise's most detailed and gorgeous world yet. Horizon 6 introduces the franchise's largest, most complex urban environment in the form of Tokyo.

> The bulk of your time will be spent exploring the open world or taking part in racing events, and Horizon 6 doesn't make too many changes to this formula. Simply put, it is the exploration of the game's map that is its best feature, even more than racing through it.

> Forza Horizon 6 came to Xbox and PC on May 15 for early access and May 19 for general release, and a PS5 port is coming later in 2026.
