## Excerpts

> The next game from Shadow of the Colossus creator Fumito Ueda got a new name and a new trailer at Summer Game Fest. Previously teased as Project Robot, the game is now titled Gen Atlas. The project is a sci-fi shooter with kaiju battles. What appeared to be a flying mech head used a beam to lift up a mech body and attach to it, and later a mech was seen carrying and swinging a giant pillar. The game is coming to the Epic Games Store, PS5, and Xbox Series X/S.
