## Excerpts

> Bungie has confirmed that Marathon is ending seasonal updates entirely, and the upcoming Symbiosis update will now launch on December 8 instead of September 22.

> The largest change in Symbiosis is the addition of Marathon's first permanent PvE mode, intended to give players another way to explore the world, progress and engage with the game outside its traditional PvPvE extraction structure.

> A smaller update is planned for October 6 that will bring a tuning and balance sweep, a new rewards pass, and miscellaneous quality-of-life improvements, resetting character progression and the in-game economy to give all players a fresh starting point.

> The shift reflects Marathon's struggles since launch, with the game's concurrent player numbers on Steam continuing to decline, now more than 96% below its launch record of 88,337 concurrent players.
