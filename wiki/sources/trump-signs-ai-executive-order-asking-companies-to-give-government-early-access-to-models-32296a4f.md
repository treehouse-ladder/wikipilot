## Excerpts

> On June 2, 2026, President Trump signed an executive order titled Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security, which asks companies building the most capable AI systems to give the federal government a look at those systems up to 30 days before they release them to anyone else.

> The order directs various agencies to establish a voluntary framework through which the federal government would get access to covered frontier models for up to 30 days before their planned release to other trusted partners in order to review them for potential cybersecurity risks. The order does not force a single company to participate.
