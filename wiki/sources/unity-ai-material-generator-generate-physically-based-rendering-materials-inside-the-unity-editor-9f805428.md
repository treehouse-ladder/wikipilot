## Excerpts

> Unity AI Material Generator lets you create physically based rendering (PBR) materials from text prompts inside the Unity Editor – no external tools required. Unity AI open beta is available now for all Unity 6 developers.

> The Unity AI Material Generator is part of the Generators toolset, which includes the Texture2D Generator, Terrain Layer Generator, Sprite Generator, Sound Generator, and Animation Generator.
