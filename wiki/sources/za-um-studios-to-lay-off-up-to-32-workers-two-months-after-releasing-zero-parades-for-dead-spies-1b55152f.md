## Excerpts

> ZA/UM has served redundancy or at-risk notices to up to 32 staffers at the studio, sharing the news on July 17.

> Although Zero Parades reviewed well, its commercial performance has not enabled us to sustain a studio of our current size.

> Throughout this difficult process, the studio has continued to consult and work with representatives of the ZA/UM Workers' Alliance. Remaining workers chose to formally unionize in October 2025, and at the time the union reported that ZA/UM employed roughly 100 people across the globe.
