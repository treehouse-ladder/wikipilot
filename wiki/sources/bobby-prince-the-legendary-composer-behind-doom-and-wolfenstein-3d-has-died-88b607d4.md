## Excerpts

> John Romero, the Doom co-designer, paid tribute to Bobby Prince, writing 'Everyone at Romero Games is deeply saddened to learn of the passing of Bobby Prince. He left an incredible mark on games and on my life.'

> George Broussard, Apogee/3D Realms co-founder, also wrote his own tribute for Prince on X, calling him 'the epitome of a Southern gentleman'. Broussard recalled that 'Bobby would often fly down for a week at a time on large projects (like Duke Nukem 3D) because he thought it was important to be in the office and mingle with the team and talk to people and dial in what sort of music was appropriate.'

> Prince served as a first lieutenant and platoon leader in Vietnam from 1969 to 1970, then built a career in counseling and law before turning to music, working remotely from Georgia as an independent contractor for id Software and Apogee (later 3D Realms).
