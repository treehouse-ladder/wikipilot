## Excerpts

> The Blood of Dawnwalker, the debut game from Rebel Wolves, a studio led by The Witcher 3 game director Konrad Tomaszkiewicz, is a hit with most critics, sitting at 83 on Metacritic.

> Critics praised the writing, the freedom of the nonlinear narrative sandbox, and the day/night vampire hook, while criticism centered on repetitive quest design, Simon Says-style parry combat, and a rough launch build.
