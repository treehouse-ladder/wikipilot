## Excerpts

> There is currently no consensus in the AI industry on how to describe the severity of an AI jailbreak in objective terms, which adds uncertainty whenever a new jailbreak technique is discovered, as developers have no agreed-upon standard for which findings to focus on most urgently.

> Narrow harmful jailbreaks can elicit some specific harmful behaviors and are typically of low to moderate severity because the narrowness limits the attacker. The most concerning category is a universal jailbreak, which unblocks a wide range of harmful behaviors.

> We adopted a defense in depth strategy with Fable 5, aiming to make jailbreaks either narrow or very expensive to produce, and combining this with thorough monitoring to quickly detect and shut down any successful attacks.
