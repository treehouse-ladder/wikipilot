## Excerpts

> On February 26, 2026, WBD's board determined that Paramount's revised $110.9 billion offer, valuing shares at $31 each, constituted a superior proposal to the existing Netflix agreement. Netflix declined to match the offer and withdrew.

> The transaction will include Warner Bros. Games, which owns several high-profile developers such as NetherRealm Studios, Rocksteady Studios, Avalanche Software, TT Games and WB Games Montreal.

> Paramount's offer was approved by the U.S. Department of Justice in June. However, a federal judge paused the deal after a lawsuit was filed on July 13 by a coalition of 12 state attorneys general, with a hearing set for August 3 to assess whether the freeze will extend further.
