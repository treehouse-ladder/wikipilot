## Excerpts

> Roblox has announced a new AI tool that can be used to generate functional 3D objects and scenes, with the company describing the tech as an instance of '4D Generation.' The tech is powered by a proprietary 'Cube Foundation Model' that 'adds the dimension of interactivity to generate functional 3D objects so they behave the way players expect.' Once a creator enables 4D generation in an experience, players can use a simple text prompt to generate a fully functional car, get in it, and drive it around. Roblox's ambition is for the tech to eventually allow creators to 'generate full scenes, including assets, environments, code, animations, and more with natural language prompts.'
