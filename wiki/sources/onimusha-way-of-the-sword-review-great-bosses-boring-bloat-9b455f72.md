## Excerpts

> Onimusha: Way of the Sword is a heartbreakingly confused and compromised game.

> The core of quality boss encounters only takes up anywhere from ten to thirty percent of its playtime, depending on how much time you spend either dying over and over or doing optional content in the open world.

> The remaining bulk of the game flits between, in the best case, a poor fit for its core mechanical design, and in the worst case, an active waste of everyone's time.
