## Excerpts

> Z.ai's GLM-5.2 is the new leading open weights model on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, scoring 51. GLM-5.2 is the same size as GLM-5.1 (744B total / 40B active parameters) but scores 11 points higher on the Intelligence Index v4.1, placing ahead of MiniMax-M3 (44), DeepSeek V4 Pro (max, 44) and Kimi K2.6 (43).

> GLM-5.2 gains over GLM-5.1 on most evaluations, led by scientific reasoning on CritPt (+16 points to 21%) and HLE (+12 points to 40%), alongside AA-LCR (+9 points to 71%), tau3 banking (+15 points to 27%) and SciCode (+7 points to 50%).

> On the first-party API it is priced in line with GLM-5.1 at $1.4/$4.4/$0.26 per 1M input/output/cache hit tokens. GLM-5.2 uses 43k output tokens per Intelligence Index task, of which 37k is reasoning, up from GLM-5.1 (26k) and higher than open weights peers MiniMax-M3 (24k) and Kimi K2.6 (35k).

> GLM-5.2 (max) is a Mixture of Experts model with 753 billion total parameters and 40 billion active parameters, with a context window of 1.0M tokens.
