## Excerpts

> Claude Haiku 5.5 scores 43 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, up 26 points one year after the last Haiku release. The result places it well above average among comparable models (median: 13).

> Claude Haiku 5.5 (max) uses ~162k output tokens per Intelligence Index task, more than Opus 5.5 (max) and ~3x GPT-6 Luna (max). Moving from xhigh to max effort adds 2 points for ~1.8x the tokens. Lower settings score lower: High 38, Medium 34, Low 29; xhigh scores 41.

> On AutomationBench-AA, Claude Haiku 5.5 scores 35%, against 53-60% for GPT-6 Luna, Gemini 3.8 Flash and GLM-5.3 Flash, though this result may understate the model's ability. During pre-release testing, a safety refusal issue caused the model to over-refuse.
