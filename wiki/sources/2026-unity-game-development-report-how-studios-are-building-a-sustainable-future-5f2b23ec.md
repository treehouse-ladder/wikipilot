## Excerpts

> Some 95% of respondents have already adopted AI in their work, with key uses identified as coding support (62%) and writing and narrative design (44%). The median Unity project development time fell 77% to 21 hours from 91 hours between January 2022 and December 2025.

> The top five applications of AI are coding assistance (62%), narrative and writing design (44%), NPC behaviour (40%), market research (37%), and automated playtesting (35%). Five percent said they would not use AI.
