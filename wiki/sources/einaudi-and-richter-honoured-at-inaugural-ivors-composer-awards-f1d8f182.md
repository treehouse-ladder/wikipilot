## Excerpts

> The winners of the inaugural Ivors Composer Awards were announced at a ceremony held on 17 November at Grosvenor House in London.

> The Flight (Joe Henson and Alexis Smith) won Best Original Video Game Score for 007 First Light.

> The other nominees in the category were Sam Slater for Saros and Pav Gekko for Spirit of the North 2.
