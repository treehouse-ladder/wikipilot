## Excerpts

> MR. RECORDS blends record shop management, musical journeys, and heartfelt storytelling into an experience entirely powered by the transformative magic of music. The soundtrack covers a wide range of original music, including funk, progressive rock, hip hop, electro, world music, and 80s pop, composed by the team at Glee-Cheese themselves. The gameplay involves running, jumping, sliding, dodging, and chaining actions together in sync with the music across 40+ handcrafted levels. MR. RECORDS will release in Q1 2027.
