## Excerpts

> Alexander Brandon, composer of Deus Ex and Unreal, has submitted 50 resumes in the past year and received only one interview. He attributes the difficulty to the impact of AI on audio production for games.

> The musician, who is also an audio director and voice actor, has a 30+ year career but says he simply can't find a new job in the industry. He was laid off from his position at Frost Giant (Stormgate) last year and attributes the difficulty to AI taking over at big studios, who don't want to bother having a full-time, in-house composer.

> He isn't an anti-AI activist and doesn't believe rejecting the technology is the best course of action, and has even found some success in using AI tools to save time with middleware, research, and documentation. He hosted a roundtable about the impact of AI in audio production at the Game Developers Conference and is part of a Special Interest Group focused on raising awareness about the ethical concerns and legal risks surrounding artificial intelligence.
