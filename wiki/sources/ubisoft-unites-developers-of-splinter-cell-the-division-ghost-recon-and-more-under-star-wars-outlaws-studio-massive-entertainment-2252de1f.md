## Excerpts

> In the wake of Microsoft's Xbox layoffs, Ubisoft has announced that Massive Entertainment will take charge of a wealth of franchises including The Division, Ghost Recon, and Splinter Cell, as well as the MOBA March of Giants and various unannounced projects.

> The new structuring puts Ubisoft Paris, Montreal, and Toronto, as well as Massive Entertainment itself, all under the Massive brand. The group is in charge of a wealth of Ubisoft franchises including The Division, Ghost Recon, and Splinter Cell, as well as the MOBA March of Giants recently bought from Amazon and various unannounced projects.

> In July, Ubisoft announced that Creative House 2 was to be helmed by Cristoph Hartmann, a 20-year veteran of Take-Two Interactive and the co-founder of 2K Games, and he was joined by former Avalanche chief development officer Natalie Francis as studio manager in Massive's Malmo office.
