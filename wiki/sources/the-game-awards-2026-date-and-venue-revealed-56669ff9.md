## Excerpts

> The Game Awards 2026 will take place on Thursday, December 10, at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles, host and producer Geoff Keighley confirmed.

> Nominations are expected to be announced in mid-November, roughly a month before the ceremony. Tickets go on sale October 30 at AXS.

> GTA 6 has a release date of November 19, 2026, which is likely too late to be eligible for the 2026 awards; eligibility cutoffs for 2026 have not been confirmed.
