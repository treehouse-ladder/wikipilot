## Excerpts

> Bit Reactor, the studio behind Star Wars Zero Company, has begun bringing furloughed staff back. CEO Greg Foertsch said the studio had 'started the process of returning more of our team back to work,' with sources saying headcount is more than half of what it was pre-furlough.

> The studio described the furloughs as a painful call made after 'unexpected business developments outside of our control' so close to launch. The game launched August 28 and around 80% of the development team was impacted; it was developed on a tight $50 million budget that appeared to run out just before release.

> Data firm Alinea Analytics estimates the game has already sold over 1 million copies, though EA has not confirmed the figure.
