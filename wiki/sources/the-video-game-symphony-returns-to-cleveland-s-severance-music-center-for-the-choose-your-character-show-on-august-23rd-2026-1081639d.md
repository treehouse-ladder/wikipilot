## Excerpts

> On August 23rd, the Video Game Symphony will return to Severance Music Center (home of the renowned Cleveland Orchestra) for their Choose Your Character! show.

> The show will showcase multiple compositions by composer Borislav Slavov, including selections from his scores for Game of the Year winner Baldur's Gate 3 and Divinity: Original Sin II.

> The Video Game Symphony will perform a ten minute piece titled 'The Vampire Killer Suite,' which will feature classic songs from the Castlevania franchise, and it will include a cadenza performed by piano virtuoso 88bit.

> This concert is also the first time the symphony will play music from the Chrono series; they will perform a version of the song 'Radical Dreamers' from Chrono Cross.
