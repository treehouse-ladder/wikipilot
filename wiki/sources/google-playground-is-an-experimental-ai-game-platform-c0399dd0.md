## Excerpts

> Google is making another big swing at building a dedicated video game platform—this time opting to produce one for games made with generative AI. The new venture, Google Playground, is a platform for making, sharing, and playing games made using generative AI tools.

> Users describe what they want in a chat-style interface, and the platform is aimed at both experienced developers and people without coding backgrounds.
