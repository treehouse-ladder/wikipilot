## Excerpts

> Krafton has appointed Kangwook Lee as chief AI officer (CAIO) to develop AI technologies that will 'enhance gameplay experiences and support development teams.' Lee has served as head of Krafton AI since 2022.

> Lee orchestrated significant external partnerships—such as the collaboration with Nvidia that facilitated the creation of AI-enabled Co-Playable Characters (CPCs).

> Lee stated that Krafton only intends to use AI to 'amplify human imagination and creativity,' as opposed to replacing workers. Krafton's AI strategy will focus on three core areas including elevating gameplay, with technologies such as CPCs intended to complement human-designed gameplay systems while preserving the handcrafted elements that define each title.
