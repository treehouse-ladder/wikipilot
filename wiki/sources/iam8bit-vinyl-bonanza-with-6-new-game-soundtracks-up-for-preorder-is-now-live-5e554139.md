## Excerpts

> Dead By Daylight is receiving a 4LP picture disc vinyl set for the game's 10th anniversary, combining Official Soundtracks 1-4 in a single premium box set with picture discs of The Trapper, The Plague, The Oni, and The Trickster. It is priced at around €150 and will be available for preorder until July 31, 2026, scheduled to release in September 2026, and can be preordered from the Dead By Daylight Store.

> Composer Jessica Curry has released her soundtrack to survival horror game Amnesia: A Machine For Pigs on vinyl as a single LP on clear vinyl. It is priced at £28 and is in stock and ready to ship.

> Mana Wave Media are releasing the soundtrack to relaxing plant nurturing game Viridi on vinyl as a single LP on 'jade' green marble colored eco-vinyl. It is limited to 300 copies and is priced at $30 and is in-hand and ready to ship.
