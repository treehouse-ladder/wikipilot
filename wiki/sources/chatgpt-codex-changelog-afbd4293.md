## Excerpts

> Goal mode is no longer an experimental feature and is available in the Codex app, IDE extension, and CLI. Appshots are now available in the Codex app on macOS: press both Command keys to send the frontmost app window to Codex with a screenshot and available text.

> GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.4 mini will no longer be available in Codex for users signed in with ChatGPT; replace gpt-5.4 with gpt-5.6-terra (GPT-5.6 Terra) and gpt-5.4-mini with gpt-5.6-luna (GPT-5.6 Luna). GPT-5.6 Terra now costs 20% less, and GPT-5.6 Luna costs 80% less.
