## Excerpts

> Nearly one in four reveals (13 of the 55 games shown, or 23.64%) either used AI in development or marketing.

> Uses ranged from in-game assets created with AI tools (Aion 2), AI for NPC interactions (Ananta), generative AI support tools used as reference (Crazy Taxi: World Tour), AI for placeholder assets (Crimson Desert: Enhanced Edition), AI-generated marketing assets (Once Human), and AI chatbots (Petit Planet).
