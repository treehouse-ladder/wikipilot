## Excerpts

> This extravagant playtest event is a waste of money, and a kick in the teeth for the fired workers who are seeing fans brought in to do jobs that would otherwise have been theirs.

> Former employees protested outside the office of MindsEye studio Build a Rocket Boy as the company held an all-expenses-paid playtest for fans, with the Independent Workers Union of Great Britain (IWGB) protesting at the studio's headquarters in Leith, Scotland.

> The IWGB estimates more than 400 employees have been laid off at the studio. Build A Rocket Boy began a redundancy process less than two weeks after the game's launch, with further rounds following in March and May 2026.
