## Excerpts

> The Denshattack! soundtrack features an all-star cast made up of main composer Tee Lopes, core composers Sean Bialo and Andrew One, and guest composers including Takenobu Mitsuyoshi (SEGA), Richard Jacques (Jet Set Radio), Shoji Meguro (Persona), Harumi Fujita (Capcom), MIYACHI and Ironmouse.

> The soundtrack was released July 15, 2026, and the full soundtrack, totaling more than 80 tracks, was released through Kid Katana Records. The OST accompanies Denshattack!, the train-flipping platformer that was revealed at Gamescom 2025.

> The soundtrack delivers an eclectic mix of electrifying breakbeat, Y2K hip-hop, punk energy, and funk grooves, all wrapped in VGM nerdom.
