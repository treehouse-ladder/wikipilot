## Excerpts

> NVIDIA ACE now supports the open source Qwen3-8B small language model for on-device deployment, enabling developers to build enhanced game characters capable of real-time reasoning through non-scripted events and dynamic responses based on player inputs. ACE autonomous game characters have made their debut in the life simulator inZOI, where players can customize 'Smart Zoi' NPCs with life goals that use AI to guide behavior, and in NARAKA: BLADEPOINT MOBILE PC VERSION, where NVIDIA ACE-powered teammates help players battle enemies, hunt for loot, and fight for victory.
