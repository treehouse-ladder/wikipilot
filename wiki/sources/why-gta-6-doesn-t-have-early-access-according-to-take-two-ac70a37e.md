## Excerpts

> Take-Two is selling fewer special versions of GTA 6 — so far only the $80 standard and $100 ultimate editions are available to preorder — and early access wasn't offered as an additional perk, when it is available for its line of 2K sports games like NBA, WWE, and PGA Tour.

> CEO Strauss Zelnick said the level of pre-orders is unprecedented and astonishing, while explaining that pricing decisions ultimately belong to Rockstar Games, and while it could have charged more for features like paid early access, the studio was aiming to provide value to its consumers.
