## Excerpts

> The rise of large language models (LLMs) and generative AI (GenAI) has transformed game development by enabling game designers new ways to increase player engagement and interaction, with one significant application being the creation of NPCs capable of dynamic, unscripted communication.

> LLM-generated dialogues have several benefits such as enhanced input flexibility or leading to more natural conversations but can create a variety of undesired side-effects.
