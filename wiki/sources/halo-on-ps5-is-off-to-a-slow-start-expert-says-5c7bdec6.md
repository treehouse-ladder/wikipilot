## Excerpts

> Halo: Campaign Evolved is having a notably slow start on the PS5 in terms of sales, according to gaming analyst Rhys Elliott.

> An estimated 351,000 copies were sold on PS5 during July and 452,000 to date, generating nearly $28 million. By comparison, Campaign Evolved has shifted 212,000 units on Steam and about 900,000 on Xbox across console and PC thus far.

> Fifty dollars for what's essentially a graphical touch-up of a 2001 game was always going to be a hard sell.
