## Excerpts

> Opus 5 reached 70.57% on OSWorld 2.0 against 55.7% for Opus 4.8, a large jump in computer-use capability. On Zapier AutomationBench it scored 26.0%, against 17.0% for Opus 4.8 and 17.4% for Fable 5.

> Claude Opus 5 achieved a verified 30.16% on ARC-AGI-3 at high effort, according to the ARC Prize Foundation, roughly 3x the next model. On Frontier-Bench it reaches 43.3% at max effort, versus 33.7% for Fable 5 and 37.5% for GPT-5.6 Sol.

> Fable 5 still edges Opus 5 on SWE-bench Pro at 80.0%, and remains Anthropic's recommendation for the most advanced multi-day autonomous agent work. Opus 5 ships with a 1M-token context window and unchanged $5/$25 per Mtoken pricing.

> Thinking is on by default on Opus 5, with the effort parameter controlling depth, whereas on Opus 4.8 requests ran without thinking unless you set thinking explicitly.
