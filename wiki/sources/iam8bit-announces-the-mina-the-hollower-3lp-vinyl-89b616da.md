## Excerpts

> iam8bit officially opened pre-orders for a 3LP vinyl soundtrack to Yacht Club Games' Mina the Hollower. The vinyl is priced at $64.99, with shipping scheduled for Q4 2026, and in Europe it's available through Black Screen Records at EUR68.

> The vinyl is pressed on Velvet Purple Splatter Vinyl, and the center labels feature a zoetrope-inspired design with Mina in action, while the jacket includes 3D-enhanced details and comes with special Mina-themed 3D glasses.
