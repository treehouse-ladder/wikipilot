## Excerpts

> Tencent has released Hunyuan3D-PolyGen, a powerful AI model that generates professional-quality 3D assets with minimal manual effort... its first attempt at delivering what they're calling art-grade 3D generation, specifically built for the professionals who craft the digital worlds we play in.

> Unlike basic mesh generation tools, PolyGen focuses on high-fidelity structure, clean topology, and support for professional workflows like rigging and UV unwrapping. Leveraging high-compression BPT representation, it can generate models with 10K+ faces.

> Developers report a 70% reduction in asset creation time, from around 8 hours to just 2.5 hours per model. Compared to industry SOTA methods, the topology neatness of models generated by Hunyuan3D-PolyGen improved by 35%, and the rate of detail loss decreased by 60%.
