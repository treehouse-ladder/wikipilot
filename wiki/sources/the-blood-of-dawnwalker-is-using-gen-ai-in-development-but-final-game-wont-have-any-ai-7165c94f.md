## Excerpts

> Rebel Wolves used AI-generated placeholder voices only during early development, primarily to test dialogue, story flow, and multilingual voice planning before spending money on full actor recordings. The early AI voice use was meant to reduce re-recording costs and avoid putting extra pressure on staff when scripts changed.

> The final game will feature the voices of real actors, and the studio assured fans that The Blood of Dawnwalker won't have any AI content in the final product. Rebel Wolves co-founder Tomasz Tinc stated: 'Nothing that's in The Blood of Dawnwalker was created using generative AI, nothing. People with their blood and flesh made this game from the beginning till the end.'
