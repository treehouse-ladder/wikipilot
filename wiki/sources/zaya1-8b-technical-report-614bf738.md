## Excerpts

> ZAYA1-8B performs competitively with models many times its size across mathematics benchmarks (AIME, HMMT), coding (LiveCodeBench), reasoning, knowledge retrieval (GPQA-Diamond), and instruction following (IFEval, IFBench).

> Incorporates a novel MLP-based expert router that improves routing stability over standard linear routers, and learned residual scaling.

> CCA performs sequence mixing in a compressed latent space, resulting in an 8x reduction in KV-cache size compared to full multi-head attention.
