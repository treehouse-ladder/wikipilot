## Excerpts

> Free: $0 per month. Direct credits cost $1.5 per credit. The free tier includes Rodin 3D generation with a basic redo allowance.

> Creator: Billed monthly - $30 / month. Billed yearly - $288 today (works out to $24 per month). Redo limits are 20 geometry and 6 material redos, with no API access.

> Business: Billed monthly - $120 / month. Billed yearly - $1,152 today (works out to $96 per month). Adds full API access, 120 or 240 API requests per minute, and 50 geometry and 15 material redos.

> Enterprise: Custom pricing, with custom API enablement and limits.

> All charges are in credits. You only pay for what you use. Rodin Gen-2.5 starts at 0.5 credits per generation.
