## Excerpts

> Tencent now holds a 26.32 percent economic interest in Vantage Studios—the new home of Ubisoft franchises including Assassin's Creed, Rainbow Six, and Far Cry. Tencent invested 1.16 billion euros (1.25 billion USD) to deleverage the group and enable the formation of this new subsidiary.

> Vantage Studios (Creative House 1) will handle Assassin's Creed, Rainbow Six, and Far Cry, focused on scaling and extending these established franchises. Each Creative House has its own leadership and is fully responsible for brand development, content strategy, and editorial direction.

> The restructuring will result in multiple project cancellations, studio closures, a return-to-office mandate requiring all teams to work on-site five days a week, and widespread 'rightsizing'. The company expects to make another 200 million euros in cuts over the next two years.
