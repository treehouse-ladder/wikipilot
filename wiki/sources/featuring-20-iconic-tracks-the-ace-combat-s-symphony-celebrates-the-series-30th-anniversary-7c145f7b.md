## Excerpts

> Following an exclusive CD release, the Ace Combat/s Symphony concert album hits major streaming platforms.

> The concert was conducted by Hirofumi Kurita and performed by the Tokyo Philharmonic Orchestra, featuring music spanning most of the franchise's history. The album features 20 iconic tracks celebrating the series' 30th anniversary.

> The concert also included music from the upcoming Ace Combat 8, with the Ace Combat 8 main theme being performed live as part of the concert earlier this year.
