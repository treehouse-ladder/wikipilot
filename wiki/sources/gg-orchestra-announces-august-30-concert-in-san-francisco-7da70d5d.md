## Excerpts

> The GG Orchestra will make its Herbst Theatre debut on Sunday, August 30, 2026, presenting an evening of beloved music from anime and video games in the heart of San Francisco.

> Joined by Sudo Choir, the orchestra will bring audiences music from Attack on Titan, Demon Slayer, Pokemon, JoJo's Bizarre Adventure, Frieren, Genshin Impact, Dota 2, Grand Theft Auto, Super Mario Bros., and The Legend of Zelda, alongside additional selections to be announced.

> The GG Orchestra is the Bay Area's Game Music Ensemble, performing epic music from video games, film, and anime in concert halls.
