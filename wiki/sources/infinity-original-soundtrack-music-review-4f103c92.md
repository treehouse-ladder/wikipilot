## Excerpts

> With the power of Kickstarter crowdfunding, the developers were able to rope in veteran composer Yuzo Koshiro for one track, included in the OST in its complete form in 2026.

> The Infinity OST is every bit as strong as some of the best NES and Game Boy RPG soundtracks, and notably stronger than many soundtracks from this era of 8-bit hardware.

> Koshiro created a high-quality piece of rock music bordering on metal, complete with guitar shredding near the end that reminds me of DragonForce for his arranged version.
