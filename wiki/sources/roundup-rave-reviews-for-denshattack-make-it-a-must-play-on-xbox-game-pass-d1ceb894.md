## Excerpts

> Denshattack! received Metacritic scores of 88 (PS5), 86 (Nintendo Switch 2), and 89 (PC). The game is currently holding a 'Generally Favourable' Metacritic score of 88.

> Denshattack! is rated 'Mighty' after being reviewed by 53 critics on OpenCritic, with an overall average score of 87, and is ranked in the top 2% of games and recommended by 94% of critics.

> Denshattack! is developed by Undercoders and published by Fireshine Games. The game launched on July 15, 2026 on Xbox Series X|S, PC, Play Anywhere, PlayStation 5, and Nintendo Switch 2, and arrived day one on Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass.

> The speed and momentum immediately call to mind the 3D Sonic games, while the emphasis on chaining tricks together feels straight out of Tony Hawk's Pro Skater. Development is led by David Jaumandreu, director of Ninja Gaiden: Ragebound, while the soundtrack comes courtesy of Tee Lopes.
