## Excerpts

> KRAFTON and NVIDIA reaffirmed their collaboration during an appearance by KRAFTON Chairman Byung-gyu Chang and NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang at a fan event for PUBG players in Seoul. PUBG Ally is planned to launch as a beta service in PUBG Arcade this month.

> PUBG Ally, developed using an on-device small language model built on NVIDIA ACE, is a representative example of KRAFTON's AI capabilities applied to actual gameplay experiences. Demonstrations of the game and PUBG Ally running on the RTX Spark platform heightened fan anticipation for new AI-based gaming experiences.

> The two companies discussed continued working together to optimize KRAFTON's major titles, including PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS and inZOI, for the RTX Spark platform.
