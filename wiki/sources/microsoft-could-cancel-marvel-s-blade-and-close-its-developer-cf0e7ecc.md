## Excerpts

> According to a report from The Verge, at least five studios, including Arkane, Compulsion Games, Double Fine, Ninja Theory, and Undead Labs, are all in danger. Microsoft is also trying to offload Compulsion Games, Double Fine, Ninja Theory, and Undead Labs.

> The report said the job cuts coming to Xbox will begin on July 6 and 'hit most parts of Xbox.'

> If buyers are found for these studios, then they'll avoid being closed, but sources stress these talks could take months in some cases.

> Marvel's Blade was originally intended to release in 2026 but slipped internally to a late 2027 window, and the game is over budget and facing delays.
