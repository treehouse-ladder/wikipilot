## Excerpts

> Sony is shutting Bluepoint Games down just five years after buying it. The studio will be closing Bluepoint Games in March 2026, with the announcement made in February 2026.

> Hermen Hulst said the decision was the result of an 'increasingly challenging industry environment' in a note to staff on Thursday.

> 70 employees are facing layoffs at the studio—which is known for modern remakes of games like Demon's Souls and Shadow of the Colossus, alongside co-development duties on God of War Ragnarok.
