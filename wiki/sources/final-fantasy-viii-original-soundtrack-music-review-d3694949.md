## Excerpts

> Nobuo Uematsu's Final Fantasy VIII compositions feature orchestras, more electronic influence than even Final Fantasy VII had, some chanting, and a pop song, representing a departure from the series' traditional sound.

> The four-hour-long score contains a lot of independent compositions not covered by major themes, with some being very good and some being the most mediocre of Uematsu's contributions to the series. Despite being a partial failure, the parts they love give the work cohesiveness.
