## Excerpts

> Gemini Omni anchors the simulations in real-world appearance and behavior, while other Gemini models provide the complex reasoning needed to help maintain a simulation's coherence and continuity.

> Genie 4 is rolling out as a research preview for trusted testers, with release planned for consumers and developers, starting with Google AI Ultra subscribers in Project Genie and early access partners of the Gemini API and Google AI Studio.

> Genie 4 is currently unable to simulate real-world locations with perfect accuracy. The model can support a few minutes of continuous interaction, rather than extended sessions.

> All video and audio generated by Genie 4 will include SynthID, DeepMind's imperceptible digital watermarking technology.

> Gemini 4 Argon and Genie 4 work together to simulate Apptronik's Apollo humanoid playing a game of checkers, breaking the task down into real-time steps.
