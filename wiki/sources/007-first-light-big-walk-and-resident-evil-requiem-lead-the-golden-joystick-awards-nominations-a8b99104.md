## Excerpts

> 007: First Light and Big Walk lead the nominations, with five nods each.

> Big Walk was nominated in Best Audio Design, Best Indie Game, Best Multiplayer Game, Best Game Trailer, and PC Game of the Year, while 007: First Light was nominated for Best Soundtrack, Best Storytelling, and Console Game of the Year, alongside nods in Best Lead Performer and Best Supporting Performer.

> Right behind those two is Resident Evil: Requiem, with nominations for Best Trailer, Console Game of the Year, and Supporting Performer.

> Public voting for the first set of categories will close on October 23, ahead of the public vote for Ultimate Game of the Year, which takes place between October 26 and October 30. The prizes will be announced during the awards ceremony in London on Wednesday, November 11.
