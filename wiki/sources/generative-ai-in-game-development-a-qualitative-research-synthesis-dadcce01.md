## Excerpts

> A qualitative research synthesis examined Generative AI's impact on game production through meta-ethnography, synthesizing 10 eligible studies. There is a persistent gap between producing high-quality 2D concept art and generating usable 3D assets, emphasizing limitations of current tools when applied to full production. While participants valued the speed of idea generation, some reported a reduced sense of authorship over AI-assisted games.
