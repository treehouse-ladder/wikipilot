## Excerpts

> According to Sensor Tower, GTA 6 has now sold 12.13 million total units via pre-purchases, which works out to about $1.18 billion in gross revenue. Sensor Tower is projecting as many as 25 million total pre-purchases for GTA 6 by launch, which would be about $2 billion in revenue. Sensor Tower's numbers are third-party estimates; Take-Two declined to share an official figure for preorders. About 75% of those sales are on PS5 and 25% on Xbox Series X|S, per the same tracker.
