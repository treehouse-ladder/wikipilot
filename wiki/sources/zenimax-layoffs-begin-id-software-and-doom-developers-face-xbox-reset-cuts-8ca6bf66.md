## Excerpts

> George Broussard, co-founder of 3D Realms, stated that layoffs have reportedly begun at ZeniMax Media, Bethesda's parent company.

> The studio's job listings disappeared from the ZeniMax careers portal on June 16, 2026, the same day broader ZeniMax layoff reports began circulating. However, Microsoft has not confirmed any shutdown of id Software.

> Developers at id Software, MachineGames, and Arkane Lyon are not considered safe under the Xbox Reset — only Fallout and Elder Scrolls teams are protected. The claim has not been confirmed by Microsoft, so it should be treated carefully.

> Microsoft and CFO Amy Hood have approved increased spending on Fallout, Elder Scrolls, and Halo beginning July 1, 2026, the start of the new fiscal year, with significant restructuring expected at ZeniMax studios.
