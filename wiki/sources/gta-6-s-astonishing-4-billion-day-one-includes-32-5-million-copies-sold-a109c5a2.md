## Excerpts

> Take-Two just announced that GTA 6 reached 32.5 million copies sold before release, with revenue reaching 3.79 billion dollars through the end of the first day on sale on November 19.

> GTA 6 has done nearly 1 billion dollars more in revenue already than Avengers: Endgame's entire run.

> Sensor Tower had been sharing unofficial sales data for GTA 6 for a long time already, but Take-Two's sales numbers are even higher than the 25 million sales that Sensor Tower reported at launch.
