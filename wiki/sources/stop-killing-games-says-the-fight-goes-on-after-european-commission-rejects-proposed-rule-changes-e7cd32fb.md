## Excerpts

> The European Commission said it will not propose any new laws requiring that publishers keep their games operational after their servers are shut down, stating that 'at this stage it cannot propose a legal obligation to keep videogames playable after they stop being provided commercially.'

> The commission did commit to taking steps to address the concerns, including initiating an exchange with the videogame industry and consumer representatives to draw up an industry code of conduct on managing videogames' end of life.

> Despite this rejection of their proposed rule changes, Stop Killing Games said 'the fight goes on,' with organizer Moritz Katzner writing: 'This movement is defined by action and we will keep acting.'
