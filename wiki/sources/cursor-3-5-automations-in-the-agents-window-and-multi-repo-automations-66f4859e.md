## Excerpts

> Cursor Automations are now available in the Agents Window, in addition to cursor.com/automations. Create and manage your automations in the same workspace as your agents.

> You can now attach multiple repos to an automation so agents reason across all required context and work across repos to deliver, test, and verify tasks.

> You can now create automations without an attached repository. Five new templates for no-repo automations were added to the Cursor Marketplace including a Slack digest agent, product analytics agent, product FAQ agent, product finance agent, and customer health agent.
