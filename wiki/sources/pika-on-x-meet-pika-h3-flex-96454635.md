## Excerpts

> Meet Pika H3 Flex, debuting today as the #1 open weight model on DesignArena's t2v board. It's built for professional iteration, with exportable audio stems, resolutions up to 4k, and faster-than-realtime generation. It can produce a 15-second clip with soundtrack in as little as 7 sec. Everything we put into this model was designed to give creators the flexibility and speed to explore with precision. In that spirit, we're making Pika H3 Flex open source from day 1. You can find the code on GitHub, the weights on @huggingface, and start playing with it in @ComfyUI.
