## Excerpts

> Grok V9-Medium is a 1.5-trillion-parameter coding model trained explicitly on Cursor developer workflows, three times the size of the current production model v8-small at approximately 500 billion parameters, with its public release beginning in mid-June 2026.

> SpaceX signed a definitive $60 billion merger agreement to acquire Anysphere, the company behind AI coding tool Cursor, on the same morning that Grok V9-Medium's public release began. Cursor had been valued specifically for running on Anthropic's Claude, OpenAI's GPT, and Cursor's own Composer models simultaneously.

> SpaceX has telegraphed that V9-Medium will be benchmarked against Claude and GPT-5.5 on SWE-bench Verified. Current SWE-bench Verified leaders are Claude Opus 4.6 and Gemini 3.1 Pro, both scoring approximately 80 to 81%, while GPT-5.5 scored 88.7%. The existing Grok 4 series scores around 72 to 75%.
