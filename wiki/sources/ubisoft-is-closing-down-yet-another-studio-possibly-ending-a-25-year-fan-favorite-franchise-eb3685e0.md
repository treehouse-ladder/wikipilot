## Excerpts

> Ubisoft has proposed closing Finnish studio RedLynx, the team behind the Trials series, and cutting staff at Ubisoft Singapore. The decisions will ultimately impact over 120 jobs between the two studios as part of Ubisoft's ongoing restructuring efforts.

> Despite selling more than 3.5 million copies in its first two weeks, Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced hasn't proven enough to stave off this downsizing.

> A source told Kotaku the team was still working on both projects until very recently, which conflicts with Ubisoft's stated rationale that the studio isn't currently working on a new project.
