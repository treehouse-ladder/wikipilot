## Excerpts

> The Xbox union layoffs sparked the first coordinated multi-studio labor action in gaming history on July 15, 2026, as hundreds of developers marched simultaneously at six Xbox locations across four cities.

> Its immediate cause is Microsoft's July 6 announcement of approximately 3,200 Xbox job eliminations — 440 of which were union-represented positions across Bethesda Game Studios, ZeniMax Online Studios, id Software, ZeniMax Workers United QA, and ZeniMax corporate.

> Workers under the OneBGS banner launched coordinated demonstrations outside ZeniMax studio offices in Rockville, Maryland; Austin and Dallas, Texas; and Montreal, Canada, with a separately organized rally at Obsidian Entertainment in Irvine, California bringing Blizzard employees out in solidarity, while United Videogame Workers-CWA staged a concurrent march at Microsoft's main campus in Redmond, Washington.

> Microsoft was legally required to sit down with the union for 'Effects Bargaining,' and the union demanded preferential transfers, stronger severance and extended healthcare, as well as recall rights to ensure their laid-off members are the first ones hired back when BGS expands.
