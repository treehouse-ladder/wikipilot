## Excerpts

> Claude Mythos Preview is a new general-purpose language model that performs strongly across the board, but is strikingly capable at computer security tasks. Claude Mythos Preview is state-of-the-art on SWE-bench Verified (93.9%), GPQA Diamond (94.6%), USAMO (97.6%), Terminal-Bench 2.0 (82.0%), CyberGym (83.1%), and Cybench (100% pass@1, saturated).

> Anthropic does not plan to make Claude Mythos Preview generally available; access is invitation-only as part of Project Glasswing because its autonomous cybersecurity capabilities are judged too powerful to ship without additional safeguards.
