## Excerpts

> Cloud Chamber, the developer working on BioShock 4 alongside publisher 2K, is 'reducing the size of the development team' amidst leadership changes.

> 80 employees from Cloud Chamber were let go, leaving approximately 170 people remaining at the studio. This amounted to about a third of the staff at the roughly 250-person studio.

> The news came mere weeks after a Bloomberg report on August 1, which stated that BioShock 4 recently failed a publisher review, which reportedly led to leadership changes and a narrative overhaul.

> Former Diablo general manager Rod Fergusson has been appointed as Cloud Chamber's new studio head. Fergusson previously took over production of BioShock Infinite and helped guide that game to completion.
