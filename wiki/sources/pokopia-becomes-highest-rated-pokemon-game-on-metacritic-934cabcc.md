## Excerpts

> Pokopia is sitting at a 90 on Metacritic, the highest aggregated score of any game in the series on the site.

> Several reviewers are saying it's a great proof of concept for The Pokemon Company to let more partner developers take on the monster-taming series, as the Dragon Quest Builders team at Omega Force has pulled off something special with the cozy life sim.

> Pokopia takes place in a version of the Pokemon world where humanity has disappeared, so it's one of the few entries where we see these critters interacting as they might if they weren't under constant threat of being captured and used for competitive sport.
