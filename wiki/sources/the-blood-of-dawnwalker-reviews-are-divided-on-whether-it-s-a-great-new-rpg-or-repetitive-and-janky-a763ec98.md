## Excerpts

> Blood of Dawnwalker reviews are split on the RPG's formula: Vice awarded a perfect 10/10 calling it one of the best RPGs since The Witcher 3, IGN gave 9/10, and Destructoid 9.5/10, while other outlets flagged repetitive quest design and a janky launch build.

> The game is the debut from Rebel Wolves, a Polish studio of former CD Projekt Red developers led by The Witcher 3 game director Konrad Tomaszkiewicz.
