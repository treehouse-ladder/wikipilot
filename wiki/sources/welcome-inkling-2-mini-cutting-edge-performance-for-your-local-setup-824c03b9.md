## Excerpts

> Inkling 2 Mini is a fast, powerful model that's small enough to run on your laptop. The middle 12 layers (layers 6 to 17) run three times per token with shared weights, giving 5.4B effective active parameters per token from 3.4B active parameters and 26.4B total parameters. Inkling 2 Mini leads every open-weights model with less than 100B total parameters on benchmarks such as DeepSWE v1.1 (48.2%) and Vals Index v2.2 (46.5%). It is released under an Apache 2.0 license with text and image input and a context window of up to 1M tokens. It is the first looped transformer the lab has released, and the team studied how looping affects monitorability.
