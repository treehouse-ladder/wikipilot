## Excerpts

> Oscar Jilsen and Mikael Mard, two of the co-founders of Coffee Stain Studios, have unveiled a new studio alongside Paradox's former product owner and programmer manager, Johan Gustafsson. Feeble Minds is a ten-person Swedish studio founded in 2022.

> Feeble Minds is working on a four-player soulslike dubbed Serpent's Gaze, and have already slapped a demo for their debut project on Steam.
