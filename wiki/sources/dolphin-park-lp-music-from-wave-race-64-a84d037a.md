## Excerpts

> Mana Wave Media released the Wave Race 64 jazz fusion arrangement 'Dolphin Park' on vinyl. The release is a single LP on 'ocean' blue colored vinyl, limited to 500 copies, and is priced at $30.

> Dolphin Park is a musical tribute to Wave Race 64, featuring full band arrangements of songs from the game with nine tracks, eight musicians, funk, sax, guitars, slap bass and good vibes. The album also features two original tracks, Victory! and Seafoam Shoreline.

> The vinyl is housed in a single-pocket tip-on jacket with new art and an obi strip, and is in hand and ready to ship.
