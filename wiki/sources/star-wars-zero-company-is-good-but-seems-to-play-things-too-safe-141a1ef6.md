## Excerpts

> Zero Company seems to play things too safe, sticking to a tried-and-true formula that works. I was hoping 'XCOM but Star Wars' would have been a little bit more than just Star Wars painted over XCOM.

> What surprised me was just how much Zero Company puts an emphasis on story and characters. Between missions, you'll return to your team's hub and, as Hawks, you can walk around and talk to your squadmates ... having a very returning-to-Garreg-Mach-Monastery or hanging-in-the-Abbey feel, like in Fire Emblem: Three Houses and Midnight Suns.

> Zero Company is a good game, but there's nothing about it that makes it really stand out from its peers.
