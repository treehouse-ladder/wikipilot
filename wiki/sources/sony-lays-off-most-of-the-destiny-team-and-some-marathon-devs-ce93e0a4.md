## Excerpts

> A Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification (WARN notice) filed in Washington has revealed that at least 292 employees will be laid off at Bungie. Employees were notified on June 25, 2026, with separations expected to take effect on July 9, 2026.

> Sony Interactive Entertainment is laying off most of the Destiny team, some Marathon developers, and eliminating a number of roles across SIE teams that support the studio's operations. Bungie said that Destiny 2 fell short of expectations these past several years and that with future projects still in early incubation, it unfortunately could not continue operating at its previous size.

> Sony says Marathon remains an important part of its portfolio and that it will continue to support the team.
