> Famous game director Fumito Ueda says his studio does not use AI for 'game development.' The designer said his new studio genDESIGN only uses genAI for administrative assistance, including coming up with schedules and summarizing meeting notes, but not creating placeholder assets for the game. Gen Atlas got a new trailer at Summer Game Fest 2026, and Ueda's stance on avoiding generative AI for creative work stands in contrast to other studios that have faced criticism for their use of AI in game development during 2026.
