## Excerpts

> Ubisoft confirmed it cut 166 jobs in Montreal and Quebec City, citing changing production needs. The 166 positions come mostly from production and support roles, according to GamesIndustry.biz.

> Some proportion of that number is made up of employees from Vantage Studio, the joint venture with Tencent that oversees Assassin's Creed, Far Cry, and Rainbow Six.

> The company is targeting 1.25 billion euros in cost reductions by March 2028. Ubisoft says that its headcount has gone down by nearly 1,100 over the last two quarters.
