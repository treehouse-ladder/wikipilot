## Excerpts

> The Painkiller RTX team leveraged generative AI, especially PBRFusion, to upscale thousands of legacy textures into high-quality Physically Based Rendering (PBR) materials, drastically cutting down repetitive manual work and allowing contributors of various backgrounds to focus on creative decisions. Quinn Baddams, team lead and founder of Merry Pencil Studios, estimates AI automation eliminated roughly 80% of repetitive work, freeing the team to focus on the 20% requiring artistic judgment. PBRFusion was always intended to be a tool, not a drop-in replacement, and the model went through three major iterations and over 1,000 hours of development before reaching the version used in production.
