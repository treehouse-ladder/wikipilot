## Excerpts

> Claude Haiku 5.5 is our cheapest, fastest, and most capable small model, built for high-volume, cost-sensitive work such as summarization, subagents, and browser use. On average, it now costs around 75% less to run.

> For prompts up to 100K tokens, Claude Haiku 5.5 is priced at $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens. For prompts over 100K tokens, it is $0.50 per million input tokens and $2.50 per million output tokens. This is 90% lower than Claude Haiku 4.5 for requests up to 100,000 tokens, and 50% lower for requests over 100,000 tokens.

> Claude Haiku 5.5 completes the Claude 5.5 family alongside Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5. It is available on the Claude Platform, AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure, and in Claude.ai and Claude Code. Sonnet 5.5 cache reads now cost 50% less: $0.10 per million tokens rather than $0.20.
