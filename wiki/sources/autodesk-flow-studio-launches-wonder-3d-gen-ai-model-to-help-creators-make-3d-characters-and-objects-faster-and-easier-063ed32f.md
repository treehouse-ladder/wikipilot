## Excerpts

> Autodesk introduced Wonder 3D, a new generative AI model within Autodesk Flow Studio, designed to help artists, studios, and emerging creators turn text and images into editable 3D assets faster and more intuitively. Wonder 3D expands access to 3D creation across game development, film, marketing, and physical prototyping.

> Wonder 3D supports USD, STL and OBJ exports, which means characters and props can move beyond the screen into 3D printing, physical prototyping, and real-world model creation. With Text to 3D, Image to 3D, and Text to Image capabilities, Wonder 3D enables creators to generate 3D assets from simple text or reference images, then refine, remix, and reuse them across projects.

> Indie developers, hobbyists, and content creators can build high-quality 3D assets without large art teams or specialized pipelines. Wonder 3D can help generate assets for storyboard development, prop creation, character ideation, and scene building.
