## Excerpts

> The trial will begin March 2 and run for 12 court days, with the trial concluding by mid-March 2027.

> Under the merger agreement, the company must pay WBD shareholders roughly $7 million for every day after September 30 that the deal has not closed. A March trial date means Paramount will owe well over $1 billion in so-called 'ticking fees' before the judge ever rules.

> The trial date represents a compromise between Paramount's request for a November trial and the challengers' request for an April start date.
