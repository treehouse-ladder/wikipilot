## Excerpts

> Ubisoft has proposed closing its Finnish studio RedLynx, the developer behind the Trials series, and cutting staff at Ubisoft Singapore.

> Ubisoft's stated reason is that the studio isn't currently working on a new project, so downsizing alone would not make it sustainable.

> Under Finnish law, the closure will result in 52 lost jobs, pending change negotiations. Ubisoft said 70 positions have been impacted at Ubisoft Singapore.

> The announcement is part of Ubisoft's wider restructuring, which began earlier in the year.
