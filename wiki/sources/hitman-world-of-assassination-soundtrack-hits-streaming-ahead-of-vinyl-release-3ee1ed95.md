## Excerpts

> The Hitman: World Of Assassination soundtrack features 44 tracks from composer Niels Bye Nielsen, spanning HITMAN (2016), HITMAN 2 and HITMAN III, including Sniper Challenge and Freelancer bonus tracks.

> Game developers IO Interactive created a bespoke sleeve design 'themed around the centres of power in the World Of Assassination,' for the 4LP boxset. The music ranges from EDM and German techno to Argentinian tango; from solo piano sonatas to South American guitar; and freely blends orchestral, rock, and synth elements. The collection was specially mastered for vinyl by Joe Caithness.

> The 4LP Hitman: World Of Assassination soundtrack vinyl is set to be released in January 2027 and the boxset is available to pre-order now.
