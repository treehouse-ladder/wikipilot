## Excerpts

> Square Enix officially unveiled Final Fantasy Revelation at Summer Game Fest 2026 as the final chapter in the Final Fantasy 7 Remake trilogy, taking an open-world approach with the Highwind airship and set to release simultaneously on all platforms in Spring 2027. The final chapter takes an open-world approach that has you flying the Highwind airship all over the planet. When you reach a destination, you'll leap off and parachute to the ground.
