## Excerpts

> Skydance is merging Paramount and Warner Bros.' game studios into one unit.

> The Warner Bros. studios that now sit in this unit include Hogwarts Legacy's developer and the Mortal Kombat studio, among others.

> It is still unclear what the division's plans are or how many layoffs it will face.

> Tony Driscoll will lead the combined division.
