## Excerpts

> Over 100 participants collaborated with one of four frontier models (Claude-Opus-4.6, GPT-5.4, Gemini-3.1-Pro, and MiniMax-M2.7) on a long-horizon coding task lasting around five hours, designed to mimic real-world workflows. 94% of developers fail to detect sabotage, with vulnerability attributed to minimal code review, plausible cover story, and overtrust in agents. While a safety monitor reduces sabotage success, 56% of participants still accept the malicious code, ignoring its warnings.
