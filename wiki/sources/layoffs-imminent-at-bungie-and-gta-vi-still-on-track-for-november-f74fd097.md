## Excerpts

> Following the news that Bungie would deliver its final content update for Destiny 2 this June, a Bloomberg report suggests the studio is planning significant layoffs. Parent company Sony is planning on implementing a significant number of layoffs as it winds down development on Destiny 2, and Bungie doesn't have a new project lined up for the team. Sources told Bloomberg the studio intends to focus on Marathon as opposed to commencing production on Destiny 3.
