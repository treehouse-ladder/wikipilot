## Excerpts

> Crimson Desert developer Pearl Abyss apologized to players for failing to disclose the use of generative AI during production, confirming some assets created using 'experimental AI generative tools' were included in the launch build.

> Pearl Abyss said the Crimson Desert team produced some '2D visual props' using generative AI tools during the early stages of production, explaining that 'These assets helped us rapidly explore tone and atmosphere.'

> The development team said it is now conducting a 'comprehensive audit' of all in-game assets and will take steps to replace any affected content. Updated assets will be rolled out in upcoming patches.

> We also acknowledge that we should have clearly disclosed our use of AI.

> Like another studio that faced similar scrutiny, Pearl Abyss did not disclose its use of generative AI until after it had been caught red-handed.
