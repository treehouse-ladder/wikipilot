## Excerpts

> Capcom wants the RE Engine's code to be easier for AI tools to read, write, and test, building the engine on a unified base language that the company says is easy for human developers to use and easy for AI to read.

> One system is an autonomous play AI for QA that can judge a game from its screen and audio much as a human player would, using two layers based on the human dual-process model: one for fast reactive decisions and one for slower deliberate reasoning.

> Capcom says some of the technology will be open-sourced to allow for AI training.
