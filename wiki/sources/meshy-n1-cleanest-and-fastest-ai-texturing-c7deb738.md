## Excerpts

> Meshy N1 is our first native texturing model. Instead of projecting multiple 2D images onto a mesh, it generates color directly on the 3D surface, which removes seams and color bleeding.

> N1 is the fastest texturing model on the market, producing textured output in about 20 seconds, compared with roughly a minute for Meshy 7.

> N1 outputs a 2K base color map only. It does not generate PBR maps and does not support 4K or 8K resolution. It works from reference images, not text prompts.

> Meshy N1 is available to all registered users. Multi-view texturing, which takes several reference images, is limited to subscribers.

> N1 is available in the Meshy API through Retexture, Image to 3D, Multi-Image to 3D, and Text to 3D refine, and is the default texture mode for T-series models in the web app.
