## Excerpts

> Meshy 7.5 began rolling out on November 24, 2026, and reached all registered users by November 27, 2026.

> With the Add Low-Poly option enabled, a single generation returns both the high-poly mesh and a low-poly mesh at about 2% of the polycount, with a normal map baked from the high-poly surface. Meshy 7.5 bakes the normal map from the raw generated geometry (several hundred million triangles) rather than the simplified high-poly download. The feature costs 10 extra credits per generation.

> Meshy 7.5 uses a new UV unwrapping algorithm. On a 33-object test set it reports the lowest angle distortion, area distortion, and stretch compared with Hi3D 3.0 and Tripo 3.1, while using more texture space than both rivals on most objects.

> On a 42-object test set spanning Latin, Cyrillic, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean lettering, Meshy 7.5 achieves the highest overall text recovery rate at 99.9%. Scoring used a vision-language model to transcribe renders of the untextured meshes.
