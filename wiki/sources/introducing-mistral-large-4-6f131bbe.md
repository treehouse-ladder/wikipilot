## Excerpts

> Mistral Large 4 is a 1 trillion-parameter natively multimodal mixture-of-experts model (1.05T total, 49B active), nicknamed 'le Chonk'. It accepts text and image inputs with a 1.6B-parameter vision encoder, supports a 1 million-token context window, function calling, and structured outputs.

> Training used 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs at Mistral's European datacenters on data spanning more than 160 languages, including every official EU language.

> Preview API pricing starts at $1.36 per 1M input tokens and $4.18 per 1M output tokens. Weights will be released by the end of October 2026.
