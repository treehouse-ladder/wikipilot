## Excerpts

> Sony-owned Sucker Punch has laid off 24 employees. The studio behind Ghost of Tsushima and Ghost of Yotei confirmed the cuts after they were first reported by Aftermath on October 27, 2026.

> Several positions across Sucker Punch will be eliminated as a result. The internal email tied the decision to both market conditions and the team's needs for its next project.

> The layoffs amount to a total staff reduction of around 10 percent of the studio.
