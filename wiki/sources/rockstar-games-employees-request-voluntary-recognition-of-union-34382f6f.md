## Excerpts

> The Rockstar Games Workers Union, backed by the Independent Workers' Union of Great Britain, has requested voluntary recognition from Rockstar Games, four months ahead of the GTA 6 launch.

> If Rockstar agrees to voluntary recognition, the union would be able to bargain legally over pay and working conditions. If it refuses, the case goes to the UK's Central Arbitration Committee for statutory recognition.
