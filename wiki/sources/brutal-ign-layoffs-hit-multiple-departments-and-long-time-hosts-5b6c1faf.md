## Excerpts

> Layoffs hit multiple departments across the video game website's business. The top video game website cut loose some of its most public faces like long-time video hosts Seth Macy and Brian Altano.

> Brian Altano has worked at IGN since 2009 and has been one of the main hosts of its popular PlayStation podcast Beyond. Macy was a senior commerce team editor at the site and frequent host on its popular Nintendo Voice Chat podcast. Additionally, Max Scoville, one of IGN's other best-known video hosts, also departed the website as part of the organizational shift.

> The IGN Creators Guild reported being blindsided by the layoff of four union members and many more people across the organization who contributed years of their careers to IGN.

> The latest round of cuts comes just months after a smaller group of employees, including engineers, were laid off just days after IGN Live wrapped up, with one IGN Creators Guild member noting the company had a habit of doing layoffs right after a successful event.
