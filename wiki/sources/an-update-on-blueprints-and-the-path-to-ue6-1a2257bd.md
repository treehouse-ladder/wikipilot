## Excerpts

> Unreal Engine 6 will not include Blueprints or the Actor Gameplay Framework. Gameplay will be built using Verse and the new Scene Graph framework. Two weeks ago, we experimented with using AI to convert all of the Blueprints for Battle Royale to Verse... a working prototype of Fortnite at about 3 million lines of Verse code after porting more than 20,000 Blueprints.

> AI will massively help with converting UE5 projects to UE6, if not make that process fully automatic.
