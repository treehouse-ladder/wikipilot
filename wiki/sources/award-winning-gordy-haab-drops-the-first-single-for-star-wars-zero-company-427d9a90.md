## Excerpts

> Gordy Haab, Grammy winner for the score of Star Wars Jedi: Survivor, along with accolades for his work on Star Wars: Battlefront II and Indiana Jones and the Great Circle, released the very first, self-titled track from the Zero Company soundtrack. This brief piece clocks in at just 1:17 and sets the tone for this tactical game set during the Clone Wars, complete with a motif that fans will easily recognize. The whole soundtrack will be released on August 27th.
