## Excerpts

> As coding agents get more reliable, Willison is not reviewing every line of code that they write anymore, even for his production-level work. He identifies an element of "normalization of deviance" here - every time a model turns out to have written the right code without close monitoring, there's a risk that he'll trust it at the wrong moment in the future.
