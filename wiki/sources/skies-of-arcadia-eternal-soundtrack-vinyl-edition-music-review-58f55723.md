## Excerpts

> The Skies of Arcadia Eternal Soundtrack Vinyl Edition by Wayo Records is a collection of roughly half the original music with two new piano arrangements, and it's a licensed SEGA product.

> The special edition box set includes commentary from the producer (Rieko Kodama) and director (Shuntaro Tanaka), insights from the music team (Yutaka Minobe, Tatsuyuki Maeda, and arranger/performer Ramon van Engelenhoven), and lovely new artwork. The first print features a lovely light blue marbled pattern that looks like a cloudy sky.

> The real charm of this collection lies in the two solo piano arrangements on the last disc by Ramon van Engelenhoven, which give 'Voyage En Arcadia' and 'Souvenir d'Arcadia' arrangements that make piano right at home in Arcadia.

> The vinyl format is well-suited to the music, and when listening, it feels like each part in a given song is clear, making it easier to hear how they complement each other. It's definitely the most fitting alternative to the game experience due to the mastering and suitability of the music for vinyl.
