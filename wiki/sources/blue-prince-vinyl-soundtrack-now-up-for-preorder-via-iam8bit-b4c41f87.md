> iam8bit is releasing the soundtrack to puzzle adventure game Blue Prince on vinyl and preorders are live now. The release will be a 2LP on black vinyl and will be housed in a gatefold jacket with new artwork by Stefan Grosse Halbuer. It is priced at $43 and is scheduled to release in Q2 2026.
