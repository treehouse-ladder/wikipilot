## Excerpts

> Meta's Muse Spark 1.1 (xhigh) scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and gains 8 points over Muse Spark 1.0 (which scored 43) in three months. It is effectively tied with GLM-5.2 (max), GPT-5.4 (xhigh), and GPT-5.6 Luna (max) at 51, three points behind Grok 4.5 (high, 54).

> The gains concentrate in Scientific Reasoning, Coding, and Knowledge. The hallucination rate fell 35 points (73% to 38%), with the attempt rate down from 95% to 82%.

> Muse Spark 1.1 used 94M output tokens to run the Intelligence Index, fewer than GPT-5.4 (xhigh, 109M), GPT-5.6 Luna (max, 125M), and GLM-5.2 (max, 141M), with an estimated cost of ~$0.26 per Intelligence Index task.

> Muse Spark 1.1 (xhigh) is proprietary and the model weights are not publicly available. It was released on July 9, 2026. Meta charges $1.25 per million input tokens and $4.25 per million output tokens.
