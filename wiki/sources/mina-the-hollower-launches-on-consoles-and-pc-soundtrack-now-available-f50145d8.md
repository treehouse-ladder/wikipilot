## Excerpts

> Yacht Club Games has released Mina the Hollower on consoles and PC alongside a 96-track original soundtrack now available on Bandcamp. Fans can spend whatever amount they want to obtain this soundtrack in various formats, such as MP3 and FLAC; it is also available as DLC on Steam.

> Mina's soundtrack uses the Game Boy sound chip combined with the Konami Sound Cartridge (052539 / SCC+), an expansion for the MSX computer, with tracks 57 and 63 written by Yuzo Koshiro.
