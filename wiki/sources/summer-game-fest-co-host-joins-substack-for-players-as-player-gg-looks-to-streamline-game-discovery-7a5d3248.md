## Excerpts

> Lucy James, who is co-hosting Summer Game Fest Live with Keighley, has made a major move in the industry. Lucy James spent over a decade as a video producer and on-air personality at GameSpot, and is now leaving to join the growing ranks of games media talent who are going independent. She's partnered with Player.gg, a platform aimed specifically at helping people discover new games that is going public this week and partnering with Geoff Keighley's Summer Game Fest to kick things off, and James' lookingfor.game newsletter will curate PC gaming recommendations.
