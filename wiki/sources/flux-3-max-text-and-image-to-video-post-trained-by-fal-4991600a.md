## Excerpts

> FLUX 3 Max is a post-trained video model from fal Research, built with Black Forest Labs on the FLUX 3 foundation model.

> FLUX 3 Max is tied for joint #1 in image-to-video on Artificial Analysis's preliminary arena, a provisional ranking, with a 1,139 Elo score that ties Seedance 2.5 in blind human preference votes.

> Three endpoints are listed: text-to-video, image-to-video, and keyframes-to-video. Text-to-video supports clips from 5 to 20 seconds, and output is 720p video with native audio. The keyframes endpoint accepts up to ten keyframes.

> fal's model listing shows $0.085/s for each variant.

> fal offers a free tier at fal.ai/flux-video-free, where signed-in users can generate five 720p videos with sound from a text prompt every day.
