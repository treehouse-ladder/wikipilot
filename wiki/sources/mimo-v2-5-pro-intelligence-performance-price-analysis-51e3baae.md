## Excerpts

> Kimi K2.6 (54) and MiMo-V2.5-Pro (54) are tied as the top open weights models by Intelligence Index, followed by DeepSeek V4 Pro (Reasoning, Max Effort) (52).

> MiMo-V2.5-Pro is a Mixture of Experts (MoE) model with 1.0 trillion total parameters, but only 42 billion active parameters are used during inference.

> MiMo-V2.5-Pro is open weights, with model weights publicly available and downloadable for self-hosting. The model is released under the MIT license. The model supports text input, outputs text, and has a 1m tokens context window.

> Pricing for MiMo-V2.5-Pro is $1.00 per 1M input tokens and $3.00 per 1M output tokens. It is slower than average and very verbose.
