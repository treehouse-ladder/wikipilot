## Excerpts

> Series creator and lead producer Kenji Kanno clarified the team's use of generative AI during a hands-off gameplay demonstration. Kanno stated that 'generative AI' is 'one small part of the creative process.' Artists and designers visited actual locations, took references, and based their designs on that, with AI being used 'as a hint or part of the ideation.' In a statement to Game Informer, Sega said the tech was used in 'the development of background assets,' and that anything AI-generated was 'still subject to review by the development team.'
