## Excerpts

> Part NieR: Automata, part magical girl visual novel, Kernel Hearts had me hooked with its fantastic score.

> The soundtrack mixes airy vocals with acoustic guitars and hip-hop blast beats.

> The soundtrack features iconic NieR vocalist Emi Evans, Pernelle (known for work on Goddess of Victory: NIKKE and Stellar Blade), and RINO.

> French composer Pretty Patterns, whose real name is Diego Cabrera, packed as many genres as possible into the 18 tracks for Ephemera Games' debut title.
