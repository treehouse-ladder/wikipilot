## Excerpts

> The show was headlined by third-party announcements and a few first-party surprises, including the very first official tease of the remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. The show kicked off with Rhythm Heaven Groove, with over 80 minigames in single-player and over 30 in co-op multiplayer launching on July 2 for Switch. Deltarune Chapter 5 was revealed, and it's out on June 5. Rayman was reimagined as a classic platformer available on Switch starting October 1.
