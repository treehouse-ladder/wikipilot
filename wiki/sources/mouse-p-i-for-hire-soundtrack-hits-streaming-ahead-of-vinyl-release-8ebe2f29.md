## Excerpts

> The soundtrack was composed by Patryk Scelina and features French electro-swing band Caravan Palace. The score is rooted in big band swing, and as a crime and investigation story, the music was approached like an old film-noir detective tale. The LP features all 19 of Patryk Scelina's original tracks alongside Caravan Palace's 'Good Mouse'. The soundtrack is getting a vinyl release via Mondo, with the vinyl shipping in June, pressed onto a Mondo Exclusive Black and White Splatter vinyl.
