## Excerpts

> Street Fighter 6 has unveiled its new roster of fighters for its fourth year during Summer Game Fest 2026, and Final Fantasy 7's Tifa is joining the fight. Also announced in Street Fighter 6's season 4 roster are three all-new characters: Bosch, Yasmine and Arjun, which will make this season's roster the first with no returning characters in the series.
