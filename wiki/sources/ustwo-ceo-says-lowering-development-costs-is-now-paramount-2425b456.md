## Excerpts

> Ustwo Games CEO Maria Sayans says lowering development costs and recruiting more contractors will be fundamental to ensuring the Monument Valley developer can thrive as a PC-first studio.

> 'We've been a little bit too romantic about the idea that we should have employees and give people long-term job security,' adding that this is something she 'hates' about the industry.

> Ustwo currently employs just under 30 people, down from a peak during Monument Valley 3's development.
