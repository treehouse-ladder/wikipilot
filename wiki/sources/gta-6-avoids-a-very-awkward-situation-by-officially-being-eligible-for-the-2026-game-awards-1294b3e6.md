## Excerpts

> GTA 6 will officially be eligible for the 2026 Game Awards, despite its November 19 release date landing after the ceremony's traditional eligibility window.

> The Game Awards confirmed the eligibility cutoff would accommodate Rockstar's long-awaited sequel, avoiding an awkward situation where the year's biggest game would be shut out of Game of the Year contention until 2027.
