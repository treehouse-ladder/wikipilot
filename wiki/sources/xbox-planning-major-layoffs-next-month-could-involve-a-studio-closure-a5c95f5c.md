## Excerpts

> Xbox is planning major layoffs next month, with reports suggesting the cuts could involve at least one studio closure. The layoffs are expected to come after Microsoft's fiscal year ends on June 30, 2026.

> Sources indicate the scale of the upcoming Xbox layoffs will be significant, potentially one of the larger rounds of cuts the division has seen in recent years, as part of the 'reset' CEO Asha Sharma has described.

> The possibility of a studio closure alongside the layoffs marks an escalation from earlier reporting, which had focused on headcount reductions rather than outright studio shutdowns.
