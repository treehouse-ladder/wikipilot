## Excerpts

> Ubisoft has initiated discussions with the Works Council regarding a proposed voluntary departure plan across Ubisoft Dusseldorf, Ubisoft Berlin and Ubisoft Mainz, aiming for roughly 100 of about 600 employees across the three studios to accept buyouts.

> German staff were hit with 185 layoffs in 2025. The Anno, Rayman, and Prince of Persia franchises fall under these studios, which sit within Ubisoft's restructuring into five creative houses.
