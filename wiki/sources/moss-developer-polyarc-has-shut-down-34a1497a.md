## Excerpts

> Polyarc, a Seattle-based VR specialist, has closed its doors after over a decade. The company was best known for developing the Moss franchise, which began life as a VR series before eventually being reimagined for more traditional platforms.

> Polyarc had significantly downsized in March 2026 after experiencing financing challenges. The decision to lay off staff came as a result of being unable to secure funding after the cancellation of a major project.

> Almost 30 people have been let go, though this number may include some staff members laid off earlier in the year. The studio confirmed its closure in a brief post on LinkedIn.
