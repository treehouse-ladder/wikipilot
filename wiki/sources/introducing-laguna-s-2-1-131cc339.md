## Excerpts

> Laguna S 2.1 is the most capable agentic coding model in its weight class by a wide margin. S 2.1 scores 70.2% on Terminal-Bench 2.1 in Poolside's agent harness with thinking enabled.

> Laguna S 2.1 is a 118B total parameter Mixture-of-Experts model with 8B activated parameters per token, built for agentic coding and long-horizon work.

> Laguna S 2.1 supports up to 1M tokens of context for long-horizon coding and research workflows. Its compact size makes it uniquely suitable for complex work on local machines.

> You can run Laguna S 2.1 locally through pool, vLLM, SGLang, Ollama, llama.cpp, MLX, or NVIDIA TensorRT-LLM, or access it through OpenRouter and the Poolside API.
