## Excerpts

> Modern Warfare 4's campaign is the best the series has produced since Modern Warfare (2019), though it doesn't do anything better than the franchise's most notable campaigns.

> The story threads the Korean invasion together with Price's revenge plot, and the narrative flows well despite shifting perspectives and a non-chronological structure. The final boss fights are the main letdown, being neither challenging nor long enough for the climax.
