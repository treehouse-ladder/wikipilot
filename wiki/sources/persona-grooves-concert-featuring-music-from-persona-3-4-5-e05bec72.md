## Excerpts

> Persona Grooves is an extraordinary performance that will let fans immerse themselves in the vibrant sounds of Persona 3, 4, and 5, as the Bartosz Pernal jazz orchestra joins forces with the renowned London Mozart Players for an unforgettable celebration of the Persona series' 30th anniversary.

> The concert presents a unique reinterpretation of Shoji Meguro's iconic music, merging the explosive energy of jazz-funk with the refined richness of a classical orchestra. Composer Shoji Meguro himself will join the stage as a special guest.

> Game Music Festival 2026 will conclude during the Saturday evening of June 27th with the Persona Grooves concert. The event will take place at Southbank Centre's Royal Festival Hall.
