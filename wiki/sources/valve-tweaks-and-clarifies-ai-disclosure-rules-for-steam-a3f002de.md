## Excerpts

> Developers do not need to disclose if they used 'AI powered tools' used for workflow purposes, but they do need to list any use of 'AI to generate content for the game' and/or 'AI content generated during gameplay.'

> If any generative AI content makes it into the game or the game's associated materials, developers will need to disclose it. When AI is used to create content, Valve says this disclosure counts for the game itself alongside associated material like marketing assets or the store page.

> For AI content generated during gameplay, developers must check a box if the game itself makes AI-generated images, text, or other content. Additionally, developers must fill out a text box detailing their use of the tools.
