> Apple unveiled a Gemini-powered Siri at WWDC 2026 on June 8, 2026. A custom 1.2-trillion-parameter Gemini model, eight times larger than Apple's current 150-billion-parameter foundation model, will power the new Siri. The new Siri runs on Gemini under a multi-year deal worth roughly  billion per year. Apple processes Gemini model queries through its Private Cloud Compute framework; Gemini model weights run within Apple's infrastructure, not on Google's servers.
