## Excerpts

> Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced is out today and is already having one of the best Steam launches in the franchise's history. Unfortunately, the celebrations are bittersweet for developers who worked on the game at Ubisoft Barcelona, as the studio's team there is facing layoffs at the end of the month after not being assigned to a new project.

> One affected developer wrote "this is not how I imagined it would end" after the record commercial launch of a game they helped ship.

> The Ubisoft Barcelona layoffs come as part of ongoing restructuring, with developers who delivered an Assassin's Creed-record Steam launch (2M day-one sales, 99,451 concurrent peak) facing job loss at month's end.
