## Excerpts

> Xbox is laying off up to 3,200 workers — 1,600 immediately, and up to 1,600 more over the coming fiscal year — while cutting four studios loose: Ninja Theory, Undead Labs, Compulsion Games, and Double Fine.

> Ninja Theory and Undead Labs are being sold to undisclosed buyers, while Compulsion Games and Double Fine are being returned to their management teams to become independent studios that keep their IP and revenue.

> Arkane's management in Lyon is beginning required consultation with its Works Council to review potential strategic options.
