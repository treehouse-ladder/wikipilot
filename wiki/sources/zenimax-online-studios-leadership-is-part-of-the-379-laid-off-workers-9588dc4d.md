## Excerpts

> A Maryland WARN Act notice shows 213 employees laid off from ZeniMax Online, and 166 from ZeniMax Media, for a total of 379. Almost all of the 379 workers were notified of their layoff on July 6, with the cuts becoming effective on September 4, 2026.

> The cuts include Joe Burba, a 14-year veteran of the studio who was appointed to run it last July, as well as The Elder Scrolls Online executive producer Susan Kath, production director Ala Diaz, and studio game director Rich Lambert.

> Over half of the layoffs come from the Elder Scrolls Online studio, which has lost at least 60 percent of its staff in the past year.
