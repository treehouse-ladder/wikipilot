## Excerpts

> Harness design plays a critical role in agent performance by shaping how large language models (LLMs) perceive, reason over, and act within executable environments.

> existing methods often overfit to the evolution tasks, rely exclusively on trajectory-derived signals, and optimize harness components jointly, causing interference across components.

> it enforces global constraints on evolution, restricting modifications to task-agnostic harness changes that generalize beyond the evolution tasks.

> It augments trajectory-derived evidence with proactive first-person feedback from the agent about harness usage, yielding richer signals for evolution.

> It decouples the optimization of different harness components before consolidating them, addressing the problem that existing methods optimize harness components jointly, causing interference across components.
