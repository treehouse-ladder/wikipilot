## Excerpts

> Grand Theft Auto VI is the headline release of the month, launching November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S after two delays.

> The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake arrives November 5 on Switch 2, a reimagining of the 1998 original with overhauled gameplay and fully voiced cutscenes.

> World of Warcraft: Forever launches November 4 on PC as a permanent new mode for Blizzard's MMORPG.

> Zero Parades: Director's Cut arrives November 3 on PC and PS5; the enhanced edition adds nearly 40,000 words of dialogue, new scenes, and alternative ways to solve challenges.

> GTA 6's launch has pushed many titles away from the month, though standouts like Pikmin 4 Nintendo Switch 2 Edition and Stellar Blade: Complete Edition remain.
