## Excerpts

> After numerous rounds of layoffs, MindsEye studio Build a Rocket Boy appears to be closing down for good. Multiple Build a Rocket Boy employees have stated that their time at the MindsEye maker has come to an end, and sources have confirmed to Kotaku that the company is winding down for good.

> On September 16, 2026, a wave of Build A Rocket Boy employees announced their departures on LinkedIn — and based on the wording, this isn't just another round of targeted cuts.

> Just over one year ago, Build a Rocket Boy released MindsEye, an ambitious but somewhat bizarre third-person shooter that proved to be something of a catalyst that drove immense negativity online, gave rise to damning legal accusations and claims of sabotage, and performed so badly commercially that it inspired multiple waves of layoffs at the firm. MindsEye was released in June 2025, landing a 39 Metacritic score on PC and a 29 on PS5, making it the worst-rated game of last year.
