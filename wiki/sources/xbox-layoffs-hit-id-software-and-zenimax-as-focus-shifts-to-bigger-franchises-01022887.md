## Excerpts

> ZeniMax layoffs confirmed: id Software losing 95 employees and Bethesda 35, as Microsoft shifts focus to bigger franchises.

> id Software, the studio behind Doom and Quake, lost 95 employees in the Xbox Reset layoffs. Bethesda Game Studios, responsible for Fallout and Elder Scrolls, lost 35 staff. Microsoft is directing Bethesda to focus on Doom, Fallout, Quake, Wolfenstein, and Elder Scrolls going forward.
