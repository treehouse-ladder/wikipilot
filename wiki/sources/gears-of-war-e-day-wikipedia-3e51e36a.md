> Gears of War: E-Day is scheduled to release on October 6, 2026, and it will be available on Xbox Series X|S, Xbox on PC, and with Game Pass.

> Microsoft confirmed that Gears of War: E-Day will launch as an Xbox console exclusive, meaning the anticipated prequel will not be released on PS5 at launch.

> As the sixth mainline installment in the Gears of War franchise, it is set 14 years before the events of the original game.
