## Excerpts

> Generative AI cast a shadow over some promising games at Gamescom 2026's Opening Night Live showcase.

> The developers of Crimson Desert apologized to players for that game's usage of genAI and promised to remove the offending assets in later updates, and after players caught genAI in 1666: Amsterdam, its developers also said they would make changes.

> The only reason we know about AI usage in some cases is because of Steam's disclosure requirement, with Project Zeta, Aion 2, Train Sim World 7, Like or Die, and PUBG Ded.Net following those rules and featuring store pages that list varying degrees of genAI usage.

> Strategy game Humankind 2 was revealed during the Gamescom pre-show to viewers who immediately accused its live-action trailer of being AI slop, and the developer was promptly forced to clarify that it was filmed at Nu Boyana Film Studios in Sofia.
