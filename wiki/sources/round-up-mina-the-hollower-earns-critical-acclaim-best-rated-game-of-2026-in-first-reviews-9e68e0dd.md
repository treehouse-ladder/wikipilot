## Excerpts

> Mina the Hollower has earned an overall Metacritic score of 93, and this makes it the highest-rated game of 2026 so far on the website, beating the 91 score for Forza Horizon 6, and the 89 ratings handed to Pokemon Pokopia and Resident Evil Requiem.

> IGN gave it a 10/10, noting 'Mina achieves the same heights in both action and style that its impressive inspirations - Zelda, Castlevania, and FromSoft RPGs - reach on a regular basis, and with far fewer resources.'

> The game releases for PS5 on 29th May 2026, for $20, and is also set for release on Nintendo Switch 1 and 2, PlayStation 5, Windows PC, and Xbox Series X|S.
