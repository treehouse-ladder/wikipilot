## Excerpts

> Fareidolia features an on-device voice recognition system that lets players use their own voice to interact with the game. The system was demonstrated in the first playable version of Nexon Games' new title at Tokyo Game Show 2026, where players could speak dialogue choices into a microphone and call characters by name inside the game's living space.

> The technology runs entirely on the device rather than routing through a separate server. The developers are using feedback from the TGS demo to determine how far the feature should be expanded.
