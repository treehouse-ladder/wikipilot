## Excerpts

> The London Mozart Players: The Art of Game Music with Troy Baker and Austin Wintory Concert at Fairfield Halls on the 19th June 2026 has been cancelled due to unforeseen circumstances. Ticket holders will be contacted directly and provided a full refund.

> An evening with voice actor Troy Baker and composer Austin Wintory, two multi-award-winning artists uniting on stage for an innovative concert experience celebrating the art of video game music. Joined by the renowned London Mozart Players, they will perform selections from acclaimed soundtracks such as Journey, Assassin's Creed: Syndicate, ABZU, The Pathless, Sword of the Sea and more.

> At the heart of the evening is a unique live element: gameplay scored in real time, with music composed and adapted on stage in response to a visual prompt — a rare opportunity to see how music and concept for a game work together moment-by-moment.
