## Excerpts

> The music team is once again led by Masashi Hamauzu and Mitsuto Suzuki, and the album is mostly new material rather than the redundant content I had feared.

> The standout tracks are the jazz pieces, including the five 'The Runaround' tracks that are mainly bebop, and the zany Happy Turtle vocal tracks such as Shootie HG's Global Ver. of 'Give in to the Riptide.'

> My main complaint is that the jazz tracks never get a seamless, climactic arrangement.
