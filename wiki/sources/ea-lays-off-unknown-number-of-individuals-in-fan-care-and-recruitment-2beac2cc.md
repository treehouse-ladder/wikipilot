## Excerpts

> The total number of impacted employees is unknown, but Kotaku found online postings both from people formerly in several remote roles in the U.S. as well as a number of laid-off individuals from EA's office in Hyderabad, India. Multiple individuals laid off from the Hyderabad office had been with the company for more than ten years.

> These are the kinds of teams that quietly hold a live game business together: player support, moderation, internal tooling, and the IT backbone that keeps production and operations moving.

> EA already told employees in October that there would be no immediate changes to the workforce after agreeing to be acquired for $55 billion by a consortium led by Saudi Arabia's PIF, Silver Lake, and Affinity Partners.
