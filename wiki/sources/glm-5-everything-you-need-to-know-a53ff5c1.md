## Excerpts

> GLM-5 is the new leading open weights model, leading the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index amongst open weights models. GLM-5 was released on February 11, 2026 and was created by Z AI.

> GLM-5 is Z.AI's first new architecture since GLM-4.5, scaling to 744B total / 40B active parameters, and integrates DeepSeek Sparse Attention.

> GLM-5 scores 50 on the Intelligence Index and is the new open weights leader, up from GLM-4.7's score of 42 - an 8 point jump. GLM-5 achieves the highest Artificial Analysis Agentic Index score among open weights models with a score of 63, ranking third overall, driven by strong performance in GDPval-AA.

> GLM-5 has a context window of 200K tokens and supports text input and output only. GLM-5 is released under the MIT license, which allows commercial use.
