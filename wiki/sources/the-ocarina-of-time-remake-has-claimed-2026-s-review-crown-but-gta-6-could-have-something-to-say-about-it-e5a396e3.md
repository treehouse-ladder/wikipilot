## Excerpts

> On Metacritic, Ocarina of Time has a review aggregate score of 96 from 81 reviews, making it the highest-reviewed game of the year so far.

> Grand Theft Auto 6 could dethrone Link, since GTA reviews have historically scored similarly high, though that game hadn't launched yet when this was written.

> The 1998 original still holds a higher Metacritic score of 99.
