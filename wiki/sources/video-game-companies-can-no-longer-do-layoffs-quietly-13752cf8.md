## Excerpts

> ZeniMax and Bethesda employees held rallies to protest Xbox's latest round of layoffs that cost 1,600 people their jobs, with 1,600 more layoffs to come in the future.

> Across multiple studios in Texas, Montreal, and California, employees gathered with signs and chants criticizing the company for continuing to fail its employees.

> This is not usual, and in addition to the behind-the-scenes work of union organizing, workers have taken the fight public.
