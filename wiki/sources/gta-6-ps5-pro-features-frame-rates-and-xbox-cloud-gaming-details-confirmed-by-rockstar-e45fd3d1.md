## Excerpts

> Rockstar has confirmed that GTA 6 is targeting 30 frames per second across all platforms, including PS5, PS5 Pro, and Xbox Series X and S.

> The PS5 Pro version will use PSSR upscaling and will feature ray tracing. Xbox Cloud Streaming will be available at launch.

> GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X and S.
