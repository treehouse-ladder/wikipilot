## Excerpts

> We introduce Player-1, a post-training framework for learning game control beyond demonstrations through synthetic experience. We construct Player-1-Gym, sixteen synthetic 2D and 3D environments designed around control demands shared across games. Player-1-9B reaches 38.33% success compared with 12.63% for the strongest evaluated baseline, with further gains over SFT on ViZDoom-10 and GameWorld.
