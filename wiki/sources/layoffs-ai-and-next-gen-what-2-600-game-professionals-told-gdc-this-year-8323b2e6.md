## Excerpts

> GDC surveyed more than 2,600 game workers on everything from the latest technological advancements to how layoffs have impacted their mental health.

> Game professionals are expressing more openness to select uses of generative AI, though many say creative and player-facing content are still largely off the table.

> 40 percent of industry professionals use generative AI in the workplace.

> This is a separate, later-in-the-year edition of the State of the Game Industry survey from the roughly 2,300-respondent edition published earlier in 2026, so the 2,600 figure and the 2,300 figure come from different editions and should not be conflated.
