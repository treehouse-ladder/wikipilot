## Excerpts

> The Stop Killing Games movement has hit a major milestone with the California Assembly's passage of AB 1921, the 'Protect Our Games Act.' The vote represents the most significant North American legislative win for the movement since it began campaigning for player rights around purchased games.

> Campaign founder Scott Ross described the vote as a 'watershed moment,' noting that the bill's passage through the Assembly sends a signal to other state and national legislatures that consumer protection for purchased games is viable policy.

> The movement has also gained traction in Europe, where EU Commission meetings have included industry stakeholders on both sides of the debate, according to separate reporting. AB 1921 still requires passage through the California Senate and the governor's signature before becoming law.
