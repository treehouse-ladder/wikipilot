## Excerpts

> Autodesk has acquired the core technology of AI-based motion capture firm RADiCAL, including Motion, which powers Radical Core and Live, and Canvas, which powers its collaborative online 3D editor.

> Radical is shutting down its current web portal, which provides access to its AI mocap services Radical Core and Radical Live, and collaborative online 3D editor Radical Canvas. Users have until 6 July 2026 to download their data.

> Founded in 2017, Radical was the first of a new wave of AI-trained motion-capture platforms. Its original offline service, Radical Core, extracts the motion of an actor from single-camera video footage and exports the animation in FBX format for use in DCC apps or game engines.
