## Excerpts

> Alibaba released Qwen3.7-Plus, a multimodal model on Bailian adding image and video understanding, deep reasoning, and tool invocation. The model is now available through Alibaba Cloud's Bailian platform.

> Qwen3.7-Plus is a multimodal large language model that understands images and video, alongside written prompts. The model includes capabilities for deep reasoning that works through problems step by step, self-programming to write and revise its own code, tool invocation, output verification, and autonomous iteration.

> On ScreenSpot Pro, the model scored 79.0, significantly outpacing legacy industry standouts like GPT-5.4 (xhigh) at 67.4 and Claude-Opus-4.6 at 49.5. On Terminal Bench 2.0-Terminus, Qwen3.7-Plus scored 70.3, outperforming DeepSeek-V4-Pro Max (67.9) and Gemini-3.1 Pro (63.5).
