## Excerpts

> Summer Game Fest saw the continuation of a trend where major publishers disclosed their use of AI in development, resulting in tidal waves of both backlash and speculation. This happened twice in the past handful of days with the upcoming Tomb Raider remake and Sega's new Crazy Taxi. However, the current requirements aren't doing their job well, with instances where it's been difficult to glean the actual scope of AI usage from disclosures.
