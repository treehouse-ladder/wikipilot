## Excerpts

> Employees at World of Warcraft and Overwatch developer Blizzard have ratified a historic union contract that includes guardrails around AI usage, layoff protections, and remote and hybrid work benefits.

> Union members represented by the Communications Workers of America (CWA) claimed the new contract—which covers 1,900 employees across the Microsoft subsidiary—sets a new industry standard.

> Laid off workers the right to be "recalled" into open positions across any Blizzard bargaining unit within 14 months after their layoff was announced.

> The contract consolidates Blizzard's many smaller unions into three larger bargaining units, marking a significant milestone in video game industry unionization.
