## Excerpts

> The Infernal Symphony opens London's Game Music Festival as the official anniversary concert celebrating 30 years of Diablo. On June 6, 2026, the Royal Festival Hall in London will host the concert featuring the iconic sounds of Diablo games arranged into a monumental symphonic opus. Maestro Marek Wroniszewski will conduct the London Mozart Players orchestra and the Hertfordshire Chorus, with arrangements by Ignacy Wojciechowski. Original soloists from Diablo IV: Lord of Hatred will join the stage. Lead Composer Ted Reedy and Music Director Derek Duke will be special guests. The concert will include the mournful guitar of Tristram, sonorous themes of Lords of Destruction, sombre strings of Kyovashad, thunderous battle anthems, and haunting choral harmonies. Tickets start at £65; the concert runs for 2 hours including 1 interval.
