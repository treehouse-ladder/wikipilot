## Excerpts

> Donkey Kong Bananza's full soundtrack has been added to Nintendo Music, bringing 229 tracks with a total runtime of 8 hours and 36 minutes.

> The soundtrack includes tracks for all of the Bananza transformations, tunes from the DK Island & Emerald Rush DLC, and more from the base game.

> Of the 229 tracks, 159 are part of the Extended-Playback Collection, meaning they can be played for up to 60 minutes each. A spoiler block is available for those who haven't completed the game yet.
