## Excerpts

> The number of games on Steam that disclose AI-generated content has grown by approximately 700% year-over-year, reaching roughly 8,000 games — about 7% of Steam's total library — that carry at least one AI disclosure tag.

> The surge in AI-disclosed games tracks closely with the availability of commercial AI generation tools: the largest single-quarter jump in disclosed games occurred in Q4 2023 following the wide release of image-generation APIs, with another significant jump in H1 2025 after text-to-3D tools became commercially viable.

> Not all AI-disclosed games are using AI for the same purposes: the most common disclosure category is AI-generated images and artwork, followed by AI-generated text and dialogue, with AI-generated audio and music being the fastest-growing category in 2025-2026.

> The 7% figure likely understates actual AI use in the broader Steam catalog, as Steam's disclosure policy applies only to AI-generated final assets shipped in the game — AI used as an internal development tool (code generation, bug detection, QA automation) does not require disclosure.
