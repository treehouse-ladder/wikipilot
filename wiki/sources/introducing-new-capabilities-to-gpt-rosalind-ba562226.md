> OpenAI introduced new capabilities to GPT-Rosalind on June 3, 2026. The update combines GPT-5.5's agentic coding and tool-use capabilities with stronger model intelligence in core drug-discovery domains. On MedChemBench, GPT-Rosalind outperforms GPT-5.5 at 27.5% vs 25.1%, while using 7.2% fewer tokens. On GeneBench, GPT-Rosalind uses 31% fewer tokens than GPT-5.5 while achieving higher accuracy.
