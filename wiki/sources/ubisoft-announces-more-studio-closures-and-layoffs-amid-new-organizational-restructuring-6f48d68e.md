## Excerpts

> Ubisoft is undergoing a new wave of organizational restructuring, announcing further studio closures and additional layoffs beyond what was disclosed in the June 10 internal communication. The company is pivoting resources away from studios that are not aligned with its Vantage Studios structure.

> The latest restructuring affects multiple studios and roles across Ubisoft's global footprint, with the company continuing to consolidate around its core IP houses under the Vantage/Creative Houses model established earlier in 2026.

> Ubisoft confirmed it is ramping staff away from several ongoing projects as part of this reorganization, directing talent either toward priority franchises or toward the exits.
