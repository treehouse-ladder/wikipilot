## Excerpts

> Xbox chief strategy officer Matthew Ball said that players can expect a 'reliable pipeline' of exclusives going forward, explaining it was important for Xbox to announce the exclusivity of both Gears of War E-Day and Clockwork Revolution simultaneously 'so that people understood this was not a one-off.' CEO Asha Sharma said the approach will be 'case-by-case,' taking a data-driven and strategic approach: 'I want to make the right decision, not the fastest decision.' Live-service and multiplayer games will continue to be multiplatform; single-player prestige titles will evaluate exclusivity individually.
