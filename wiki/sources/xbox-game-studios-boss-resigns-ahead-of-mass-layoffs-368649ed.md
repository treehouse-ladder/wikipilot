## Excerpts

> Craig Duncan is stepping down as head of Xbox Game Studios. The move comes as teams inside Xbox brace for the latest round of mass layoffs and potential studio closures following new CEO Asha Sharma's announcement of a 'reset' last week.

> After nearly 14 years working under Xbox and 18 months leading Xbox Game Studios, Craig Duncan is stepping down and leaving Microsoft. Duncan replaced Alan Hartman as Head of Xbox Game Studios in November 2024 and oversaw developers including Halo Studios, Turn 10, The Initiative, and Double Fine.

> Louise O'Connor, Chief of Staff at Xbox Game Studios, will also exit Microsoft. The studios that reported to Duncan will report to Xbox Chief Content Officer Matt Booty until a replacement is found.
