## Excerpts

> Throughout more than a week's worth of GTA 6 gameplay leaks, the leaker known as Cyberleek has covered each of the 15 unauthorized clips they've released with watermarks and QR codes promoting a cryptocurrency token called $CYBERLEEK.

> Cyberleek has reportedly pulled out $250,000, with the leaker transferring close to $250,000 in the early hours of August 27 into various cyber wallets, leading the coin's value to plummet.

> While the leaker claims their activities are an effort to pressure Rockstar into moving away from digital pre-orders, since day one they have been using the leaks to ask for money in the form of crypto currency.
