## Excerpts

> We introduce ExeWorldBench, to our knowledge the first benchmark to comprehensively evaluate world understanding and executable realization through code-based 3D world construction. ExeWorldBench comprises three progressively challenging tasks: Mechanism Executability, Compositional Executability, and Intent-Driven Executable World Synthesis. Extensive experiments on frontier models reveal substantial limitations in executable world modeling.
