## Excerpts

> Qwen-Image-2512 is the December update of the Qwen-Image text-to-image foundational model, a 20B-parameter model released under the Apache-2.0 license.

> The results show that Qwen-Image-2512 is currently the strongest open-source model on AI Arena blind evaluations.

> The official repository is approximately 57.7 GB in total, with the transformer weights at 40.9 GB; community quantized builds (MFlux Q8 at 36.1 GB down to Q3 at 23.4 GB) are available for lower-VRAM and Apple Silicon use.
