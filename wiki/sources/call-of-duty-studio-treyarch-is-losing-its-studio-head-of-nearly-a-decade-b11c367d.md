## Excerpts

> Treyarch studio head Mark Gordon is retiring from the Call of Duty studio after 22 years, ten of which he spent as studio head. The studio announced Gordon would be retiring from his role 'to focus on his next chapter.' Current COO Kevin Hendrickson and director of production Yale Miller will take on Gordon's role together as co-studio heads.
