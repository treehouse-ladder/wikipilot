## Excerpts

> Players had an overall positive experience and found the game novel. They appreciated the freedom of expression and felt deeply immersed when NPCs responded in a believable manner.

> Issues such as unnatural conversational flow, incorrect or inconsistent responses, and uninformative dialogue disrupted immersion and gameplay.

> A VR adventure-puzzle game called Office Whispers features four GenAI-based NPCs with diverse human characteristics.
