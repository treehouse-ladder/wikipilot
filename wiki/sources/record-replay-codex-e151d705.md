## Excerpts

> Record & Replay lets you demonstrate a workflow on your Mac and turn it into a reusable skill. Use it when the workflow is repetitive, depends on your preferences, or is easier to show than to describe in a prompt.

> After you stop recording, Codex inspects the captured workflow and drafts a skill. The skill explains when to use the workflow, what inputs it needs, what steps to follow, and how to verify the result.

> Once created, Codex uses the skill as reusable context for the task. It can then complete the workflow with the tools available in the current environment, including Computer Use, browser actions, and installed plugins.

> Record & Replay is a macOS feature. Initial availability excludes the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland. Users or their administrator must also enable Computer Use.
