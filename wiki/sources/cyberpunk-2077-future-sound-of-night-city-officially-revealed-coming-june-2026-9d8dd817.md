## Excerpts

> An official new soundtrack compilation, titled Cyberpunk 2077: Future Sound of Night City, brings the electronic music of Cyberpunk 2077 to physical media on June 5. This project from the Swiss label WRWTFWW Records marks a major moment for collectors who want to own a piece of the game's sensory identity.

> Curated by Polish artists Rafal Grobel and Wojeich Sokil, Future Sound of Night City is primarily concerned with showcasing the 'Polish beats' of Cyberpunk 2077. The album contains 22 tracks, and it can either be purchased as part of a three-vinyl set, as a CD, or as a digital download.

> Options for the vinyl set range from 45 EUR to 115 EUR, or roughly between $53 USD and $135 USD. Only 15 of the tracks included in the album will be found on the vinyl and CD, as the other seven will remain digital exclusives.
