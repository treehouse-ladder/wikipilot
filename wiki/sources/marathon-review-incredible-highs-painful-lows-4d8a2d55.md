## Excerpts

> Marathon is the latest online shooter from Destiny and Halo maker Bungie, which launched on March 5, 2026. The game is available on PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S, with full cross-play and cross-save support, and is priced at 40 USD for the Standard Edition.

> Bungie's slick extraction shooter is great at creating tense battles, incredible highs, and painful lows, with a future that's full of promise. The new Marathon is a PvPvE extraction shooter built around survival, loot, and social interactions.

> The game is full of spacey, boxy guns created using a phenomenal, bright retro-future aesthetic, guns are bulky, heavy, and incredibly satisfying to shoot, and each weapon feels suitably deadly.
