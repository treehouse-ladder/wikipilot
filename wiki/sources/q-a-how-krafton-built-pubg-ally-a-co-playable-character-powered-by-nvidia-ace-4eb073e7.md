## Excerpts

> PUBG Ally is an AI teammate built by KRAFTON for PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS, powered by NVIDIA ACE. KRAFTON calls it a co-playable character (CPC): a new category distinct from non-playable characters (NPCs), designed to cooperate, adapt, and remember across sessions.

> KRAFTON developed PUBG Ally using on-device automatic speech recognition, a 2B-parameter small language model, and text-to-speech to interact naturally with players and adapt to real-time game events.

> The technical pipeline separates fast, reflexive actions (handled by a behavior tree) from slower, language-driven reasoning (handled by the language model), allowing Ally to respond immediately during combat while still supporting natural voice communication and contextual understanding.

> PUBG Ally supports English, Korean, and Chinese, uses structured long- and short-term memory to personalize gameplay across sessions, and was refined through iterative playtesting with real players.
