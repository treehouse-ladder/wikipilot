## Excerpts

> Take-Two-owned 2K has announced a new AAA studio called Small Axe Studios, based in Vancouver, which aims to 'redefine the future of sports video games.' The studio is led by EA veteran Aaron McHardy, who is serving as vice president, executive producer, and studio head.

> McHardy was previously the executive producer of the Madden and College Football franchises, as well as the FIFA team, over a tenure of almost 18 years. McHardy is far from the only EA veteran in the current staff, with head of design Gary Paterson, head of operations Tom Pan, head of engineering Mike Thompson, and more rounding out the roster.

> The studio's current focus is on building '2K's next blockbuster sports franchise.' The new studio is a formal expansion of '2K Sports Lab,' an internal research organization created in 2023.
