## Excerpts

> Valor Mortis mixes elements of Dark Souls and BioShock with nonlinear exploration and platforming, but it feels stretched thin.

> The combat is competent but lacking emotional weight, while the story's themes of war and fanaticism land well.

> The game's art direction and creature designs stand out, and I'm glad the game exists, mainly to encourage other studios to explore similar mixes of setting and theme.
