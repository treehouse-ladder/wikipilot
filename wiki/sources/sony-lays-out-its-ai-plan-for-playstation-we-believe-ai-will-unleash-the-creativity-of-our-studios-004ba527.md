## Excerpts

> We believe AI will unleash the creativity of our studios, power a more curated platform, and enhance the PlayStation experience for both players and creators.

> Sony created a tool called Mockingbird that quickly animates 3D facial models based on performance capture, with animation work that would have taken hours now completing in a fraction of a second.

> Teams at Naughty Dog, San Diego Studio, and others have already adapted the tool, including in released titles like Horizon Zero Dawn Remastered.

> The vision, design, and emotional impact of games will always come from studio talent and performers, with AI meant to augment their capabilities rather than replace them.
