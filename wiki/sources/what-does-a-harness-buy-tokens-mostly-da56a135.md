## Excerpts

> On a 45-task hard subset, swapping the harness flips about as many task outcomes as rerunning the same harness; on the 447-task pool, Claude Code and mini-SWE-agent land within five points of each other.

> With the same model and tasks, cost per task varies by up to 3x across harnesses, driven mainly by the fixed preamble each harness sends on every step: about 16.6k tokens for Claude Code, 7k for OpenCode, and 0.8k for mini-SWE-agent.

> The provider's cached-input price scales the bill but does not reorder harnesses.
