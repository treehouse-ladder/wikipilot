## Excerpts

> RPGAgent is an LLM-driven multi-agent system specifically designed to assist novice game creators in transforming a short story outline into a playable game, guided by the Elemental Tetrad framework.

> Specialized agents exchange structured data to generate coherent narrative, scene, and gameplay mechanics, ensuring structural correctness and consistency between story and world. By combining LLM-based generation with procedural content creation, the system offers a controllable and interpretable workflow.

> In a within-subjects study with 18 participants, RPGAgent outperformed a GPT-assisted baseline in both user experience and creative satisfaction during the prototyping of playable RPGs.
