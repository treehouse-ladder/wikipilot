## Excerpts

> Marvel's Wolverine is set to release on September 15, 2026. It is a PS5 exclusive and will not be coming to PS4. Insomniac Games opened PlayStation's State of Play on June 2, 2026 with a seven-minute extended gameplay trailer for Marvel's Wolverine. The trailer featured familiar faces like Jean Grey and Sabretooth. The story sees mutants who have been taken by a group known as the Reavers, with Wolverine lending his claws to the mutant cause. Standard Edition pre-orders went live for $69.99.
