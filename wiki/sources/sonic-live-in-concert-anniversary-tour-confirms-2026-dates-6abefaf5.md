## Excerpts

> A string of US tour dates kicks off in September ahead of a world premiere at London's Eventim Apollo.

> The team at Sega assembled rock musicians and vocalists, as well as a live band, to celebrate 35 years of Sonic The Hedgehog.

> The concert features the most recognisable music from across the Sonic franchise, from the 1991 original game to 2025's Crossworlds. The U.S. leg will visit more than 30 cities, while a UK tour is scheduled to follow in February 2027.
