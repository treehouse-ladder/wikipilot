## Excerpts

> In its first three days on the market, the game sold through 1.9 million copies and generated $130 million in revenue, according to market data from Alinea Analytics.

> Wolverine is currently the PS5's #1 first-party game of 2026, and has even beaten Death Stranding 2's lifetime sales.

> Wolverine is already the top-performing new PlayStation Studios game on PS5 this year, leapfrogging MLB The Show, Marathon, Saros, and Marvel Tokon.

> This is notable because Wolverine is performing strongly despite some initial lackluster reviews and a weak aggregate rating on Metacritic, and some viral rage-bait around the game's QTEs, objective markers, and other on-rails gameplay elements.
