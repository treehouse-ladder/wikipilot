## Excerpts

> Gemini 4 Hydrogen is the fast, low-cost tier of the Gemini 4 family, built for agentic workflows and everyday tasks, sitting alongside the frontier Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini 4 Argon models; it is the successor to Gemini 3.8 Flash.

> Gemini 4 Hydrogen is priced at $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens, with a 1 million-token context window.

> Gemini 4 Hydrogen offers low, medium, high (default), and a new xhigh thinking level, and is available through the Gemini API, Google AI Studio, and Android Studio.

> Gemini 4 Hydrogen scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at default settings.
