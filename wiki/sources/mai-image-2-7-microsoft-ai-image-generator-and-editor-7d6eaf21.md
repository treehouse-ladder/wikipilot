## Excerpts

> MAI Image 2.7 is Microsoft AI's image generation and editing model, available on fal as four pay-per-use endpoints: text-to-image and edit for both the standard model and the cheaper Flash variant.

> The endpoint ids are microsoft/mai-image-2.7/text-to-image, microsoft/mai-image-2.7/edit, microsoft/mai-image-2.7/flash/text-to-image, and microsoft/mai-image-2.7/flash/edit.

> A 1024x1024 output costs approximately $0.035 for the standard MAI-Image-2.7 model and approximately $0.010 for MAI-Image-2.7 Flash.

> Content generated through the fal API can be used in commercial projects; see fal's terms of service for full details on usage rights and licensing.

> MAI-Image-2.7 supports editing with reference images and fine-grained edits that change one object without disturbing the rest of the image.
