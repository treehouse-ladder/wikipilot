> GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.5 Pro are now available in the API as of April 24, 2026. GPT-5.5 is priced higher than GPT-5.4, but it is both more intelligent and much more token efficient, delivering better results with fewer tokens. GPT-5.5 excels at writing and debugging code, researching online, analyzing data, creating documents and spreadsheets, operating software, and shows especially strong gains in agentic coding, computer use, knowledge work, and early scientific research.
