## Excerpts

> Tripo Agentic Canvas is a beta node-based workflow tool in Tripo Studio whose agent plans and runs 3D pipelines from generation through refinement to export.

> The agent turns a text goal into a proposed set of nodes and settings, and asks clarifying questions only when answers would change the result. Nothing runs until you approve the plan, and credits are charged only once generation starts.

> The canvas includes nodes for image generation, HD Model and Smart Mesh generation, retopology, UV, texturing, PBR, rigging, and animation. A separate AI 3D Lab node handles mesh repair, printability checks, part assembly, and scene building.

> The export node covers formats such as STL, 3MF, FBX, and GLB. Tutorials also show exporting to Unreal Engine 5 and Blender projects.

> Agentic Canvas is in beta, available to Max and Team users with a gradual rollout to others.
