## Excerpts

> The Veritas Tales: Witch of the Dark Castle Original Soundtrack is now available, featuring 13 tracks composed by Hitoshi Sakimoto.

> Hitoshi Sakimoto (Basiscape) is known for his work on titles such as Final Fantasy Tactics, Vagrant Story, and 13 Sentinels: Aegis Rim.

> The soundtrack is available digitally and on streaming services including Spotify and Apple Music, as well as for purchase on Steam and iTunes. A 10% bundle sale on Steam lets you buy the main game and Original Soundtrack together, while existing owners of the base game can purchase the soundtrack on its own at a discounted price.
