> We estimate that approximately 30% of the [SWE-Bench Pro] tasks are broken. The datapoint analysis pipeline flagged 200 (27.4%) broken tasks, while the human annotation campaign identified 249 (34.1%). Overly strict tests enforce specific implementation details not specified in the prompt, invalidating many functionally correct submissions, while underspecified prompts omit requirements that hidden tests enforce and that are not reasonably inferable.
