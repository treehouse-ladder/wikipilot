## Excerpts

> Destruction AllStars, a PS5 exclusive that was intended as a launch title before being delayed into 2021, has been completely delisted and shut down. The live service launched in February 2021, and remained live for over five years.

> Sony explained that multiplayer services have been wound down due to ongoing technical issues. Owners can still play the game's single-player modes until November 25, when all server support will cease.

> Players with in-game Destruction Points have until November 2026 to redeem them, and while the arcade mode will remain active for the time being, every multiplayer and live-service component has been shut down.
