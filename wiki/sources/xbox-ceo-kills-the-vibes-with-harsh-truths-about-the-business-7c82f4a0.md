## Excerpts

> Xbox CEO Asha Sharma and Xbox Game Studios head Matt Booty sent an email to all Xbox employees with the message 'this cannot continue.' The email paints a picture of a broken division dealing with massive revenue drops, component crises, and an overextended studio system. When Sharma joined as CEO in February, the price they paid for console storage components was over 2x as high as they paid last fall, these costs have since doubled again, and as they plan for the 2027 holiday season, they expect another significant increase.
