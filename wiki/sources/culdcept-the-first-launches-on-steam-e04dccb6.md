## Excerpts

> Culdcept the First, a modern worldwide re-release of Japan-exclusive Saturn game Culdcept, launched on PC via Steam on July 30, 2026. The collector's editions are called the Cepter Edition and will come with an art book, a two-CD soundtrack composed by Yuzo Koshiro, nine acrylic standees of the game's characters and 10 art cards. Culdcept will come to Switch, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series digitally on Sept. 8.
