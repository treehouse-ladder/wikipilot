## Excerpts

> Inkling-Small is a multimodal, Mixture-of-Experts model with controllable reasoning effort that matches Inkling at a quarter of the size. Like Inkling, it features native reasoning over audio and images, variable thinking effort, a context window of up to 1M tokens, and well-rounded performance across a range of benchmarks. The weights are released under Apache 2.0 and available for download on Hugging Face to support research, fine-tuning and integration into third-party products.
