## Excerpts

> Undead Labs studio head Philip Holt said the studio was 'irresponsibly transparent' with staff before layoffs, telling the team early that cuts were likely coming as part of the Xbox divestiture and pivot to independent ownership in late September.

> Holt declined to give a number for the cuts, saying that reducing the layoffs to a statistic would take attention away from the people affected. The studio itself described the layoffs as 'significant' on LinkedIn.

> State of Decay 3 is still on target for a 2027 release. The studio is now independent and employee-owned, with stock to be issued to staff in the coming weeks; the buyout vehicle is called Full Measure. An earlier sale to Splash Damage fell through.
