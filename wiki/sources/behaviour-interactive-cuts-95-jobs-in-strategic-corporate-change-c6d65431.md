## Excerpts

> Behaviour Interactive revealed it would be reducing 95 jobs, 70 of which would be from the mothership Montreal studio.

> Citing the 'challenging context' of the current industry, Behaviour elected to focus on its 'historical strengths,' which means 'leading in horror and service, and exploring the possibilities of location-based entertainment.'

> the company determined that it had 'too many' employees to support that shift and that some of the affected workers didn't have the skills needed to support the new direction.
