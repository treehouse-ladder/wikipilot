## Excerpts

> Square Enix Jazz Vol.2 draws from the full breadth of the Square Enix catalog rather than a single game or franchise, with 12 arrangements running 69 minutes and 34 seconds, spanning Final Fantasy Legend II, Final Fantasy VI, NieR, Legend of Mana, Final Fantasy, Chrono Trigger, Final Fantasy XII, Seiken Densetsu 3, NieR:Automata, Final Fantasy XV, and Romancing SaGa 2.

> The Dancing Mad arrangement from Final Fantasy VI is an absolute monster arrangement that arranges only the final portion for the encounter with Kefka, running a solid five minutes with fantastic improv sections; drummer Ken Higeshiro brings a legendary floor tom big band sound reminiscent of Benny Goodman, playing in 15/8 instead of 4/4.

> These are all great tracks demonstrating the power of the Nakagawa/Kawamura arranging duo. This is the best place to start if collecting music from the Square Enix Jazz series.
