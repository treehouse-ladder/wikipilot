## Excerpts

> With max effort, Sonnet 5.5 gains 18 points over Sonnet 5 and moves to #2 on the Intelligence Index, behind only Opus 5.5 (max).

> Sonnet 5.5 scores 70.6% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, an agentic coding evaluation, compared to Sonnet 5's 10.3%. It scores two points below Opus 5.5 on GDPval-AA, a test of real-world work across a variety of occupations.

> Sonnet 5.5 is priced at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with up to 90% cost savings with prompt caching and 50% cost savings with batch processing. Pricing for Sonnet 5.5 will cost up to an estimated 30% less to run than Sonnet 5 for typical workloads billed by token.

> On several benchmarks, Sonnet 5.5 at Low or Medium effort beats Sonnet 5's best score for about a tenth of the cost per task. Our coding evals showed a third fewer tool calls and roughly half the shell runs to finish a task.

> It's the first Sonnet model to beat Pokemon Red working only from screenshots.
