## Excerpts

> Games industry mergers and acquisitions reached US$2.3 billion in Q2 2026 across 54 transactions, with dealmaking now at a 'post-pandemic' high matching the Covid-era boom, according to a new report.

> Mid-market gaming content acquisitions — deals over US$100 million — drove the growth. The largest transaction was Scopely's US$1 billion acquisition of Loom Games, while WeMade's founder stake sale to NeoPulse (~$591m) was the quarter's largest disclosed deal.

> Despite the M&A recovery, gaming stocks continued a broader decline, with mobile-first Western publishers dropping 13% year-to-date and mobile-first Asian publishers tumbling between 37% and 42%.
