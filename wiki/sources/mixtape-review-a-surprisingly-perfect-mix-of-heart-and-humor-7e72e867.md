## Excerpts

> Mixtape is a game about a teenage girl who tries to link every memorable moment of her life to a fitting song, described as coming-of-age comfort food.

> The game follows three high school friends, Stacy, Slater, and Cassandra, as they spend one more night together before each of them heads off on their own post-high-school journeys.

> Mixtape is from Beethoven & Dinosaur, developers of BAFTA award-winning game The Artful Escape.
