## Excerpts

> Kimodo is a kinematic motion diffusion model for high-quality human(oid) motion generation.

> Kimodo is controlled through text and constraints to generate high-quality 3D human and robot motions. Constraints include full-body keyframes, sparse joint positions and rotations, 2D waypoints, and dense 2D paths.

> Kimodo is trained on 700 hours of optical motion capture data.

> The release includes code and a CLI to generate motions on both human and robot skeletons, an interactive timeline demo, a benchmark built on the BONES-SEED dataset, and annotations. Models are available for SOMA, Unitree G1, and SMPL-X skeletons.
