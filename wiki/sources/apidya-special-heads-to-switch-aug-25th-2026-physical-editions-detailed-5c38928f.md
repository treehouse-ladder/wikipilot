## Excerpts

> Apidya' Special is a 2026 remake of the 1992 Amiga classic from Kaiko, published by ININ Games, launching digitally on PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox, and Nintendo Switch on August 25, 2026.

> The soundtrack set of two CDs features the complete original soundtrack, newly produced studio versions of the classic compositions, and original Amiga recordings from the game's historic release.

> The Special Edition includes an extensive package of exclusive content, highlighted by a comprehensive History Artbook documenting the legacy and development of Apidya and an Official Soundtrack Collection by legendary composer Chris Hülsbeck.
