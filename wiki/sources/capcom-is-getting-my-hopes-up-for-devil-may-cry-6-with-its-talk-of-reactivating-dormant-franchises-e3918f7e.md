> Onimusha: Way of the Sword sold 1 million copies on launch day, bringing lifetime sales for the franchise up to 10 million, which is impressive for a series that has been dormant for nearly 20 years.

> Commenting on the early sales milestone, Capcom said that it wants to repeat Onimusha's success with other games that haven't seen a new release in many years, with the company focusing on "reactivating IPs that haven't had a new title launch recently."
