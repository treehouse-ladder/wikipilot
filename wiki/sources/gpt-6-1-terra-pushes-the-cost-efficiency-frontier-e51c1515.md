## Excerpts

> OpenAI has released GPT-6.1 Terra, pushing the cost efficiency frontier. GPT-6.1 Terra is more intelligent for less than a third of the cost of GPT-5.6 Terra at max effort.

> GPT-6.1 Terra (max) gains 6 points in the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index over GPT-5.6 Terra, at less than a third of the cost per task ($0.40 vs $1.40). The low and medium effort settings are Pareto-optimal for cost efficiency.

> GPT-6.1 Terra lists at $1 per million input tokens and $5 per million output tokens, versus $2/$12 for GPT-5.6 Terra.

> Effort-level Intelligence Index scores: Low 29, Medium 38, High 42, Xhigh 45, Max 51. Non-reasoning is 30.

> On the Coding Agent Index v2.0 GPT-6.1 Terra scores 48, which is 6 points below GPT-6.1 Sol (54) and 17 points above GPT-6 Luna (31).

> GPT-6.1 Terra's gain of approximately 20 points on Terminal-Bench 4.0 is the largest reported jump, and it shows a 50 point drop in hallucination rate measured in AA-Omniscience.
