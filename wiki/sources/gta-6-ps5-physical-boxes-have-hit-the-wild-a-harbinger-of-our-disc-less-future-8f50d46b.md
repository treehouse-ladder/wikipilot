## Excerpts

> Physical cases for the PlayStation 5 version of the game are already appearing in the wild ahead of stores being allowed to sell them on November 12.

> Players can buy a physical GTA 6 box at retail stores, but it won't include a disc, only a code for a digital download.

> You cannot actually play the game until November 19 when the game goes live. Rockstar also moved up the preloading date for GTA 6 from November 12 to November 10 for the digital and code-in-a-box versions.

> Two retailers, VGP and Loot Box Gaming, said they would not carry the code-only version.
