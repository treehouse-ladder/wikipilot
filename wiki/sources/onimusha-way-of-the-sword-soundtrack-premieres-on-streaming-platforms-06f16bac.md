## Excerpts

> Music for the action-adventure game was composed by music director Marika Suzuki and lead composer Nozomi Ohmoto, in collaboration with co-composers Kota Suzuki, Tomoki Kameyama, and Daisuke Shinoda.

> "Ghost in the Silence" features singer-songwriter Cait Foley and Japanese-language vocalist mizuki.

> The Onimusha: Way of the Sword Original Soundtrack by Capcom Sound Team spans 82 tracks across roughly four hours of music.
