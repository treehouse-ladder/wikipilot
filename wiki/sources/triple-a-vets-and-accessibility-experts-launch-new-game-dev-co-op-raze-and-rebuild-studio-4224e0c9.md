## Excerpts

> Raze and Rebuild Studio is a worker-owned video game co-operative formed by triple-A developers and accessibility experts, describing itself as 'rising from the ashes of the games industry' in reference to recent industry layoffs and studio closures.

> The co-op's founding members include former Monolith Productions writer Tanya DePass, business development and accessibility consultant Tarja Porkka-Kontturi, accessibility expert Laura Kate Dale, and Global Game Jam co-founder Ian Schreiber.

> The studio launched without any initial external funding but is pursuing development on its debut project while eyeing potential grants and speaking with outside partners.
