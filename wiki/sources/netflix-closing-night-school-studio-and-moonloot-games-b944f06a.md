## Excerpts

> Netflix is closing Oxenfree developer Night School Studio and Helsinki-based Moonloot Games, and is eliminating some additional games roles across the games team, though Netflix declined to share how many people were laid off.

> The decision reflects an internal shift toward focusing specifically on kids gaming, party games, story-driven fare and games with mainstream hooks.

> Night School Studio released horror game Unhinged in late June, just weeks before the closure. Over its 12-year run it created narrative games including the award-winning Oxenfree series. Moonloot was founded in 2022 and was working on an Animal Crossing-style life sim but never released a game.
