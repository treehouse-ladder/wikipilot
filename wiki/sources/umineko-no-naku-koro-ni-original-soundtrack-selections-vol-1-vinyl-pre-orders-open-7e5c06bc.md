## Excerpts

> Materia Collective has opened pre-orders for Umineko no Naku Koro Ni Original Soundtrack Selections Vol. 1. The collection is scheduled to ship in Q3 2026 for $39.99. Approved by 07th Expansion and featuring compositions by zts, the set will be available as a deluxe 2xLP pressing on 180-gram 'Golden Epitaph' vinyl.

> The release marks the first time music from Umineko no Naku Koro Ni has received an official vinyl release outside Japan.

> It comes packaged in a matte-finished slipcase and gatefold jacket featuring gold foil accents. New artwork was created by Red Coconut Studio with assistance from Andrés Goji.
