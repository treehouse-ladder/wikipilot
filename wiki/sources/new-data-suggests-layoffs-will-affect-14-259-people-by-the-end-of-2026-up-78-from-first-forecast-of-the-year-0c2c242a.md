## Excerpts

> New figures from the ASGC Games Industry Layoffs Tracker forecast that 14,259 workers will lose their jobs by the end of the year, up 78% from its initial January estimate of 8,025.

> At least 9,781 gaming workers had already been affected by July 26, exceeding the total number of layoffs recorded throughout 2025, which stood at 9,197.

> Amir Satvat said the pace of layoffs is accelerating faster than our models initially predicted. The latest forecast includes 381 jobs higher than an estimate published on July 10, following further cuts at studios including Ubisoft Barcelona, ZA/UM, 11 Bit Studios and Red Rover Interactive.

> North America accounts for 66% of announced gaming layoffs and 79% of affected workers, while Europe represents another 30% of reductions, with the two regions together accounting for approximately 95% of global industry layoffs.
