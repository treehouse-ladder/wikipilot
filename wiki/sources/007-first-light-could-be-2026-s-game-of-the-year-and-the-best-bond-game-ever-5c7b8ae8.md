## Excerpts

> Based on hands-on previews, the game could end up being one of the best games of the year, and also the best James Bond game ever created. Reviewers came away both impressed by its scale and ambition, and relieved to say that it sticks the landing while playing it a bit safe.

> IO's approach takes players through linear sections that funnel into sandboxes and then back on loop, ensuring players get both movie-like explosive action and set-pieces, as well as thinking-on-your-feet problem-solving. Stealth gameplay is a standout, which makes sense given Hitman's pedigree.

> First Light launches on May 27, 2026 on PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and Switch 2.
