## Excerpts

> Fugu Max and Fugu Ultra v2 were released on September 11, 2026, with the same underlying orchestration architecture aimed at two different goals: lower cost and maximum capability.

> Fugu is an orchestration model that decides how a pool of specialized language models should work together on a task, routing each subtask to the leanest model that can solve it, then combining the results behind one API.

> Fugu Ultra v2 is best or joint-best on five of eight benchmarks in Sakana's own evaluation, including 48.3 on Chartography against Claude Opus 5 at 27.3 and 74.3 on DeepSWE.

> Fugu Max is priced at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens, while Fugu Ultra v2 costs $5 input and $30 output per million tokens.
