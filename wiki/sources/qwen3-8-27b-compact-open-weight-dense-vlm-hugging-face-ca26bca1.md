## Excerpts

> Qwen3.8-27B is a compact, deployment-friendly dense model: a native vision-language model that understands images and videos, with flexible thinking control.

> Qwen3.8-27B natively supports context lengths of up to 262,144 tokens. Additionally, Qwen3.8-27B will be available as a hosted version with more production features, e.g., 1M context length by default.

> Qwen3.8-27B has 27B parameters, 64 layers in a 3:1 hybrid of Gated DeltaNet linear attention and gated full attention (24Q/4KV, head dim 256), vocab 248,320, context 262,144 native.

> Qwen3.8-27B often rivals or exceeds larger models like Muse Glimmer-30B and even Opus 4.6 Max on several benchmarks — scoring 73.0 on Terminal-Bench 2.1, 61.7 on SWE-bench Pro, 84.3 on OSWorld-Verified computer-use, 81.9 on AndroidWorld mobile-use, and 90.3 on LiveCodeBench v6.

> On SWE-bench Pro, Qwen hits 61.7% vs Claude Sonnet 5 at 63.2%, and on OSWorld-Verified, Qwen hits 84.3% vs Claude Sonnet 5 at 81.2%.

> The Qwen3.8-27B model is released under the Apache 2.0 license.
