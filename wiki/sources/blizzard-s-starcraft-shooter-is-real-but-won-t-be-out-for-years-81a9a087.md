## Excerpts

> Blizzard describes StarCraft as an "open-world, story-driven sci-fi shooter" that is set 70 years after the events of StarCraft II: Legacy of the Void.

> The new StarCraft is a third-person, single-player game that puts one player inside one suit of armor, responsible for maintaining and upgrading that equipment as they move through an open world.

> The project is led by Blizzard GM Dan Hay, a veteran of Ubisoft's Far Cry series who served as creative director on Far Cry 5 and oversaw subsequent entries through Far Cry 6.

> Blizzard revealed at BlizzCon that it is making a brand new StarCraft open-world shooter, with a release date set for 2030. No gameplay was shown; the cinematic was the entirety of the public reveal.
