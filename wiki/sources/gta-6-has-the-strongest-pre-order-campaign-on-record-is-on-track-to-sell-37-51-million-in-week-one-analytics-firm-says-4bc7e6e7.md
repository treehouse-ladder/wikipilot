## Excerpts

> Market research firm Newzoo reports that Grand Theft Auto 6 has 'the strongest pre-order campaign ever recorded,' with the game earning roughly $180 million in digital pre-order sales across the US and the five largest European markets during the last week of June.

> Newzoo estimated the total global spend was around $260 million in the first week of pre-orders.

> It is estimated GTA 6 will achieve $3.25 - $5.2 billion in cumulative global sales by the end of launch week, selling between 37 and 51 million copies.
