## Excerpts

> Meshy-6 introduces cleaner geometry, sharper hard-surface details, and powerful new workflows featuring Low Poly Mode, multi-color 3D printing, and upgraded APIs to help you create production-ready 3D assets faster and with greater control.

> A dedicated Low Poly Mode built specifically for game developers produces efficient wireframes optimized for real-time performance, making assets easier to integrate into game engines without extensive manual retopology.
