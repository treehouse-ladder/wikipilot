## Excerpts

> VidAnimator introduces a mixed-initiative framework that enables stylized 3D characters to replicate human motion from videos, structured into four stages: Upload of Video and 3D Character Data, Video-to-Human Motion Capture, Character-to-Stylized Motion Transfer, and Application of Results in Provided Scenes. The Skinning-Weight Editor and MoTrans Interface enable weight adjustments and previews from Character-to-Stylized 3D Model.
