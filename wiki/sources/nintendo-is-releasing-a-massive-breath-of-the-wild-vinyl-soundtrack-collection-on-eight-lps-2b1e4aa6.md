## Excerpts

> Nintendo is teaming up with Laced Records on a couple of The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild vinyl soundtrack releases, including a massive 8-LP box set that ships with some franchise-friendly bells and whistles.

> The collections won't be shipped until June 19, 2026. Both collections are available to pre-order right now via Laced Records and My Nintendo Store.

> All told, there are 130 newly remastered tracks in the 8-LP box set. The records are split into themes, mirroring a player's progression through Hyrule, revolving around exploring the Kingdom of Hyrule, freeing the Divine Beasts from Calamity Ganon's hold and discovering the mysteries of the Shrines.

> Breath of the Wild is an interesting choice for an initial vinyl release, given that the game's soundtrack is known for mood-setting vibes but not really known for bops.
