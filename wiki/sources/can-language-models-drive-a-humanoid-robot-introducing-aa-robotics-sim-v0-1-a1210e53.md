## Excerpts

> AA-Robotics-Sim v0.1 places frontier language models in control of a simulated Unitree G1 humanoid across ten household and industrial tasks. The models run inside coding agent harnesses, and the humanoid has to walk, look around, and use two dexterous hands at once.

> GPT-6.2 Astra leads AA-Robotics-Sim v0.1 with a score of 71.0, 13.5 points ahead of Claude Fable 5.2 at 57.5 and GPT-6.2 Sol at 53.7.

> In the v0.1 setup, models are given practice episodes, keep only the notes and code they wrote, and are then scored on ten new evaluation scenes with different rooms, lighting and task details. A task's score is the mean of its 10 evaluation episodes, and the AA-Robotics-Sim score is the mean of the 10 task scores.

> AA-Robotics-Sim sits alongside AA-Robotics, where language models control a robot arm, and AA-3D, which evaluates AI models building interactive 3D experiences.
