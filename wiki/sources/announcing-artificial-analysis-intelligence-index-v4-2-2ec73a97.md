## Excerpts

> The Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.2 combines performance across ten evaluations: AA-Briefcase, GDPval-AA v2, τ³-Banking, Terminal-Bench v2.1, SciCode, Humanity's Last Exam, GDP.pdf, CritPt, AA-Omniscience, and AA-LCR v1.1.

> v4.2 is an interim update that accelerates elements of our upcoming v5 release to keep pace with frontier models, pushing the Index toward harder, more realistic tasks and a much larger share of secret test data.

> 40% of the Index weighting is now private, held-out test sets — double from v4.1 — including AA-Briefcase, AA-Omniscience, and solutions for CritPt.

> GPQA Diamond, an exceptional scientific reasoning evaluation, has now been saturated and has been removed from the Index.

> GDP.pdf, created by Surge AI, evaluates single-turn professional document reasoning across 100 PDFs and ten domains; models must synthesize evidence distributed across 4,592 pages, graded against 1,275 expert-authored atomic criteria.

> On Intelligence Index v4.2, Claude Fable 5.1 (max with fallback) tops the leaderboard with a score of 57, followed by GPT-6 Astra (max) at 55, GPT-6 Astra (xhigh) at 54, Claude Opus 5 (max) at 54 and Claude Opus 5 (xhigh) at 53.
