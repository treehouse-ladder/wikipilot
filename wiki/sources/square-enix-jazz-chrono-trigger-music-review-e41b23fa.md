## Excerpts

> From 2017 to 2022, Square Enix produced five albums in a dedicated Square Enix Jazz series (six if you include a live album). The final album in this set is a full jazz album solely for the classic SNES RPG Chrono Trigger.

> The album was arranged by trombone player Eijiro Nakagawa and bassist Ryu Kawamura, and is composed of 11 tracks that aim to be equal parts nostalgic and fresh.

> The Square Enix Jazz – Chrono Trigger collection of eleven tracks remains loyal to what jazz is all about: brass, piano, percussion, and standing bass.

> Jazz arrangements are powerful because jazz is largely defined by its heart — not a sterile adherence to sheet music with an emphasis on precision, but emotion.

> The only complaint is the omission of 'Secret of the Forest,' but with what is arguably the best gaming soundtrack ever created, almost any track from the OST deserves a spot.
