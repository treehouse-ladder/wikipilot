## Excerpts

> That's No Moon, the studio working on the recently announced Crossfire, has reportedly laid off 14 staff on August 11, 2026. The report is based on multiple LinkedIn posts by employees at the studio, which started on Friday and have increased in number since.

> A senior environment concept artist said they were 'one of the fourteen affected' by the reported layoffs at the company—a number that was echoed by a producer on the team. Various affected employees shared their experiences on social media, with an associate environment artist and procedural technical artist both mentioning being hit with unexpected layoffs.
