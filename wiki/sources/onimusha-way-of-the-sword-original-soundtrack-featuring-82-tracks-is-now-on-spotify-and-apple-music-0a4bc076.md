## Excerpts

> Capcom has announced that the original soundtrack for its action game Onimusha: Way of the Sword is now available to stream on Spotify and Apple Music.

> The soundtrack spans four discs and features 82 songs totaling about four hours.

> The OST was composed by Daisuke Shinoda, Kota Suzuki, Marika Suzuki, and Nozomi Ohmoto.

> You can listen now on Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal, and Qobuz.
