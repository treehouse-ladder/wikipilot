## Excerpts

> Claude Opus 5.5 (max) leads the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5 at 58, ahead of GPT-6 Astra (max) at 57 and GPT-6.1 Sol (max) at 55. Anthropic and OpenAI hold the top five models.

> Intelligence Index v5 combines performance across nine evaluations: AA-Briefcase v1.1, AutomationBench-AA, Terminal-Bench 4.0, FrontierCode 2.0, AA-Omniscience v1.1, GDP.pdf, HLE-Diamond, CritPt v2 (Hard Subset), and Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1.

> No single model leads all nine evaluations. Claude Opus 5.5 leads three of the nine.

> Claude Opus 5.5 (max) costs $10.04 per Intelligence Index task, compared with GPT-6 Astra (max) at $6.24 and GPT-6.1 Sol (max) at $1.27.

> The top open weights model, Kimi K3.1 Pro (weights not yet released), scores 46. Among models with released weights, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (max) and GLM-5.3 (max) score highest at 32.
