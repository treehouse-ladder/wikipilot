# GAMEGO: Training Game-Dev Agents with Synthetic Trajectories Anchored in Real-World Assets

## Excerpts

> We introduce GAMEGO, a framework that transforms brief game seeds — short natural-language descriptions — into comprehensive Product Requirements Documents grounded in industry game-dev practices, then synthesizes development trajectories from those PRDs to train specialized coding agents.

> GameGoData contains 55,060 development trajectories spanning 2D, 2.5D, and 3D games; GameGoBench provides 124 diverse game queries as a held-out evaluation benchmark.

> GameGoCoder, trained on GameGoData, outperforms matched baselines on GameGoBench and achieves comparable performance to frontier models on game-development tasks, demonstrating that domain-anchored synthetic data can close much of the gap with large proprietary models.

> GAMEGO anchors its synthetic trajectories in real-world game assets — art, mechanics, design patterns — grounding the generated PRDs in the conventions and constraints that professional game developers actually encounter.
