## Excerpts

> The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt Remastered launches on September 29 for PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2, and it's free if you already own The Witcher 3. This marks the first time the game will run natively on Switch 2.

> The remastered version includes improved controls, a skill tree revamp, expanded photo mode, both past expansions Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine, and tons more. All current owners will gain access to the two expansions for free.

> The Witcher 3 Remastered includes upgrades across visuals, combat, traversal and several of the RPG's existing systems, with new textures, updated rendering, advanced path tracing and improved foliage among the visual changes.
