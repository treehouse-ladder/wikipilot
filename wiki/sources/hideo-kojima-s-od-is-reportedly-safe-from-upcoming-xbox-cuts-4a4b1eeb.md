## Excerpts

> Hideo Kojima's Xbox console-exclusive horror game, OD, is reportedly safe amidst reports and rumors of studio closures, layoffs, and game cancellations at Xbox.

> According to a report from IGN, a source familiar with Microsoft's Xbox plans has stated OD remains in development at Kojima Productions and will still be published by Xbox.

> OD is being made in collaboration with director Jordan Peele and is set to star Sophia Lillis, Hunter Schafer, and the late Udo Kier.
