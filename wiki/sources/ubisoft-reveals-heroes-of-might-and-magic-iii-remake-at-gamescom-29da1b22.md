## Excerpts

> Ubisoft announced a Heroes of Might and Magic III remake at Gamescom Opening Night Live. The remake is being developed by Ubisoft's Chengdu and Shanghai studios in China, with support from its Paris and Montpellier studios.

> Built completely from the ground up on the Snowdrop engine, this remake trades the original's fixed perspective for a full 3D environment with free camera control. It features 4K high-resolution graphics, dynamic lighting effects, and real-time weather and seasonal change systems.

> The game maintains data compatibility with thousands of user-created maps and campaigns built by fans over the past 25 years.
