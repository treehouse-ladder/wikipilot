## Excerpts

> Area music can now be built in FMOD Studio and Wwise, the music switcher takes a stinger that lands on the next bar or beat, and GameMaker gets its first set player.

> A one-shot can now be assigned in the Switcher with a timing choice of next bar, next beat, or immediately. The game fires it over the music in Unity, Godot, the web, FMOD, Wwise, Unreal and GameMaker.

> In Wwise area music is a Music Switch Container on a State Group named after your folder, and in FMOD an event with a labeled parameter.
