## Excerpts

> A new Ultra 4K mode scales geometry generation from 2048 cubed to 4096 cubed, and as of this release is the only 3D generative model to reach it, with raw meshes up to 80 million triangles before simplification.

> On a new Detail Richness benchmark, Meshy 7.1 ranks first at 1K, 2K, and 4K render resolutions.

> Meshy 7.1 is open to all registered users, while only subscribed users are able to access Ultra 2K and Ultra 4K resolutions as well as download models generated by Meshy 7.1. Ultra 4K is available for single-view generation only.
