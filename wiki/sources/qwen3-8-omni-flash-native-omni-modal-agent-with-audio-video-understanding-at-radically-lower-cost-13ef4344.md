## Excerpts

> Qwen3.8-Omni-Flash is our first omni-modal model built around agent capabilities, integrating native audio-video understanding, reasoning and tool calling into a single model, with a 1M-token context window while maintaining text performance comparable to a text-only model of the same size.

> The API price per hour of audio input decreases by more than 98%, and the price per hour of audio-visual input decreases by more than 93% compared to the previous Qwen3.5-Omni-Plus; video input costs drop by about 89%.

> For audio-visual agents, coding, and long-horizon tasks, the model improves by 36.5 points on WildClawBench-MM and 22.3 points on AgenticVBench, while scoring 69.6 on UniClawBench; its audio-video capabilities are close to Gemini 3.8 Flash.

> Averaged across the WildClawBench-MM and UniClawBench agent tests, agent performance improved by 19.5 points over the previous generation, and averaged +25% across 29 evaluations over Qwen3.5-Omni-Plus.
