## Excerpts

> Ideogram's pricing FAQ says the company does not claim ownership of your generated images, and you're free to use them for any purpose, including commercial use.

> Open weights are a quantized version of the model published on Hugging Face under the Ideogram Non-Commercial Model Agreement. Under that license, commercial use isn't permitted.

> The self-serve commercial license covers smaller self-hosted commercial use. Its price is listed as $300 for 10,000 images per month. Customer-facing use, full-precision weights, and custom terms require the Enterprise license.
