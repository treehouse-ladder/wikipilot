## Excerpts

> Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full are Meta's first frontier-class models, designed for personal and professional AI agents. Meta reports state-of-the-art results across benchmarks for agents, memory, and coding. Muse Wonder is available through the Meta Model API and Muse Code; Muse Wonder Full is available to Muse Code subscribers. Meta's model card introduces the Personal Superintelligence Index (PSI), an internal composite of seven personal and knowledge-work evaluations, and claims comparable performance to leading closed models for coding and specialized work.
