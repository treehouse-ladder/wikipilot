## Excerpts

> Resident Evil Veronica, a remake of Code Veronica, appears to use first-person perspective and is coming to Steam, PS5, PC, and Switch 2 in 2027. Studio MDHR announced a new Cuphead game that is hand drawn. The Wolf Among Us 2 will release in 2027, and the original game is getting a remaster coming in holiday 2026. Palworld's 1.0 update received a cinematic trailer and will launch on July 10 after over two years on Early Access. Fumito Ueda, the creator of Ico, Shadow of the Colossus, and The Last Guardian, revealed his previously-hinted-at Project Robot, now renamed Gen Atlas.
