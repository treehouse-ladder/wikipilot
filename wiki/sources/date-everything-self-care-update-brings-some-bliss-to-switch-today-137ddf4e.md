## Excerpts

> Date Everything!, the dating sim from Itchio-popular developer Sassy Chica, has received its 'Self-Care Update' on Nintendo Switch today. The update adds new date routes, expanded dialogue trees, and quality-of-life improvements including the ability to skip previously seen dialogue.

> The game, which lets players romance everyday objects and concepts (alarm clocks, houseplants, the concept of Monday), has become a word-of-mouth hit since its original release, selling over 500,000 copies across platforms.

> The Self-Care Update also adds a new 'comfort mode' that removes fail states and time pressure, broadening the game's accessibility beyond players comfortable with traditional dating-sim mechanics.
