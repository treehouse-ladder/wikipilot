## Excerpts

> Songs made while subscribed (to a Pro or Premier plan) are granted commercial use rights. This allows you to distribute songs to Spotify, Apple Music, etc, using the distributor of your choice. For any song that you download from the platform as a paying subscriber, you have the commercial rights to the music. Free-plan songs cannot be monetized and there is no retroactive license.
