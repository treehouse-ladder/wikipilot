## Excerpts

> We separate harness into two mechanisms: task decomposition, which structures a task into sub-goals, and guided execution, which reshapes local action distributions during execution. More elaborate harnesses are not uniformly better: increasing decomposition or guidance can sometimes improve execution, but can also reduce final task success. The decomposition allows quantifying how workflow granularity, retry budgets, and guidance-induced action reweighting shape the performance limits of harness design, and reveals concrete failure modes, including over-decomposition, over-pruning, and hallucinated execution.
