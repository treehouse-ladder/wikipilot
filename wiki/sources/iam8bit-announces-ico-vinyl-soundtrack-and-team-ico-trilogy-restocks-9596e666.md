## Excerpts

> iam8bit has announced a new vinyl release for ICO, alongside returning pressings of Shadow of the Colossus and The Last Guardian. The ICO Vinyl Soundtrack is priced at $31.99 and is pressed on Ocean Wave Vinyl, with over 40 tracks composed by pentagon and Michiru Oshima, along with new artwork by Nimit Malavia.

> The Shadow of the Colossus 2xLP Vinyl Soundtrack and The Last Guardian 2xLP Vinyl Soundtrack are each $42.99, with Shadow of the Colossus on Translucent Colored Vinyl including Kow Otani's score, while The Last Guardian features Takeshi Furukawa's soundtrack.

> The Team ICO Trilogy Soundtrack Bundle is being offered for $119.99, which includes all three soundtrack releases and comes with the Team ICO Trilogy Slipcase, a premium storage case designed to house the complete set. Pre-orders are now open through August 31 at iam8bit.com.
