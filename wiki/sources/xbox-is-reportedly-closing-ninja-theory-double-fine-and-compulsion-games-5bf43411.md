## Excerpts

> Ninja Theory employees were informed on Monday that the studio would be closing, according to The Verge, but the team is attempting to find a buyer that can keep them operational.

> Double Fine is 'in active negotiations to spin off,' Bloomberg says, but job losses are expected even for studios that manage to buy their independence.

> The closure marks the first confirmed studio shutdown under the Xbox Reset framework that CEO Asha Sharma and chief content officer Matt Booty outlined in a publicly posted memo six days earlier.
