## Excerpts

> During the June 2026 Nintendo Direct, a fresh look at Kingdom Hearts 4 was revealed, showing that it will be available on Nintendo Switch 2 when it launches. The trailer, about a minute and a half long, showed glimpses of Sora, Strelitzia (from the mobile game), and other new characters in the mysterious, Shibuya-like city of Quadratum. The new trailer lacked a release window.
