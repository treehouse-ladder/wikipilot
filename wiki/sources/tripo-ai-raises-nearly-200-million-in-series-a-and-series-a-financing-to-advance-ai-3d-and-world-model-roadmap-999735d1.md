## Excerpts

> Tripo AI announced the completion of its Series A+ and Series A++ financing rounds, raising nearly $200 million in total. The new capital will be used to expand Tripo AI's AI 3D and world model research teams, accelerate core algorithm development, strengthen data and infrastructure systems, and broaden the company's global product and ecosystem presence.

> Alongside the financing, Tripo AI introduced Project Eden, a world model research initiative designed to support persistent, reusable, and multiplayer interactive environments. Project Eden is Tripo AI's research initiative for building persistent world models through a decoupled architecture that separates world state from visual rendering.

> Project Eden is built on three distinct layers: the Structured State Layer (which maintains the underlying 3D world state), the State-to-Observation Interface (which converts the world state into semantic and geometric conditions), and the Generative Rendering Layer (which produces detailed visual output in real time). By decoupling state transition from visual rendering, Project Eden is designed to unlock persistent, modifiable, and multi-agent worlds.
