## Excerpts

> ElevenLabs introduces Music v2.5, its best performing music model yet. Music v2.5 is now the default for prompted and reference generation in ElevenMusic.

> In a blind test across 47,885 pairs, v2.5 was the preferred take the majority of the time. The biggest gap was in vocal-led and acoustic-heavy genres.

> Music v2.5 is live in ElevenCreative and the ElevenLabs API. The API model ID is music_v2_5, and Music endpoints and SDKs now accept music_v2_5. Music v2 remains available.

> The music_v1 model is deprecated and will be removed on January 31, 2027. Please migrate to music_v2_5 for continued service.
