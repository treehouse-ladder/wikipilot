## Excerpts

> Electronic Arts announced that it's sold itself in a $55 billion deal to an investor consortium made up of Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, Jared Kushner's Affinity Partners, and the private equity firm Silver Lake. If the deal closes, EA will remain headquartered in Redwood City, California, and will continue to be led by CEO Andrew Wilson. The $55 billion take-private deal is the largest leveraged buyout (LBO) of all time.
