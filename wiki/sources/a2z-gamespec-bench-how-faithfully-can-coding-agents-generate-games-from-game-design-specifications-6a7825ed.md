## Excerpts

> A2Z GameSpec-Bench is a benchmark of 100 long-form GDDs for evaluating end-to-end specification-driven game development. The corpus contains 50 Small designs with compact scope and 50 Big designs with extensive content. Each GDD is turned into a Dependency-Aware Contract that records individual rules, constraints, and prerequisite relations between rules.

> Requirement-specific feedback improves GDD Fidelity by 10.9% relative to self-revision after two rounds. Current agents struggle to jointly satisfy interdependent requirements across code implementation and actual play.
