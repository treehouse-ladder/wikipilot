## Excerpts

> The soundtrack for psychological horror walking sim Eclipsium is heading to vinyl via Materia Collective. The album features ambient, post-rock, and industrial noise.

> The 2LP is available for pre-order for $39, alongside a CD edition for $15, with the vinyl slated for release later this year.

> The score was composed by Hudson Bikichky in his debut indie project, drifting between ambient textures, pillowy post-rock, and crushing industrial noise.
