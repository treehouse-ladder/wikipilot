## Excerpts

> As of April 2026, Catalog will no longer accept titles that use 'Generative AI' for art, audio, music, text, or dialog. However, Catalog titles that have used AI assistance in the coding process will be flagged as such and specify the extent that it was used (for example, 'Lua debugging') so the customer can decide whether to support it or not. Panic's ban includes (but isn't necessarily limited to) large language models (LLMs) like ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, AI-based image generation models like Stable Diffusion and Midjourney, and AI-based audio creation models like MuseNet and Udio.
