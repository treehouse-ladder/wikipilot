## Excerpts

> We here at Blizzard feel very lucky and happy that we're not using generative AI. We're just writing what we think is cool, and are happy to do that.

> AI in music still feels quite a way off ... Music is not there yet. Usually, you hear it, and you instantly know something is not right about it, or the fidelity isn't there.

> World of Warcraft has a pretty broad and diverse musical identity ... The music is about being in the background to support the gameplay and the story without overstepping boundaries, unless it's a big cinematic moment.
