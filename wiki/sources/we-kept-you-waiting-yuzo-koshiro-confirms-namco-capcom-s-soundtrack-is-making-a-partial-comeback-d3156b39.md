## Excerpts

> Yuzo Koshiro confirmed that after about 20 years since the game's 2005 release, the Namco × Capcom soundtrack is finally being made available for streaming, beginning on various services starting April 11, 2026. The game was released on PS2 in 2005, developed by Monolith Soft, and featured the talents of Soichiro Morizumi, Takuji Kawano, Kazue Saito and Kazunori Haruyama alongside Koshiro. This marks the first time the soundtrack has been officially available on streaming platforms after being unavailable for two decades.
