## Excerpts

> BAFTA Games Awards nominee Kazuma Jinnouchi, known for his work on franchises like Halo, Star Wars, Metal Gear, and Ghost in the Shell, takes the helm as the game's lead composer.

> The featurette features Guild Wars 3 audio lead Chase Thompson and music lead Nadia Wheaton discussing the creative philosophy behind the score and offering a peek at the orchestra recording sessions with the City of Prague Philharmonic Orchestra from earlier this year.

> ArenaNet approached the music with four core pillars: Respect the legacy. Melody is paramount. Negative space. Sense of place.

> Jinnouchi said: I wanted to make it feel like someone's reading your tale – so it's like your mom reading you an old story. And I wanted to have that sort of warm feel to it.

> He will be joined by contributing composers Asuka Ito, whose credits include compositions for Nintendo franchises like Rhythm Heaven and Pilotwings, and longtime Guild Wars 2 composer Maclaine Diemer.
