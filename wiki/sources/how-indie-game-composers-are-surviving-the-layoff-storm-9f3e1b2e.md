## Excerpts

> Gaming has lost its shine for investors, resulting in less cash available to fund projects that break away from live-service games.

> The job market for video game composers is deteriorating—work is drying up while more people are looking for gigs, leaving junior composers competing against industry heavyweights and tempted to undercut them.

> Many composers add sound design to their skillset, making their profiles more versatile and appealing to indie studios.

> The huge issue freelancers share is the lack of long-term visibility.
