## Excerpts

> Take-Two's financials are steady while CEO Strauss Zelnick claims that Grand Theft Auto VI pre-orders are exceeding expectations, tracking far above the company's initial expectations.

> On the earnings call, Zelnick said: No one's ever seen anything like this before at Take-Two, or in the industry.

> Third-party estimates from Sensor Tower put GTA VI at roughly 9.69 million total pre-purchases, equating to about 942 million dollars in gross revenue, with PS5 accounting for around 75 percent of pre-orders versus 25 percent on Xbox Series X|S.

> Grand Theft Auto VI is scheduled to launch November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with a PC version potentially following in early 2027.
