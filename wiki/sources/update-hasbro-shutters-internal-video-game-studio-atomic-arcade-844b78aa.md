## Excerpts

> Atomic Arcade, the Hasbro and Wizards of the Coast-owned studio that was making a game based on the iconic G.I. Joe character Snake Eyes, has been shut down.

> Despite the studio closure, Hasbro stated that 'The Snake Eyes game is not cancelled. The team is currently taking time to evaluate the path forward for the game.'

> Atomic Arcade was established in 2021 and had been developing a 'high-quality video game' focused on G.I. Joe character Snake Eyes. In 2024, Hasbro and its subsidiary, Wizards of the Coast, had invested $1 billion in its video game development business, including Atomic Arcade's Snake Eyes game.
