## Excerpts

> Castlevania: Belmont's Curse currently stands at 86 on Metacritic and 85 on OpenCritic, with scores this high not being seen in the series since 2005's Dawn of Sorrow.

> KitGuru places the game sixth overall in the franchise, with only five entries scoring higher, including the GBA trilogy, Dawn of Sorrow and Symphony of the Night.

> The game was co-developed by Konami with Evil Empire, with production support from Motion Twin, and follows Rose Belmont, Trevor Belmont's daughter, exploring Dracula's castle with the Vampire Killer whip.
