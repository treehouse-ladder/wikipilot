## Excerpts

> Black Pony Immersive is led by Harvey Smith, creative director of Dishonored 2 and Dishonored: Death of the Outsider, and Ben Horne, most recently the production director on Redfall. The duo worked together at Arkane Austin for years until parent company Microsoft shut down the studio in 2024.

> The studio has been operating for about a year and already has 26 people on staff. Black Pony Immersive is building single-player, first-person narrative games centered on exploration and creative problem-solving, with Deus Ex, Dishonored, Prey, and Thief named as the main touchstones.

> The team is staffed with veterans from Insomniac, Epic, Avalanche and Volition studios, is based in Austin, Texas with remote workers, and has secured funding from an unnamed publisher.
