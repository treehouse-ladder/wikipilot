## Excerpts

> We propose CapCode, a framework for constructing coding datasets with randomized tests whose best achievable non-cheating performance is deliberately capped below one. This capped-performance design gives evaluation scores a clearer interpretation: scores substantially above the cap are implausible and therefore provide evidence of cheating. CapReward reduces cheating during training by penalizing performance beyond the cap, leading to smaller open-hidden performance gaps.
