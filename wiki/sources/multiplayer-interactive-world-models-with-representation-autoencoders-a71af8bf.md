## Excerpts

> We introduce the first multiplayer world model for highly dynamic environments that conditions on the action streams of multiple agents and learns to attribute scene changes to the correct player.

> We study this in the game of Rocket League, where players compete and cooperate under fast, tightly coupled dynamics.

> The 5-billion-parameter latent diffusion model generates four-player matches in real time, producing 20 frames per second on a single Nvidia B200 GPU.
