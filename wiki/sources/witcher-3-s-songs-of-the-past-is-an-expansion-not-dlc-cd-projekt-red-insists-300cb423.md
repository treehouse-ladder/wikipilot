## Excerpts

> Songs of the Past is The Witcher 3's third expansion, following Hearts of Stone (2015) and Blood and Wine (2016). CD Projekt Red insists it is a full expansion, not DLC. The new expansion arrives in 2027 on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC.

> Songs of the Past has players returning to the role of Geralt of Rivia in a brand-new adventure, taking players to the brand new realm of Letten, an idyllic land of rural tradition that hides a dark secret just below the surface.
