## Excerpts

> Midsummer Studios, the game development company founded by Firaxis Games veteran Jake Solomon, is shutting its doors. Solomon shared the news in a post on X, where he shared 'pre-pre alpha' footage of Burbank, the studio's life sim game, but did not share any details on the reason for the closure.

> The closure concludes a two-year run for Midsummer, which started operations in 2024 on the back of $6 million in funding raised by Transcend Fund in partnership with Tirta Ventures, Betaworks Ventures, 1Up Ventures, F4 Fund, Krafton, and Day Zero Productions.

> Jake Solomon founded Midsummer Studios in 2024 with the ambitious mission statement that it would 'reinvent the life sim genre.' The company raised more than $6 million from a variety of investors, but it seems Midsummer will close without releasing any games.

> Burbank did use some form of AI (Solomon did not specify if it was generative AI or classic machine learning-driven techniques) to write and voice its characters. However, all of the art was created by talented artists, and the studio had no interest in replacing any developers with AI.
