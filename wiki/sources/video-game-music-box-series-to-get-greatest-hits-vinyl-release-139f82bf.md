## Excerpts

> Best Of Video Game Music Box is available to pre-order now via Materia Records and brings together a handpicked collection of beloved themes from across the world of video games, reimagining familiar compositions through the delicate and nostalgic sound of the music box into something intimate, reflective, and dreamlike.

> The compilation will feature arranged music from The Legend Of Zelda: Ocarina Of Time, Super Mario Galaxy, Undertale, Pokemon Red & Blue, Kingdom Hearts and Deltarune. The release will be a single 180g LP on black vinyl.
