## Excerpts

> Embark Studios' extraction shooter Arc Raiders has sold 14 million copies worldwide, according to publisher Nexon in its fiscal report for the fourth quarter ended December 31, 2025.

> The game displayed exceptional retention and strong player metrics after peaking at roughly 960,000 concurrent players in January, with around six million weekly active users.

> After just 15 weeks on shelves, Nexon said Arc Raiders defied the standard quick-up-quick-down trajectory of a hit game to establish itself as a blockbuster franchise.

> Arc Raiders has since sold over 16 million units worldwide, with an additional 4.6 million units sold in Q1 2026.
