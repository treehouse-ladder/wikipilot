## Excerpts

> Nvidia's Nemotron 3.5 Super VL is now the leading US open weight model, scoring 26 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index.

> Nemotron 3.5 Super VL has 120B total parameters with 12B active, a 1M-token context, and accepts text and image input. The weights are released under the OpenMDW-1.1 license, which permits commercial use.

> Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B is the first model in NVIDIA's new Bolt family, with 74B total and 9B active parameters, a 256k context window, scoring 21 on the Intelligence Index. Weights are publicly downloadable but no API providers currently serve it.

> The US still trails the United Arab Emirates (K2 Horizon 375B A23B, 31) and China (MiMo-V2.6-Pro, 46) on open weights intelligence.
