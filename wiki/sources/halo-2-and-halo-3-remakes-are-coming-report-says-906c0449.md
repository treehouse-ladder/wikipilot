## Excerpts

> According to a reputable leaker, Microsoft is "definitely" working on remakes of Halo 2 and Halo 3 and they are in "active development." The reported remakes of Halo 2 and Halo 3 are in "early development" and are moving ahead "regardless of how well Campaign Evolved does."

> These games, if real, would join a third unannounced Halo game that is said to be a Fortnite-like live-service multiplayer game. For now, all that's confirmed is Halo: Campaign Evolved, which releases on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC.

> Halo Studios has not confirmed any plans for a Halo 2 or Halo 3 remake.
