> DeepSeek-V4 consists of two Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) language models — DeepSeek-V4-Pro with 1.6T parameters (49B activated) and DeepSeek-V4-Flash with 284B parameters (13B activated) — both supporting a context length of one million tokens. The model weights are licensed under the MIT License. V4-Pro-Max scores 80.6% SWE-bench Verified and 93.5 LiveCodeBench. V4-Pro's Codeforces rating of 3,206 surpasses GPT-5.4's 3,168.
