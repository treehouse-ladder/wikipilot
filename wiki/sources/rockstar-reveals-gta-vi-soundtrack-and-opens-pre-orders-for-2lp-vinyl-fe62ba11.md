## Excerpts

> Rockstar launched pre-orders for a 2LP vinyl pressing of GTA VI: The Album, a 34-track collection of original music featuring artists like Yung Lean, Future, Metro Boomin, Keith Richards, and PinkPantheress.

> The album is set to land on November 19, coinciding with the game's launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

> The album features 34 original tracks that capture the electric energy of Grand Theft Auto VI's world of Vice City and Leonida, with a genre-defying roster of the most exciting artists in the world.

> More information about the game's dynamic score and the next evolution of its in-game radio will be revealed later.
