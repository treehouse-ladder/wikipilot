## Excerpts

> When Incube8 Games finally published the long-awaited Infinity for Game Boy Color in 2026, they pulled out all the stops. Originally developed from 1999 to 2001 by Affinix Software, Infinity was designed to push Nintendo's handheld to its absolute limits.

> Legendary composer Yuzo Koshiro (Ys, ActRaiser, Etrian Odyssey) gets a guest spot! The Infinity arrange album features several new arranged tracks, with a few recorded in 2001 and circulated through demos released by composer Eric Hache at the time.

> Koshiro knocked it out of the park with the arranged version of Another Challenger Enters, delivering a high-quality piece of rock music bordering on metal, complete with guitar shredding near the end.
