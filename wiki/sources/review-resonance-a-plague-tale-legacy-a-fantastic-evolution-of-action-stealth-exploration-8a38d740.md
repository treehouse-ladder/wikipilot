## Excerpts

> Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy is Asobo's finest adventure to date.

> Asobo's prequel adds toe-to-toe combat and hands-off puzzling to the Plague Tale recipe to winning effect, though the focus has shifted from the stealth-based gameplay of earlier entries.

> The game is set 15 years before A Plague Tale: Requiem, where players control Sophia, a fierce young plunderer, on the run but determined to uncover the secrets of her past.
