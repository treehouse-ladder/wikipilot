## Excerpts

> Kling Image 4.0 was released on November 17, 2026. It generates images natively at 2K, 4K, or 8K, selected with the resolution parameter (2K by default).

> Setting result_type to series returns a coherent set of 2 to 10 images from one prompt, in order (Image Sets).

> Kling Image 4.0 renders up to 2,000 characters in one image, with support for several languages including English, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean.

> The kling/v4/text-to-image endpoint is priced at $0.063 per unit on fal; billing is pay-per-use through the API.

> Kling Image 4.0 can serve as a keyframe generator for Kling 4.0 video.
