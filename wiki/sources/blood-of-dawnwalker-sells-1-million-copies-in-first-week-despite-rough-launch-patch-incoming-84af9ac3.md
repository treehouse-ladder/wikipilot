# Blood of Dawnwalker sells 1 million copies in first week despite rough launch, patch incoming

## Excerpts

> Rebel Wolves has confirmed that Blood of Dawnwalker sold over 1 million copies across PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X/S in its first week on sale, calling it "beyond our expectations for a debut title from a new studio." Game director Konrad Tomaszkiewicz acknowledged in a dev letter that the launch build fell short of their quality bar and outlined the 1.1 patch schedule: the update will address the frame-pacing and input-lag issues on PC that reviewers flagged, adjusts stamina values in parry-based encounters, and fixes a progression-blocking quest in Act 2 Chapter 3.

> In the same letter, Tomaszkiewicz addressed the "Simon Says parry" criticism directly: "We hear you. We designed the combat to feel learned-and-mastered, not trial-and-error. The 1.1 window adjustment is our first step; we're also looking at adding a practice mode so players can internalise timing before a boss encounter ends their run." The 1.1 patch is scheduled for October 10.
