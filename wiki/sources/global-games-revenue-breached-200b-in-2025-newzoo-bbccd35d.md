## Excerpts

> The global games market passed $200 billion in revenue for the first time in 2025, closing at $201.6 billion according to Newzoo's latest quarterly update to the Global Games Market Report.

> Global gaming revenue was up 9.1 percent year over year, with PC and mobile doing most of the heavy lifting while console grew more modestly.

> Mobile remained the largest segment at $113.3 billion. PC reached $43.6 billion, posting its strongest annual growth in Newzoo's historical dataset at +12%. Console revenue reached $44.7 billion, up just 2.8%.

> For 2026-2028, Newzoo forecasts the market to grow at a 5.1% CAGR, reaching $234.4 billion by 2028.
