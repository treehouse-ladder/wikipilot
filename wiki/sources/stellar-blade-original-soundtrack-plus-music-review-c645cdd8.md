## Excerpts

> The Stellar Blade Original Soundtrack PLUS is a bonus pack of 16 additional songs that, for one reason or another, were not included in the original release. The second half of PLUS is almost entirely devoted to instrumental versions of various tracks from across the wider OST.

> The album starts off with 'Lullaby of a Secret,' featuring breathy French vocals by Pernelle, which swells and calms like the sea with gliding strings punctuated by piano, followed by 'Just Fine,' another vocal track in English lead by Pealeaf with chilled acoustic guitar.

> 'Battle Droid' fuses a dance beat, a simple vocal line, slight rock influences, and a little gothy choral sample, while 'Boss Challenge' is probably the most 'traditional' video game music-sounding piece here, and it's quite pleasant to have a track with a simpler format to act as something of a palate cleanser.

> SHIFT UP's Stellar Blade soundtrack turned out to be one of the reviewer's favourites of 2024, taking them by surprise with its blend of contemporary popular music genres, electronics, and experimentation. Stellar Blade Original Soundtrack PLUS, as well as the game's huge original soundtrack, are available for streaming and purchase wherever you find music.
