## Excerpts

> ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview scores 31 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, well above average among comparable models (median: 23).

> ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview is open weights and can be self-hosted. The model weights are publicly available and can be downloaded. Its license is Apache 2.0.

> ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview has 280 billion total parameters with 14 billion active parameters, and a 1M token context window.

> ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview was released on November 23, 2026.
