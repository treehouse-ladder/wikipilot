## Excerpts

> AlphaEvolve and FunSearch have demonstrated the potential of combining large language models with evolutionary search for automated algorithm discovery, but discovery success is shaped not only by model capability but also significantly by the design of the execution infrastructure, i.e., the harness.

> We investigate effective harness design through three questions: under a fixed token budget, is it better to produce many algorithms with brief thought or fewer algorithms with deeper thought? How should the harness handle evaluation hacks, where generated programs exploit the scoring function? And how can agents that require full filesystem access execute safely in parallel?
