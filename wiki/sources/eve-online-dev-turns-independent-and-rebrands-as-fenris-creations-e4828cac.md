## Excerpts

> EVE Online developer CCP Games became an independent studio and rebranded as Fenris Creations after being sold back to its management team by former owner Pearl Abyss in a deal worth $120 million. Pearl Abyss had originally acquired CCP Games in 2018 for around $425 million. Fenris has entered into a research partnership with Google DeepMind that will see Google acquire a minority stake in the company, with the focus being on advancing understanding of intelligence in complex, dynamic systems.
