## Excerpts

> Skydance's gaming division is now laying off scores of developers just a month after its parent company merged with Warner Bros.

> The affected studio was formed when Skydance Interactive and Skydance New Media were both merged into a new entity called Paramount Games over the summer.

> Insider Gaming reports that at least 30 people at Paramount Games were impacted. It's unclear if anyone on the Warner Bros. side was cut as well.
