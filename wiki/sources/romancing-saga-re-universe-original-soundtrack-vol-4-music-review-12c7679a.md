## Excerpts

> The first and third battle themes, 'Quartet Vibration' and 'Quartet Requiem,' are lyrical in nature, which is not typical for Kishikawa performances. Her other vocal track, 'Tetra Realm,' is non-lyrical, instead relying on her jazzy scat singing improvisation.

> Junko Iwao also handles vocals for a battle theme, which I believe is the final story battle for the Cloud Realm story arc: 'Two-Winged Princess.'

> The vocal theme 'Mayu-hime' is the best and most surprising track on the album, with Junko Iwao on vocals and choral work from several singers including KOCHO.

> Total Time: 61'07"
