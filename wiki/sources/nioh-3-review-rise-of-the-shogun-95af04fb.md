## Excerpts

> Nioh 3 expands the possibility space of the series, and at its best moments on the early maps, playing it is surprisingly relaxing with interesting discoveries nearby as you explore.

> Nioh 3 expands and refines combat systems while introducing more exploration and discovery through its 'open field' design. However, what's gained from this ambitious scope is hampered by a surprising lack of challenge and a deadweight story that overstays its welcome.

> Nioh 3 is much more curated and focused. The game consists of multiple maps rather than a single interconnected world, with sections that still feel like classic Nioh featuring multiple paths that coalesce, connected to other freely explorable areas.
