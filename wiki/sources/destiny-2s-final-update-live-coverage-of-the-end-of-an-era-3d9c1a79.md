## Excerpts

> Destiny 2's final content update, Monument of Triumph, has gone live. After years of expansions, seasons, and episodes, the live-service era of Destiny 2 is officially over. The game remains playable but will receive no further new content from Bungie.

> The final update marks the end of Destiny 2's eight-year run as an active live-service title. Players gathered online to experience the conclusion together, with many long-time guardians logging in to mark the occasion.

> Bungie confirmed that the game will remain online indefinitely following the final update, though all seasonal content and expansions will be locked behind their respective purchase gates. Monument of Triumph adds a free final quest and commemorative items for all players.
