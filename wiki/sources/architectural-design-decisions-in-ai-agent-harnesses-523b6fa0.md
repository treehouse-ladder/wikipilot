## Excerpts

> This paper presents a protocol-guided, source-grounded empirical study of 70 publicly available agent-system projects, addressing three questions: which design-decision dimensions recur across projects, which co-occurrences structure those decisions, and which typical architectural patterns emerge. The study identifies five recurring design dimensions (subagent architecture, context management, tool systems, safety mechanisms, and orchestration), with the corpus favoring file-persistent, hybrid, and hierarchical context strategies among other patterns.
