> On June 15, 2026, Claude Code's programmatic and agentic usage moves from your general subscription compute pool into a dedicated credit system billed at full API rates. Pro subscribers receive $20 in Claude Code credits per month, Max 5x subscribers receive $100, and Max 20x subscribers receive $200.

> The change separates interactive chat usage from automated agentic workloads — long-running background sessions, dynamic workflows, and routines now draw against the dedicated credit pool rather than the shared compute window.
