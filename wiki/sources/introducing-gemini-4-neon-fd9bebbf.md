## Excerpts

> Gemini 4 Neon ranks #1 on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index with a score of 58.6.

> On Terminal-Bench 4.1 Gemini 4 Neon scores 65.2%, and on FrontierCode 2.0 it scores 46.2% at its default high setting.

> On SWE-sweep, Gemini 4 Neon already leads on the mini-SWE harness (12.8%) and more than triples to a state-of-the-art 39.0% in Google Antigravity.

> Gemini 4 Neon is priced at $3 per million input tokens ($0.15 per million cached input tokens, a 95% discount) and $15 per million output tokens, with a 1 million-token context window.

> The knowledge cutoff for Gemini 4 Neon is March 2026, though in some domains the model's knowledge may be less up to date.
