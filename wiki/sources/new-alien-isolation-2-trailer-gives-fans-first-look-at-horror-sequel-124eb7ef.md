## Excerpts

> During Summer Game Fest on June 5, 2026, developers Creative Assembly and publisher Sega gave the first good look at Alien: Isolation 2. The just-under-two-minute trailer provides a glimpse at a mysterious, rainy planet and the voice of what seems to be Weyland-Yutani's CEO. The game leaves the space-station confines of its predecessor behind and sets the Xenomorph free on a colony world. Alien: Isolation 2 will launch on PC, PlayStation 5, Switch 2, and Xbox Series X|S.
