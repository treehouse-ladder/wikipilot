## Excerpts

> Grok Imagine 1.5 Preview debuted at number one on the Artificial Analysis Video Arena Image-to-Video leaderboard with an Elo rating of 1404. The model turns a single still image into fluid, cinematic video with native synchronized audio, extends clip lengths to 15 seconds, and supports 480p or 720p output at 24 fps. It is available via the xAI API in preview alongside Grok Voice.
