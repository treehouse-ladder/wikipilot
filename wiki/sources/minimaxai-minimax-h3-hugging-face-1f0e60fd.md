## Excerpts

> MiniMax H3 is released as two task-specific checkpoints, each containing a specialized Omni Transformer Model together with the required processor, tokenizer, text encoder, Visual VAE, and standalone Audio VAE components. The total repository size is 288 GB. The weights are released under the MiniMax H3 Community License Agreement, which permits commercial use for organizations under 0M annual revenue with attribution, though open weights are temporarily limited in certain regions until the regulatory and compliance framework becomes clearer. On the Artificial Analysis leaderboards H3 ranks #1 in Video Editing, #2 in Text-to-Video, and #3 in Image-to-Video.
