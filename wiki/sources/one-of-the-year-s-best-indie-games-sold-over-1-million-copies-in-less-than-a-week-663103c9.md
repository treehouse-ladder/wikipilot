# One of the Year's Best Indie Games Sold Over 1 Million Copies in Less Than a Week

## Excerpts

> Big Walk has sold over one million copies in just six days, developer Tummy Games announced today. That makes it one of the fastest-selling indie games of 2026.

> Big Walk is now the highest-rated and best-reviewed game of 2026 according to Metacritic, with a score of 94 — surpassing the previous leader Mina the Hollower (93).

> The quirky adventure game, which launched on August 5 for PC and consoles, follows a cast of animals on an epic walking journey. Critics praised its heartfelt story, charming art style, and memorable characters.

> With over a million copies sold in under a week, Big Walk has established itself as both a critical darling and a commercial success story — a rare combination in 2026's difficult market for mid-budget indie titles.
