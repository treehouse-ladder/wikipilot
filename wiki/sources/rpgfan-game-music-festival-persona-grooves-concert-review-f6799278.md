# Game Music Festival: Persona Grooves

RPGFan feature review of the Persona Grooves concert at Southbank Centre, London, part of the Game Music Festival 2026 series organized by the Game Music Foundation. Shoji Meguro attended.

## Excerpts

> The London Mozart Players formed the backbone, but the Persona series demands a little something extra, so they were accompanied by the immensely skilled Bartosz Pernal jazz orchestra, rounded off by two talented vocalists, Paula Golebiowska and Natalia Szcypula. Composer Shoji Meguro took to the stage to the sound of a deafening standing ovation.

> All of the arrangements were creative, but the Sonata arrangement of "No More What Ifs" with its soft strings and multiple sax solos was a particular highlight amongst many. Persona Grooves ended with the true showstopper that is "Memories of You," with Golebiowska and Szcypula navigating the difficult vocal sections perfectly.
