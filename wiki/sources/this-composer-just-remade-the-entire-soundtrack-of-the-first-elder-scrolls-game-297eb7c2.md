## Excerpts

> Independent composer Ryan Zachariah Martin remade the entire soundtrack of 1994's The Elder Scrolls: Arena, originally composed by Eric Heberling, using software unavailable in 1994 to present the tracks as they were originally conceived and to remove the technical barriers of the early Bethesda era.

> Martin said 'I am most proud of the character creation track' and of the intro theme, 'I think I captured the feel of what the intro was originally aiming for, with an emphasis on the beauty and simplicity of the melodies, rather than a bombastic orchestral piece.'
