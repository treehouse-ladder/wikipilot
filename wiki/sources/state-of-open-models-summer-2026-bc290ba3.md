## Excerpts

> Qwen now has 151,000 derivative repositories on the Hub — 2.6× Meta's count — cementing its position as the most-forked open-weight model family. Chinese labs account for the majority of large-model releases in 2026, spanning architectures from 754B to 2.78T parameters.

> Autonomous agents have become the primary traffic source on the Hub. Claude Code leads agent-tagged download traffic with a 44.4% share in July 2026, with the next cluster of agentic tools each below 15%.

> Models below 1B parameters continue to dominate by volume: 83% of all-time Hub downloads come from sub-1B checkpoints. This on-device-first gravity is structurally separate from the frontier-capability race.

> The gap between the top closed frontier and the top open-weight models has narrowed to roughly one benchmark generation on standard coding and reasoning evals, though closed models retain a visible lead on long-context and multi-modal tasks.
