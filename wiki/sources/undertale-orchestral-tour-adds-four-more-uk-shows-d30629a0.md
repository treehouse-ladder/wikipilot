## Excerpts

> Undertale has confirmed a 2026 orchestral tour titled "Undertale: The Determination Symphony." The tour features a 25-piece orchestra performing new arrangements of the beloved Undertale soundtrack. UK dates have been confirmed: London (September 26), Manchester (September 27), Birmingham (September 28), Liverpool (November 12), Halifax (November 13), Grimsby (November 14), Newcastle (November 15).
