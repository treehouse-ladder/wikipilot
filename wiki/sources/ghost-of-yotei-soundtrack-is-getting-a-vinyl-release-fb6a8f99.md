## Excerpts

> The Ghost of Yotei vinyl soundtrack is scheduled for release as a gold shimmer vinyl 2LP containing the complete music score composed by Toma Otowa. The album comes in a deluxe gatefold jacket with an exclusive illustration of the Yotei Six, a 12-inch insert, liner notes, and production staff credits. While Toma Otowa is the primary composer, Clare Uchima and Justin Johnson contributed as featured co-composers on select tracks, with additional music from DJ Mitsu, Takumi Kaneko, and other artists. Milan Records opened preorders for the Ghost of Yotei vinyl soundtrack album on April 10, 2026.
