## Excerpts

> The final announcement of the show was Final Fantasy VII Revelation, the final chapter of the Final Fantasy VII Remake trilogy, which features the Highwind and new gameplay featuring Cid and Vincent Valentine. The fourth season of downloadable content for Street Fighter 6 was revealed with three original characters in Yasmine, Arjun, and Bosch, as well as its next guest character: Tifa Lockhart from Final Fantasy VII. Remedy showed a new trailer for Control Resonant, the upcoming sequel starring Dylan Faden, coming out on September 24.
