## Excerpts

> MiniMax H3 generates 15-second 2K video with native stereo audio from unified text, image, video, and audio context. Previous video stacks split into text-to-video, image-to-video, first-and-last-frame, subject reference, motion reference, and video editing; MiniMax H3 folds those into one pretraining paradigm where reference and editing relationships are expressed in natural language. At 2K, H3's per-second price is less than a third of mainstream models; at 768p, less than half the price of mainstream 720p. Open weights are promised 'in the coming days,' not shipped; the API is the only path today.
