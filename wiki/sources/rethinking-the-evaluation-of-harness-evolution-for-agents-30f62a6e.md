## Excerpts

> Harness evolution is itself an iterative search procedure that repeatedly evaluates and revises candidate harnesses using task feedback, and it should therefore be compared with simple task-level search baselines under matched feedback and inference budgets to determine whether its gains arise from improved harness design or from additional search alone.

> Because the search and the final evaluation share the same benchmark, the reported gains risk overfitting to that specific task set.

> Experiments on Terminal-Bench 2.1 with GPT-5.4 and Claude Opus 4.6 show that automatic harness evolution does not consistently outperform simple test-time scaling methods and exhibits limited generalization.
