## Excerpts

> Sean Murray, managing director at Hello Games, said his plan is to wait and see what becomes the industry norm over the next few years. On major publishers' AI investments he said: cool, good luck. You know, let's see how that works out. I will watch from afar.

> I think making video games is really hard and I think people are going to learn that it's still really hard and the hard things are still hard, of making a game feel really nice to play.

> My day-to-day role is let's just enjoy making games the way we've always made games. And like, that's still working for us and we still enjoy it.
