## Excerpts

> Bethesda Game Studios union workers under the OneBGS banner held a 'Save Our Devs' march on July 15 in protest of the mass layoffs across Xbox, which slashed over 440 positions across BGS, ZOS, id, ZWU (QA) and ZeniMax corporate.

> Microsoft is legally required to participate in 'Effects Bargaining,' giving workers the right to negotiate exactly how the layoffs impact their people. The union says Microsoft has stalled those talks, and is pushing Microsoft to relocate laid-off members into open roles across Xbox.

> The march was scheduled for July 15 at 12:30 PM ET across four ZeniMax studio locations in Rockville, Austin, Dallas, and Montreal, as well as the Microsoft campus in Redmond, Washington.
