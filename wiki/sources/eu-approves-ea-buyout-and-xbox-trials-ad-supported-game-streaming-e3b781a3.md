## Excerpts

> The European Commission has approved the proposed $55 billion leveraged buyout of Electronic Arts by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund (PIF). Two private equity firms, Silver Lake and Jared Kushner's Affinity Partners, are also involved in the deal. The European Commission approved the $55 billion deal after concluding it will not "raise competition concerns."

> Completion is still expected to take place in the first quarter of 2027. The European Commission is separately examining the acquisition under its Foreign Subsidies Regulation, designed to prevent companies from using financial support provided by non-EU governments to gain unfair advantages.

> Xbox is experimenting with ad-supported game streaming, opening a lower-friction entry point for players who may not subscribe to Game Pass, while also opening a new monetization layer that could influence how cloud distribution is packaged and priced.
