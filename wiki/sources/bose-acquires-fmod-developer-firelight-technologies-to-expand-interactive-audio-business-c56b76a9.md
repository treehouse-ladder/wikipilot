## Excerpts

> Bose announced on October 1, 2026 that it is acquiring Firelight Technologies, the Melbourne-based developer of the FMOD audio middleware.

> The acquisition follows Bose's investment in acquiring StreamUnlimited Engineering in June 2026.

> Gaming is an initial area of focus for the combination. Over time, Bose also plans to explore potential applications for FMOD technology in virtual and augmented reality, automotive systems and other markets.

> FMOD will remain open and broadly supported, with continued investment in the platform and its community of developers, partners, and customers.
