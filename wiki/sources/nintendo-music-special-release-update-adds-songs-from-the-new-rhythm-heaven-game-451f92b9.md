## Excerpts

> Rhythm Heaven Groove is out this week on the Switch and to celebrate the Nintendo Music app has been updated with a 'special release'. This adds seven tracks from the new game and has a run time of 15 minutes.

> Rhythm Heaven Groove — or Rhythm Paradise Groove as it's known in Europe — is the next entry in the Rhythm Tengoku series, which began on GBA in Japan back in 2006, with sequels on DS, Wii, and 3DS making their way to the West. The game features music composed by Tsunku, the legendary Japanese pop-music producer who has composed for the series since its inception.
