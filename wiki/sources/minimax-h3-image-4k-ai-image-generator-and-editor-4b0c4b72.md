## Excerpts

> MiniMax H3 Image is a 4K AI image generator and editor. Content generated through the fal API can be used in commercial projects, subject to fal's terms of service.

> MiniMax's official pricing lists H3-Image standard text-to-image at about $0.022 per image at 1K, about $0.023 at 2K, and about $0.069 at 4K, with a limited-time 50 percent launch discount bringing 4K to about $0.034 per image.

> The open-weights model on Hugging Face is covered by the MiniMax-H3-Image Community License Agreement, which describes free use for research, personal, and commercial creation within its terms; hosted API services and revenue above a threshold require separate commercial licensing from MiniMax.

> Community GGUF and low-VRAM quantizations of MiniMax H3-Image are available on Hugging Face.
