## Excerpts

> Tripwire Interactive has laid off 36 employees in a second round of cuts this year. The studio confirmed the workforce reduction on X and said it is still working on Killing Floor 3 and Maneater 2.

> The layoffs come days after the company announced Tripwire Presents, an initiative to scale its publishing operation for smaller games.

> The earlier round of cuts, in March 2026, laid off 23 employees as part of a restructuring, bringing the studio's 2026 total to 59.

> Killing Floor 3 came out last year but has struggled to match its predecessor's numbers.
