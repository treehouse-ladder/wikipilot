## Excerpts

> The sandbox title Pokemon Pokopia snatched the ultimate Grand Award at this year's Japan Game Awards 2026 ceremony.

> Pokemon Pokopia took home the Grand Award by ditching the combat-heavy formula of the classic series, opting for a chill slow-life vibe. The game reeled in a massive demographic ranging from 5-year-olds to 79-year-olds, shifting 5 million copies globally within roughly four months of its March 5 launch.

> Capcom's Pragmata grabbed the Innovation Award. The Movement Award was given to MECCHA CHAMELEON. Team Nine's Letterplay walked away with the Game Designers Award.
