## Excerpts

> Rec Room announced that the platform will go offline on June 1, 2026, closing a decade-long chapter that attracted 150 million players worldwide but never managed to turn a profit. Token purchases end May 1; after May 18, creators will no longer be able to earn new tokens; a final creator payout will be processed on June 1. The company stated 'we never quite figured out how to make Rec Room a sustainably profitable business.' Snap is acquiring some of the Rec Room assets. The shutdown reflects the broader collapse of the metaverse investment bubble that once valued the platform at $3.5 billion.
