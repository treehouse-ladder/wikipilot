## Excerpts

> Deltarune Piano Collections: Volume 1 was released by Materia Collective only a few months after Chapters 3+4, but includes songs only from Chapters 1 and 2 of Deltarune. The arranger and performer is Trevor Alan Gomes, who is an accomplished arranger and pianist.

> Notable tracks include Rude Buster, which features quick tempo and heavy syncopation, BIG SHOT with its crashing chords and cascading arpeggios, and Field of Hopes and Dreams, described as the star of the show.

> The collection is full of intense, fast-tempo tracks, with even mid-tempo pieces like Scarlet Forest having moments of intensity, and there isn't much here that ventures toward slow, soft, or relaxing arrangements.
