## Excerpts

> GWM Worlds 2 is our latest General World Model that generates interactive real-time simulations in continuous 720p video at 24 fps with 48000 Hz audio.

> GWM Worlds 2 allows you to define the environment, subjects, visual style, physical rules and ambience of any world. Once inside, you steer it with text actions addressed to any subject or to the scene itself, alongside continuous camera motion.

> The world continues from each new input instead of following a fixed clip or script so sessions have no preset length.

> The new version adds generated audio, richer subject and scene control, and supports first- and third-person navigation, independent camera and subject control, multi-character dialogue with generated speech, agentic control for training and evaluating AI agents, and multiplayer sessions via LiveKit.
