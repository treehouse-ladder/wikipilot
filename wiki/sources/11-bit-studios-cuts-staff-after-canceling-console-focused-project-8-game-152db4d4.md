## Excerpts

> 11 Bit Studios canceled Project 8, an unannounced narrative-driven console game, due to a shift in market trends. Project 8 was intended to be the company's first title designed specifically for console gamers.

> Project 8 began development in 2018 and had 37 team members as of September 30, and is estimated to reflect as a PLN 48.4 million ($11.8 million) loss for 11 Bit Studios.

> An undisclosed number of talents involved in the title will be dismissed as a result of its cancellation. However, half of the Project 8 workforce will be given the chance to transition to existing teams or as-yet-unannounced initiatives.
