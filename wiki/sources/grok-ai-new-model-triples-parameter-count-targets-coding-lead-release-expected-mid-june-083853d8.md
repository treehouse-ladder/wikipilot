## Excerpts

> Grok V9-Medium has completed training at 1.5 trillion parameters — three times the current production 0.5T V8-small model. It was trained on Cursor data, meaning real developer workflows from one of the most widely used AI code editors, rather than solely on public code repositories. Musk announced on May 25, 2026 that training is complete and public release is approximately two to three weeks out, placing the expected launch in mid-June 2026.
