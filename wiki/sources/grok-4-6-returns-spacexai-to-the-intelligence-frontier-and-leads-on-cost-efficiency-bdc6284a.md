## Excerpts

> Grok 4.6 scores 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, in line with GPT-5.6 Sol (max), behind Claude Opus 5 (max, 63) and Claude Fable 5 (max with fallback, 62), and just ahead of Kimi K3. Grok 4.6 gains 5 points over Grok 4.5 on the Intelligence Index just over one month after its release, or +23 points compared to Grok 4.3.

> Grok 4.6 achieves a GDPval-AA v2 Elo of 1753, behind only Claude Opus 5 and with overlapping confidence intervals with Claude Fable 5 and Qwen3.8 Max. Grok 4.6 debuts on AA-Briefcase, our private benchmark of long-horizon agentic knowledge work tasks, with an Elo of 1577.

> Grok 4.6 resolves tasks in ~53 turns and ~0.5B input tokens on average, against ~103 turns and ~2.0B input tokens for Claude Opus 5 (max). Long-horizon agentic work accumulates context rapidly, so a model that reaches a comparable answer in half the turns and a quarter of the input tokens has a cost advantage well beyond its per-token pricing.

> Headline pricing is unchanged from Grok 4.5 at $2/$6 per 1M input/output tokens, 60%+ below Claude Opus 5 ($5/$25) and GPT-5.6 Sol ($5/$30). τ³-Banking (50.7%) tests multi-turn customer service with tool use and places Grok 4.6 in the top two, while Terminal-Bench v2.1 (88.4%) puts it level with the leaders on terminal-based software tasks.
