## Excerpts

> Both concerts are scheduled for June 27, 2026, with Ballads of the Underworld at 1:30pm BST and Persona Grooves at 8pm BST. Tickets start at £57 (~$75) for both shows.

> Persona Grooves will feature songs from the Persona 3, 4, and 5 soundtracks. The Bartosz Pernal Orchestra and London Mozart Players will perform, and Shoji Meguro will be a special guest.

> Ballads of the Underworld will focus on songs from both Hades games, with the London Mozart Players as the orchestra, led by Grammy-nominated and BAFTA-winning conductor Austin Wintory.
