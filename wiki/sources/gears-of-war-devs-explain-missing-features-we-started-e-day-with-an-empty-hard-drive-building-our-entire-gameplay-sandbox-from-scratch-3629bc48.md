## Excerpts

> We started E-Day with an empty hard drive building our entire gameplay sandbox from scratch to ensure a more unified gameplay experience across Campaign, PVE and PVP.

> Missing features at launch include Meat Shields, Chainsaw Duels, select weapons from the franchise's past, and online features like Private Lobbies and leaderboards.

> The Coalition's team stated they are working hard to deliver the strongest and most stable launch experience with an eye to how and what to deliver and improve on fan-favorite features either post-launch or in their next title.
