## Excerpts

> The nominees in the video game category this cycle are Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora - From the Ashes, 007: First Light, Ghost of Yotei, Marvel's Wolverine, and Star Wars: Zero Company.

> Unbeatable's writer and director RJ Lake said the game's predominantly vocal soundtrack was not a good fit for the category as currently envisioned, and that D-Cell received a response from the Grammys after submitting a consideration form indicating that vocal songs are not what the voting judges are looking for in this category.

> Lake noted the guidance against vocal songs is not written anywhere as a strict rule in the award's description, but was conveyed as what voters were said to expect; the category recognizes score soundtrack albums comprised predominantly of original scores.

> One of the nominees, 007: First Light, includes a prominent Lana Del Rey song, though it is unclear whether that influenced its nomination.
