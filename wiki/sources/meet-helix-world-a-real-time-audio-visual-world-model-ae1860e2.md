## Excerpts

> Helix World is a real-time interactive audio-visual world model. Starting from a single image and steered by keyboard and mouse, it generates the next stretch of video and the matching stereo sound at the same time, at 24 frames per second on a single NVIDIA H800 GPU. When the camera turns, the sound field turns with it.

> On the WBench navigation split, Helix World scores 79.9 with a real-time factor of 0.77. On HelixBench, its spatial-acoustic consistency score is 41.8, versus 12.7 for EchoWM, the only other compared model that generates sound.

> Output is 768x512, speed was measured on H800 datacenter GPUs, and the public weights are a preview. Visual quality is slightly below the best silent models (79.9 vs 82.0 on WBench).
