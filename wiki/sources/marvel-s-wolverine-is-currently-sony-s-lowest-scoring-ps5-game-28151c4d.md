## Excerpts

> With a Metacritic score of just 77, Marvel's Wolverine is scoring worse than Sackboy: A Big Adventure, making it the lowest-rated game from within PlayStation Studios' collective of developers in all six years of the console.

> Most reviews from well-known sites are observing how dated it feels as a linear third-person action game, with outlets like Eurogamer, VGC, IGN, GameSpot, and GamesRadar mostly offering it a 6, while Giant Bomb offered it the lowest score so far with a 5.

> Marvel's Wolverine from Insomniac Games has been met with a resounding fine, with no one tearing the X-Men-themed action game to pieces, but instead the most recognized outlets offering it a collective shrug.
