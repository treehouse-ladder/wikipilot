## Excerpts

> Sensor Tower's tracker reports the game has sold more than 6.45 million copies, generating more than $630 million. PS5 holds the majority of preorders at 74%, versus 26% on Xbox Series X|S.

> Sensor Tower's Karl Kontus said reorders could be in the area of 25 million when the game releases, which would amount to "unprecedented preorder performance." At that level, revenue would be about $2 billion, enough to cover the game's development budget and most of the marketing spend.

> Neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has shared official preorder or sales figures since preorders opened in June; these are third-party estimates.

> GTA 6 is set for release on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
