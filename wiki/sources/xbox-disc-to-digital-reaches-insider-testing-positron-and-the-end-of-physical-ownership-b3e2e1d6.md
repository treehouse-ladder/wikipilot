## Excerpts

> Xbox's disc-to-digital conversion program, codenamed Positron, is reaching Xbox Insiders in mid-July 2026. Owners with physical disc libraries will be able to convert qualifying Xbox One and Xbox Series X titles into permanent digital licenses, including Xbox Cloud Gaming and Play Anywhere access, without visiting a retailer or paying a fee.

> Players insert a supported Xbox One or Xbox Series X disc into the drive, permanently converting the physical game into a digital license for their Microsoft account. Xbox 360 and original Xbox titles are not eligible.

> The digital entitlement is tied to the disc: selling or gifting the physical disc to another Microsoft Account holder transfers the entitlement to that account and removes it from yours, preserving the second-hand game market. The disc retains its role as proof of ownership and cannot be used to generate duplicate free copies.
