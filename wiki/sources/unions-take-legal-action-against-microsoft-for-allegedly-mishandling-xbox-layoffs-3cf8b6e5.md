## Excerpts

> Labor unions in the United States and Canada have filed unfair labor practice (ULP) charges against Xbox maker Microsoft for allegedly mishandling its latest round of mass layoffs. The CWA filed the ULP against Microsoft and numerous Xbox subsidiaries on July 15, 2026.

> A ULP charge has been filed against Microsoft and subsidiaries including Xbox, ZeniMax Media, id Software, Bethesda Game Studios and more for allegedly failing to provide information to labor union Communications Workers of America (CWA) and engaging in bad faith bargaining, coercive actions, and unilateral changes to employee contracts.

> A CWA spokesperson told Game Developer that Microsoft did not engage in decisional bargaining with the union before announcing its latest round of mass layoffs on July 6 and informing union members they had been laid off.
