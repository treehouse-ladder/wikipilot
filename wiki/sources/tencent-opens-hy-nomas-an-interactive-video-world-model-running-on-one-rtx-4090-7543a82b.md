## Excerpts

> Tencent released Hy-Nomas under Apache 2.0 in the week of October 21, 2026, with weights on Hugging Face (tencent/Hy-Nomas). The model is an 8B diffusion transformer trained for interactive video world generation.

> A distilled, INT8-quantized version runs on a single RTX 4090 with CPU offload, generating a 100-second clip at 1280×704, 24 FPS, with peak GPU memory of 20.53 GiB. Wider reports also cite 720p output.

> During generation, users can issue new free-form text instructions or camera trajectory controls at each segment boundary — multi-turn steering without restarting from scratch.

> The release includes Hy-Nomas-Bench, a benchmark of 280 test cases designed to evaluate interaction controllability in video world models. The accompanying arXiv paper appeared October 27, 2026.
