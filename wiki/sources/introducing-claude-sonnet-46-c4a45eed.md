## Excerpts

> Claude Sonnet 4.6 was released on February 17, 2026. It features a 1M token context window in beta. Pricing remains the same as Sonnet 4.5, starting at $3/$15 per million tokens. Sonnet 4.6 scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, an 8-point jump from Sonnet 4.5. Sonnet 4.6 leads all models tested on GDPval-AA and TerminalBench, outperforming even Claude Opus 4.6. Sonnet 4.6 achieved 86.5% on ARC-AGI-1 and 60.4% on ARC-AGI-2 with 120k thinking tokens and High effort.
