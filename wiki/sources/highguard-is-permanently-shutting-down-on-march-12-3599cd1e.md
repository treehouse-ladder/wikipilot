## Excerpts

> Highguard, which launched in late January 2026, had a strong initial player count but couldn't sustain momentum, leading to layoffs in mid-February and the announcement on March 3 that the game would close after less than two months.

> When the servers closed on March 12, the game had lived for just 45 days. Studio head Chad Grenier attributed the closure to lack of revenue, stating 'Not enough revenue to keep anyone employed to work on it, unfortunately'.
