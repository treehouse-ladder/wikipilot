## Excerpts

> The Verge source points to a July 6 date for the potential redundancies, and notes the number of affected roles could be north of one thousand. Arkane itself may be facing closure if Microsoft doesn't spin the studio off to a buyer, alongside other major studios it is looking to sell, like Double Fine, Compulsion Games, Ninja Theory, and Undead Labs. If buyers are found for these studios, then they'll avoid being closed, but sources stress these talks could take months in some cases.
