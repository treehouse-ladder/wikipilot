## Excerpts

> Ubisoft has unveiled an R&D experiment called 'Teammates' that leverages generative AI technology to turn NPCs into what it calls 'living companions.'

> Teammates puts NPCs in a first-person shooter setting with advanced AI features that allow them to respond dynamically to real-time voice commands and adapt their behavior to each situation, revealing distinct personalities along the way.

> Teammates was built by a team of 80 people and leverages Google Gemini in combination with in-house middleware. Teammates runs on the company's proprietary Snowdrop game engine, which powers many of its triple-A projects.

> The team uses behavior trees and adds a layer with their LLM to take decisions based on context, controlling design-wise what characters can say and do while letting the LLM decide based on context how it should be done.

> Teammates has already been shared with a few hundred players in a closed playtest, with their valuable feedback shaping the future of the team's research.
