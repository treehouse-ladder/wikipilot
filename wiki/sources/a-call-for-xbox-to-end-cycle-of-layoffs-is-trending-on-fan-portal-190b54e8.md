## Excerpts

> A call to 'end studio closures and stop the cycle of layoffs' has become the top-trending post on the Xbox Player Voice portal with over 3,000 upvotes, stating the layoff of 3,200 workers at XBOX is unacceptable and demanding no layoffs for 2 years, good-faith union negotiation, and investing in future developers instead of AI technologies.

> The post's demands mirror the CWA/OneBGS union asks: no layoffs for two years, good-faith union negotiation, no executive bonuses during layoffs, and investing in future developers "instead of AI technologies."

> The viral Player Voice post extends the Xbox Reset's labor backlash from workers and unions to the broader player base, demonstrating that consumer discontent over Xbox's layoff practices is now organized enough to surface on Microsoft's own official feedback platform.
