> If an AI system creates or alters content, the company must disclose this, unless it's for legal purposes or the content is artistic or satirical.

> The European Commission stated that, in principle, AI-enabled video games face no obligation under the AI Act, but companies could voluntarily adopt additional codes of conduct.
