## Excerpts

> Project Glasswing gave a small set of large US technology firms early access to Claude Mythos Preview, which we use to study the effects of frontier AI on cyber defense across defenders, external researchers, and attackers.

> Treated firms found more flaws themselves. External bug-bounty researchers then reported about half as many vulnerabilities in those firms' code, and more in other firms' code, without leaving the market.

> On attackers, we find no evidence that attackers exploited the additional vulnerabilities. The share of vulnerabilities exploited within a month of disclosure fell, mostly because fewer flaws were already exploited when disclosed.

> The restricted release produces an asymmetry in software security between firms in different countries, since disclosures from firms with access more than doubled relative to Chinese and Russian firms without access.
