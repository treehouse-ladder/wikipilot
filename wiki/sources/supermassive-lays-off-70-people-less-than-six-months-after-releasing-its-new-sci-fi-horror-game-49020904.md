## Excerpts

> Supermassive Games laid off 70 people after releasing Directive 8020 earlier in 2026. The announcement came on October 5, 2026.

> Directive 8020 had some of the studio's best horror game moments, but was dragged down by bad writing and missing online co-op. Supermassive announced just the week before that online multiplayer would finally be patched in sometime in October, nearly five months after the game had launched.

> The studio had warned about the coming layoffs back in August, stating 'This is a necessary step to help ensure the sustainability of the company'.
