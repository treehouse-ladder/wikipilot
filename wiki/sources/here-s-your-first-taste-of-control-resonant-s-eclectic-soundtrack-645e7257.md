## Excerpts

> The team behind Control: Resonant have shared six songs from the game's funky, genre-hopping soundtrack.

> Developers Remedy Entertainment have shared a six-track EP of music made exclusively for the game, dedicated to Dylan's Federal Bureau Of Control handler Zoe De Vera.

> The six songs include 'Zoe's Mixtape', which contains Vilma Jaa's viral hit 'Manalan Mailla'. Control: Resonant is out today (September 25) for the Xbox Series X/S, PlayStation 5 and PC.
