## Excerpts

> GameStop staff took to Reddit to voice how badly the physical pre-order launch had gone. One store that expected at least 500 pre-orders on June 25 received just 5; stores with a target of 200 pre-orders recorded as few as 11 or 30 copies.

> The poor physical pre-order numbers were attributed to the game's physical copy only including a code to download the game digitally rather than a disc, and no Ultimate Edition being available in physical form. Stores like GameStop that rely on the used-games market and in-store pre-orders may face more store closures and layoffs.
