## Excerpts

> Halo: Campaign Evolved peaked at around 25,000 concurrent players on Steam on launch day, the lowest peak concurrent player count the series has seen on Steam.

> For comparison, Halo Wars 2 had 30,000 players within the first week of release in 2017, meaning Campaign Evolved underperformed even compared to that spinoff title. A large share of players are on Game Pass, which is not reflected in Steam concurrency metrics.
