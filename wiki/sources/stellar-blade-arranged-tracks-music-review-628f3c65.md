## Excerpts

> The album features 14 tracks with a runtime of approximately 59 minutes and 46 seconds. It includes jazz trio arrangements of songs like 'Don't Forget Me,' 'Flooded Commercial Sector,' 'Clock Tower,' 'White Night,' and 'Wasteland – Reboot,' plus orchestral arrangements and a piano solo, concluding with a remix version of 'Don't Forget Me' by Youngjee.

> The album acts as somewhat of a divider, splitting the jazzy first half from the more orchestral second half. The orchestral arrangements begin with 'The Song of the Sirens,' which employs one of the most memorable motifs from the main Stellar Blade OST.

> While 'Star Descent' carries on a guitar-focused sound, it features multitude of layers, rhythms, and melodies that change too much, giving it a chaotic feel that lacked clarity. The final version of 'Don't Forget Me' features vocals and is likely the reviewer's favorite song on the album.
