## Excerpts

> Fortis Games has shut down after Las Vegas Sands ended its funding, with as many as 440 employees affected.

> The studio confirmed that its game Twilight Towers would shut down on November 16th after failing to retain enough players; seven days later, the entire studio had closed.

> Fortis was founded in 2022 by Steven Chiang, Shawn Foust and Calvin Lau, former EA, Zynga and WB Games veterans, and grew through acquisitions of Doppio Games, Oktagon Games and Metagame.

> LinkedIn posts from former staff suggest Las Vegas Sands closed the developer and cancelled all contracts with immediate effect, leaving over 400 people out of work.
