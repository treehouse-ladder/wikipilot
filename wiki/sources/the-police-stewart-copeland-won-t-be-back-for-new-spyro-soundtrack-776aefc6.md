## Excerpts

> Stewart Copeland has confirmed he won't be back to create the soundtrack for Spyro: A Realm Beyond. Copeland composed the music for the first four mainline Spyro The Dragon games – Spyro The Dragon, Ripto's Rage, Year Of The Dragon and Enter The Dragonfly.

> He returned to the franchise in 2018 to remaster the music for updated classics collection Spyro Reignited Trilogy, as well as composing a new song for the game.

> When explaining his decision, Copeland stated: 'It's a new generation. They want a new generation of sound and everything… they've got to modernise it.'
