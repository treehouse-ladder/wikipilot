## Excerpts

> Tripo P1.0 represents a fundamental architectural departure, introducing a new approach to spatial generation built on a unified probabilistic space. Instead of predicting geometry sequentially, it resolves the global structure of a 3D object holistically, enabling clean topology, stable geometry, and reliable engine compatibility. The result is production-ready assets generated in as little as two seconds. Smart Mesh (P1.0) is now available in Tripo Studio, allowing creators to generate clean, optimized topology meshes directly from AI. Smart Mesh generates clean, structured low-poly topology directly during generation, making assets far easier to use in games, XR, and interactive applications.
