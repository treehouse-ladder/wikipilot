## Excerpts

> Nagoshi Studio, the development studio formed by Yakuza creator Toshihiro Nagoshi after leaving Sega, has closed as NetEase cuts its funding.

> Shareholders officially dissolved the developer on October 31 via a resolution later published in the Japanese government gazette Kanpo, recorded in the November 2 edition.

> Bloomberg reported in March that Nagoshi Studio would need approximately $44.4 million to complete Gang of Dragon's development. NetEase said it would stop financing Nagoshi Studio in May.
