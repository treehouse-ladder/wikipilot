## Excerpts

> The game's iconic Gregorian choral menu music is easily one of the medium's most memorable scores; music so well-known and memetic that it transcended the Halo/Xbox fandom.

> The blood-pumping rhythmic score originally from Martin O'Donnell and Michael Salvatori is faithfully remastered and remains as it was.

> The audio design and music are so integral to what makes Halo what it is; all of the classic sounds, like the beeping shield warning or the chirpy screaming of Grunts, are retained in full glory.
