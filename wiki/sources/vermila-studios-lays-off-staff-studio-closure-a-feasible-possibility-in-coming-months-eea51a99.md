## Excerpts

> Vermila Studios, the developer behind Blumhouse-published survival horror game Crisol: Theater of Idols, has laid off its entire staff, with CEO David Carrasco confirming 19 employees have been let go and warning that a full studio closure is 'a feasible possibility in the upcoming months.'

> Unfortunately, due to financial reasons, we've had to dispense with the staff at Vermila Studios. The circumstances are quite difficult and it is a feasible possibility in the upcoming months for a full shutdown.

> Crisol: Theater of Idols launched in February 2026 for PS5, PC and Xbox Series X/S to generally positive reviews, but the studio could not sustain operations five months after release.
