## Excerpts

> On October 6, 2026, Skydance Corporation completed its $111 billion acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery, making it the new owner of Warner Bros. Games — encompassing Rocksteady (Batman: Arkham), NetherRealm Studios (Mortal Kombat), Avalanche Software (Hogwarts Legacy), and TT Games (LEGO titles). The company's announcement of the deal closing contained no mention of its new video game holdings.
