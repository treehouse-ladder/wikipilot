## Excerpts

> Ramen released Aura 15.0, the latest update for its best-in-class multi-agent AI assistant supporting both Unreal and Unity game development. Aura 15.0 enhances the product with Sandbox Mode, Aura Skills to execute custom workflows and a first-of-its-kind unlimited usage. Sandbox Mode allows the agent to make changes in an isolated environment without breaking the project. Version 15.0 delivers up to two times speed and accuracy improvements on the Dragon Agent (Python) and Telos (Blueprint Agent). Over 30,000 game developers have benefited from the suite of AI assistance spanning Aura, Coplay and Flopperam. Aura was launched by Ramen in January 2026 and serves as its flagship AI Agent.
