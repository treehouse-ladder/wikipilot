## Excerpts

> KOEI TECMO Europe and Black Screen Records announced the official Atelier Ryza (Original Soundtrack Trilogy) vinyl box set. The vinyl box set features 45 songs across three LPs, including the most-loved musical highlights of all three games.

> Liner notes are provided by Producer Junzo Hosoi & Sound Director Mari Yoshida (GUST). The vinyl box set is priced at 69 EUR. The estimated shipping date is June 2026.
