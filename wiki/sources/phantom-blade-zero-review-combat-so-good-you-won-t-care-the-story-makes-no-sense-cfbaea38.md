# Phantom Blade Zero Review: Combat So Good You Won't Care The Story Makes No Sense

Source: GameSpot, 2026-10-30

## Excerpts

> The game is not without issues, and they do hold it back from reaching the genre's upper echelons, but the highs are stratospheric. It's too easy to simply button-mash your way to victory against most enemy types.

> GameSpot awarded Phantom Blade Zero 8/10, praising the fast, fluid combat and spectacular boss fights while noting a weak story and difficulty balance issues that keep it from the top tier of the action genre.

> IGN awarded 9/10 and GameSpot 8/10; the outlier is Eurogamer (60/100). Reviewers consistently praised the fast, fluid combat and boss fights; main criticisms are a weak story, difficulty balance, and frame rate inconsistencies on base hardware.
