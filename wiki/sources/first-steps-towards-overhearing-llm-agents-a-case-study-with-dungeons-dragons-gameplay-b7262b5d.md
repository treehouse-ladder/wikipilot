## Excerpts

> Overhearing agents are LLM agents that don't actively participate in conversation but instead listen in on human-to-human conversations to perform background tasks or provide suggestions.

> We explore this paradigm through Dungeons & Dragons gameplay, using large multimodal audio-language models to assist a Dungeon Master, presenting the first real-time multimodal agentic AI system and comparing end-to-end, audio-enabled LLMs to traditional transcription-based pipelines.

> We find that some large audio-language models have the emergent ability to perform overhearing agent tasks using implicit audio cues. Input segmentation and multimodal fusion are central technical challenges for real-time operation.

> We examine five audio-enabled multimodal LLMs: GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini, Ultravox v0.5 (Llama 3.3 70B), Qwen2.5-Omni (7B), and Phi-4-multimodal-instruct (5.6B).
