## Excerpts

> Rodin Gen-2.5 was discussed in an interview where QX Zhang, CTO of Hyper3D, discussed its philosophy including controllable AI workflows, Smart Low-Poly topology generation, 3D-native texturing, batch generation, and integration into real-world game and VFX pipelines.

> Rodin is about building a controllable AI-to-3D workflow that can connect with game development, VFX, real-time rendering, 3D printing, and enterprise production pipelines. Rodin Gen-2.5 is designed to bring more control into the full AI-to-3D production pipeline, from generation speed and geometry complexity to texture quality, local detail, part-level editing, topology, and downstream optimization.

> Smart Low-Poly topology generation produces production-ready meshes with proper topology for animation and support for high-poly to low-poly baking with normal maps for real-time performance.
