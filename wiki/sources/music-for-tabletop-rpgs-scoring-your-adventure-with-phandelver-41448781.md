## Excerpts

> The Lost Mines of Phandelver soundtrack by composer Josh Barron features town music, pretty nature environmental themes, scary dungeon themes, standard battle music, and special boss battle music, with musical stings/jingles for quest completion and leveling up.

> The added aural component was immediately noticeable as an improvement to the typical starting experience, with the character creation theme 'Who Might Ye Be?' bringing a sense of wonder to the group.
