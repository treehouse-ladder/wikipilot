## Excerpts

> Build A Rocket Boy experienced mass layoffs of approximately 170 employees just days after the launch of MindsEye's first DLC, bringing the total staff down to around 80. This was the third round of layoffs, following mass layoffs in June 2025 and March 2026. The situation has been turbulent for the studio, with employees taking the company to court over allegations of monitoring software installed on their devices and action being taken by The Independent Workers of Great Britain over claims of crunch and mishandling by company leadership.
