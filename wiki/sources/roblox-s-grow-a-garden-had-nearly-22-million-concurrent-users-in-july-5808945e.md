## Excerpts

> Roblox's record-breaking game Grow a Garden attracted 22 million concurrent users in July, surpassing Fortnite's existing record of 15.3 million concurrent players.

> On July 19, Grow a Garden peaked at 21,963,800 concurrent users, officially surpassing its previous record of 21.3 million set on June 21 during the Summer Update.

> The free-to-play farming simulator was released on March 25 and was originally created by a 16-year-old anonymous user. It became the top experience based on spending within one month of release.

> Grow a Garden pushed Roblox to its highest platform-wide concurrent user numbers yet.
