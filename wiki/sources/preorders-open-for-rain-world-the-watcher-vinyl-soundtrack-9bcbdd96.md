## Excerpts

> Preorders for the vinyl soundtrack are open at publisher Black Screen Records, planned for release on two discs in July 2026.

> The double LP is on stunning Pearl Dawn vinyl, featuring a total of 54 tracks, with exclusive gatefold artwork by Kelocitta with UV spot varnish. It is priced at 39,00 euro.

> The music was crafted by James Primate and Lydia Esrig, and also features works by Serpanoy, Pinpoint, snoodle, Intikus and Kaeporo. The soundtrack is being released by Akupara Games, Videocult and Black Screen Records.
