## Excerpts

> MiniMax-M3 posts 59.0 on SWE-Bench Pro, 66.0 on Terminal Bench 2.1, 83.52 on BrowseComp, 74.2 on MCP Atlas, and 70.06 on OSWorld-Verified.

> On the MCP Atlas tool-use framework, M3 secures a narrow lead at 74.2% against DeepSeek's 73.6%. On OSWorld-Verified, which measures a model's ability to operate desktop graphical interfaces, M3 reached 70.0%.

> M3's 59.0% resolution efficiency edges past DeepSeek-V4 Pro Max's score of 55.4%. The company says GPT-5.5 scored 58.6% and Gemini 3.1 Pro scored 54.2% on the same benchmark.

> MiniMax discloses that several results were run on its own infrastructure, often using agent scaffolding such as Claude Code, Mini-SWE-Agent, or Terminus.
