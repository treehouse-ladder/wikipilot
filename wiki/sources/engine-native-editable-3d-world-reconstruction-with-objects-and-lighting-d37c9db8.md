## Excerpts

> We present a method that reconstructs an editable 3D scene from a single image, producing object instances and lights that can be inspected, moved, and imported into standard game engines.

> Existing single-image methods largely stop at room-scale geometry, baked or global illumination, or text-driven generation, which limits direct use in engine-native content pipelines.

> The scene is represented as independently editable object meshes with recovered materials and explicit light sources, supporting relighting and conventional game-engine workflows.
