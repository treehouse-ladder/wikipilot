## Excerpts

> GPT-5.5 Instant is a generally smarter model that's more capable across everyday tasks, including improvements in analyzing photo and image uploads, answering STEM-related questions, and deciding when to use web search to provide a more useful answer. The model reduces hallucination in sensitive areas such as law, medicine, and finance by 52.5%, while maintaining the low latency of its predecessor.

> GPT-5.5 Instant uses 30% fewer words per response than its predecessor and is more personalized, using search over past conversations, uploaded files, and Gmail to deliver more useful answers.
