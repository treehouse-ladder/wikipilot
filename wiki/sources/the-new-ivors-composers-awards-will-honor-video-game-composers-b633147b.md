## Excerpts

> The Ivors Composers Awards will take place in London on November 17, 2026, and will welcome composers across the movie, television, video game, and music industries. Entries will open on June 1, 2026.

> Recognizing video game music has been a tradition at The Ivors Academy since Killzone 2 was awarded the first such honor in this category at the Ivor Novello Awards in 2010, with other games that have received accolades including Horizon Zero Dawn, Sea of Thieves, Ori and the Will of the Wisps, Star Wars: Jedi Survivor, and Farewell North.
