## Excerpts

> GCA turns a natural-language request into a runnable Phaser 4 browser game. It first builds a multimodal game design document that couples mechanics and scene contracts to grounded visual references. Eight specialized agents carry out production: scenes are implemented serially, assets are produced in parallel, and one reduced manifest joins the two branches into a single build. Build-linked real-UI checks and a code-blind critic then verify the build and decide whether the game may ship.
