## Excerpts

> Nagoshi Studio, the studio formed by Yakuza creator Toshihiro Nagoshi at NetEase, has been shut down before it even released its first game.

> NetEase has not officially cancelled Gang of Dragon, but it is unclear if there is any appetite at NetEase to take the project over the finish line.

> Co-founder and director Daisuke Sato changed his bio to ex-Nagoshi Studio, and the studio's social media accounts have not been updated since February.
