## Excerpts

> Xbox Backward Compatibility on PC makes it easier to play classic Xbox games from the past across PC and handhelds, including the ROG Xbox Ally and Xbox Ally X.

> The first four titles are BLiNX: The Time Sweeper, Conker: Live and Reloaded, Crimson Skies: High Road to Revenge, and Fuzion Frenzy. Each is available for purchase on PC and included with all Xbox Game Pass plans.

> If you already own a digital license for these games on console, that license can carry over to your PC or handheld, meaning you only need to purchase the game once.

> According to Xbox VP of next generation Jason Ronald, this marks the beginning of a broader effort to preserve Xbox games from the past and bring them to PC over time.
