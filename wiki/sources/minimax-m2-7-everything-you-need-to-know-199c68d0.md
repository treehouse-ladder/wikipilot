## Excerpts

> MiniMax-M2.7 scores 50 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among other open weights models.

> MiniMax-M2.7 is a Mixture of Experts (MoE) model with 230 billion total parameters, but only 10 billion active parameters are used during inference. The model supports text input, outputs text, and has a 205k token context window.

> MiniMax-M2.7 costs $0.30 per 1M input tokens and $1.20 per 1M output tokens, maintaining the same pricing as its predecessor.

> MiniMax-M2.7 scores +1 on the AA-Omniscience Index, up from MiniMax-M2.5 (-40), with a reduction in hallucination rate to 34%, one of the lowest hallucination rates recorded.

> MiniMax-M2.7 is open weights. The model weights are publicly available and can be downloaded for self-hosting.
