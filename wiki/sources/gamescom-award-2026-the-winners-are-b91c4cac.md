## Excerpts

> The gamescom award selects the event's highlights in 19 categories overall, divided into the groups Arts, Platform and Global.

> CD Projekt Red and Sega took home three awards each, while Capcom and Ubisoft secured two awards each.

> The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt Remastered and DLC Songs of the Past won Best Trailer/Announcement.

> Capcom's Onimusha: Way of the Sword won Best Gameplay and Best Sony PlayStation Game.

> Sega won Best Audio for Alien: Isolation 2, Best PC Game for Total War: Warhammer 40,000, and Best Lineup.

> Ubisoft won Best Nintendo Switch 2 Game with Rayman Legends Retold, and its business lounge was named Best Business Booth.

> The special jury award HEART OF GAMING went to the charity speedrunning event Games Done Quick (GDQ).
