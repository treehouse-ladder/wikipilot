> The higher leverage potentially weakens EA's credit profile, leading to a loss of its investment grade rating. In that case, bondholders can push for 101 percent repayment rather than standard repayment. One group of bondholders is doing so for two $750 million tranches, totaling $1.5 billion. In response, EA is transforming its bonds into assets that resemble U.S. Treasury securities. The deal includes $20 billion of debt financing committed by JPMorgan Chase Bank, $18 billion of which is expected to be funded at close.
