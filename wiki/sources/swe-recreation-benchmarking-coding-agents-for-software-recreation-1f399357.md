## Excerpts

> SWE-Recreation covers 54 real applications (28 websites and 26 Android apps) with 1,446 hidden requirements. Agents get only rendered pixels and act through pointer and keyboard input, with no access to the DOM, source, logs, or back-end; the agent explores the product, builds a replica, and checks its own work. Across the 54 applications, five frontier systems reach at most 0.530 weighted fidelity and 22.9% fully reproduced requirements under application-equal averaging.
