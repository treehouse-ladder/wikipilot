## Excerpts

> Halo: Campaign Evolved launched in early access, but people who paid for it couldn't access it due to a technical hitch. Early Access started on July 23, with the full game opening up to all players on July 28.

> Reports came in of people unable to play Halo: Campaign Evolved's early access via its Premium Edition or Premium Edition Upgrade on Xbox. The issues primarily affected Game Pass subscribers who bought the standalone $20 Premium Upgrade for early access.

> Fans on Xbox and Steam faced various issues including apparent server outages. Player reviews included complaints like "Login failed," "Cannot play the single player game as servers are overloaded. Screw your DRM."

> After Microsoft fixed Xbox Live's recent outage, Halo Studios announced on X that it had reportedly solved the early access issues, but many people responded that they still couldn't access it.
