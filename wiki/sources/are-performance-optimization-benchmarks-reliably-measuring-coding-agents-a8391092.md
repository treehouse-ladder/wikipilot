## Excerpts

> Leaderboard scores can conflate runtime instability, benchmark-specific scoring rules, and how many tasks are already solved by at least one public submission.

> When official reference patches were replayed across four common types of Google Cloud machines, they satisfied the original benchmark validity rules in every cross-machine replay for only 39/102 GSO tasks, 11/140 SWE-Perf tasks, and 411/498 SWE-fficiency tasks; SWE-Perf was especially fragile because many reference patches produce close-to-zero runtime changes.

> Among eight public submissions shared by GSO and SWE-fficiency, the official rankings disagreed on 9 of 28 pairwise submission comparisons, and SWE-fficiency's leaderboard scoring rule assigned the worst ten tasks overly high score weights of 58.5%-82.8%.
