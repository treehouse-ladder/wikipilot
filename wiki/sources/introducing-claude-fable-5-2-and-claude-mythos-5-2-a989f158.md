## Excerpts

> Claude Fable 5.2 is priced at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, down from $10 and $50 for Fable 5.1.

> Fast mode runs up to 2.5x faster. It costs $16 per million input tokens and $80 per million output tokens.

> Cache reads are $0.20 per million tokens. For workloads that need to run in the US, US-only inference is available at 1.1x pricing for input and output tokens.

> Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 are the same underlying model. Mythos 5.2 is available only through our trusted access programs.

> On FrontierCode 2.0 at Xhigh effort, Fable 5.2 reaches 49.1%, the highest score of any released model we've tested. At High effort it roughly matches Opus 5.5's best score at about half the cost per task.

> On BenchCAD 2.1 at Max effort, Fable 5.2 scores 83.5, ahead of Opus 5.5 (65.3) and Fable 5.1 (44.4).

> On RoboDojo Sim at Max effort, Fable 5.2 scores 64.9%, more than double GPT-6 Astra (29.8%) and Fable 5.1 (27.6%).

> If you're unsure which model to use, start with Claude Opus 5.5 for most workloads.
