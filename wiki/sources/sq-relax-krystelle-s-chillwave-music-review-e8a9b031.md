## Excerpts

> SQ Relax - Krystelle's Chillwave is a 2026 Square Enix arranged album of chillwave reinterpretations of game music, fronted by Krystelle, the mascot of the Square Enix Music Channel's Chill BGM livestreams.

> The album collects 20 arrangements by nine arrangers, including a reharmonized 'Liberi Fatali' from Final Fantasy VIII and 'The Day Will Come' from Final Fantasy V.
