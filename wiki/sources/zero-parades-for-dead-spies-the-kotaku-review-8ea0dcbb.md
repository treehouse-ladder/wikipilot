## Excerpts

> Zero Parades: For Dead Spies is an excellent Disco Elysium successor that still establishes its own identity.

> You play as Hershel Wilk, aka Cascade, a disgraced spy who is put back into action and who yearns for redemption.

> The new emotions system and action sequences further evolves Disco Elysium's already incredible cRPG gameplay. Where Harry had morale and health in Disco Elysium, Hershel has fatigue, anxiety, and delusion.
