## Excerpts

> A strike was scheduled to begin on September 25, 2026, after negotiations over a collective agreement between the Microsoft subsidiary King and its unionized workforce reached an impasse. However, at the eleventh hour, a deal has been reached.

> The collective bargaining agreement signed by King will present employees with more opportunities to 'influence their work situation,' and workers will also gain the right to organize and receive information in 'good time' when changes impacting the workplace are being made, for instance in the event of reorganisations.

> The agreement will take effect on April 1, 2027, with an agreement for adaptions for King's operations and circumstances.
