## Excerpts

> Stellar Blade: Blood Rain is the next game from Stellar Blade developer Shift Up and looks to continue the high octane action delivered by the 2024 game. The game was revealed in a stylized and action-packed trailer showing a new protagonist exploring a dystopian cyberpunk world fighting grotesque monsters with Stellar Blade's signature stylish flair. While the first game focused on swords as its main form of offense and defense, Blood Rain seems more melee-focused. While the first game was PS5 exclusive, Blood Rain will not be published by Sony.
