## Excerpts

> Beast of Reincarnation, Game Freak's action RPG set to release on August 4 on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series S/X, and Steam, has leaked online and is reportedly being played by pirates.

> Beast of Reincarnation isn't the only high-profile title that's made its way into pirates' hands before release this year, with other AAA titles including Lego Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight, Forza Horizon 6, and Subnautica 2 also being cracked before their official release dates.

> Both Game Freak and Beast of Reincarnation's publisher, Fictions, have yet to officially comment on the alleged leak.
