## Excerpts

> The first track from Olivier Derivière's soundtrack for 'Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy' has been released — 'The Land'. The track features vocals from Vasiliki Anastasiou alongside the Amalgamation Choir.

> Derivière explained that 'The Land' was the first song he wrote for the project and develops and reflects Sophia's spirit: determined, resilient, and ever-moving forward.

> Resonance, although part of A Plague Tale universe, is a very different game and therefore needed a fresh musical approach without leaving the core emotions and musical traits of the franchise.

> The score features the rousing Amalgamation female choir, the intimate Yaylı tambur performed by renowned musician Evgenios Voulgaris, and emotional solo vocals by Vasiliki Anastasiou. Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy launches August 27 for PlayStation 5, PC and Xbox Series X/S.
