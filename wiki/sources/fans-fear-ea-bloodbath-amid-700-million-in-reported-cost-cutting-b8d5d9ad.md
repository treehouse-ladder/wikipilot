## Excerpts

> The publisher has told debt investors that it will cut $700 million in annual costs including $170 million in "organizational efficiencies." In other words: mass layoffs. The deal included approximately $18 billion in debt that EA must now help repay, with annual interest payments reaching roughly $1.8 billion.

> "Organizational efficiencies" is code for layoffs, something many at EA have been bracing for ever since the deal was first announced. However, EA has not confirmed the number of jobs or studios that could be affected.
