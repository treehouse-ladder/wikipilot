## Excerpts

> A spokesperson for OtherSide Entertainment stated: 'After the cancelation of a game under development codenamed Argos, OtherSide Entertainment had to sadly say goodbye to 17 team members, effective end of May.' The studio, led by immersive sim veterans Warren Spector and Paul Neurath — creators of Ultima Underworld, Thief: The Dark Project, System Shock 2, and Deus Ex — had previously revealed Argos: Riders on the Storm in 2022 as Spector's return to the genre. The cancellation follows OtherSide's May 2026 launch of Thick as Thieves.
