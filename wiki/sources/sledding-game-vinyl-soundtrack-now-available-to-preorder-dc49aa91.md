## Excerpts

> Composer Wrigley is releasing their soundtrack to the game Sledding Game on colored vinyl. The release will be a single LP on 180g blue marble colored vinyl, priced at $35 and scheduled to release in late June/early July, 2026.

> The vinyl will be housed in a single-pocket jacket with game art including a 'cheektastic' side b center label. The game's soundtrack was worked on by James Wrigley and Introverted Spacemen.
