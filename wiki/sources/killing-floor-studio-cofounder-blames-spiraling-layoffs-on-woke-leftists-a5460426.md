## Excerpts

> Tripwire Interactive co-founder John Gibson blamed the studio's layoffs on 'woke leftists' who he says ousted him as CEO years ago.

> Some observers have linked the cuts to Killing Floor 3's rough reception on Steam. Gibson's view is his own; the studio has not endorsed it.

> Gibson stepped down as CEO days after his 2021 comments supporting Texas's anti-abortion law; co-founder Alan Wilson took over, and Tripwire was later sold to Embracer Group.
