## Excerpts

> 'My worry is that generative AI is poisoning the well' — Take-Two's former head of AI Luke Dicken shares his concerns that the dawn of generative AI technology has polluted the conversation surrounding broader AI usage in video games. Dicken argues that the hype cycle has caused studios to conflate generative AI with the broader behavioral AI and procedural generation work that underpins legitimate game AI research, and that the backlash against genAI is now damaging that distinct field.

> Dicken's team was founded in 2019 at Zynga as an R&D innovation group, focused primarily on procedural content generation tooling and machine learning — not generative AI. Take-Two disbanded the team in April 2026.
