## Excerpts

> Summer Game Fest 2026 set a new record with 3.8M Peak Viewers. Broadcasts dedicated to the event generated nearly 8M Hours Watched, while peak audience climbed to 3.8M concurrent viewers. After surpassing 3M Peak Viewers in 2025, Summer Game Fest grew by another 25% in 2026, setting a new series record. More than 6,200 channels and streamers covered the 2026 show, compared to around 1,300 during Summer Game Fest 2025. The official The Game Awards YouTube channel drew over 1M Peak Viewers during the 2026 showcase.
