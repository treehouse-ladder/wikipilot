## Excerpts

> A number of developers previously employed at Halo Studios have posted on LinkedIn confirming layoffs at the studio. Halo Studios producer Nick Treitman confirmed he was impacted, stating that after 6.5 years working on Halo and dodging multiple rounds of layoffs, it has finally caught up to me.

> The layoffs come as new reports claim Halo: Campaign Evolved's development was troubled throughout, taking up more resources than originally planned and causing other projects such as a Halo multiplayer game to be canceled.

> The development team was told that assets created for the Blam engine could easily be brought over to Unreal Engine 5, however, 100 percent of the assets had to be redone to become compatible.
