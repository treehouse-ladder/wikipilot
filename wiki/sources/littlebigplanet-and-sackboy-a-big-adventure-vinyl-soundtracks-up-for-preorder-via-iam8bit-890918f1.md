## Excerpts

> iam8bit have opened up preorders for the vinyl soundtracks to platformers LittleBigPlanet and Sackboy: A Big Adventure.

> LittleBigPlanet will be a 2LP available in two vinyl color variants – a limited edition "earth and moon" black and green swirl + grey and black swirl colored vinyl variant (300 copies), and a standard "earth" blue and "moon" green colored vinyl variant. The limited edition will be housed in a special zipper die-cut jacket, while the standard will be housed in a regular jacket. Both will feature new artwork by Haley Wakefield.

> Sackboy will be a 2LP on "dreamer orb" green and grey swirl colored vinyl. It will be housed in a jacket with new artwork by Haley Wakefield.

> The limited edition of LittleBigPlanet is priced at $70 while the standard edition and Sackboy are priced at $43 each. All are scheduled to ship in Q4 2026. As of this post the limited edition has unfortunately sold out, but information about it can be found from iam8bit here.
