## Excerpts

> iam8bit has opened pre-orders for the LittleBigPlanet and Sackboy: A Big Adventure vinyl soundtracks, with releases scheduled for Q4 2026. The LittleBigPlanet 2LP vinyl set features 15 hand-selected songs from the original soundtrack alongside new artwork from Haley 'bbanditt' Wakefield; the limited edition is priced at 0 while the standard edition is 3. Sackboy: A Big Adventure will be a 2LP on 'dreamer orb' green and grey swirl colored vinyl, housed in a jacket with new artwork by Haley Wakefield, with 20 songs from the original game soundtrack. Both releases also include CD versions.
