## Excerpts

> We normally get only one Triple-I Initiative showcase per year, but 2026 is proving to be the exception. The October 8 showcase featured 45 minutes of back-to-back trailers with eight exclusive world premieres from studios including Amanita Design, Norsfell, Strange Scaffold, and Team17. Announced titles include Aftergods (top-down survivalist ARPG), Among Us Story: On Guard (narrative detective standalone launching February 17, 2027), Gods with Badges (turn-based tactics from Strange Scaffold), Machinarium 2 (Amanita Design sequel), The Talos Principle 3 (series finale from Croteam), The Escapists 3 (Team17, 2027), and Sulfur leaving early access October 28.
