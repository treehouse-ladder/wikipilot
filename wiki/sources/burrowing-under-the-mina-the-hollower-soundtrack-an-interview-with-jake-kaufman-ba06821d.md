## Excerpts

> Mina the Hollower's soundtrack was created in Furnace Tracker, configured for Game Boy and MSX-SCC, with more than 100 tracks composed by Jake Kaufman and two guest tracks from Yuzo Koshiro.

> Two areas in the game, Septemburg and Nox's Bayou, were inspired by his parents' musical tastes — his father's fingerstyle folk guitar and his mother's love of Zydeco — resulting in what Kaufman calls 'Cajunvania' tracks; 'Gator Gallop' from Nox's Bayou is his standout track.
