## Excerpts

> The music of Donkey Kong Bananza was composed by Naoto Kubo (as sound director and music lead), Daisuke Matsuoka, Reika Nakai, Yuri Goto and Tsukasa Usui. Kubo said they aimed for a soundtrack with 'a wide range of expression', and used music to immerse the player and establish each layer's atmosphere. In a series first, Bananza uses mainly live-performed orchestra with some rock elements. Original Donkey Kong Country composer David Wise confirmed via social media that he was not involved, with the score based in part on his SNES-era work as well as Eveline Fischer and Robin Beanland's contributions; the DK Rap is also incorporated.
