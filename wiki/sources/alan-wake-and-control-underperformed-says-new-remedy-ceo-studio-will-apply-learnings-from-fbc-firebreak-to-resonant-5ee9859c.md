## Excerpts

> On March 18, 2026, Remedy released the final major update for FBC: Firebreak titled 'Open House' and announced the cessation of new content development to refocus on narrative-driven titles like Control 2 (codenamed Resonant). In Control Resonant, you play as Dylan Faden in a Manhattan taken over by paranatural forces, tasked by the FBC to find the missing Director, his sister Jesse Faden, wielding a shapeshifting weapon called the Aberrant. Control Resonant releases September 24 on PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S. The new CEO says Remedy has learned a lot about self-publishing from FBC: Firebreak — positioning a game and managing a community — which they're applying to Resonant.
