> Inkling Small scores 40 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, within a point of its flagship sibling Inkling with less than a third of the total and active parameters. No open weights model at its size or smaller scores higher on the Intelligence Index. Inkling Small is an open weights reasoning model with 276B total parameters (12B active MoE), text, image, and speech input, and a 256K token context window. It achieves the same score on Terminal-Bench v2.1 (55%) as Inkling, and scores higher on Humanity's Last Exam (32% vs. 30%), GPQA Diamond (89% vs. 87%), CritPt (8% vs. 5%), and SciCode (49% vs. 46%).
