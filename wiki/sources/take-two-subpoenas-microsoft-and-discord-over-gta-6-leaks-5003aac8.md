## Excerpts

> Take-Two Interactive has filed DMCA subpoenas with Microsoft and Discord demanding they identify the person or persons behind the leaks of Grand Theft Auto 6 gameplay footage.

> The subpoenas are issued under Section 512(h) of the DMCA, which lets a copyright holder compel a service provider to identify an alleged infringer without first suing anyone.

> GTA 6 clips started appearing online ahead of a planned reveal, and Take-Two is using the law to try to track down and stop the individual, later expanding the search with additional subpoenas to X and YouTube.
