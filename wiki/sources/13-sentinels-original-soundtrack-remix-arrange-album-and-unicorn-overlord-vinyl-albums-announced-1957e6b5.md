## Excerpts

> Game Music Records is releasing vinyl versions of the 13 Sentinels: Aegis Rim Original Soundtrack, the 13 Sentinels Remix & Arrange Album, and the Unicorn Overlord Original Soundtrack.

> The release is tied to the 40th anniversary of composer Hitoshi Sakimoto's career, and marks the first physical release for these Basiscape soundtracks.
