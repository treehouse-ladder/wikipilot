## Excerpts

> Final Fantasy XIV Piano Collections - Till Dawn My Tears Flow is Keiko's sixth outing with Final Fantasy XIV, compiling music from the first three arrange albums.

> The album has eight tracks: five solo piano pieces and two vocal tracks, with Amanda Achen joining Keiko on 'Smile' and 'Bee My Honey.'

> Keiko goes full Rachmaninoff with this arrangement of 'Roses of May (Dawntrail),' keeping the bold and sometimes dissonant arrangement choices of the in-game version.

> On 'Windswept Echoes,' Keiko utilizes a Melodion to cover the melody.

> I would have liked more Dawntrail material, but this release is a must-hear for fans of Final Fantasy piano music, and it would rank in the upper half of my Final Fantasy piano album rankings.
