## Excerpts

> The Mewgenics soundtrack — composed by Ridiculon (Matthias Bossi and Jon Evans) — is coming to vinyl, available to pre-order ahead of a late 2026 release. The release is available as either a 1LP or 3LP set, featuring a 'comprehensive collection' of main album tracks alongside a 'painstakingly-curated selection' of the most iconic radio tunes. There are seven color variants for the 3LP version and two for the single LP filled release. The 1LP is priced at £59.99 while the 3LP is £119.99, with pre-orders live at Lost In Cult and IGN. The OST features a genre-defying blend of dark cabaret, jazz, rockabilly, proto-punk, and space-age lounge music. All editions ship with brand new artwork from official Mewgenics artist Sony-Shock alongside liner notes from composers Matthias Bossi and Jon Evans.
