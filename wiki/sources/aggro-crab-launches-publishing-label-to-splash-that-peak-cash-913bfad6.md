## Excerpts

> Aggro Crab Presents will support projects that require $500,000 or less to launch within two years.

> Aggro Crab head of publishing Corey Warning said the team has no interest in publishing titles that leverage generative AI slop.

> This new initiative was made possible thanks to the success of Peak, which has topped 20 million players worldwide since launching in June 2025. Aggro Crab Presents has already signed two projects: Rebounder, a precision platformer from developer ThirtyThree, and Bubby Darkstar's psychedelic horror title Subway Midnight.
