## Excerpts

> The full list of nominees for the 2026 Ivors Composer Awards has been announced, with video game soundtracks 007 First Light, Saros and Spirit of the North up for the Best Original Video Game Score award.

> The Ivors Composer Awards will take place at London's Grosvenor House on November 17, 2026.

> The nominees for Best Original Video Game Score are 007 First Light (The Flight), Saros (Sam Slater) and Spirit of the North (Pav Gekko).
