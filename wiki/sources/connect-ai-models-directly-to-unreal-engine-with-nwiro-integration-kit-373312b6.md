## Excerpts

> The Nwiro Integration Kit is a new solution designed to connect third-party AI services directly into Unreal Engine workflows. Teams can connect tools such as Claude Code, Codex CLI, Meshy AI, Tripo AI, and other supported services directly within Unreal Engine.

> Rather than locking developers into a single model or proprietary ecosystem, the Integration Kit embraces a bring-your-own-AI approach, allowing creators to connect services they already use and pay for directly inside the engine.

> For programmers, integrations with coding-focused tools such as Claude Code and Codex CLI can help accelerate scripting, debugging, and implementation tasks, while for artists and content creators, services like Meshy AI and Tripo AI provide direct access to AI-assisted asset generation workflows.

> Rather than introducing another recurring subscription layer, Nwiro offers the Integration Kit as a one-time purchase, allowing users to continue leveraging their existing AI subscriptions and services. The Nwiro Integration Kit is available now on Fab.
