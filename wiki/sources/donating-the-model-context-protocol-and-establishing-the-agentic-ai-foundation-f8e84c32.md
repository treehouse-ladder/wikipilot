## Excerpts

> Anthropic is donating the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF), a directed fund under the Linux Foundation, co-founded by Anthropic, Block and OpenAI, with support from Google, Microsoft, Amazon Web Services (AWS), Cloudflare, and Bloomberg.

> MCP will join goose by Block and AGENTS.md by OpenAI as founding projects.

> The donation to the Linux Foundation demonstrates Anthropic's commitment to ensuring MCP remains a neutral, open standard.
