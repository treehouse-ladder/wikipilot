## Excerpts

> The 5LP boxset will feature 79 tracks from the original 2013 swashbuckling Assassin's Creed: Black Flag. The vinyl release includes Brian Tyler's original game score, multiplayer music from The Flight and Christian Henson as well as fan-favourite sea shanties and Olivier Deriviere's score for the Freedom Cry DLC.

> French musician Woodkid will be contributing new music and reworked sea shanties to the Black Flag Resynced soundtrack. Laced Records is offering exclusive editions with 'Seven Deadly Seas' colour discs. A 2LP vinyl is also available, featuring sea shanties and select tracks from Brian Tyler's Assassin's Creed: Black Flag score. Both vinyl are set to ship in early 2027 but fans can pre-order them now via Laced Records.

> I am so beyond excited that this score has been pressed into the grooves of vinyl – what an absolute treat it has been to revisit this game and music. It's hard to believe that 13 years have gone by since the initial recording, because it remains such an important moment in my career.
