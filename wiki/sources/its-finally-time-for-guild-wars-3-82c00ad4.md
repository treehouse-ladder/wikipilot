## Excerpts

> Guild Wars is getting its third incarnation, as announced at Summer Game Fest today. It's planned for a Steam and PS5 release sometime in the future, and there's a beta beginning in fall 2027. ArenaNet studio head Colin Johanson said that the new Guild Wars would focus on the 'joy of movement' and that its combat would 'usher in an entirely-new type of MMO experience.' The game will be another MMORPG and will continue to take place in the fantasy world of Tyria.
