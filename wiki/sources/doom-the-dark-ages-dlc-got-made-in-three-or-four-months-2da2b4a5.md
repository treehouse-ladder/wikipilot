## Excerpts

> Andrew Willis, former producer at developer Id Software, revealed in an interview that there were two months where he was working at least 12 hours every day, and the majority of the game got made in three or four months.

> Willis recalls having reached at least 80 hours a few times, and noted that there were multiple instances in which more things continued to be added to the game, leading to more development time requirements.
