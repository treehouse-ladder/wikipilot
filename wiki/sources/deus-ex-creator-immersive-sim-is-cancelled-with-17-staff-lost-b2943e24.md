## Excerpts

> After the cancelation of a game under development codenamed Argos, OtherSide Entertainment had to sadly say goodbye to 17 team members, effective end of May. Argos 'could have been a huge success in normal times,' but the current gaming landscape, deemed 'brutally challenging,' rendered the project's continuation 'unviable for now.' OtherSide laid off 17 team members shortly after the launch of Thick as Thieves on May 20. Argos: Riders on the Storm was originally revealed in 2022, said to be Warren Spector's triumphant return to the immersive sim genre he co-created.
