## Excerpts

> As of July 30, 2026, all regulatory approvals required to complete the Merger have been obtained, and Electronic Arts currently expects the Merger to close on or about the close of trading on August 4, 2026.

> EA was being acquired by the Saudi Arabia Public Investment Fund (PIF), Silver Lake and Affinity Partners in the largest leveraged buyout ever.

> EA stockholders will receive $210 per share in cash. The company will continue to be led by CEO Andrew Wilson and its headquarters will remain in Redwood City, California.
