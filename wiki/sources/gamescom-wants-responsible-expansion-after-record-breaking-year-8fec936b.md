## Excerpts

> Gamescom 2026 broke records after attracting over 1,600 exhibitors from 67 countries and selling out 233,000 square meters of gross exhibition space.

> Felix Falk, managing director of Gamescom co-organiser Game, said that growth is only possible because there is a real demand from the global video game industry for a platform that connects companies and communities.

> Any expansion plans must be organic and responsible. Gamescom has existing events in Germany, Latin America and Southeast Asia, with focus on strengthening Gamescom Latam and Gamescom Asia x Thailand Game Show, and on connecting these events more closely with the main event in Cologne.
