## Excerpts

> Canadian musician and filmmaker Pilotpriest (Anthony Scott Burns) is composing the soundtrack to Silent Hill: Townfall, with the first track 'Home' debuting April 7, 2026. Burns is best known for directing and scoring the psychological horror film 'Come True,' and his signature style blends retro electronics with dark, atmospheric soundscapes evoking isolation and mystery. Burns cited Akira Yamaoka's haunting Silent Hill score — warbly ethereal drones, hypnotic melodies, stark industrial percussion, and dirt-laden frequencies — as foundational, saying he was given the rare opportunity to explore and experiment with the goal of creating something emotionally honest, rooted in fear, and entirely true to the spirit of the series.
