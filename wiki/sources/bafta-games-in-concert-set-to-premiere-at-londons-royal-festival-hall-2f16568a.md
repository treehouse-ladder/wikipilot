## Excerpts

> The first BAFTA Games in Concert will take place in London's Royal Festival Hall on Saturday, January 31, 2026. BAFTA Games in Concert will feature the BBC Concert Orchestra in a world premiere performance under the direction of acclaimed composer and conductor Austin Wintory (Journey, Assassin's Creed Syndicate).

> The show will premiere in London in January while the tour will kick off at The Glasshouse in Gateshead in May. The BAFTA Games In Concert tour will visit Bristol, Edinburgh, Glasgow and Birmingham before wrapping things up at Manchester's Aviva Studios on June 7.

> The shows will feature music from Baldur's Gate 3, Everybody's Gone To The Rapture, Assassin's Creed, Tomb Raider: Legend, Cuphead, Hitman: Contracts, Journey, Helldivers 2, Fallout 4, Disco Elysium, Ori And The Will Of The Wisps, Returnal, Uncharted 2: Among Thieves, Far Cry 4, and Virginia.
