## Excerpts

> Anthropic is expanding Project Glasswing, its joint industry initiative to find and fix critical software vulnerabilities using AI, to about 150 new organizations across more than 15 countries. The AI firm dubbed the model its most powerful yet, able to identify thousands of zero-day vulnerabilities over several weeks. Named partners include Okta, Samsung, SK Hynix, SK Telecom, NATO, and the EU cybersecurity agency ENISA.
