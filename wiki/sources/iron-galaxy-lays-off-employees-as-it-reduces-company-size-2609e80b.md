## Excerpts

> Iron Galaxy Studios — co-developer of the PC ports of The Last of Us Part I and Part II Remastered — announced layoffs of an undisclosed number of workers (reportedly up to 90), saying it is 'adopting a new posture to accept these current market conditions as permanent' and that it would be 'impossible for us to sustain the team size that we've carried this past year, even after our downsizing from last year.'

> The cuts come little more than a year after the studio cut 66 developers in 2025 as 'a last resort' for survival.
