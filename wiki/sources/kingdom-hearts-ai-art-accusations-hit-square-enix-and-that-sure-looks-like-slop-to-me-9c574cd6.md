## Excerpts

> A rush of Kingdom Hearts 4 news out of the Nintendo Direct June showcase soured due to credible suspicions that generative AI was used in the lead art for the series' newly announced Nintendo Switch 2 collection.

> Fans quickly noted that Donald has an odd number of fingers – five on his left hand, four on his right. The layering in Sora's hair is also bizarrely inconsistent, and several background details carry the telltale meltiness of AI art.

> A cathedral-like building in the background is described as a casserole of nonsense, with corners that collapse in illogical waves, buttresses that look like they've been microwaved for too long, and several sections of trim that are both patternless and prone to vanishing into nothing.
