## Excerpts

> We never actively tried to make a game we thought was going to lose money, Schafer said, pushing back on the idea that Double Fine only makes niche 'arthouse' games.

> Double Fine's newest titles, Keeper and Kiln, weren't a commercial success and the studio wasn't profitable; Schafer noted that no level of profitability likely would have saved the studio from divestment absent a breakout hit after Phil Spencer's departure.

> Following its separation from Xbox in July 2026, the now-independent Double Fine laid off 23 staff and is running a Kickstarter for its Amnesia Fortnight game jam while exploring ports, remasters, and pitching new games to investors.
