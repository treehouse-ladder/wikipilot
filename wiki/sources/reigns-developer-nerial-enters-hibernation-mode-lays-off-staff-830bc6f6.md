## Excerpts

> Nerial, the celebrated British game studio behind the Reigns franchise, Card Shark, and The Crush House, announced it is entering 'hibernation mode,' ending development and laying off its remaining employees.

> The studio shared the news in a post authored by co-founders Francois and Tamara Alliot, marking an end to its 13-year journey of making innovative and unusual games.

> Having been acquired by Devolver Digital, Nerial became an internal studio where each of their games needed to be a Devolver game.

> Nerial consists of nine people, with Alliot and his wife Tamara continuing to make games together, while seven other individuals are being let go.
