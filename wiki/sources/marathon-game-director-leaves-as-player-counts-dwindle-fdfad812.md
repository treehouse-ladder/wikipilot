## Excerpts

> Joe Ziegler confirmed that July 17 is his last day at Bungie and he's passing the reins over to assistant game director Del Chafe III, a 15-year veteran of the studio, who will continue shepherding the excellent but struggling extraction shooter alongside creative director Julia Nardin.

> Joe Ziegler is the third to hold and exit the role in two years. Marathon's leadership team has been jumbled around following mass layoffs at Bungie in June, with roughly 300 confirmed layoffs across Bungie, or nearly 40 percent of what its estimated headcount was earlier in the year.

> Marathon launched in March with a peak concurrent player count of 88,337, according to Steam DB, and its current player count as of writing is 6,775. The Steam concurrent player count has dropped below 1,000 on multiple days, placing the game near the threshold where matchmaking begins to degrade.
