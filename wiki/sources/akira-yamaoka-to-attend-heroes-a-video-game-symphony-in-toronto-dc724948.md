## Excerpts

> JMP Entertainment announced a special performance of Heroes: A Video Game Symphony in Toronto, Canada's The Bowl at Sobeys Stadium on September 17, 2026. Legendary composer Akira Yamaoka (Silent Hill series, Contra series, Snatcher) will attend the show and participate in a special meet and greet with fans who purchase a VIP experience.

> Heroes: A Video Game Symphony debuted in 2023 and is unique among game music concerts for its narrative framework drawn from Joseph Campbell's monomyth, with each stage representing a chapter in the concert and music selections curated to invoke the themes of each stage.
