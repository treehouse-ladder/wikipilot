## Excerpts

> OpenAI is preparing to preview its latest cybersecurity-focused model, GPT-6 Cyber, in the coming weeks. GPT-6 Cyber is the fourth cybersecurity-focused model OpenAI has released this year, following GPT-5.4 Cyber in April 2026, GPT-5.5 Cyber in June, and GPT-5.6 Cyber in August.

> Next week, OpenAI also plans to ship a dozen or more other products at its annual DevDay event, set for Sept. 29. Most of those releases are not related to cybersecurity but rather other areas of the business, both enterprise- and consumer-focused.

> OpenAI plans to preview GPT-6 Cyber alongside a first-of-its-kind cybersecurity-focused product to help organizations deploy it to automate security workflows.
