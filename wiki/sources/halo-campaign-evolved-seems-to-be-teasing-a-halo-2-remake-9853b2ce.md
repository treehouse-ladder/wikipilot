## Excerpts

> Players who complete Halo: Campaign Evolved on Legendary difficulty unlock a new ending featuring Sergeant Johnson relaxing after the game's events. On Legendary, Johnson looks at the player and says "See you in the sequel," and there's also a post-credits scene where 343 Guilty Spark heads toward a gas giant, which lines up with Halo 2's opening setup.

> A final scene shows Guilty Spark flying toward a mysterious planet seen in Halo 2 where it meets the Arbiter, a co-protagonist of the sequel — this scene was not in the original game and was clearly included to tease the next remake.

> The Bonus Missions in Campaign Evolved also provide evidence that Halo Studios has already been working on the Halo 2 remake project.
