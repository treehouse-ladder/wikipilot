## Excerpts

> Austin Wintory has won the Grammy for Best Score Soundtrack for Video Games and Other Interactive Media for his work on Giant Squid's Sword of the Sea — his first Grammy win after multiple nominations dating back to 2013, when Journey became the first video game nominated in the Score Soundtrack for Visual Media category.

> Sword of the Sea, in which players surf a hoversword across a parched desert world on a mission to restore the ocean, carried a sweeping orchestral score that judges cited for its use of sustained melodic arcs and dynamic harmonic tension as the environment transforms in response to player movement — a textbook application of vertical remixing to narrative-driven world-building.

> The nominee list also included Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora – Secrets of the Spires (Pinar Toprak) and Star Wars Outlaws: Wild Card & A Pirate's Fortune (Cody Matthew Johnson & Wilbert Roget II).
