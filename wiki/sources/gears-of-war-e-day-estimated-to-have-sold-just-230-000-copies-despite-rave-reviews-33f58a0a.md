## Excerpts

> Rhys Elliott of Alinea Analytics released a report that said the game has sold about 230,000 copies so far, with about 168,000 on Steam and about 62,000 on Xbox.

> Correction 10/10/2026, 8:17 a.m. ET: The estimated week one sales are 230,000, not 320,000.

> Alinea estimates that 1.7 million players accessed E-Day via a Game Pass subscription instead of buying it, 722,000 of whom paid $30 for the Premium Edition upgrade.

> Estimated revenue is about $37 million, with Xbox accounting for almost three-quarters of it.

> Mat Piscatella of Circana argued the Game Pass revenue wasn't fully cannibalized, since a subscriber had to pay for Game Pass in the first place and not everyone who played through it would have bought the game outright.
