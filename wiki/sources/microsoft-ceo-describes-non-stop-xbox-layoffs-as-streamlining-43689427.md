## Excerpts

> Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella has described the company's ongoing Xbox layoffs as a natural process of 'streamlining.'

> The 5,750 figure represents the number of workers Microsoft has laid off across its gaming division since acquiring Activision Blizzard, though the full scale of Microsoft's post-merger reckoning remains unclear.

> Nadella said he felt 'fantastic' about the franchises in Xbox Game Studios and that Microsoft expects to return the business to growth in fiscal 2027.

> Developers told Game Developer that the division is at risk of falling apart after losing talent and institutional knowledge, and Microsoft is facing legal action from labor unions in the United States and Canada over the layoffs.
