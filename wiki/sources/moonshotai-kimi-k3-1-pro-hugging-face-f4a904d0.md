## Excerpts

> Kimi K3.1 Pro is an open-weight, natively multimodal agentic model, and our most capable 3T-class model to date. With a 1-million-token context window, Kimi K3.1 Pro delivers substantial improvements in coding, 3D creation, financial research, factual accuracy, and other agentic capabilities.

> Architecture: Mixture-of-Experts (MoE); 2.8T total parameters with 104B activated parameters; 93 layers; 896 experts with 16 selected per token; 160K vocabulary; 1,048,576-token context length; MoonViT-V2 vision encoder with 401M parameters; Image-Text-to-Text.

> Quantization: MXFP4 weights / MXFP8 activations (quantization-aware training).
