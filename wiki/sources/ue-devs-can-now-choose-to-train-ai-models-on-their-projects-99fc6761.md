## Excerpts

> Sellers can still choose whether to allow AI training on their content by using the "NoAI" tag.

> The "NoAI" tag will not be applied to your projects or your Content by default; you must actively designate your projects containing your Content for the "NoAI" tag to apply.

> This change doesn't grant Epic any rights to your content or code.

> Epic removed two restrictions from Section 6e (General Restrictions) of the EULA: one that barred using Unreal Engine source code as input to generative AI programs, and one that restricted using MetaHuman to train AI. The change applies to both new and existing users.
