## Excerpts

> Sony has canceled the live-service PS5 game Fairgame$ from Haven Studios, but confirmed that no layoffs are tied to the decision.

> Per a PlayStation statement, Haven Studios will shift its focus to collaborating with other development teams across PlayStation Studios.

> Sony still says live-service games are part of PlayStation's future; its corporate materials named Bungie's Marathon as a key live-service title and did not mention Fairgame$.
