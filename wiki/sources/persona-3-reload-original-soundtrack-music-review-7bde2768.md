## Excerpts

> Persona 3 Reload, the 2024 remake of the beloved PS2 JRPG, plucks the low-hanging fruit by remastering and slightly updating an all-timer soundtrack, though it may leave long-time listeners wanting more. 'Color Your Night' is a very Persona 5-inspired nighttime exploration theme for Port Island, featuring a piano melody interplaying with a groovy bassline and Lotus Juice's singing, making it the best addition. The updates opt for cleaner, better-balanced instrumentals over the compressed, electronic elements of the original songs. It's a bit of a misfire that the soundtrack sticks so closely to the one that's been available for twenty years, though it speaks to the original's high bar of quality that it holds up so well.
