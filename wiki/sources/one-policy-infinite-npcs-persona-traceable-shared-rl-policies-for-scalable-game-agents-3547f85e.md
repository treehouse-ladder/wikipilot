## Excerpts

> Life simulation games require hundreds to thousands of non-player characters (NPCs) that behave consistently with distinct personalities while remaining controllable through designer-authored natural language. Existing methods fail on at least one of persona consistency, controllability, or real-time inference.

> We introduce PCSP (Persona Conditioned Shared Policy), a single reinforcement learning policy conditioned on frozen LLM embeddings of free-form persona descriptions.

> On a 300-persona life-simulation benchmark, PCSP achieves compositional zero-shot persona identification up to 17x above chance, Spearman rho approximately 0.73 semantic-behavioral alignment, and 22x faster inference than an LLM-as-policy baseline.
