## Excerpts

> Swedish trade unions Naturvetarna, DIK and Akademikerforbundet SSR announced sympathy strike action in support of Unionen's efforts to secure a collective agreement at King.

> King employees in Sweden could go on strike on September 25th after trade unions failed to reach an agreement with the company over a collective agreement covering pensions, insurance, working hours, and the ability to influence conditions at work.

> King chief operating officer Ulrika Hojgard said the studio currently offers 'fair and competitive employment terms' to its workers and believes benefits and conditions are in line with, or exceed, what would be provided under a Collective Bargaining Agreement.
