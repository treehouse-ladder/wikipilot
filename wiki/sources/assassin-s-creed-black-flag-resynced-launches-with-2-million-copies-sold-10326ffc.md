## Excerpts

> Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced has sold 2 million copies on its first day of availability, following its July 9 release.

> The game reached a peak of 99,451 concurrent players on Steam within 24 hours of launch, the highest concurrent player count ever recorded for an Assassin's Creed title on the platform, and ranked number one on Twitch on July 9.

> The game has been well received critically, with an 85% score on OpenCritic and an 84 on Metacritic, making it the highest rated Assassin's Creed game since the original Assassin's Creed IV Black Flag.
