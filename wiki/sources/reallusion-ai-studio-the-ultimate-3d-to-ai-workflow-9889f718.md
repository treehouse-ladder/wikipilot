## Excerpts

> Reallusion announced the launch of AI Studio on May 25, 2026, a groundbreaking creative ecosystem designed to transform artists' hard-earned 3D skills into their ultimate competitive advantage in the AI era. AI Studio is a 3D-guided AI workflow that turns 3D layouts, camera paths, and animations into cinematic AI creations. Instead of forcing artists to start over with unpredictable text prompts, AI Studio uses 3D as a precision control layer for cameras, motion, and layout, while letting AI handle the visual richness, expressions, and physics. The release directly embeds cutting-edge AI models into iClone and Character Creator, consolidating multiple leading generative engines including Flux and Nano Banana for image creation, alongside Kling AI, Veo 3, Wan, LTX, and Scail for professional video generation. The crown jewel of this launch is a direct creative alliance with ByteDance to natively integrate Seedance 2.0.
