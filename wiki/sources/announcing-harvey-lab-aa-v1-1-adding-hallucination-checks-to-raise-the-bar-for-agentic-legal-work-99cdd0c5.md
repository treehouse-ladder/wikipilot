## Excerpts

> Harvey LAB-AA v1.1 adds hallucination checks to the legal agent benchmark. Its headline metric is the Hallucination-Gated All-Pass Rate, which gives a task no credit if any deliverable contains a material hallucination, even when every rubric criterion passed.

> Grok 4.7 (xhigh) leads at 9.4%, ahead of Muse Spark 1.3 (max) at 8.9% and GPT-6 Astra (max) at 8.6% on the Hallucination-Gated All-Pass Rate. Without the hallucination gate, Muse Spark 1.3 would lead with a 26.7% All-Pass Rate, but about two-thirds of those passes contain a material hallucination.

> Across the models tested, more than 60% of otherwise passing results contain a material hallucination, and several models score 0% after the gate. The benchmark uses Harvey's private dataset of 120 tasks across 24 legal practice areas. Scores from v1.1 are not comparable with v1.0.
