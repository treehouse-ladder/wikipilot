## Excerpts

> According to a Bloomberg report by Jason Schreier, Epic Games has told developers working on its unannounced Disney shooter—a game mixing Star Wars and Marvel characters—to stop writing code by hand and instead oversee and review code generated by AI models such as Anthropic's Claude.

> An internal memo reportedly instructs that all new art assets must go through an AI-driven process, with artists acting as concept creators and editors of the generated results rather than building assets from scratch.

> Epic CEO Tim Sweeney posted that 'We have a huge pivot underway here at Epic, moving developers around to retool for maximum AI acceleration,' adding the goal is not to do the same work with fewer people but to enable everyone to build more and better.

> The Disney shooter, previously targeted for a March 2027 release, has been delayed with no new date; multiple developers told Bloomberg they are skeptical of the AI-first approach.
