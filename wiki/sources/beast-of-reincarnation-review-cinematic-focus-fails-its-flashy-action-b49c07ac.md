## Excerpts

> Beast Of Reincarnation's Cinematic Focus Fails Its Flashy Action

> An overall lack of polish hampers its intense combat. The parry-heavy skirmishes are fantastic, with enemies being deadly and aggressive, but flat performances and robotic animations undermine its story, and bland environments sour its art direction.

> Beast of Reincarnation is an ambitious game that often overextends itself, with the lack of polish tainting its versatile combat.
