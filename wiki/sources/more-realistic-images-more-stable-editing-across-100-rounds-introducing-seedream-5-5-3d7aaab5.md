> Introducing Seedream 5.5: more realistic images, more stable editing across 100 rounds.

> Seedream 5.5 introduces native RGBA generation, producing images with full and partial transparency.

> Its multi-turn editing preserves unedited regions at the pixel level and reduces detail loss and image degradation across successive edits, supporting work on the same image across 100 rounds.

> Seedream 5.5 achieves leading results in text structure and text-to-image realism compared with other major models. A Pro version of the Seedream 5.5 series will follow.
