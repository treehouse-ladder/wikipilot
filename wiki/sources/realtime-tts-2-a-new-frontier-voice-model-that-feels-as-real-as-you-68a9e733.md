## Excerpts

> Realtime TTS-2 is built from the ground up for realtime conversation and listens to the prior turns of the exchange, so your tone and pacing carry forward. It takes voice direction in plain English, so you steer the read the way a director would. It holds one voice identity across over 100 languages. Voice Direction lets developers steer delivery using plain-language prompts inline at inference time. Instead of selecting from a fixed emotion enum like [sad] or [excited], developers pass a bracket tag like [speak sadly, as if something bad just happened] directly in the text. #1 realtime TTS by real users on the Artificial Analysis Speech Arena with sub-130ms first-chunk latency, down to 0 per million characters.
