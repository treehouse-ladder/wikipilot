## Excerpts

> The Pokémon Legends: Z-A + Mega Dimension Super Music Complete is a 5 CD set that reached online retailers only four months after the roll-out of the Mega Dimension DLC.

> While Pokémon Legends: Z-A + Mega Dimension Super Music Complete is not the strongest Pokémon soundtrack of the past decade, it sports a handful of truly impressive musical numbers.

> The Game Freak musicians did a great job with the key cutscene music, the reworks of classic Junichi Masuda themes, the tournament themes, the finale, and the entire Mega Dimension DLC scenario.
