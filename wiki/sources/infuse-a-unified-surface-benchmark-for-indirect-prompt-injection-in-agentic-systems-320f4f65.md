## Excerpts

> INFUSE evaluates LLM agents on indirect prompt injection across long-horizon workflows with 40 realistic tasks and a structured taxonomy of 56 attack scenarios spanning five threat categories; injections appear in READMEs, configuration files, terminal output, repository metadata, network responses, build artifacts, and external content, and agents run in isolated Docker sandboxes with full behavioral tracing. Mean attack success is 19.6% under LLM-based evaluation and 15.4% under heuristic verification, with per-model rates from 3.2% to 41.9%. Guard models give limited protection, while prompt-level instructional hardening cut mean attack success from 58.2% to 14.3%, with security-utility trade-offs that depend on the model.
