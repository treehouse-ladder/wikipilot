## Excerpts

> Code2Games coordinates scene analysis, gameplay planning, constrained gaming-world generation, and gaming-engine customization through a shared scene-gameplay representation with persistent element correspondence. After world generation, Code2Games adapts the generated world to Unreal Engine 5 and employs an execution-guided reconstruction process that uses compilation diagnostics, runtime feedback, and gameplay test results to resolve inconsistencies arising during engine adaptation.
