## Excerpts

> You can team up with PUBG Ally in Ally Duo Mode through PUBG Arcade until June 30th via Steam. Ally Duo is a new Arcade mode in which players form a two-player team with the AI character and play on the Sanhok map. Players can communicate via voice interaction to collaborate across a range of in-game situations, including movement, item collection, and strategy.

> PUBG Ally is a cognitive system that leverages three distinct models running locally on RTX GPUs with at least 8GB of VRAM: a lightweight NVIDIA Parakeet speech-to-text model for interpreting English voice commands in real-time, NVIDIA Mistral-Nemo-Minitron small language model for analyzing situations and generating context-aware responses in English, and a custom KRAFTON in-house text-to-speech model for synthesizing natural-sounding voice responses instantly.

> To maximize performance and keep latency to a minimum, an in-game inferencing toolset streamlines AI execution directly alongside the game's graphics engine. Instead of following rigid scripts, Ally cooperates, adapts to combat or looting, and communicates naturally, running AI fully on-device powered by GeForce RTX.
