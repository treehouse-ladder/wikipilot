## Excerpts

> Meta's Muse Ember (max) scores 36 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, and it is the cheapest model to reach that score.

> Muse Ember (max) is priced at $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens, competitively priced against a median of $0.24 and $0.80.

> Muse Ember (max) costs about $0.14 per Intelligence Index task, while Claude Haiku 5.5 (max) costs almost 3x more at $0.41 for the same score; the xhigh variant scores 34 at about $0.12 per task.

> Muse Ember declines to answer 64% of questions, which keeps its hallucination rate at 17.4%. It beats Claude Haiku 5.5 on Terminal-Bench 4.0 (40.9% vs 32.8%) but trails it on FrontierCode 2.0 (27.2% vs 34.9%).
