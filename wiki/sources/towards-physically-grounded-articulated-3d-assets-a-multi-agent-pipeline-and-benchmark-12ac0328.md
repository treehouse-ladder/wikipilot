## Excerpts

> We present Artisan, which builds on Articraft with a multi-agent pipeline and physical simulation feedback, shifting the goal from making articulated objects look right to making them move and function correctly: collision-free motion, stability under gravity, and accurate mass and material properties.

> We introduce a benchmark covering four dimensions—spatial fidelity, physical plausibility, downstream task performance, and overall quality—built from 100 reference assets from ArtVIP and LightWheel, 50 stability prompts, and 50 ABO objects with reference masses.

> Across more than ten representative models, Artisan achieves competitive spatial fidelity while posting the lowest collision rates and the highest stability rates among the evaluated methods.
