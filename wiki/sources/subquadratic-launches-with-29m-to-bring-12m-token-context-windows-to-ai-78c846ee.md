## Excerpts

> SubQ is a large language model from Miami startup Subquadratic, launched May 5, 2026, claiming to be the first LLM on a fully subquadratic (linear-scaling) attention architecture with a 12-million-token context window.

> The company raised a $29M seed round, founded by CEO Justin Dangel and CTO Alex Whedon, former Head of Generative AI at Meta.

> SubQ outperforms GPT-5.5 on retrieval benchmarks at long context, while being made available through an API with a 12-million-token context window.
