## Excerpts

> Kid Katana Records is releasing Ninja Gaiden 4 as a limited 4LP slipcase (also available in black), and it was teased late last year and is now officially confirmed. A 2LP dedicated to the Ninja Gaiden 2 Black original soundtrack is being released alongside the Ninja Gaiden 4 box set.

> The Ninja Gaiden 4 box set is priced at €75, or €89 for a special edition, while the Ninja Gaiden 2 Black double LP costs €42.

> Ninja Gaiden 4 features a soundtrack composed by Masahiro Miyauchi and Takumi Higuchi, with vocals from Victor Borba on a few tracks.
