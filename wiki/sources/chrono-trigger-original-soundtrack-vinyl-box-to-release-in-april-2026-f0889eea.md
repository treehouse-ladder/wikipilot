## Excerpts

> To commemorate the 30th anniversary of Chrono Trigger, Square Enix will release a vinyl box set of the game's original soundtrack on March 25, 2026 in Japan and April 2026 for North America.

> The box includes a total of 64 tracks across 4 LPs from the original soundtrack. Each LP jacket showcases premium artwork created by Akira Toriyama during the game's original release, and the box case lid includes a commemorative message written by composer Yasunori Mitsuda.
