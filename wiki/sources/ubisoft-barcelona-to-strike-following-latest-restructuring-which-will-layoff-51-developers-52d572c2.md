## Excerpts

> Ubisoft Barcelona will go on strike following the studio's latest restructuring, which will see 51 developers laid off — roughly 28% of the studio's pre-downsizing workforce.

> Faced with the recent cuts, Ubisoft Barcelona employees intend to strike on Tuesday and Thursday afternoons between June 30 and July 16, totaling six partial work stoppages over three weeks.

> The studio, which previously contributed to Assassin's Creed, The Division, Ghost Recon, and Rabbids, will now focus exclusively on Rainbow Six Siege.

> The employees' demands include reinstating the jobs of the 51 laid-off colleagues, a five-year guarantee against further collective dismissals, the fulfillment of previously agreed internal promotions which the company has frozen, and the return of a hybrid work model of 60% remote work each month.
