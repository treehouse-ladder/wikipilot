## Excerpts

> Persona Live 2026: Awakenings was held to celebrate the popular JRPG franchise's 30th anniversary, and it was the first U.S. show of the Persona Music Experience series, taking place at the Dolby Theatre in Hollywood, California.

> The show started with Colors Flying High, the opening theme from Persona 5 Royal performed by Lyn, and she was tremendous, unafraid to belt or play to the crowd. Persona Live 2026: Awakenings had no shortage of energizing music — all gas, no brakes.

> The show had such an immediate response when pre-sales started that another show was added to the two originally announced, with the original show selling out within hours.
