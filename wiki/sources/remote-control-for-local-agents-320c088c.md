## Excerpts

> View and respond to agents running on your own computer from the Cursor iOS app. The agents keep running on your computer, and the app connects to that machine, so the computer has to stay powered and connected. Remote control is on by default, except for Enterprise organizations; Enterprise admins can enable it under Org settings > Security and identity > Remote control. Available today in the Cursor iOS app.
