## Excerpts

> Over 30 fired Rockstar Games employees have lost a request for interim relief following an ongoing fight with the Grand Theft Auto 6 maker. After a two-day hearing, Employment Judge Frances Eccles denied that request.

> A UK employment tribunal ruling stated that it 'was unable to conclude that it appears likely that the tribunal will find that the principal reason for the claimants' dismissal was their membership of the IWGB,' meaning the legal battle will continue with no immediate back pay for the fired developers.

> The final trial, which will run from September 10 to October 15, 2026, will allow fired IWGB union workers to continue bringing blacklisting claims against the studio.
