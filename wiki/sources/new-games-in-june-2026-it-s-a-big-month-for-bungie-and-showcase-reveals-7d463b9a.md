## Excerpts

> Summer Game Fest 2026 airs live on June 5 at 2 p.m. PT from the Dolby Theatre in Los Angeles, hosted by Geoff Keighley and Lucy James. Day of the Devs follows at 4 p.m. PT, then Southeast Asian Games Showcase, Wholesome Direct, Story Rich Showcase and others lead into Xbox Games Showcase 2026 on June 7 at 10 a.m. PT (with a Gears of War: E-Day reveal confirmed immediately after at 10 a.m. PT). First gameplay footage of Star Wars: Zero Company is confirmed for the SGF stream.
