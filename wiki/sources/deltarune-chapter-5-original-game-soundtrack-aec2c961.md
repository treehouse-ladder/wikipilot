## Excerpts

> DELTARUNE Chapter 5 (Original Game Soundtrack) by Toby Fox, released by Materia Collective on June 24, 2026, alongside the launch of Chapter 5.

> The Chapter 5 soundtrack features significantly more guest musicians than previous chapters, including 'I guess I'm in love' (feat. Itoki Hana), 'Garden of Hopes and Dreams' (with insaneintherainmusic), 'Rakuichi Buster' (feat. Rakuichi), and 'Flower Man' (feat. Camellia).

> Notable tracks include Pirate Dojo, Inappropriate Recycling, 4rd Sanctuary, Festival, Weirder Birds, Petal Dance, Flower King, Flower Castle, and Walking Home.
