## Excerpts

> Kwalee Labs disbanded its nine-person team on June 16, just 26 days after launching a critically praised Game Pass title, because a flat upfront licensing fee left the studio with no ongoing revenue once retail sales failed to generate sufficient income.

> Despite the closure, Luna Abyss received strong critical acclaim. Polygon called it 'quietly one of the best shooters of 2026,' and it has over an 80 on Metacritic.

> The title's peak concurrent players on Steam was 18,597 near launch, however its most recent 24-hour peak was only 310 players, which marks a dramatic drop-off.

> Unfortunately as of yesterday, the entire team has been made redundant; a decision that was completely outside of our control.
