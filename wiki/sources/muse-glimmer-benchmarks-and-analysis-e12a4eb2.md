## Excerpts

> Muse Glimmer (high) achieves a score of 35 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index.

> Muse Glimmer is a 30B-parameter model released under Apache 2.0. It's small enough to self-host on a single GPU, with weights at ~60 GB in BF16 and ~18 GB in 4-bit precision. The model can run at full context on a single H100 at BF16 precision, or on a higher-spec MacBook or RTX 5090 at 4-bit.

> Muse Glimmer is a distilled version of Muse Spark 1.2, designed with a focus on efficiency to minimize system requirements, and trained and evaluated for end-to-end agentic task completion, multi-step reasoning, and optimized for local deployments.
