# DELTARUNE Chapter 5 (Original Game Soundtrack) | Toby Fox | Materia Collective

Materia Collective release page for the DELTARUNE Chapter 5 original soundtrack by Toby Fox. Released June 24, 2026. 40 tracks across digital platforms. Most collaborative Deltarune chapter OST to date, with guests including Camellia, insaneintherainmusic, Itoki Hana, and Rakuichi.

## Excerpts

> The DELTARUNE Chapter 5 Original Game Soundtrack by Toby Fox was released on June 24, 2026. Several other people helped in this soundtrack, including Camellia, Itoki Hana, Carlos Eiene (insaneintherainmusic), Rakuichi, and Marcy Nabors. The 40-track album is available on Bandcamp, Spotify, Steam, Apple Music, Deezer, Amazon Music, Tidal, Qobuz, SoundCloud, and YouTube Music.

> Unlike most other Chapter OSTs which were mostly composed by Toby Fox alone, Chapter 5 features guest collaborators on multiple tracks, including "Flower Man feat. Camellia" and "Garden of Hopes and Dreams" (Toby Fox & insaneintherainmusic).
