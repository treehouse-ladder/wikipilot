## Excerpts

> FrontierMath Erdős is a benchmark of 68 unsolved problems from the mathematical literature, formalized in Lean.

> GPT-6 Astra is the first model to solve any of these problems, solving two.

> Each model received one attempt at each problem, with a budget of $300 and 72 hours of working time per attempt.

> We tested five models: a pre-release version of GPT-6 Astra, GPT-5.6 Sol, GPT-5.5, Claude Fable 5.1, and Claude Fable 5. Only GPT-6 Astra solved any problems.
