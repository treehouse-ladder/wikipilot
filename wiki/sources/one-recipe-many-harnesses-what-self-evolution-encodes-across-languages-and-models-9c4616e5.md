## Excerpts

> Self-evolving harnesses are closed-loop systems in which an agent inspects its own rollouts and edits its prompts, tools, and memory. Prior work shows they reliably improve coding agents, but it remains unclear whether they encode benchmark-specific adaptations, language-specific engineering knowledge, or compensation for limitations of the underlying model.

> We hold an evolution recipe fixed across a grid of eight programming languages (Multi-SWE-Bench) and three base models. The recipe routes every edit through a typed failure signal and records it as a falsifiable contract, making each modification attributable after evolution.
