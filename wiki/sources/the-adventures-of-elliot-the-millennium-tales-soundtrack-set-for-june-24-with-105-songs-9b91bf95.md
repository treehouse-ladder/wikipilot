## Excerpts

> The Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales Original Soundtrack will release on June 24, 2026, for ¥4,400, across four CDs containing 105 songs.

> The soundtrack was composed by Tomohiro Nakamichi and Hayato Moriya, and the 4 CD set includes 105 tracks (with four that don't appear in the game) as well as liner notes from the pair.

> The soundtrack accompanies The Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales, which will be released on Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam on June 18, 2026.
