## Excerpts

> Fumito Ueda's new studio genDESIGN, which is making Gen Atlas under a publishing agreement with Epic Games, only uses genAI for administrative assistance. According to Ueda, 'Everything that is considered game development' is 'made by humans.' The AI is used for 'coming up with schedules' and 'summarizing meeting notes,' but not, for example, creating placeholder assets for the game.
