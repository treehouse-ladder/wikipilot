## Excerpts

> Compulsion Games has completed its separation from Xbox and is once again an independent studio, with its management team having acquired the studio and its staff.

> Microsoft transferred the developer's IP back to the studio, meaning Compulsion Games now holds all rights to South of Midnight, We Happy Few, and Contrast.

> The studio's leadership is now taking meetings with various parties to secure its financial future, as Microsoft was previously the financial backer of Compulsion and its projects.
