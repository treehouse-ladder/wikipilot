## Excerpts

> DualShockers gave Halo: Campaign Evolved 6.5 out of 10, praising the FPS campaign but noting that PS5-specific performance headaches were holding the package back in meaningful ways.

> The Keyes and The Maw missions were slowing down and chugging so badly that they felt borderline unplayable, with framerate issues being particularly problematic during intense combat scenarios.

> At least half the missions on base PS5 have awful framerate issues, while performance is more stable on other platforms. A performance mode on PS5 Pro targets 60 frames-per-second and achieves that for the most part, though it is not a locked 60FPS.
