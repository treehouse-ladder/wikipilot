## Excerpts

> Hazelight Studios has accrued over 50 million total sales across its three games. The breakdown is: A Way Out sold 13 million, It Takes Two sold 30 million, and Split Fiction sold 7 million. It took the games eight, five, and one year to reach their respective figures. Split Fiction, released on March 6 of last year, accrued a million sales within 48 hours of launch, making it the studio's fastest-selling game to date.
