## Excerpts

> Starseeker: Astroneer Expeditions launches today on Xbox Series X|S, PC, and day one on Xbox Game Pass. Developed by System Era Softworks, Starseeker is a standalone expansion-scale release in the Astroneer universe, focused on multiplayer expeditions to new procedurally-generated planets.

> The game launches with no microtransactions or battle pass — all content is included in the base purchase. System Era Softworks confirmed all future content updates will also be free, funded by the base game price.

> Starseeker represents System Era's largest Astroneer release since the 1.0 launch in 2019, with new biomes, cooperative mission types, and a narrative campaign framework built around planetary expeditions.
