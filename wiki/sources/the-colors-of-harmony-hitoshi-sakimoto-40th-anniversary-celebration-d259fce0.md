## Excerpts

> On Saturday, 13 June, 2026, RPGFan attended The Colors of Harmony: Hitoshi Sakimoto 40th Anniversary Celebration, a day of events at Fairfield Halls in Croydon, London, celebrating both the Final Fantasy series and Sakimoto's contribution to video game music.

> The main event was the Colors of Harmony concert, featuring the London Mozart Players. The concert focused primarily on Final Fantasy XII and Final Fantasy Tactics, split into two 45-minute halves, with the first half including vocalists who performed tracks from 13 Sentinels and Odin Sphere selections.

> The day also included a Q&A with three voice cast members from Final Fantasy Tactics: The Ivalice Chronicles: Joe Pitts (Ramza), Gregg Lowe (Delita), and Eleanor Bennett (Ovelia). Following the Q&A was a 45-minute interview with Hitoshi Sakimoto himself, hosted by Alex Moukala. The concert ended with a standing ovation as Sakimoto joined the orchestra and conductor on stage.
