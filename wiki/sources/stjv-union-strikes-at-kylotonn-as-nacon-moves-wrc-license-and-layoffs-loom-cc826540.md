## Excerpts

> Following the closure of Nacon's subsidiary studio Spiders, France's video game workers' union STJV publicly condemned the company's management and urged players to boycott Nacon products.

> The STJV has gone on strike at Nacon subsidiary Kylotonn, with expectations that around 70% of employees (~80 people) would be laid off. This follows the closure of Spiders, while other subsidiary studios Cyanide and Big Bad Wolf wait to learn their own future.

> The union attributes Spiders' shutdown to malice and mismanagement from Nacon and Spiders' leadership rather than recent insolvency filings.
