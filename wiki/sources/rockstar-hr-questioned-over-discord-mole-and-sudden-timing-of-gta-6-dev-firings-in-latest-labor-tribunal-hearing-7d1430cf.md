## Excerpts

> Among the claims is that someone under the alias 'AB' was passing screenshots of union activity in the Discord server to Kinloch for over two years. While Rockstar maintains that AB shared information with management about worker activities of their own volition, AB is not among the witnesses that will be allowed to be questioned during the hearing.

> The fired developers contend that information was passed to Kinloch showing the group was just 12 members away - 10 percent of the workforce - from the threshold needed to have a recognized union at Rockstar. But Kinloch contends that he never had any interest or knowledge of their union status.

> Rockstar says the workers were dismissed for gross misconduct, specifically for leaking confidential information in a Discord channel, not for union activity.

> Closing submissions in this hearing are expected on November 17 and 18. The hearing was originally expected to run until October 16, 2026, so the schedule has shifted. Separately, Rockstar Games devs working on GTA 6 will vote on whether or not to establish a union at the studio in a vote running from November 12 through 26.
