## Excerpts

> The Future Games Show Summer Showcase and FGS Live From Los Angeles were broadcast on Saturday, June 6. The show featured over 40 games from AAA and indie studios, including 11 world premieres.

> EXODUS - Archetype Entertainment shared the Extended Gameplay Reveal of EXODUS to headline this year's Summer Showcase. EXODUS is coming to PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X & S in early 2027.

> Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis - Game Director Raul Siqueira shed light on the world, puzzles and 3D exploration mechanics, with the game coming to PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X & S and Nintendo Switch 2 on February 12, 2027.
