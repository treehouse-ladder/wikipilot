## Excerpts

> Sony has canceled Fairgame$, the live-service heist shooter from Montreal-based Haven Studios, following a recent playtest, and the company says no layoffs are tied to the decision.

> PlayStation executives wrote in a memo that the playtest showed strong operational and community results, but that player feedback and retention results were mixed and fell short of the goals the company had set, also pointing to challenging market conditions in the PVP shooter genre.

> Jade Raymond left Haven Studios in 2025, though the project quietly continued development; the game had been delayed and was reportedly targeting Spring 2026 before this cancellation.

> The cancellation follows Sony pulling multiplayer shooter Concord from storefronts and then shutting down its developer, Firewalk Studios.
