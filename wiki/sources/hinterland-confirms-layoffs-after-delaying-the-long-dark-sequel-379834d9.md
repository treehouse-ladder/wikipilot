## Excerpts

> Hinterland is laying off 10 full-time employees after delaying The Long Dark sequel Blackfrost. Studio founder and CEO Raphael van Lierop shared the news on LinkedIn and explained the company has also decided against extending the fixed-term contracts of four employees.

> Blackfrost, the sequel to The Long Dark, needs more time in development before launching in Early Access, and pushing the game out beyond the end of 2026 means pushing significant expected revenue out as well.

> The layoffs help preserve financial resources required to get the game to launch and continue supporting other games.
