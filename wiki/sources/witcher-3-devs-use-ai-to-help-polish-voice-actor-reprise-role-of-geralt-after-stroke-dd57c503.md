## Excerpts

> Jacek Rozenek, the original Polish voice of Geralt, has consented to a special exception for CD Projekt Red to use AI to aid his voice acting in the announced expansion Songs of the Past, after a stroke changed his voice. The studio used Respeecher — which it previously used for a late actor's voice in Cyberpunk 2077's Phantom Liberty — training on his previous Witcher recordings. Rozenek is still being hired and paid for the new recordings, with AI adjusting them to sound closer to his earlier voice. Localization producer Mikolaj Szwed called it an ethical use of AI because it had the performer's consent and served a restorative purpose, adding that such arrangements are rare and were made only because of Rozenek's health.
