## Excerpts

> Electronic Arts is closing BioWare, the studio behind Mass Effect and Dragon Age, after more than 30 years of game development.

> EA is undergoing a major restructuring that will eliminate about 400 jobs across the company, roughly 3 percent of its workforce, with about 50 of those cuts affecting BioWare staff.

> The next Mass Effect game, the fifth in the series, has been canceled.

> BioWare is shutting down, while Respawn and DICE are teaming up on a new Star Wars project, Star Wars: Battlefront 3; BioWare's Edmonton office will close.
