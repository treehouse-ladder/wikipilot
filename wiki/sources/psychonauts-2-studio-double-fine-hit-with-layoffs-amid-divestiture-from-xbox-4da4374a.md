## Excerpts

> Double Fine Productions founder Tim Schafer announced that 23 members of staff have been let go from a team that had been about 80 strong before this.

> Double Fine was granted independence from Xbox alongside South of Midnight developer Compulsion Games earlier this month; both studios retained their IP and game catalogs as part of the split.

> 'As a small, tightly-knit team, these actions are not taken lightly. Only the survival of our studio would ever make us consider such a painful action. Our transition to becoming an independent company also means becoming a size that we can sustain.'
