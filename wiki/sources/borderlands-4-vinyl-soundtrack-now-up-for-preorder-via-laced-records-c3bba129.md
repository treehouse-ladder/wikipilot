## Excerpts

> Laced Records have opened up preorders for the vinyl soundtrack to the latest entry in the looter shooter series Borderlands – Borderlands 4. The release will be a 2LP available in two vinyl color variants – an exclusive "Max Mayhem" red and yellow swirl colored vinyl variant, and a standard black vinyl variant. It will be housed in a gatefold jacket with artwork by Gearbox. It is priced at £36 and is scheduled to release in February, 2027.

> The Borderlands 4 soundtrack has contributions from composers Finishing Move Inc., Cris Velasco, Joshua Carro, and Christian Pacaud. The set features 22 of the 24 score tracks, alongside the standout single "This Land" by Des Rocs. Each side of the vinyl release is dedicated to one of the major biomes of in-game planet Kairos – The Fadefields, The Terminus Range, Carcadia Burn and the hostile Dominion City.
