## Excerpts

> Bill Skarsgård, best known for portraying Pennywise in Andy Muschietti's It universe and Count Orlok in Nosferatu, has been announced as the lead star of Hideo Kojima's upcoming action-espionage game Physint. The news was announced at the tail end of Xbox's Tokyo Game Show 2026 showcase, alongside a new behind-the-scenes look at Kojima Productions' upcoming horror title OD – KNOCK.

> Swedish actor Bill Skarsgård will portray the lead role in Physint, and the game's cast also includes Charlee Fraser, Don Lee, and Minami Hamabe.

> The director said the upcoming project is continuing to make steady progress. The Physint news was the first update we've gotten on the highly anticipated spiritual successor to Metal Gear Solid since PlayStation's big breakup with Kojima that led Physint to be signed by Xbox.
