## Excerpts

> Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B 4GB, the third-generation small language model (SLM) for NVIDIA ACE AI game characters, releases Friday, Oct. 23.

> Starting Friday, Oct. 23, test drive the third-generation Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B 4GB inside SkyrimNET, the NPC modding platform for The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim by MinLL.

> SkyrimNET supports both cloud and local inference across multiple models, with the latest Nemotron models delivering best-in-class performance for efficient and fast in-game interactions.

> SkyrimNET transforms every NPC into an unscripted, interactive character equipped with personal memory, voice and autonomy for dynamic conversations and context-aware quests generated on the fly.

> Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B 4GB is optimized for intelligence per GB, combining natural conversation with multi-turn tool use, instruction following and grounding in application state for physical and conversational agents on GeForce RTX, Jetson, and RTX and DGX Spark.
