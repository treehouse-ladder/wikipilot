## Excerpts

> The concert was recorded across two days in February 2020 at Billboard Live Tokyo, and is based almost entirely on the Square Enix Jazz -Final Fantasy VII- studio album released earlier that year.

> Arrangers Eijiro Nakagawa and Ryu Kawamura produced five studio albums, as well as this live album, between 2017 and 2022. Arranger Eijiro Nakagawa makes especially good use of harmonic structure and takes some liberties with reharmonization.

> There are tracklist changes: 'Cosmo Canyon' isn't included, 'Main Theme' (the Final Fantasy series theme, not 'Main Theme of Final Fantasy VII') is new to this album, and 'Fanfare' is also new to this album.

> Ryoji Ihara's tenor sax solo is highlighted as a favorite, with pure volume coming out of that horn and intensity in the encore performance.
