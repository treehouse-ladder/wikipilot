## Excerpts

> Microsoft announced a family of seven new models across image, voice, transcription, thinking, and coding. MAI-Thinking-1: A first reasoning model that's exceptionally strong in reasoning and software engineering tasks, a 35B active parameter Mixture of Experts with a 256K context window. MAI-Code-1-Flash: Rolling out to all GitHub Copilot plans starting June 2026. MAI-Image-2.5: Live in PowerPoint. MAI-Voice-2: A speech generation model with beautiful prosody, native-sounding delivery and fine-grained emotional control, available in 15 languages. MAI-Transcribe-1.5: The model achieves state-of-the-art average WER across 43 languages and leads in 18 of them.

> When running MAI models on the MAIA 200 chip end-to-end, Microsoft sees 30% better performance per dollar as well as a 1.4x performance-per-watt gain versus GB200.
