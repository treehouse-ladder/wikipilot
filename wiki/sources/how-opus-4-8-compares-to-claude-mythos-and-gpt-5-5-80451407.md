> Claude Opus 4.8 scored 78.8% on the CyberGym leaderboard, placing it third behind Claude Mythos Preview at 83.1% and GPT-5.5 at 81.8%. On CyberGym, Mythos produced a full working exploit on 70.8% of Firefox targets against Opus 4.8's 8.8%. Mythos leads on the hardest software work, posting 77.8 to Opus 4.8's 69.2 on SWE-bench Pro. On GPQA Diamond, a set of graduate-level science questions, the two models tie at roughly 94. Anthropic's system cards point to a general-intelligence gap over Opus 4.8 that is modest and uneven, while the cyber gap is vast.
