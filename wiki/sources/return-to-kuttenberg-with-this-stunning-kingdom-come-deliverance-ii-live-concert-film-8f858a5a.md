## Excerpts

> Warhorse Studios published the official concert film on their YouTube channel—a live performance that took place in early 2026 inside the stunning St. Barbara's Church in Kutna Hora, also known as Kuttenberg.

> The concert showcases the local philharmonic orchestra alongside a 110-singer choir, clocking in at just over an hour. In-house composer Jan Valta conducted the entire performance.
