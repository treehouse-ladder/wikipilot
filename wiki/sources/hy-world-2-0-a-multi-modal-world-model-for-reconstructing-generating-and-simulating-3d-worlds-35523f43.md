## Excerpts

> HY-World 2.0 is a multi-modal world model framework for world generation and world reconstruction that accepts diverse input modalities — text, single-view images, multi-view images, and videos — and produces 3D world representations (meshes / Gaussian Splattings). The World Generation capability synthesizes high-fidelity, navigable 3D scenes through a four-stage method using HY-Pano 2.0, WorldNav, WorldStereo 2.0, and WorldMirror 2.0 & 3DGS learning. HY-World 2.0 is an open-source state-of-the-art world model with all model weights, code, and technical details released to facilitate reproducibility and advance research in this field.
