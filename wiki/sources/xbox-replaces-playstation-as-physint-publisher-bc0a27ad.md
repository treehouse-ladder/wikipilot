## Excerpts

> Xbox agreed to publish Hideo Kojima's stealth and action-espionage title Physint after PlayStation Studios ended its involvement with Kojima Productions on the project.

> Kojima wrote that Kojima Productions received the cancellation notice from PlayStation Studios unexpectedly, by email, in mid-June 2026, and spent the three months that followed searching for a new publisher.

> Physint was first revealed during the PlayStation State of Play in January 2024 with PlayStation Studios as its publisher.
