> At the September 2026 State of Play, PlayStation revealed that Double Fine's surreal adventure Keeper is shadow-dropping on PS5, and it's available starting today for PS5.

> Keeper launched in 2025 at a $30 price point and now sells for $20. Along with the surprise PS5 release, Keeper is also getting a Psychedelic Mode.

> After its liberation from Microsoft's ownership, the newly independent Double Fine Productions has been quite busy.
