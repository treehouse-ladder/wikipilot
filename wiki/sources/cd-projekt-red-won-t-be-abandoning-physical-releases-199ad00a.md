## Excerpts

> During a conversation with Game Developer at Gamescom 2026, CDPR joint-CEO Michal Nowakowski said the company has an affinity with physical products and will always strive to put out a box that's worth the price of admission.

> Nowakowski's comments come after PlayStation maker Sony announced it would be ditching physical media entirely in 2028. He noted that Xbox, PlayStation, and Nintendo control the manufacturing of physical media, stating it's not like they can go against them.
