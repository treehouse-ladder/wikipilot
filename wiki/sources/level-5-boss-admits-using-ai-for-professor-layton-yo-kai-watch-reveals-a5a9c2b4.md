## Excerpts

> Level-5 president and CEO Akihiro Hino confirmed the company used generative AI to make its game footage 'more spectacular,' but said Level-5 is using AI to digitize 'creative work made by people,' such as 'converting original artwork into polygons,' and insisted that 'no simplistic AI-generated output data is included in the games that are released.'

> Hino stated that 'the parts that relate to the charm and individuality of the released works—such as scenarios, character designs, and basic settings—are all created by human hands,' and that the company only uses AI to automate slow and technical processes, not creative ones.

> The showcase was quickly met with criticism after social media users gathered evidence that the Level 5 Vision presentation used generative AI; the CEO issued a public apology on X following the global backlash.
