## Excerpts

> The Library of Congress inducted Bobby Prince's 1993 Doom soundtrack into the National Recording Registry, selected alongside 24 other recordings from more than 3,000 public nominations, as 'audio treasures worthy of preservation for all time based on their cultural, historical or aesthetic importance in the nation's recorded sound heritage.'

> Doom is only the third video game music entry chosen, following the Super Mario Bros. theme and Minecraft: Volume Alpha. All of the music was written before the game had completed levels, and despite the limitations of 1993-era sound card drivers, Prince ensured that his sound effects could cut through the music by assigning them to different MIDI frequencies.
