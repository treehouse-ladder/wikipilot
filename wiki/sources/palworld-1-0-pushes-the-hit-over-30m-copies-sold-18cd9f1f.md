## Excerpts

> Palworld has reportedly sold approximately 30.5 million copies and generated $700 million in gross software revenue following its 1.0 launch, according to estimates from Alinea Analytics.

> Around 86% of the game's premium sales came from Steam, representing approximately 27.1 million copies. Version 1.0 launched on July 10, with a 30% launch discount helping generate another 1.8 million estimated sales.
