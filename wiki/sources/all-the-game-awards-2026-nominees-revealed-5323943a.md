## Excerpts

> The Game Awards 2026 nominees were announced on November 17, 2026, with the ceremony scheduled for December 10 in Los Angeles.

> The Game of the Year nominees are 007 First Light, Control Resonant, Grand Theft Auto VI, Phantom Blade Zero, Resident Evil Requiem, and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.

> Grand Theft Auto VI leads all games with eight nominations, despite launching on November 19, two days after the nominees were revealed. The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time picked up four nominations in total.
