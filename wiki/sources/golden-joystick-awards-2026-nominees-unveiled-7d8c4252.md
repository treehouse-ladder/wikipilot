## Excerpts

> The 44th Golden Joystick Awards feature 21 voting categories; 007 First Light and Big Walk lead with six nominations each, and Resident Evil Requiem has four.

> Console Game of the Year nominees: 007 First Light, Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave, Forza Horizon 6, LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight, Nioh 3, Onimusha: Way of the Sword, Pokemon Pokopia, PRAGMATA, Resident Evil Requiem, and Saros.

> PC Game of the Year nominees include Big Walk, Crimson Desert, Escape from Tarkov, Mewgenics, Valheim, and Star Wars Zero Company.

> Public voting closes October 23, 2026, and the winners will be revealed at the ceremony on November 11, 2026.
