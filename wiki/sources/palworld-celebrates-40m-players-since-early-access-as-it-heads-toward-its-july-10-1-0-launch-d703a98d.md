## Excerpts

> Palworld has crossed 40 million players, a milestone developer Pocketpair confirmed just days before the game leaves early access.

> 1.0 is a free update. Anyone who already owns Palworld on Steam, the Microsoft Store, Xbox or PS5 receives it at no additional cost, and it launches day one on Xbox Game Pass for both PC and console.

> The update adds Wing Pack aerial gear, the World Tree endgame zone, Sky Islands, PvP mode, and Genetic Recombination breeding.
