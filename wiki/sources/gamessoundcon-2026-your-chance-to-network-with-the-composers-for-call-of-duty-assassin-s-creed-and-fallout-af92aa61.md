## Excerpts

> Sonia Coronado (Sony) will be discussing the use of Adaptive Music featured in Ghost of Yotei.

> The program includes talks like "AI in the Audio Department: What's Actually Useful" and "A Frank Discussion About AI for Sound Designers" and "Games Music, AI and Human Connection" from game audio icon, Guy Whitmore.

> Other speakers include a franchise-themes panel with Inon Zur (Starfield, Fallout 3, Fallout 4), Wilbert Roget II (Call of Duty: WWII), Garry Schyman (Bioshock, Middle-earth), Jason Graves (Dead Space), and Jesper Kyd (Assassin's Creed, Hitman, Warhammer).

> GameSoundCon 2026 runs October 20-21 at the Marriott Burbank Convention Center, a hybrid event with in-person and online access.
