## Excerpts

> Gemini 3.5 Live Translate is based on Gemini 3 Pro. Input: Audio with a token context window of up to 128K. Output: Audio and text, with up to 64K token output.

> Translation Quality is evaluated using AutoMQM - an error-based automatic metric which identifies and categorizes translation errors to produce a fine-grained quality score.

> Gemini 3.5 Live Translate is less capable than Gemini 3.1 Pro, therefore based on Gemini 3.1 Pro, we are confident that Gemini 3.5 Live Translate is also unlikely to reach any Critical Capability Levels (CCLs).
