## Excerpts

> Gemini 3.5 Live Translate operates as a streaming model, beginning to translate while the speaker is still talking and staying only a few seconds behind to maintain context and conversational flow.

> The model focuses on continuous speech-to-speech translation while preserving the original speaker's tone, pacing, and vocal characteristics.

> The technology automatically detects more than 70 languages without requiring manual language selection and can generate low-latency translated speech.
