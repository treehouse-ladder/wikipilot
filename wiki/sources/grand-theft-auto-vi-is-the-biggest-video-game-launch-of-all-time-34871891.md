## Excerpts

> Grand Theft Auto VI is officially the biggest video game launch ever. Take-Two said GTA 6 reached 32.5 million copies sold before release, with an estimated 3.79 billion dollars in day-one retail sales. This makes it the biggest entertainment release, for any medium, in history, Take-Two said.

> That is a new pre-order sales record across all consoles, according to Rockstar's parent company.

> The 3.79 billion dollars seems well on its way to helping Take-Two hit its projected 3.35 to 3.5 billion dollars in net bookings for the fiscal quarter.
