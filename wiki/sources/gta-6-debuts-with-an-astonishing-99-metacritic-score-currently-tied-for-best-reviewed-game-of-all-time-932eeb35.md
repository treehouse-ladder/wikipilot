## Excerpts

> GTA 6 has debuted with a 99 on Metacritic, tying it with the original 1998 release of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time as the highest-rated game of all time.

> None of the reviews on Metacritic are negative, with the lowest score being a 9 out of 10, and on OpenCritic, 100 percent of critics recommend the game.

> GTA 6's 99 makes it Rockstar's highest-rated game ever, edging out GTA 4 by a point and Red Dead Redemption 2 by two points.

> The score could be bumped down a bit as more reviews are added in the coming days and weeks, but it is unlikely to budge a whole lot.
