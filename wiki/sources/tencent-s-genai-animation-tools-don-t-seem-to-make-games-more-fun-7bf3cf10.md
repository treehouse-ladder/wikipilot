> Tencent's MoreFun subsidiary showcased AI tools for AI-generated voices and genAI-powered NPCs in a sponsored GDC session, but the demonstrations did not convincingly improve gameplay enjoyment. 52 percent of survey respondents believe generative AI is having a negative impact, with workers in visual and technical art (64 percent), game design and narrative (63 percent), and game programming (59 percent) holding the most unfavorable views.
