## Excerpts

> Meccha Chameleon, a multiplayer hide-and-seek game built by a single Japanese developer in approximately two months, crossed 3 million copies sold on Wednesday, June 17, and claimed the No. 1 spot on Steam global sales charts, outselling Forza Horizon 6 and Destiny 2 without a publisher, a marketing budget, or a press campaign.

> According to estimates from the analytics service Gamalytic, Meccha Chameleon had generated approximately $8.7 million as of June 17.

> The game reached No. 1 on Steam's global sales chart through a combination of a low price point ($5.99, or $4.79 at launch), Twitch-native design that generated viral clips, and a viewer-participation mode that let streamers' audiences join games directly.
