## Excerpts

> Remedy Entertainment placed Control Resonant at a $60 price point, as part of their financial report for the first half and second quarter of 2026. Remedy's CEO stated that 'Control Resonant will be available across all major platforms from day one and is priced attractively for an AAA title.'

> The game is scheduled to release on September 24, and preorders are available for the game's various physical editions. The $60 standard edition appears to be the main offering for the PS5 and Xbox Series X versions.
