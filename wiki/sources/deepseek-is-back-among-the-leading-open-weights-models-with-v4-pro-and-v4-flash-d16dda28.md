# DeepSeek is back among the leading open weights models with V4 Pro and V4 Flash

## Excerpts

> DeepSeek V4 Pro (Reasoning, Max Effort) scores 52 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, making it the #2 ranked open weights reasoning model behind only Kimi K2.6 (54). DeepSeek V4 Flash (Reasoning, Max Effort) achieves a score of 47. V4 Pro has 1.6T total / 49B active parameters and V4 Flash has 284B total / 13B active parameters, with Pro positioned for maximum capability and Flash for faster, lower-cost inference. Both models are hybrid thinking/non-thinking. The top open weights models on Artificial Analysis are: 1. Kimi K2.6 (54), 2. MiMo-V2.5-Pro (54), 3. DeepSeek V4 Pro (Reasoning, Max Effort) (52).
