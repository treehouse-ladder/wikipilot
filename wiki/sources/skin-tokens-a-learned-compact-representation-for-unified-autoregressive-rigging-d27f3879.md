## Excerpts

> SkinTokens is a learned, compact, and discrete representation for skinning weights, and TokenRig is a unified autoregressive framework that models the entire rig (skeleton and skinning weights) as a single token sequence. The core innovation reframes the traditionally ill-posed, high-dimensional continuous regression problem of skinning into a more tractable token sequence prediction task. Given an input 3D mesh, it generates a complete skeleton hierarchy and skin weights that can be directly imported into standard 3D pipelines for character animation and simulation. The SkinTokens representation leads to a 98%-133% improvement in skinning accuracy over state-of-the-art methods, while the full TokenRig framework, refined with RL, enhances bone prediction by 17%-22%.
