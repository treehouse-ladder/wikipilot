## Excerpts

> Three-dimensional content generation has progressed from producing isolated, visually plausible shapes to constructing structured assets that can be deployed in real-time interactive environments, requiring 3D content that goes beyond surface appearance to satisfy engine-level constraints on topology, UV parameterization, and physically based materials.

> Topology generation involves restructuring raw geometry into artist-friendly mesh connectivity that supports real-time rendering and further downstream processing. MeshCraft encodes meshes into continuous face-level tokens via a transformer-based VAE and generates full topology in parallel through a flow-based DiT with face-count control, while SpaceMesh represents connectivity as continuous halfedge latents and achieves speeds orders of magnitude faster than autoregressive methods.
