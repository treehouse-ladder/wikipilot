## Excerpts

> According to Bloomberg, making Call of Duty: Black Ops 6 available for free to paid Game Pass subscribers on console and PC cost Microsoft $300 million in lost sales of the 2024 best-seller. A former Microsoft employee disclosed that Call of Duty console and PC sales were $300 million lower in 2024 than they were in 2023.
