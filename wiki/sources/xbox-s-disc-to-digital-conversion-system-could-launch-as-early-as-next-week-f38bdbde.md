## Excerpts

> Xbox's disc-to-digital conversion system, reportedly codenamed Positron, could launch to Xbox Insiders as early as next week, according to reports. Xbox insider Jez Corden posted 'Positron cometh,' signalling that Insiders could soon get access to the feature.

> The feature would let players convert supported physical discs into digital licenses, arriving amid the PlayStation controversy over Sony ending physical disc production in 2028 and GTA 6 shipping without a disc.
