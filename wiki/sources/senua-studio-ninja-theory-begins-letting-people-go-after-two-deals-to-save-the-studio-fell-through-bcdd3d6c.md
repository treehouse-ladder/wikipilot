## Excerpts

> Last month Xbox announced that two deals to save Ninja Theory had fallen through and that the studio would be entering UK's consultation process ahead of mass layoffs and a presumed shutdown.

> Multiple now-former employees posted on LinkedIn that their roles were at risk of redundancy, or that they were seeking new opportunities.

> One gameplay programmer wrote 'It's incredibly sad knowing that we won't get to finish what we were working on,' noting that 'So much care, talent and effort went into it.'

> Just four months ago at Xbox's summer showcase, Ninja Theory announced a brand new game simply titled Senua, with this reveal done in hopes of attracting the interest of a buyer for Ninja Theory.
