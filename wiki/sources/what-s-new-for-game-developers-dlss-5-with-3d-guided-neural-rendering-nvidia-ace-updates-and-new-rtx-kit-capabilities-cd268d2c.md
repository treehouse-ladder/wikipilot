## Excerpts

> Nemotron Speech 3.5 Streaming is a 600M-parameter automatic speech recognition (ASR) model built to keep latency low while staying accurate. Qwen3 TTS is a 600M-parameter text-to-speech model that generates high-quality audio and supports custom fine-tuning.

> The In-Game Inference SDK adds early support for RTX Spark (Developer Preview), NVIDIA's new AI and graphics platform for slim laptops and ultra-efficient desktops, plus Gemma4 integration and llama.cpp performance work.

> DLSS 5 with 3D-Guided Neural Rendering takes the engine's rendered frame as its base and adds lighting and material detail; its one-frame-in, one-frame-out design uses game-engine motion vectors to keep results consistent as players move, running locally on a single GeForce RTX 50 Series GPU at up to 4K, with developer controls for model selection, Structure and Tone Intensity, and masking.
