## Excerpts

> Seedream 5.0 Pro is ByteDance's multimodal image creation model. Its headline features are dense infographic generation, interactive precision editing, layer separation, and multilingual text rendering.

> Seedream 5.0 Pro renders native text in up to 14 languages and outputs up to 2048x2048. ByteDance notes there is still room to improve in finer-grained text rendering and pixel-level editing consistency.
