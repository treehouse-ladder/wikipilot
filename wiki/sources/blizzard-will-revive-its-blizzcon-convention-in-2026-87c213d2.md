## Excerpts

> BlizzCon will make a two-day return on September 12 and 13, 2026 at the Anaheim Convention Center. It'll be the first BlizzCon since 2023, and since Microsoft acquired Activision Blizzard.

> Blizzard expressed enthusiasm about the event's return, stating that they aim to meaningfully elevate this iconic celebration and create an unforgettable experience for all who take part in it.
