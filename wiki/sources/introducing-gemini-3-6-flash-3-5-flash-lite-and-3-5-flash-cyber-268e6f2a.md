## Excerpts

> Gemini 3.6 Flash is our latest workhorse model, delivering better coding, knowledge work, and multimodal performance at a lower price than 3.5 Flash.

> 3.6 Flash is available to developers via the Gemini API in Google AI Studio and Android Studio, in Google Antigravity, in the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform and Gemini Enterprise app, and to everyone in the Gemini app.

> Gemini 3.6 Flash is priced at $1.50 per 1M input tokens and $7.50 per 1M output tokens.

> We are also introducing Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, our most cost-efficient model, and Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber, a specialized variant tuned for cybersecurity workloads.
