## Excerpts

> Game composers are shifting from delivering files to designing systems.

> The hybrid model — AI for procedural variation, humans for core creative vision — is where most studios are landing.

> While generative audio cuts production costs by over 50% in narrow circumstances — such as ambient procedural content in a 100-hour open-world game — this hides new costs like specialized talent, software licensing, and engine performance overhead.

> Copyright remains genuinely unsettled: in the US, fully AI-generated content can't be copyrighted, and contract clauses protecting composer work from AI training are now standard practice.
