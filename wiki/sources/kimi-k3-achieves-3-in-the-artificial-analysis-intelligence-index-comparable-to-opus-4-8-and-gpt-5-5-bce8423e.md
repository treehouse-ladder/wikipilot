## Excerpts

> Kimi K3 scores 57 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. Its intelligence is comparable to Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5 but remains behind Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol.

> Kimi K3 reaches an Elo rating of 1668 on GDPval-AA v2, surpassing GPT-5.5 (1494) and Claude Opus 4.8 (1600).

> Kimi K3 costs an average of $0.94 per Intelligence Index task, which is similar to GPT-5.6 Sol ($1.04), and ~1/2 the price of Opus 4.8 ($1.80).

> Kimi K3 is released with native image and text multimodal input.

> Kimi K3's token usage on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index decreased significantly, using 21% fewer output tokens than K2.6.
