# Rockstar Games Workers To Vote On Unionization Right As Grand Theft Auto VI Launches

Source: Game Developer, 2026-10-30

## Excerpts

> UK workers at Rockstar Games will vote on whether to establish a union with the Independent Workers' Union of Great Britain (IWGB) from November 12 through 26, running through GTA 6's launch on November 19.

> If successful, the established union would be the largest recognized video game sector union in the UK.

> The vote runs alongside an existing unfair-dismissal tribunal over the 30+ staff Rockstar says were fired for gross misconduct, whose closing arguments fall November 17 and 18 — straddling GTA 6's launch window.
