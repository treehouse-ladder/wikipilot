## Excerpts

> Spyro: A Realm Beyond was unveiled during the Xbox Games Showcase, with the game taking flight in Spring 2027. The game is coming to Xbox Series X/S, PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC.

> The game is crafted by Toys For Bob, representing a new evolution of Spyro built to send the purple dragon soaring higher than ever.

> Spyro has subtle visual changes to suggest he's grown, most notably an enhanced wingspan so he can be much more capable in flight, and Tom Kenny is returning to voice the character.
