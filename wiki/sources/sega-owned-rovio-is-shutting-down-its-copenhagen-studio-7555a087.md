## Excerpts

> Following a redundancy process, Rovio will discontinue all its operations in Denmark.

> Around 20 employees worked on the now canceled Sonic Blitz project. Sonic Blitz was a hybrid runner and card collection game for mobile devices.

> Sega West COO and Rovio CEO Alex Pelletier-Normand said retention didn't meet expectations, noting that the studio needs to see people engage with the game for a long period of time.
