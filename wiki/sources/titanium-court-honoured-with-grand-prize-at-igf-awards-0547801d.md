## Excerpts

> Titanium Court, developed by AP Thomson, scooped up the prestigious Seumas McNally Grand Prize for Best Independent Game at the 28th annual Independent Game Festival (IGF) awards. Titanium Court was also awarded the prize for Excellence in Design.

> Other notable winners at the showcase included horror title Horses, Baby Steps, and Perfect Tides: Station to Station. Baby Steps won Excellence in Audio.
