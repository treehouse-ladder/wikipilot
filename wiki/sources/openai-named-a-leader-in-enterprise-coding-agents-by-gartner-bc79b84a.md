> > OpenAI has been recognized as a Leader in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for Enterprise AI Coding Agents. This evaluation reflects OpenAI's progress in supporting enterprise-scale deployments of Codex, which is used by more than 4 million people each week.
> 
> > Gartner recognized Codex's strengths across agentic software development, enterprise governance, sandboxing, and flexible deployment options, and highlighted Codex's broad developer surface—including the Codex app, IDE extensions, CLI, SDKs, and cloud-based orchestration—as well as enterprise controls such as approval gates, RBAC, customizable policies, OS-level sandboxing, and auditable workspace governance.
> 
> > Codex has enterprise deployments at Cisco, Datadog, Dell Technologies, and NVIDIA. For example, Cisco used Codex to develop the majority of its AI Defense security platform, shortening delivery time from several quarters to weeks.
