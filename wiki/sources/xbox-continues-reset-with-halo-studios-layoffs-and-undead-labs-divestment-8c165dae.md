## Excerpts

> Xbox has laid off another 268 employees as part of a major reset it commenced in July 2026. The latest redundancies include workers at Halo Studios, other first-party teams, and the Xbox Game Studios management and central functions layer.

> Booty said the division is now "roughly three-quarter of the way through [our] previously announced restructuring."

> Ninja Theory is facing closure after two separate agreements for the Senua developer collapsed, with Xbox beginning consultation with employees on a proposed closure while continuing to explore other paths forward. Arkane Lyon remains under consultation pursuant to French labor laws, with this process expected to continue through the end of the year.
