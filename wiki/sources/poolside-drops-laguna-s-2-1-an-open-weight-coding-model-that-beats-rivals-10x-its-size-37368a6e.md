## Excerpts

> Laguna S 2.1 scores 70.2% on Terminal-Bench 2.1, placing it 11th on the company's compiled leaderboard — ahead of DeepSeek-V4-Pro-Max, a 1.6-trillion-parameter model that scored 64.0. On SWE-Bench Multilingual, it posts 78.5%, and on SWE-Bench Pro's public dataset, 59.4%.

> The weights are available on Hugging Face under an OpenMDW-1.1 license. It is small enough to run on a single NVIDIA DGX Spark.

> The model went from the start of pre-training on May 22 to public launch in under nine weeks, trained on 4,096 Nvidia H200 GPUs.

> On Terminal-Bench 2.1 and SWE-Bench Pro, Laguna S 2.1 matches or exceeds models several times its size, including DeepSeek-V4-Flash, NVIDIA's Nemotron 3 Ultra or Thinking Machines' Inkling.
