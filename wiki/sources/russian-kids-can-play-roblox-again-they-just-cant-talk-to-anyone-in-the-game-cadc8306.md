## Excerpts

> Roblox has returned to Russia after a period of restricted access, but Russian players face significant limitations: all chat and social features are disabled for users in Russia, meaning they can play games but cannot communicate with other players in-platform.

> The return follows negotiations between Roblox and Russian authorities, with the communication restrictions apparently satisfying regulators' concerns about children interacting with foreign nationals online. The arrangement mirrors broader Russian internet governance patterns.

> The Roblox-Russia situation highlights the tension between global UGC platforms and national internet regulation, where access is permitted under conditions that effectively silo users from the global player base.
