## Excerpts

> Tokyo Game Show 2026 has canceled its final day, Monday September 21, as Typhoon Dujuan approaches, ending the show's first-ever five-day run early.

> The 30th-anniversary edition was the biggest Tokyo Game Show in the event's history, with a record 1,138 exhibitors from 53 countries across 3,999 booths, and CESA had expected around 300,000 visitors across the five days.

> CESA cited forecasts of strengthening wind and rain and the risk of transportation disruptions, and said some of the stage events scheduled for September 21 may instead be streamed online as official broadcasts.
