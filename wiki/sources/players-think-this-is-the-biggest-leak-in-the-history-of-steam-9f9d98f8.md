## Excerpts

> Thirteen terabytes of Steam depot data, reportedly spanning from 2003 to 2013, has leaked online within the past 24 hours.

> Those investigating the contents have reported that it contains beta builds for thousands of games, including Portal 2, Left 4 Dead 2, Dragon Age: Origins, and Batman: Arkham Asylum.

> The fact that the Steam2 depot data only goes up to 2013 may have something to do with Valve switching to the new storage system in 2013.
