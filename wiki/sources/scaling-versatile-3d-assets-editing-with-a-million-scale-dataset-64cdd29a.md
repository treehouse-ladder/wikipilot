## Excerpts

> Alchemy3D-1M is a large-scale 3D editing dataset containing 1.25M assets and 1.38M editing pairs across seven editing types.

> Alchemy3D is a unified framework for training and evaluating versatile 3D asset editors that covers data construction, model architecture, and benchmark evaluation.

> It also introduces GEdit3D-Bench, a large-scale, open-world benchmark with a multi-dimensional evaluation protocol.

> The model family supports image- and text-conditioned editing, few-step inference, and transfer to multi-view 3D part segmentation.
