## Excerpts

> Ubisoft Barcelona, one of the studios that worked on the successful Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced, has gone on strike to protest layoffs of 51 employees, with the strike running through Thursday, July 16.

> The studio contributed the underwater levels to Black Flag Resynced, which sold 2 million copies in a single day. Workers said the company has turned its back on them, being laid off instead of celebrating the series' latest hit.

> Striking developers demanded the cancellation of the proposed 51 layoffs, stronger job security guarantees, the return of the studio's previous remote-working policy allowing up to 60% remote work, and promised promotions and salary increases. Barcelona is being restructured to focus solely on Rainbow Six projects.
