> The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time is the highest-rated game of 2026, albeit with the caveat that this might only last for a couple of weeks, anyway.

> At the time of writing, its Metascore was 96, based on 48 reviews, none of which dips below an 8.

> Eurogamer called it a masterpiece, and Game Informer called it a wonderful recreation of the 1998 experience with improvements on basically every element.
