## Excerpts

> Resident Evil Requiem won Ultimate Game of the Year at the 2026 Golden Joystick Awards, taking 17 percent of the public vote, one point ahead of Phantom Blade Zero at 16 percent, with Crimson Desert third at 15 percent.

> Capcom's game took four awards in total, including Console Game of the Year, Best Game Trailer, and Best Lead Performer for Angela Sant'Albano. Capcom also won Studio of the Year.

> Crimson Desert won PC Game of the Year, Meccha Chameleon won Best Multiplayer Game and Best Indie Game (Self-Published), Dispatch won Best Storytelling, and Valheim won Best Indie Game.
