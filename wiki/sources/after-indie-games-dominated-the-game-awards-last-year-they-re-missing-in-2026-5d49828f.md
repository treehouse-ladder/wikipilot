## Excerpts

> The Game of the Year category completely snubs all indie games for the first time in three years, with none of the six nominees being an independent production.

> Mina the Hollower, Big Walk, Cairn, and Mewgenics are among the indie games that absolutely could've occupied a spot in that category based on their critical reception.

> Big Walk and Mina the Hollower are two of the highest-rated games of the year on Metacritic, and their omission from Game of the Year likely reflects recency bias given both released earlier in 2026.
