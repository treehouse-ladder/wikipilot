## Excerpts

> The complete Dispatch soundtrack pressed to vinyl by Lost In Cult Records arriving Q4 2026, with a 3LP boxset featuring the original score and licensed tracks available in standard black disc or limited-edition Astral Variant.

> The soundtrack features Andrew Arcadi and Skyler Barto's melancholic score alongside Bershy's breakout hit "Radio", and is being pressed to vinyl as a 12", 140g, 3xLP set.
