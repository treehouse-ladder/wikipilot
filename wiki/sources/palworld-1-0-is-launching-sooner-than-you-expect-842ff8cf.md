## Excerpts

> Palworld 1.0 will launch on July 10, developer Pocketpair announced during Summer Game Fest. After multiple years in early access, the legally distinct Pokemon-like game Palworld is getting its 1.0 version very soon. The developer stated they are 'fully committed to delivering a deeper, more evolved, and definitive Palworld experience worthy of a full launch.' Palworld originally launched on PC and Xbox in January 2024, becoming a gigantic success and breaking records.
