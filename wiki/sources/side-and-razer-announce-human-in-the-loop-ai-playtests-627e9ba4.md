# Side and Razer announce human-in-the-loop AI playtests

## Excerpts

> The tool, named Razer Cortex: Playtest Program—Powered by Side, tracks in-game activity and performance while collecting player sentiment; a Razer Cortex user can sign an NDA and receive a download key for a game in development, then earns Razer Silver after completing a required play time and submitting feedback. The tool uses AI to analyze data from user playtests, and the information is then sent to QA analysts to help them catch bugs that 'could have potentially flown under the radar in a lab setting.' The companies claim this will lead to 'more testers and more playtest hours' at the 'same or lower cost' than traditional playtests.
