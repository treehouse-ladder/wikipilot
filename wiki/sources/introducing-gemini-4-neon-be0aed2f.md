## Excerpts

> Gemini 4 Neon ranks #3 overall on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index with a score of 61, and #2 on the Vals Index. It also reports #1 results on Zapier's AutomationBench and Andon Labs' Vending-Bench 2.

> Gemini 4 Neon costs $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens, with a 1 million-token context window.

> Gemini 4 Neon is rolling out in the Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Gemini for business, Google Antigravity for paid AI Pro and Ultra subscribers, and the Gemini app for Pro and Ultra subscribers.
