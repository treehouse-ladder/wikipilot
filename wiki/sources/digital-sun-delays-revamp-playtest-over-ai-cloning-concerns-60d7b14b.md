## Excerpts

> Digital Sun Games, the studio behind Moonlighter, has narrowed the public playtest of its upcoming 'Tower Defensevania' game ReVamp to its Discord community, citing concerns about generative AI-enabled cloning.

> The studio said it is concerned by recent news of games being reverse-engineered, cloned, and published before the original is even out, and judged that a large public build was too risky with launch still months away.

> Digital Sun isn't alone: Alliance Arts and Who's You canceled the Steam Next Fest demo for the upcoming game Drunken Goddess Reflux over similar concerns.

> The studio acknowledged that showing games early is how indie studios get noticed and find fans, and the article argues this cloning pressure could hurt smaller studios' market prospects and may push platforms like Steam to adopt new policies that disincentivize the behavior.
