## Excerpts

> Following the Xbox Games Showcase and the announcement of major post-fiscal-year-end layoffs, Pure Xbox has compiled an updated roadmap of all confirmed Xbox Game Studios titles coming in the second half of 2026 and into 2027.

> The roadmap includes Halo: Campaign Evolved (July 28), Gears of War: E-Day (Fall 2026), Fable (February 2027, delayed from its original 2026 window), and Avowed's upcoming expansion. Several studios affected by the upcoming layoffs are not represented in the near-term pipeline.

> The post-layoff roadmap is notably leaner than 2025's, with fewer than 10 first-party titles confirmed across the 18-month window, reflecting the 'reset' framing from Xbox CEO Asha Sharma's internal communications.
