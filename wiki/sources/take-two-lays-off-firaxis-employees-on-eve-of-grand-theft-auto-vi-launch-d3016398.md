## Excerpts

> Take-Two has laid off employees at Civilization and XCOM developer Firaxis Games on the eve of the launch of Grand Theft Auto VI, with Bloomberg reporting around 60 people cut, roughly a third of the studio.

> Firaxis studio head Heather Hazen is leaving the company, and a new, unannounced XCOM game had reportedly struggled to hit internal development milestones.

> A 2K spokesperson said the size of the studio is being reduced to focus on design, technology, and the player experience.
