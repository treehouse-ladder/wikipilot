## Excerpts

> Rockstar Games has officially revealed the GTA 6 cover art and confirmed that pre-orders for Grand Theft Auto VI start on June 25, 2026.

> Pre-orders for Grand Theft Auto VI will officially begin on June 25 on digital storefronts and at other select retailers.

> The cover features Jason and Lucia, an alligator, Boobie Ike, and other imagery from Vice City and its surrounding areas. The cover art follows the traditional picture collage format of previous games in the series.

> It's slated to release on November 19, 2026. Notably, pricing for the game editions has not yet been officially revealed by Rockstar Games.
