## Excerpts

> The 2026 Epic AI MegaJam runs November 12-15, 2026. Teams of up to five humans can build a playable game in Unreal Engine or UEFN with as many AI agents as they want, joining online or in person in San Francisco.

> Standout entries can earn development continuation grants of up to $50,000, with each main category paying $7,500 for first place. Partners include Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, and SpaceXSI; Anthropic offers $25 in Claude API credits.

> The Unreal Engine 5.9 MegaJam Preview is described as the first Unreal Engine release built primarily with agentic workflows. Unreal MCP launches automatically with the editor and expands its toolsets, and expanded Python APIs let agents do more inside the editor.
