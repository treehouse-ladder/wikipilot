## Excerpts

> Developer Lillymo Games has partnered with JSQRD to release the official vinyl soundtrack for Saint Slayer: Spear of Sacrilege, bringing the game's full 26-track score to a physical format. The soundtrack is composed by Josh Davis, whose work draws from classic 8-bit traditions. Set to ship in early July 2026, the first pressing will be limited to 300 copies. The release features a single 180g purple and white pinwheel splatter disc, housed in a full-color premium jacket.
