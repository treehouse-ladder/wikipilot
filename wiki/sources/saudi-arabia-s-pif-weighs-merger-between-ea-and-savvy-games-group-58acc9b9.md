## Excerpts

> Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund is weighing a merger of Electronic Arts with Savvy Games Group to create one of the world's biggest gaming firms and ensure better coordination between its assets, according to a Bloomberg report. No decision has been made.

> A combined company would bring together EA's console and PC publishing (EA Sports FC, Battlefield, The Sims, Apex Legends) with Savvy-owned assets such as Scopely. Any merger is unlikely to proceed until Savvy completes its $6 billion acquisition of Moonton, and would require regulatory approval.

> EA's $55 billion take-private closed on August 4, 2026, with PIF holding about 93.4% of the company. Savvy CEO Brian Ward stepped down on September 1, 2026, with PIF executive Turqi Alnowaiser serving as interim CEO. EA, Savvy, and PIF all declined to comment.
