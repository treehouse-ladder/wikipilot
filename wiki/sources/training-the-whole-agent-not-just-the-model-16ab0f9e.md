## Excerpts

> HarnessAdapt-RL trains the policy and adapts its harness during reinforcement learning.

> Using a 9B base model, HarnessAdapt-RL improves over GRPO by 9.8 points on SWE-bench Verified, 12.6 on Multilingual, and 6.4 on Pro, and also beats GRPO trained with the human-designed OpenHands harness.

> Skill gains persist after their removal at evaluation, but removing them at deployment costs up to 7.7 percentage points.
