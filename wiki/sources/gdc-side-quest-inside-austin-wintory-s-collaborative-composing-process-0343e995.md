## Excerpts

> Composer Austin Wintory has built a career on creating high-quality video game scores and advocating for improved collaboration between the artists and engineers who bring video games to life. At the annual GDC Festival of Gaming, Wintory hosts the Developer's Concert.

> The performance blends monologues from Wintory and special guests and explores different genres of video game music to celebrate the hard work that goes into making video games.

> GDC Side Quest is hosted by GDC Festival of Gaming Marketing Manager Beth Elderkin, and in this episode she sits down with Wintory for a conversation about curating the Developer's Concert, his career in video game composing, and how his work intersects with the art and business of video game development.
