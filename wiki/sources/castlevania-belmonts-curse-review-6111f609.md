## Excerpts

> Much of the music was created by Dead Cells' composer Yoann Laulan, with tracks that shift at set points and during certain combat and puzzle moments. The review describes a blend of bells, organs, and orchestral swells with trap and hip-hop touches. It says the score works without leaning on the series' famous themes, which appear sparingly. 'Bloody Tears' is singled out as recognizable from far off. Scored the game 97. — Matt Wardell, RPGFan, October 13 2026.
