## Excerpts

> Subnautica 2 launched in early access on May 14, and the Steam store page estimates the game will remain in Early Access for 'about 2 to 3 years.'

> Subnautica 2 wants to tell players how to play it, with heavy-handed over-direction and a gated ocean that necessitates experiencing the game in the order it intends. Subnautica 2 is never quiet, with so much dialogue and capital-p Plot that players never feel like they can just swim off in a new direction.

> Despite the legal controversies surrounding its development, the game attracted hundreds of thousands of concurrent players on Steam, peaking at 467,582, with combined concurrents across Steam, Epic, and Xbox topping 651,000.
