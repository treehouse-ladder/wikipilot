# Silent Hill Townfall becomes fastest-selling entry in the series, Konami confirms series roadmap

## Excerpts

> Silent Hill: Townfall has sold over 500,000 copies in its first four days on sale, making it the fastest launch week for any Silent Hill title in the franchise's 27-year history. Konami president Mio Fukuda cited the strong first-person horror reception and the series' "return to consistent annual release" strategy as drivers, and confirmed that the Silent Hill publishing program will continue in 2027 with at least one new entry in active development at a studio Konami has not yet disclosed.

> Screen Burn Interactive's Jon McKellan said the studio plans two free post-launch content updates through March 2027, adding new radio transmissions, a hidden ending, and an optional new location called The Lantern Mill. A paid story DLC titled 'The Fog Rolls In' is also in development; no date or pricing has been set.
