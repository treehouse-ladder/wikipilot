## Excerpts

> Xbox CEO Asha Sharma and Xbox chief content officer Matt Booty have sent a memo to workers stating that Xbox is preparing to reset in order to 'revive' the company. The message states 'The model that got us here won't be the one that takes us forward.' The move will result in major cuts across the division shortly after the conclusion of Microsoft's fiscal year on June 30, 2026. Sharma noted that console storage component prices had doubled since the previous fall, and are expected to increase over 5x prices from two years earlier. The Microsoft Gaming moniker is going away; the division is returning to calling itself Xbox.
