## Excerpts

> 11 bit studios and Black Screen Records released the Frostpunk 2 soundtrack on vinyl, featuring the award-winning score by Piotr Musial for the first time in its entirety on LP. The release is a luxurious set with music on gold and black 'yolk' vinyl, packed in a gold embossed gatefold sleeve.
