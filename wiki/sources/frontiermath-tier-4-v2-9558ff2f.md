> FrontierMath: Tiers 1-4 (v2) is live. We concluded an audit that addressed errors in 42% of problems. Rankings are similar but scores are higher across the board. The current leaders are GPT-5.5 (xhigh) with 85% on Tiers 1-3 and Google's AI co-mathematician with 76% on Tier 4.

> On 2026-06-12, Epoch released a major update, addressing errors in 42% of problems. Following this update, the full FrontierMath dataset consists of 338 problems, split into a base set of 295 problems (Tiers 1-3) and an expansion set of 43 exceptionally difficult problems (Tier 4).

> The v2 release corrected 123 problems in Tiers 1-3 and 12 problems in Tier 4. It also removed 5 problems from Tiers 1-3 and 7 problems from Tier 4.

> GPT-5.5-xhigh's FrontierMath 4 score jumped from 35% to 73% after EpochAI fixed errors in the benchmark.
