## Excerpts

> The soundtrack features Peter McConnell's 'big band, bebop, and bones' style, and the reviewer describes it as easily the best jazz-based video game soundtrack they've ever heard. The music stands up better on its own on the CD than it did within the game itself, with praise for the use of genuine instruments rather than synthesized instrumentation. iam8bit, in partnership with Double Fine and Disney, is repressing the Grim Fandango 2xLP Vinyl Soundtrack — 51 tracks pressed on two vintage black vinyl discs — for the game's 25th anniversary, originally released five years ago for the game's 20th birthday.
