## Excerpts

> FromSoftware confirmed that The Duskbloods, its Switch 2 exclusive multiplayer game, will hold a closed Network Test this August. Sign-ups open on July 22, 2026 and will be accepted until July 28, with participants selected in order of sign-up and notified on August 7.

> The Network Test will run across five sessions between August 21 and August 23, 2026. Groups of eight players will join a single session while FromSoftware tests game balance, multiplayer and server load. Players must own a Nintendo Switch 2 and have an active Nintendo Switch Online membership to participate.

> The Duskbloods is a PvPvE multiplayer game for up to eight players, marking a departure from FromSoftware's typically single-player-focused Soulsborne titles, and is one of the highest-profile Switch 2 exclusives on the horizon.
