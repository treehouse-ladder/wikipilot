## Excerpts

> AI coding agents select frameworks, scaffold infrastructure, and wire integrations, often in seconds, yet these are architectural decisions that almost no one reviews as such. The paper identifies five mechanisms by which agents make implicit architectural choices and proposes six prompt-architecture coupling patterns that map natural-language prompt features to the infrastructure they require. The patterns range from contingent couplings (structured output validation) that may weaken as models improve to fundamental ones (tool-call orchestration) that persist regardless of model capability. The authors term the phenomenon "vibe architecting" - architecture shaped by prompts rather than deliberate design.
