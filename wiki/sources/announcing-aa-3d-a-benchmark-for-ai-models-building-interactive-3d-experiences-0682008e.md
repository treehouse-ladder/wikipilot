## Excerpts

> Claude Sonnet 5.5 is first at 1194, statistically tied with three other Anthropic and OpenAI models.

> Each model works as an agent in an offline sandbox and turns a written brief into a three.js web page. The benchmark has 55 tasks, each a written brief for one interactive scene.

> A run stops after three hours or 250 turns, and the result is an index.html page. The sandbox includes Blender, Rapier physics, Python, a headless browser and a library of CC0 assets.

> Scores come only from blind pairwise votes. A panelist sees two scenes built for the same task by two different models, interacts with both, and selects the one they prefer. Ratings use Bradley-Terry maximum likelihood estimation, then get rescaled to an Elo-like range.

> Meta's Muse Spark 1.3 is seventh at 1035. The best-placed open weights models, Kimi K3 (982) and MiMo-V2.6-Pro (977), are 11th and 12th. GPT-6 Luna costs $0.20 a scene and still ranks eighth.

> The most detailed fifth of scenes in each task wins 63% of its votes, against 31% for the least detailed fifth.
