## Excerpts

> GameMaker's GMRT runtime becomes free to use starting January 1, 2027, letting developers ship games for free on PC, mobile, browser, and GX Games. The change comes from a new update by the Opera-owned engine developer.

> Developers previously needed a professional license to ship games on GMRT or GMS2.

> Developers targeting PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, or Nintendo Switch 2 will still need to purchase an enterprise license.

> GMRT was announced in April 2026 and brought new tooling, including support for AI-based programming workflows on Claude Code.
