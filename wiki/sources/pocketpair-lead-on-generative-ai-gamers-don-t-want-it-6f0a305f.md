> Pocketpair communications director and publishing manager John Buckley: 'Gamers don't want it, and if the gamers don't want it, I guess that's it, right? Not much of a conversation to be had.' Buckley compared the push for AI to 'early crypto stuff,' noting 'It feels like everyone who is super gung-ho about it isn't from the industry. They're, dare I say, outsiders looking to get rich quick.'
