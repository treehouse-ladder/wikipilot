## Excerpts

> We're expanding our cybersecurity initiative Daybreak and introducing GPT-5.6-Cyber, a new model for advanced, authorized cybersecurity work.

> Built on GPT-5.6 Sol, it is trained to improve capabilities on several specialized cybersecurity tasks (e.g., finding zero-day vulnerabilities and developing exploit chains) and to reduce refusals for certain higher-risk, dual-use cyber tasks.

> GPT-5.6-Cyber completes 95.0% of cybersecurity requests, compared with just 1.5% for GPT-5.6 Sol, and 2.0% when used with Daybreak Blue access.

> OpenAI researchers used Daybreak Red to identify two previously unknown vulnerabilities in V8 that could be chained to escape the heap sandbox.

> Daybreak Red, which uses GPT-5.6 Cyber, is intended for advanced, authorized workflows, including proof-of-concept exploit development, exploit-chain validation, penetration testing, and red teaming. Access requires additional approval and is subject to stronger verification, monitoring, access controls, and human oversight.
