## Excerpts

> EA has laid off an undisclosed number of Battlefield developers just months after the record-breaking launch of Battlefield 6. There have been layoffs across all four studios within the division, which comprises internal studios Criterion, DICE, Ripple Effect, and Motive.

> The shooter sold 7 million copies within three days of launching on October 10, 2025. The news comes around four months after EA said Battlefield 6 shattered records to deliver the biggest launch in franchise history.

> We've made select changes within our Battlefield organization to better align our teams around what matters most to our community. Battlefield remains one of our biggest priorities, and we're continuing to invest in the franchise.
