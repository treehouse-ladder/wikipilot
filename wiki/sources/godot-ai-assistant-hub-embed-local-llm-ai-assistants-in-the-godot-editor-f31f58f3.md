## Excerpts

> Designed for local LLMs first, but capable of using remote options.

> With a unique take on LLMs centered around the idea of having different assistant types for different tasks.

> Designed to give you control over what your assistants can or cannot do.

> This plugin does not run LLM models directly, but acts as an interface between Godot and your local LLM.
