## Excerpts

> Ninja Theory has cancelled Project Mara, its experimental photorealistic horror title that was revealed in 2020. Studio Head Dom Matthews explained that Senua, the studio's third title in the series, requires all of the studio's effort.

> Matthews stated: These decisions are never easy, but I did so to take the opportunity to have all of the talent and expertise in the studio, all 85 creatives, working together to realize the potential of what Senua can be.
