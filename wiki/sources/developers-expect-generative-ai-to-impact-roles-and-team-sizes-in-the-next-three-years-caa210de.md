## Excerpts

> A survey taken by 100 speakers at this year's Gamescom Dev Conference showcases hesitation and mixed feelings on generative AI use.

> Overall, 83 percent of respondents expect AI to affect team structure or productivity in some way.

> 36 percent of the speakers believe AI will change roles rather than reduce teams, while 33 percent expect smaller team sizes. Additionally, 14% think it will raise individual output, and 17% do not expect workforce reductions.

> The clearest near-term use case is code and production: 34% said those areas would benefit most from AI assistance.
