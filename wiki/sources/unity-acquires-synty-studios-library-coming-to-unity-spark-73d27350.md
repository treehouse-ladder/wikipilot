## Excerpts

> Synty Studios, the maker of the POLYGON asset packs, is joining Unity, and its library will be available in Unity Spark, Unity's upcoming AI-native game creation platform, starting with the closed beta later this year.

> Most of the artists who made the packs are joining Unity full time, and they'll keep making new ones.

> Synty's library now holds more than 760,000 stylized, low-poly 3D assets.

> Current users face no immediate changes. Free Synty assets stay free, and there are no plans to change Synty's pricing at this time.

> Synty's packs will continue to be available for the engines and tools they support today, and anything you've already bought, for any engine, is unaffected.

> Spark also draws on the Asset Store, which lets creators combine Synty assets with assets from other publishers.
