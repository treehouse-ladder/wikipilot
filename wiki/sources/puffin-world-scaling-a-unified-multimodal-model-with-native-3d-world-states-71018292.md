## Excerpts

> Puffin-World is a unified multimodal world model that perceives, simulates, generates, and reconstructs the 3D world within one framework. It represents the world through three complementary native world states: Physics (gravity field and latitude), Geometry (depth, describing the underlying 3D spatial structure), and Appearance (images and sequences, describing what an observer sees).
