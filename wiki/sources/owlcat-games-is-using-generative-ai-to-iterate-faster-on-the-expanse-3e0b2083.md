## Excerpts

> Owlcat PR manager Katharina Popp told Eurogamer that the studio doesn't use AI 'to create any assets that will be in the game.' Instead, it is used for 'a lot for prototyping, trying things out, placeholders. They will all be replaced at the end.' Everything that will be in the final version will definitely 100 percent be human made. The studio emphasizes that they don't use it to write, they don't use AI voice actors.
