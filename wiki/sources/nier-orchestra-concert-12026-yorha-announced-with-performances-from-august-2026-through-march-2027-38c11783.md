## Excerpts

> The NieR:Orchestra Concert 12026 [ YoRHa ] is an upcoming international concert held in various locations from August 2026 to March 2027. Conducted by Eric Roth, it will feature a full orchestra and chorus as well as vocals by Emi Evans and J'Nique Nicole.

> The U.S. tour will start in Seattle, Washington on August 7, 2026, and conclude in San Jose, California on March 6, 2027. The tour will make stops in Seattle, Chicago, Atlanta, Denver, Los Angeles, Boston, Houston, Washington, DC, and San Jose.

> A new narrated story will also be featured with recorded recitations by Yui Ishikawa, Reina Tanaka, Mio Hanana, and Chihira Mochida, presenting episodes related to the Pearl Harbor Descent Operation, the prequel story to NieR:Automata. Yoko Taro and composer Keiichi Okabe will attend the Seattle performance.
