## Excerpts

> Running With Scissors canceled a newly revealed Postal series entry one day after announcing it, responding to backlash after fans said the reveal trailer appeared to use AI-generated artwork.

> The game, Postal: Bullet Paradise, was a fast-paced first-person shooter spinoff developed externally by Goonswarm Games. The backlash occurred just days after Running With Scissors railed against generative AI usage in gaming.

> Goonswarm Games announced that the studio would shut down and cease operations following the backlash. Goonswarm Games later acknowledged that 'promo art does appear to include or be influenced by AI-generated material,' while maintaining that no in-game content was made with generative AI.
