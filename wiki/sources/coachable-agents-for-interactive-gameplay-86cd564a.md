## Excerpts

> Coachable agents are trained to perform a task in a variety of different styles, and to apply those styles on-demand at execution time, using style-conditioned UVFA to produce agents that can succeed at complex tasks—like walking, driving, or fighting in a video game—while being able to modify their style on command.

> The techniques allow an end user to choose the final behavior at run time, giving them flexible control over the final executed performance. Demonstrated in the AAA video games Horizon Forbidden West and Gran Turismo, and in an open-source humanoid test domain.
