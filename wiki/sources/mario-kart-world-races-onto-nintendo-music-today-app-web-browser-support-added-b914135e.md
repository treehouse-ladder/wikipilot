## Excerpts

> Nintendo Music version 1.6.0, released on June 2, 2026, bundles a marquee content drop with a significant platform expansion. The launch of a browser-based web player at music.nintendo.com allows users to stream Nintendo Music directly from any desktop or laptop browser without installing anything. The update introduces native iPad support, Apple CarPlay, Android Auto compatibility, Siri voice search, and a browser-based version of Nintendo Music. To coincide with the web version and new apps, Nintendo has added 130 tracks from Mario Kart World to Nintendo Music — composed by Atsuko Asahi (music lead), Maasa Miyoshi, Takuhiro Honda and Yutaro Takakuwa, with over 200 remixes from previous Mario games for a total of 323 songs.
