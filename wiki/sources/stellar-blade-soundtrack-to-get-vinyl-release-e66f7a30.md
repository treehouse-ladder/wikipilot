## Excerpts

> The game's brilliant soundtrack is coming to vinyl, with Laced Records launching both a 2LP and 4LP version of the Stellar Blade soundtrack. The 4LP box set contains 44 tracks.

> Stellar Blade's soundtrack comprises vocal-led electronic, K-Pop, rock & metal songs; as well as improvisational jazz and delicate acoustic styles. Fan-favourites include 'Flooded Commercial Sector', 'Everglow', and 'Raven'.

> Vinyl sets include liner notes written by the game's Sound Director, Jooeun Hwang, revealing hidden details about key tracks from the game and the dynamics of the different music teams.
