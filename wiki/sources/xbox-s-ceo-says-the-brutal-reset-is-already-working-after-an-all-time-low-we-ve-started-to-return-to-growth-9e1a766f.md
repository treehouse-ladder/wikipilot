## Excerpts

> Xbox CEO Asha Sharma, speaking at an internal company townhall on October 6, 2026: "We're making the right decisions to reinvest in the business, after an all-time low, we've started to return to growth, more than 2 to 3 times what we were, and we expect that to continue."

> Sharma cited strong starts for Gears of War: E-Day, Minecraft Dungeons 2, and World of Warcraft: Forever, and claimed a 30-percent boost in brand sentiment year-over-year.

> Chief content officer Matt Booty cautioned that Xbox is only 75 percent through the reset, with hundreds more layoffs still ahead.
