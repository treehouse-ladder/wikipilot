## Excerpts

> The IWGB Game Workers' Branch released a joint proclamation with members from Game Workers Unite Ireland, CSVI of Spain, and FIOM-CGIL Milan Work Council.

> GenAI is currently harming the games industry, its workers and the games they produce.

> The manifesto itself tackles the subject of genAI from several different angles, covering socioeconomic, environmental, ethical, and production-quality concerns, and recommends that workers avoid it.

> The manifesto's FAQ addresses critics who call the unions anti-future, responding that workers have the right to shape their future.

> 40 percent of game industry professionals use generative AI tools as part of their job, up from 36 percent the previous year.
