## Excerpts

> Moonshot's Kimi K2.6 is the new leading open weights model, landing at #4 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (54) behind only Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI (all 57). Kimi K2.6 is a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) model with 1T total parameters and 32B active.

> Kimi K2.6 has a comparatively low hallucination rate of 39% (reduced from Kimi K2.5's 65%). Kimi K2.6 achieves an Elo of 1520 on the GDPval-AA evaluation.
