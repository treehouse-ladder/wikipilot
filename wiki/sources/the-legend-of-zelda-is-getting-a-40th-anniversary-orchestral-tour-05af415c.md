## Excerpts

> The Legend of Zelda 40th Anniversary Concert series will be held across the globe next year and include a variety of music from the Legend of Zelda series, from nostalgic tunes to modern melodies.

> The Legend Of Zelda 40th anniversary concert tour will kick-off with six shows in Japan before visiting four cities in the USA, two cities in Australia and three cities in the UK, with just one stop on mainland Europe.

> The tour will begin with a series of Japanese dates in January 2027 before visiting North America, Europe and Australia. The tours begin in Japan, with the first show on January 23rd in Tokyo. After that, there will be a North American tour beginning February 22nd in Chicago, a European tour starting on April 3rd in London, and an Australian tour kicking off shortly after.

> The Legend of Zelda tour will be using local orchestras for each stop on the tour.
