## Excerpts

> Wuthering Waves Concert Tour: To the New World is now heading to Los Angeles, giving North American fans the chance to experience the music of Wuthering Waves live at Peacock Theater on October 17, 2026.

> The concert will feature live performances of music from across the game, celebrating the world of Solaris-3, its characters, and the soundtrack that has accompanied players through two years of updates, stories, regions, and major events.

> The concert tour has already performed in Shanghai, Tokyo, Seoul, Taipei, and Singapore, and every region except Los Angeles has sold out tickets. Tickets went on sale on July 2, 10:00 AM (PDT) through AXS, Peacock Theater, and Crypto.com Arena.
