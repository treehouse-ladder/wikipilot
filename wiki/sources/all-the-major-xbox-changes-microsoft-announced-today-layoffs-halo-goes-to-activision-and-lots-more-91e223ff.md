## Excerpts

> Xbox laid off 268 more individuals across its organization as it continues its previously announced "reset," consolidating several of its studios and entering consultation with Senua studio Ninja Theory.

> Microsoft's Forza developers, Playground and Turn 10, are now merging to become one studio that will focus on the Forza and Fable franchises. "These two studios have been creative and franchise partners for over a decade, and today's changes will formally move their work into one team."

> Activision is expanding its remit and is now developing the next Halo title led by a "new, purpose-built team." Additionally, internal Xbox studios Rare and World's Edge have also been moved under the Activision umbrella.

> Obsidian is joining Bethesda and will keep working on Grounded, as well as its previously announced new Fallout project.
