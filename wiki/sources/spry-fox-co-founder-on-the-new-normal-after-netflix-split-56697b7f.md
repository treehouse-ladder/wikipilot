## Excerpts

> Spry Fox joined Netflix in 2022 but was sold back to co-founders Daniel Cook and David Edery in December 2025.

> Co-founder David Edery explained that the team had to make sacrifices to regain their independence, with both Daniel Cook and Edery reducing their salaries to $20,000/year and spending their own cash to buy the studio back.

> Now that the studio is independent, its mission has shifted from launching Spirit Crossing exclusively on Netflix to bringing the title to a variety of platforms.
