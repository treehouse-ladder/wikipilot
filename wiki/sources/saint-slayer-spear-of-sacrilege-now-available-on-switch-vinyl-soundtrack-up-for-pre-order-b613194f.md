## Excerpts

> Developer Lillymo Games has partnered with JSQRD to release the official vinyl soundtrack for Saint Slayer: Spear of Sacrilege, bringing the game's full 26-track score to a physical format. The soundtrack is composed by Josh Davis, whose work draws from classic 8-bit traditions while supporting the game's tone and pacing. Set to ship in early July 2026, the first pressing will be limited to 300 copies.

> The release features a single 180g purple and white pinwheel splatter disc, housed in a full-color premium jacket and inner sleeve. The package includes artwork by Lachlan Pini (key art) and Joshua Gossage and JSQRD (pixel art), along with a bonus 'top secret' guide containing hidden passwords tied to the game.
