## Excerpts

> Two days after launching Total War: Shogun 2 – Complete Edition on October 13, Sega and Creative Assembly announced they were pulling it from sale and reinstating the original 2011 release, with Sega's October 15 statement saying the edition fell short of expectations and its own standards.

> Players alleged generative AI involvement, pointing to suspected AI-generated artwork and poorly-executed AI upscaling—including a samurai depicted with a sixth finger—though the accusations remain thus far unconfirmed and nowhere does Sega state whether AI tools played a part in the art.

> The Complete Edition's Steam page reportedly carried no generative-AI disclosure label despite Steam's disclosure requirement, and neither Sega nor the developers have said which AI tool, if any, was used.
