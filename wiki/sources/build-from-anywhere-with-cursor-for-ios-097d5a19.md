> Build from anywhere with Cursor for iOS — launch and manage always-on agents from anywhere by opening the Cursor mobile app, choosing a repo, and launching an agent the same way you would on the desktop app. Pick any frontier model, describe ideas out loud with voice input, and use slash commands to guide Cursor. Remote Control: take an agent running on your computer and keep directing it from your phone. Track agent status with Live Activities on the lock screen and get push notifications when an agent finishes, needs input, or is ready for review. Review demos, screenshots, logs, and diffs from your phone and leave follow-up instructions or merge the PR directly from the app.
