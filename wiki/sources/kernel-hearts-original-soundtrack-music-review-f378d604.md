> Pretty Patterns composed it, with vocal and arrangement work from vally.exe, RINO, Pernelle, and Emi Evans. The album runs 20 tracks, spanning just one hour. The reviewer describes it as synth-pop with choir vocals and a sacred feel. 'As above' is singled out as the end-credits theme sung by vally.exe. 'This OST is in the running for my top pick of 2026.' — Patrick Gann, RPGFan, October 10 2026.
