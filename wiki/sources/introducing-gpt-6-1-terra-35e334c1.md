## Excerpts

> GPT-6.1 Terra is a fast, efficient model for high-volume professional work, with strong image analysis and computer use capabilities. It is the successor to GPT-5.6 Terra.

> GPT-6.1 Terra's standard API prices are 50% lower than GPT-5.6 Terra and GPT-6.1 Sol: $1 per million input tokens, $0.05 per million cached input tokens, and $5 per million output tokens.

> On Terminal-Bench 4.0 at maximum reasoning effort, GPT-6.1 Terra completes 50.3% of tasks, more than double GPT-5.6 Terra's published score of 21.5%.

> On our internal error-inducing prompt set, the low-effort error rate dropped from 53.6% for GPT-5.6 Terra to 16.7% for GPT-6.1 Terra.

> GPT-6.1 Terra is available to all Plus, Pro, Enterprise, and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex, and to developers via the API as gpt-6.1-terra.

> GPT-6.1 Terra meets the Critical capability threshold in cybersecurity under our Preparedness Framework, and maintains the safeguards developed for GPT-6 Astra.
