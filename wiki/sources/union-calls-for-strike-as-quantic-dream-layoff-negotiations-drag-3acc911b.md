## Excerpts

> A French union group is calling for another strike against Quantic Dream as negotiations over roughly 115 layoffs drag on at the Star Wars Eclipse developer.

> Quantic Dream is a subsidiary of NetEase, a financially strong group that is able to offer decent layoff packages, but the constant quest for more stinginess leads to an absurd situation, in which the executives' offer would be less than what our comrades won at Don't Nod, a company with way less financial support.

> The number of affected Quantic Dream workers has risen to 115, representing a quarter of its workforce in France. The cuts were precipitated by the MOBA flop Spellcasters Chronicles.
