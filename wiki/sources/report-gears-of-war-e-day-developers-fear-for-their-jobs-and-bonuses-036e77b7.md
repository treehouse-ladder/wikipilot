## Excerpts

> Developers of Gears of War: E-Day have told Eurogamer they fear for their jobs as soon as the game is released.

> E-Day developers were working under the assumption of receiving a so-called 'Metacritic bonus,' a pay-out should the game achieve a given Metacritic score based on critics' reviews. However, according to the terms, to be eligible for this you still have to be working at the company at the time of the pay-out, which is expected to be 'near the end of October.'

> Staff are unhappy about it and on edge because we honestly expected the layoffs to hit us post-release, but that seems to not matter now.

> Microsoft-owned studio The Coalition has so far emerged pretty unscathed from this year's brutal rounds of Microsoft gaming layoffs, but following the loss of the game's story director, Juan Vaca, two weeks ago, and the barrage of thousands of job losses from Xbox teams this year, many more on the team feel certain their time could be up soon, too.
