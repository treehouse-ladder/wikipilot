## Excerpts

> Panache Digital Games found that 'there were indeed some early versions of assets that made their way into the prologue,' including 'some in-game portraits and external marketing assets.' The studio has explicitly stated that the Early Access version and the final full game will not include any assets generated by AI. 'We own up to this oversight and apologise for any upset caused.'
