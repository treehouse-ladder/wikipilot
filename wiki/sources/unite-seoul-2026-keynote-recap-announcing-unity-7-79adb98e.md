> Unity Neural brings neural inference into the compute pipeline via URP, including an upgraded Upscaler Framework and Neural Texture Compression.

> Neural Texture Compression uses a trained model to compress textures near-losslessly, with texture runtime memory cut by over 50% and disk size reduced by about 70%.

> Unity Neural begins rolling out in Unity 6.7, with more to come in Unity 7.
