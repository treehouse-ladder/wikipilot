## Excerpts

> A new scene-generation model will build entire editable and playable 3D scenes from a single prompt.

> Build handles gameplay mechanics, environment, characters, visual style, sound, and more—without leaving Roblox.

> Procedural Models generate parametric 3D assets from a text prompt or image, and creators can set parameters to reshape and refine with no manual modeling or regeneration required. Cube, Roblox's 3D foundation model, turns a prompt into game-ready objects, from simple props to vehicles that drive and weapons that shoot.

> Select Build features, including the ability to publish games, will be available in public alpha to users in New Zealand beginning July 28. During alpha testing, Build will be available to age-checked users 9 and older, and published games that pass safety checks will be globally available for age-checked users 16 and older.
