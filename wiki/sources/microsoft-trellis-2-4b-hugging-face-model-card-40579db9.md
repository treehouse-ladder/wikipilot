## Excerpts

> TRELLIS.2-4B is a 4B-parameter large 3D generative model developed by Microsoft Research, Tsinghua University, and the University of Science and Technology of China. It reconstructs and generates arbitrary 3D assets with complex topologies, sharp features, and full Physical-Based Rendering (PBR) materials including transparency/translucency. The model is released on Hugging Face supporting resolutions ranging from 512 cubed to 1536 cubed, available under the MIT license.
