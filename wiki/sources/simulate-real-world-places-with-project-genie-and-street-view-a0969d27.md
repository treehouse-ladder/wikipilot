## Excerpts

> We're connecting Street View to Project Genie, our general-purpose world model that can generate diverse, interactive environments.

> Tap a Maps pin to choose a place in the U.S. and optionally select a style for your world, like Desert Sands or Stone Age. Then, describe your character and Genie will use this information to create an imaginative world with its starting location tied to Street View's real-world imagery.

> Street View allows for not only simulating a world anchored to a real place, but also shifting the point of view to other types of agents, like a human or a robot.

> We're launching Street View in Genie to some Ultra users in the United States starting today, with access rolling out at scale over time.
