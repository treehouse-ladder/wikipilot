## Excerpts

> Supercell, the company known for the likes of Clash Royale and Brawl Stars, has signed a deal to acquire Merge Mansion developer Metacore. The acquisition is expected to be completed at the end of September.

> The terms of the transaction will not be disclosed. Once the deal is closed, Merge Mansion will officially become part of Supercell's live games portfolio.

> Its restructure and resulting acquisition by Supercell aims to better align with the core business, improve efficiency of operations, and respond to an increasingly competitive market environment.
