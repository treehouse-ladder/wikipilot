## Excerpts

> Xbox is planning to shut down Compulsion Games, developer of Peabody Award-winning South of Midnight and We Happy Few, according to Kotaku. The studio's LinkedIn page indicates around 90 individuals work there, though the actual total is believed to be higher.

> According to one source at the studio, Compulsion leadership is in 'negotiations' with Microsoft over the studio's fate, but the details of these negotiations have not been disclosed.

> This comes just days after Xbox CEO Asha Sharma and Microsoft Game Studios head Matt Booty revealed Xbox's recent financial and logistical struggles, amid reports that mass layoffs were expected. Xbox Game Studios head Craig Duncan and chief of staff Louise O'Connor were stepping down earlier this week.
