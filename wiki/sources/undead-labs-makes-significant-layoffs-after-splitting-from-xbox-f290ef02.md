## Excerpts

> Undead Labs has successfully divested from Xbox and is becoming an 'independent, employee-owned studio.' However, the company announced that it is laying off a 'significant number' of employees.

> Although the studio didn't specify how many roles have been cut, it confirmed some of those impacted worked on State of Decay 3. Undead Labs will now work with a new publisher to release State of Decay 3, which is still slated for a day one launch on Xbox Game Pass.

> State of Decay 3 is still in active development at Undead Labs, with a closed beta happening sometime before the end of the year.
