## Excerpts

> Nintendo has uploaded 10 tracks from Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave in a Special Release album on Nintendo Music. The 10 track special album includes songs like The Blaze - Cycle of the Sun, Legend of the Endless Flame, Hope Endures, and more.

> You need an active Nintendo Switch Online subscription to use Nintendo Music. Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave launches on September 17 for Nintendo Switch 2.

> Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Special Release album featuring 10 tracks and 30 minutes of music is now available to stream on Nintendo Music.
