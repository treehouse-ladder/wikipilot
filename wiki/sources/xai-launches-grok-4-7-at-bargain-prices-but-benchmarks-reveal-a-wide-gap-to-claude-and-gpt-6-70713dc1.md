## Excerpts

> On the independent Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (v4.3.2), which combines ten benchmarks, Grok 4.7 scores 46 and lands mid-pack, while Claude Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 lead with 53 each.

> On Terminal-Bench 4.0, Grok 4.7 hits just 26 percent, versus 60 percent for GPT-6 Astra and 55 percent for Claude Fable 5.1.

> Grok 4.7 xHigh used roughly 81,000 output tokens per Intelligence Index task, versus 36,000 for Grok 4.6 High and 27,000 for GPT-6 Astra Max. Artificial Analysis calculated about $3.74 per Intelligence Index task for Grok 4.7 xHigh and $2.73 for Grok 4.7 High.

> Grok 4.7 costs $2.00 per million input tokens and $6.00 per million output tokens, the same pricing as its predecessor Grok 4.6, and has a context window of 500k tokens.
