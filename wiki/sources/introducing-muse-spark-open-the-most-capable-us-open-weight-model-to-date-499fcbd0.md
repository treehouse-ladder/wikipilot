## Excerpts

> Muse Spark Open is the most capable US-built open-weight model to date, built for coding and AI agents.

> The model has 816 billion total parameters with 42 billion active, a 1 million token context window, and native multimodal input across text, image, video, and audio.

> The weights are available for download on Amazon SageMaker AI, Google Cloud Model Garden and Hugging Face. Hosted APIs are offered by Baseten, Fireworks AI, Groq, Modal, Nebius, OpenRouter, and Together AI.

> It runs on SGLang, vLLM, TensorRT-LLM, Hugging Face Transformers, Apple MLX, llama.cpp, and Unsloth, with optimizations for AMD, Apple, Intel, and Nvidia silicon.

> We will generally not open-source our most powerful models, so Muse Wonder Full remains closed.
