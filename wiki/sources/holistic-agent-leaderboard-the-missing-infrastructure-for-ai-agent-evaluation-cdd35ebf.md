## Excerpts

> Evaluation infrastructure for AI agents is non-standardized, prohibitively slow, and error-prone. The Holistic Agent Leaderboard (HAL) provides a standardized evaluation harness that orchestrates parallel evaluations across hundreds of VMs, reducing evaluation time from weeks to hours while eliminating common implementation bugs. It conducts three-dimensional analysis spanning models, scaffolds, and benchmarks. The harness was validated by conducting 21,730 agent rollouts across 9 models and 9 benchmarks in coding, web navigation, science, and customer service with a total cost of about 0,000. The analysis revealed surprising insights, such as higher reasoning effort reducing accuracy in the majority of runs.
