## Excerpts

> The free plan includes 100 credits per month with no credit card required. Paid tiers are Pro (1,000 credits/month), Premium (3,000), Ultra (8,000), and Studio (5,500 shared across a team).

> The official USD pricing page lists Pro at 20 USD/month and Studio at 70 USD/month; the EUR version lists Pro at 19 EUR and Studio at 67 EUR.

> All paid plans include commercial-use rights and a private license.
