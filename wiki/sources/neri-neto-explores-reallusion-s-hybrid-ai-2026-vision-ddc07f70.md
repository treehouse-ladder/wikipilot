## Excerpts

> Neri Neto is a seasoned technology journalist and skilled game programmer behind Neriverso, known for crafting engaging mechanics and contributing to captivating titles like his upcoming game, Faceless Residence. Neri's workflow using Reallusion tools centers on maximizing efficiency and quality, emphasizing how tools like iClone 8 and Character Creator 5 allow him to produce realistic characters and animations at speeds previously unattainable. With Headshot 3 for CC5 you can start with either an image, mesh, or a text prompt, and instantly generate a character from it, with all of this happening directly inside Character Creator, and you end up with a fully compatible character ready for Unreal Engine 5, while still having complete control to customize every detail. Additionally, AccuFACE 2 facial mocap plugin for iClone 8 delivers unparalleled precision in facial motion capture and lip-sync, simplifying the animation process and making it compatible with both Reallusion characters and Unreal Engine 5 MetaHumans.
