## Excerpts

> Screen Burn's iteration of Silent Hill is fantastic and proves that its shift to first-person stealth gameplay is highly effective.

> Silent Hill: Townfall expertly translates Silent Hill's uniquely disturbing brand of survival-horror to first-person.

> Silent Hill: Townfall is developed by Screen Burn Interactive (formerly known as No Code) and features a first-person perspective rather than the series' traditional third-person view. Townfall is the first mainline Silent Hill game to use a first-person perspective.

> Townfall does a remarkable job of capturing the atmosphere of a small Scottish town. You play Simon Ordell, an American who washes up on the dock of the remote fictional Scottish town of St. Amelia.
