## Excerpts

> Ubisoft is actively ramping staff off Rainbow Six Siege and several other live-service projects as part of its ongoing restructuring. Sources indicate the moves are connected to the Creative Houses / Vantage Studios reorganization rather than a plan to wind down Siege itself.

> The staffing changes at Siege come as Ubisoft restructures Ubisoft Barcelona to focus exclusively on Rainbow Six, suggesting a consolidation of Siege development into a smaller, Barcelona-centric team rather than the distributed model that existed across multiple studios.

> Other projects reportedly affected by the staff reallocation include unnamed live-service titles that did not survive the internal prioritization review under the Creative Houses model.
