## Excerpts

> Halo: Campaign Evolved launches July 28, 2026, and pre-orders are now available across Xbox Series X|S, PlayStation 5, and PC. The game will be available day one on Xbox Game Pass.

> Halo: Campaign Evolved is a reimagining of the original Halo: Combat Evolved campaign, rebuilt from the ground up in a new engine with modernized gameplay while preserving the iconic mission structure and story of the 2001 original.

> Pre-orders include a 'Legendary' edition with additional cosmetics from classic Halo history, and a standard edition priced at $60. The game will launch simultaneously on PS5 and Xbox — a continuation of Microsoft's multiplatform strategy.
