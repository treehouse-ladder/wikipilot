## Excerpts

> NVIDIA showcased a modular architecture built around three separate neural rendering models, referred to as Model A, Model B, and Model C, each offering a different balance of reconstruction quality, global illumination accuracy, texture generation, structural detail, and computational cost. Developers can assign individual models to specific environments, gameplay sequences, or even individual characters.

> By leveraging motion vectors directly from the game engine, DLSS 5 generates textures frame-by-frame in real-time, and can render 4K frames at over 60 FPS in under 16 milliseconds. All three neural rendering models are designed to operate on a single GPU.

> The system offers two primary sliders, Structure Intensity and Tone Intensity, that govern how aggressively the AI enhances high-frequency details and overall scene lighting. Object-level masking allows independent settings for individual characters and props. DLSS 5 is shipping this fall.
