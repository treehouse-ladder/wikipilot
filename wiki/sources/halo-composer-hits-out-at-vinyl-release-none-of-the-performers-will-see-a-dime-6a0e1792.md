## Excerpts

> Halo composer Martin O'Donnell hit out at a new vinyl reissue of the original trilogy's score, claiming he wasn't consulted about the release. Taking to social media after the 'Halo Original Trilogy Soundtrack Collection' was announced, O'Donnell called the news 'fascinating' before sharing a few thoughts.

> O'Donnell noted that Microsoft doesn't have the original multitrack recordings, making remastering impossible despite Microsoft's claims. Most notably, 'None of the composers or performers will see a dime' from the vinyl release, according to O'Donnell's statement on social media.

> This incident highlights ongoing payment disputes between the Halo composers and Microsoft. Marty O'Donnell and Mike Salvatori previously shared that a lawsuit between the pair and Microsoft had been resolved in April 2022, following a long-running lawsuit that was filed in 2020.
