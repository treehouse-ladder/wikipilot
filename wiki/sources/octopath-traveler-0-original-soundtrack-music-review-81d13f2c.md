## Excerpts

> The Octopath Traveler 0 Original Soundtrack released on December 10, 2025, with music composed by Yasunori Nishiki. The soundtrack consists of 2 CDs, released by Square Enix (catalog number SQEX-11181~2).

> About 40 percent of the OST is music lifted from Champions of the Continent, all found on the second of the soundtrack's two CDs. The first disc features the main theme and four elaborated versions (Fury, Sorrow, Compassion, and Requiem), plus various town and battle themes, with Requiem featuring a violin front and center with an even slower tempo.

> The soundtrack includes a full Wishvale suite, where Wishvale is the town you build from scratch in Octopath Traveler 0 with new recruits, similar to the base forts in the Suikoden series.
