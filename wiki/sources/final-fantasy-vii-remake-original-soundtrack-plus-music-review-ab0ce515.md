## Excerpts

> Aerith's theme receives a lot of treatment on this soundtrack, more so than on the Remake Original Soundtrack, and these also help to dispel the darker atmosphere of the soundtrack.

> Among the standout tracks are the two 'Shinra Combat Simulator' tracks with their calming ethereal atmosphere, the wistful electric guitar rendition of Hamauzu's 'Jesse's Theme' in 'Flying Over the Undercity,' and the humorous 'Rock that Dress, Aerith,' which refashions her theme as a short but fun rock rendition.

> All of these battle tracks are enjoyable, but they largely further the feeling that this PLUS soundtrack is redundant.
