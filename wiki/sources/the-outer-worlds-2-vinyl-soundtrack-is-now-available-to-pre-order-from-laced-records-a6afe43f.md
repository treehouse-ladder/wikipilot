## Excerpts

> Laced Records announced a 3LP vinyl set for Obsidian Entertainment's The Outer Worlds 2 (Official Game Soundtrack) release, set to ship in July 2026 and running at $62 USD. The soundtrack is available in a limited color vinyl exclusive format and a regular black vinyl release.

> The soundtrack is composed by Oleksa Lozowchuk, Antonio Gradanti, and Jim Bonney. Oleksa Lozowchuk served as principal composer and music director, in collaboration with co-composers Antonio Gradanti and Jim Bonney, with featured collaborators Interleave and Juliette Jones.

> The release features 44 songs across 3 vinyl records with each disc covering one of the major factions: Volume 001: Truth, Justice, & Dispute Resolution introduces the sound of arrival of the Earth Directorate; Volume 002: Wireless Free Arcadia represents Auntie's Choice with bright melodies, big band swing; Volume 003: Mental Enlightenment & Refreshment pairs the Protectorate and the Order of the Ascendant. The specially mastered tracks are presented in deluxe Art Nouveau-inspired packaging.
