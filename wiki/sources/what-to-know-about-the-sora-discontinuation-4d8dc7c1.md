## Excerpts

> The Sora web and app experiences were discontinued on April 26, 2026. The Sora API will be discontinued on September 24, 2026.

> OpenAI's deprecations page lists sora-2, sora-2-pro, and their dated snapshots, all with a 2026-09-24 shutdown date and no recommended replacement. OpenAI notified API developers on March 24, 2026.

> Users can export content at sora.chatgpt.com/sunset. After discontinuation and any final export window, OpenAI says it will permanently delete data tied to Sora use.

> An OpenAI support response said they haven't verified an open-weight or self-hosted Sora release and have no release plan or timeline.
