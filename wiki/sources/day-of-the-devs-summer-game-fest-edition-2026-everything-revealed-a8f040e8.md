## Excerpts

> Day of the Devs hosts a showcase around Summer Game Fest to highlight some of the wildly creative indie games in development around the world. The non-profit received over 1,700 submissions for this year's Summer Game Fest showcase, which was narrowed down to 20 titles.

> Messhof, the indie studio behind Nidhogg and Wheel World, kicked off the showcase by revealing Blood Dungeon, a fantasy dungeon-crawling platformer take on Vampire Survivors.

> Frozenbyte revealed Trine 6: Together in Time, the latest in a long-running series of co-op 2D puzzle-platformers, releasing on PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch, and Nintendo Switch 2 on September 25.

> Saibot Studios and New Blood Interactive showed off Tenebris Somnia, a survival horror game that blends pixel art action with spooky live-action FMV scenes.
