## Excerpts

> Three former BioWare developers have started an Edmonton-based indie studio called Studio Reset, founded by Kaelin Lavallee, Kris Schoneberg, and Francis Lacuna, whose collective credits include the Mass Effect trilogy, Dragon Age, Anthem, and Hinterland's The Long Dark.

> The debut project is a neon-noir supernatural mystery set in a stylized Canadian city, built around a 'Parallax Deduction' mechanic in which multiple investigators each provide a different perspective on a case — 'perspective is part of the evidence' — and a 'no moon logic' design philosophy where solutions come from observation and reasoning rather than obscure trial-and-error.

> After years spent contributing to large-scale narrative worlds, the founders are choosing a smaller, more intentional model: original IP, sustainable ambition, and experiences shaped around the players they are made for.
