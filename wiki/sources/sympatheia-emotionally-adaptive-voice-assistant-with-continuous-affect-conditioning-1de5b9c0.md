## Excerpts

> Sympatheia is a speech-to-speech dialogue framework conditioned on affect inferred from the user's speech and continuous valence-arousal control signals. The researchers constructed Sympatheia-18k, an emotion-conditioned synthetic spoken dialogue corpus with 12 emotion anchors. The system enables an emotionally adaptive voice assistant whose responses dynamically modulate vocal affect along continuous valence and arousal axes.
