## Excerpts

> GLM-5.3 uses the same base model as GLM-5.2, with all improvements driven by post-training.

> GLM-5.3 improves from 4.6 to 28.3 on Terminal-Bench 3.0, from 46.2 to 66.9 on DeepSWE v1.1, and from 23.8 to 28.5 on Agents' Last Exam.

> GLM-5.3 delivers a significant improvement in coding capabilities, achieving a 50% performance gain over GLM-5.2 on Z.ai Code Bench.

> At Max effort, GLM-5.3 reaches 34.5% at roughly 75K output tokens per task, compared with 23.4% at 96K for GLM-5.2.

> It delivers markedly stronger agentic coding results than GLM-5.2 at every effort level while consuming fewer output tokens.
