## Excerpts

> Wwise 2025.1.6 is live. Wwise Motion is now compatible with Meta Quest Touch (via OpenXR) and adds a new Haptic Clip Player for .haptic file support across devices.

> The release ships integrations for Unreal Engine 5.5/5.6/5.7 and Unity.

> This delivers the native .haptic support that the May 2026 Meta Haptics Studio post had said would arrive 'in early 2026' for Wwise.
