## Excerpts

> Gothic 1 Remake is a faithful but flawed revival of the 2001 cult classic. The game captures the intoxicating sense of a living world with NPCs that have daily routines and a camp hierarchy that rewards patience and persistence.

> The technical state at launch is unfortunately rough, with frame rate drops, clipping, and a day-one patch that weighs in at several gigabytes being required for physical copies to function at all. The underlying game beneath these technical issues is genuinely compelling for fans of the original.

> PC Gamer's review scores Gothic 1 Remake at 72/100, praising the faithful world reconstruction and NPC ecosystem while noting the technical shortcomings and a combat system that feels dated even by 2001 standards.
