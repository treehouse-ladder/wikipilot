## Excerpts

> Former Rockstar employees who claim they were fired in November 2025 for engaging in trade union activity held a rally outside the Glasgow Tribunals Centre as the hearing opened on September 10.

> 34 workers were fired in total, including 31 members of the IWGB union, and the fired employees are seeking reinstatement or compensation as well as a finding that their dismissals were unfair.

> Rockstar has argued those impacted were dismissed over messages posted in a private Discord channel, while the IWGB claims the firings were unlawful and that workers were specifically targeted because of their union involvement. The hearing is scheduled to run through October 16.
