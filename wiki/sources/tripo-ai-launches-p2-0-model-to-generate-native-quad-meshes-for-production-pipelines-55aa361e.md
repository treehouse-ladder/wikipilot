## Excerpts

> P2.0 is the first model in the industry able to natively generate quad meshes (four-sided polygons) instead of the triangulated output every AI-based 3D generator has used until now. It can generate up to 25,000 quad faces or 50,000 triangular ones.

> Following the release of P2.0 Preview in August, the official launch of P2.0 added two new features: multiple versions generation per prompt and Mesh Edit.

> A quad mesh follows the natural curves of faces and bodies more closely and is easier to deform during animation than a triangular mesh, cutting down the manual cleanup work needed before an asset goes into production.
