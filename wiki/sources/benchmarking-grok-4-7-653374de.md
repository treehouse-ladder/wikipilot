> Grok 4.7 (xhigh) scores 46 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among other reasoning models in a similar price tier (median: 24).

> Grok 4.7 scores +2 points over Grok 4.6 on the Intelligence Index, with strong performance on agentic knowledge work tasks. Grok 4.7 gains +111 Elo over Grok 4.6 (high) on AA-Briefcase, our private benchmark for long-horizon agentic knowledge work, scoring 1657 Elo and placing it just behind Claude Opus 5 and Claude Fable 5.1 at the frontier.

> Grok 4.7 (xhigh) scores 46 on the Intelligence Index using 81k output tokens per task, more than double the 38k used by Grok 4.6 (xhigh).
