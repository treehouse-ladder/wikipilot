## Excerpts

> Star Wars Zero Company is reportedly launching on August 27, 2026, for PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S with a Standard Edition priced at $49.99 on PC and $59.99 on consoles. The game is an upcoming turn-based squad tactics strategy game being developed by Bit Reactor and Respawn Entertainment, with Bit Reactor founded by many former members of Firaxis Games. Multiple heavy hitters are landing in late August / September as games try to avoid the November 19 release of GTA VI.
