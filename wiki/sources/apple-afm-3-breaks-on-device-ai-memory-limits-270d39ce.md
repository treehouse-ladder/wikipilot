## Excerpts

> AFM 3 Core Advanced is a 20-billion-parameter, natively multimodal on-device model with a sparse architecture activating 1 to 4 billion parameters per request. Weights are stored in NAND flash rather than DRAM, and routing decisions are made per prompt because NAND-to-DRAM bandwidth is too slow to swap weights token by token.
