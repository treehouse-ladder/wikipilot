## Excerpts

> SWE-1.7 is the result of broad improvements across our RL pipeline: better infrastructure, more stable training, higher-quality data, and new techniques for long-horizon tasks.

> Since SWE-1.7 was trained from a Kimi K2.7 base, which had already undergone extensive RL post-training, the large additional gains from our own training challenge the idea of a 'post-training ceiling' and suggest that RL can push capabilities much further than previously believed.

> SWE-1.7 exhibits noticeably different behavior from Kimi K2.7 Code, its base model, and is significantly more aligned and trustworthy than K2.7 or other frontier open-source models.

> SWE-1.7 completes a task on our FrontierCode benchmark for $1.97, near-frontier coding intelligence at a fraction of the cost, available in Devin at 1000 tokens per second via Cerebras.
