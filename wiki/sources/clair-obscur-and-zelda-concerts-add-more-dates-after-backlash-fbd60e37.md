## Excerpts

> Both Clair Obscur: Expedition 33's 'A Painted Symphony' orchestral concert tour and the Legend of Zelda 40th Anniversary Concert have announced additional tour dates following fan backlash over the limited number of tour stops and ticket availability.

> Clair Obscur: Expedition 33's 'A Painted Symphony' adds 24 new tour stops across Europe and North America for its 2027 leg, while the Zelda 40th Anniversary Concert adds stops in more North American cities in response to demand.

> The addition of dates to both concerts reflects a broader surge in demand for live game music performance, with franchise concert tours consistently selling out within hours of ticket release.
