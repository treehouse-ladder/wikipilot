## Excerpts

> MiniMax H3-Image: Bringing 4K Image Generation into the 10-Second Era. H3-Image can produce a 4K (4096 x 4096) image in under 10 seconds and accepts up to 16 reference images.

> The model handles text-to-image, reference-based generation, and image editing.

> H3-Image is a 33.1B-parameter diffusion transformer distilled to 6 sampling steps with no classifier-free guidance.

> H3-Image is available on the MiniMax API Platform, MiniMax Code, and Hailuo AI, and the open weights are released on Hugging Face under the MiniMax-H3-Image Community License Agreement.

> MiniMax is offering 50 percent off the standard price for image generation requests at 4K resolution and above during launch week.
