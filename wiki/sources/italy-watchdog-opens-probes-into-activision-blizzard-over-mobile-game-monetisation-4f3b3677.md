## Excerpts

> Italy's antitrust authority launched a probe into several Microsoft-owned video-game makers over their use of virtual currencies. The authority's statement said Activision Blizzard is among the targets, without naming the others.

> Purchases are often priced in virtual currencies rather than euros and may involve multiple layers of conversion, making it harder for consumers to understand how much they are spending.

> The watchdogs will also determine whether there is a widespread infringement of EU consumer protection rules affecting consumers in at least three EU member states.

> The network launched actions last month against nine video-game companies, including Riot Games, Ubisoft and Supercell.

> Italy opened separate national probes in December into Diablo Immortal and Call of Duty Mobile, with the watchdog saying parental controls for the games appear to default to a less restrictive version that enables minors to make in-game purchases.
