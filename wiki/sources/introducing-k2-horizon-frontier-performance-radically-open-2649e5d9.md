## Excerpts

> IFM is releasing K2 Horizon, a connected fleet of six models: 375B-A23B, 36B-A4B, 32B, 7B, 3.7B, and 0.9B. K2 Horizon represents the largest fully open-source model launch in AI history.

> It is the first fully open model fleet for agents and is the first open model family to expose the complete development process through agentic post-training.

> For every model, IFM is opening the training lifecycle from pretraining through reasoning and agentic post-training. They are releasing intermediate checkpoints, training data or detailed data-construction recipes, open architecture, mixture compositions, training code, configurations, fine-grained logs, evaluation results, and final weights. The models and code are released under the Apache 2.0 license.

> The 375B (23B active) is K2 Horizon's flagship model, built to compete with open-weight models on reasoning and agentic work, and ready for the enterprise.
