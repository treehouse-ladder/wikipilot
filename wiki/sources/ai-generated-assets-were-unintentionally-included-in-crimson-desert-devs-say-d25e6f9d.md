## Excerpts

> In a statement, Pearl Abyss said that some 2D visual props were created as part of early-stage iteration using experimental AI generative tools, and that some of these assets were unintentionally included in the final release.

> We should have clearly disclosed our use of AI, the developer said, adding that it is conducting a comprehensive audit of all in-game assets and has added an AI use disclosure section to Crimson Desert's Steam page.

> The admission drew comparisons to Clair Obscur: Expedition 33, which was stripped of the Indie Game of the Year and Debut Game awards it won at The Indie Game Awards 2025 after a similar disclosure that generative AI had been used.
