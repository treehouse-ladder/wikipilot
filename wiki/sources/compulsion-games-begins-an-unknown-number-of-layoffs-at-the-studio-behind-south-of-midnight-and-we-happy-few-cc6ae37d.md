## Excerpts

> On June 25, 2026, layoffs had started at Compulsion Games, the team responsible for developing South of Midnight. Several current and former Compulsion Games employees have updated LinkedIn profiles or posted messages indicating they are available for work, suggesting the studio has begun reducing staff.

> Microsoft hasn't officially confirmed layoffs at Compulsion Games or disclosed how many employees may have been affected. Until the company comments publicly, the exact scale of the reductions remains unclear.

> Alongside Compulsion, multiple other studios including Double Fine and Ninja Theory are also reportedly in negotiations with Xbox to get their studios out from under Microsoft's ownership to avoid complete closure.
