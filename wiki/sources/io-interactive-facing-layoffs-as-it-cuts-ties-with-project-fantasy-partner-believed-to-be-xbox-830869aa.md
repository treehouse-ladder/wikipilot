## Excerpts

> IO Interactive, the studio behind Hitman and 007 First Light, is facing layoffs after the external partner on its online RPG Project Fantasy ended the relationship. A Bloomberg report confirmed Xbox as Project Fantasy's publisher.

> Xbox had signed on as the publisher for Project Fantasy in 2023. IO Interactive says it will continue to develop the game, but will have to find other funding or self-publish.
