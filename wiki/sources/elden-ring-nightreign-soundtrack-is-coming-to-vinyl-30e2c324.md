## Excerpts

> The epic, orchestral soundtrack to 'Elden Ring: Nightreign' is coming to vinyl for the first time as a 7LP set.

> The Elden Ring Nightreign: The Vinyl Collection (Limited Edition) features 57 tracks heard throughout the Limveld map, as well as further tracks taken from The Forsaken Hollows DLC. This exclusive set gives you the chance to dive into your own expedition at any time with over 3 hours of orchestral music.

> The set will be limited to 4999 copies.
