## Excerpts

> Warren Spector, the veteran game developer who worked on the likes of System Shock, Deus Ex and most recently Thick as Thieves, is retiring after 43 years. Spector shared the news via LinkedIn, where his profile now shows 'retired' as his current job.

> He cited his age, health issues, and how much the industry has changed, adding that the business is 'just not as much fun for me anymore.' Now 70, he helped shape the immersive sim around player choice, interconnected systems, and open-ended problem solving.

> In his career he shipped 17 full games and around 'nine or so' add-on packs. Spector mentioned plans to 'write some books, do a lot of reading, do some lecturing, maybe do some consulting.'
