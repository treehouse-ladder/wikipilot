> A group of investors including Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund has secured EU antitrust approval for its $55 billion acquisition of video game developer Electronic Arts, the European Commission said on July 23, 2026.

> The Commission said the acquisition would not raise competition concerns. The EU executive is also scrutinising the deal under its Foreign Subsidies Regulation, seen as a bigger hurdle, with a decision due by July 30.
