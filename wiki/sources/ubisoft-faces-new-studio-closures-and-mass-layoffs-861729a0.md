> Ubisoft is cutting up to 380 employees across the company, as part of broader restructuring. The company is instituting cuts across its Global Publishing division and seeking a reorganization of its Barcelona studio to focus exclusively on Rainbow Six projects. Ubisoft Winnipeg and Ubisoft Belgrade are being shuttered.
