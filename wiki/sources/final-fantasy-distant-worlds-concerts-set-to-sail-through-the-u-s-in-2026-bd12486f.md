## Excerpts

> Presented by AWR Music Productions and led by renowned conductor Arnie Roth, Distant Worlds has become one of the most enduring and respected video game concert tours since its debut in 2007.

> On June 12 and 13, 2026, Distant Worlds returns to the famed Carnegie Hall in New York City for "Celebrating Nobuo Uematsu!," a special concert event featuring beloved Final Fantasy music compositions chosen by Nobuo Uematsu himself. Nobuo Uematsu will be in attendance while Arnie Roth conducts, and vocalists RIKKI (Final Fantasy X) and Amanda Achen (Final Fantasy XIV) also make appearances.

> The series spans music from across the Final Fantasy franchise, spotlighting themes by legendary composers such as Nobuo Uematsu, Masayoshi Soken, Yoko Shimomura, Masashi Hamauzu, and others.
