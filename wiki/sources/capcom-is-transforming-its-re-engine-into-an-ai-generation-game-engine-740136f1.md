## Excerpts

> Capcom is planning to evolve its proprietary RE Engine into what programmer Satoshi Ishida calls an "AI-generation game engine," detailing the plan under a project named REX at the Capcom Open Conference RE:2026.

> The REX upgrade is being applied in stages, so developers don't have to rebuild their projects from scratch, and it targets performance, development tools, efficiency, and QA at the same time.

> Capcom has reiterated that it will not use AI-generated assets in its games, with graphics, music, and in-game dialogue remaining the work of human developers.
