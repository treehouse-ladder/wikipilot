## Excerpts

> Rockstar Games have announced French electronic music duo Justice as composers of Grand Theft Auto VI's original dynamic score.

> Rockstar has said that more details about the music of Grand Theft Auto VI, including the dynamic score, the next evolution of in-game radio, will be revealed in the coming months.

> The announcement came with the new single "Flower in a Hurricane," which is featured in the official launch trailer for the game. A separate album, 'GTA VI: The Album' will consist of 34 new, original tracks, due 19 November 2026, the same day as the game.
