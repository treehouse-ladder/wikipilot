## Excerpts

> The Game Music Festival has confirmed its participation in SXSW London 2026, scheduling a session titled From Sanctuary to the Stage: How Game Soundtracks Immerse Audiences Onscreen and Onstage, which will be held on 5 June 2026 at the Shoreditch Town Hall Assembly Hall in East London.

> The lineup of speakers includes Blizzard Entertainment Music Director Derek Duke, who has worked on titles such as Diablo and Overwatch, alongside Diablo IV Lead Composer Ted Reedy.

> The Game Music Festival is also hosting The Infernal Symphony, a concert celebrating the 30th anniversary of the Diablo franchise, which is set for June 6, 2026 at the Royal Festival Hall at the Southbank Centre, acting as the opening event for the festival.
