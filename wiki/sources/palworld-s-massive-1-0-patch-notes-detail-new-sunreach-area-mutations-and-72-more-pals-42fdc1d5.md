## Excerpts

> Palworld 1.0 adds 72 new Pals to the world, bringing the total number of Pals up to an incredible 287.

> The major highlights include updated story missions, a brand-new area to explore, revamped settlements, two new mechanics, and 72 more Pals to add to your collection.

> The player cap has also been raised from 65 to 80, and new equipment can be unlocked. This includes a new glider type and the Plasma Multicutter.
