## Excerpts

> Gemini 3.8 Flash (high) scores 59 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, up 3 points from Gemini 3.7 Flash. With medium reasoning it scores 57, and with low reasoning it scores 52.

> The 3 point improvement is primarily driven by stronger performance on agentic evaluations such as tau-3-Banking (tool use), Terminal-Bench v2.1 (coding) and GDPval-AA v2 (real-world tasks).

> Google has released Gemini 3.8 Flash, its fourth Flash model in under four months.
