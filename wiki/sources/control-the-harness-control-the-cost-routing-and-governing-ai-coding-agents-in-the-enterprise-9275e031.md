## Excerpts

> A harness decides which model answers, what the model reads, how the prompt cache is used and which subagents run, so it picks the rate on the price sheet and sets the volume bought at it.

> We built Jev, a fast, customizable router, a classifier with calibrated probabilities, that labels every prompt against a bring-your-own taxonomy of agentic requests.

> The router moves work only where no running conversation has to rebuild its cache: at session start, in side lanes and at subagent launch.

> On long tool-heavy sessions the highest-priced model costs less than the next tier, as repricing about 10,000 real sessions from public datasets confirms.

> In an emulated enterprise of 10,000 seats with user behaviour taken from these datasets, the router recovers 14 to 21% of model spend at Anthropic's list prices of 21 September 2026, $3.3M to $5.0M a year.
