## Excerpts

> > Cosmos 3 represents a major leap forward in world foundation models (WFMs) for physical AI: a single, unified omni-model that combines world generation, physical reasoning, and action generation in one model. No more juggling between different models and inference pipelines - Cosmos 3 does it all. By supporting highly flexible input-output configurations, Cosmos 3 seamlessly unifies critical modalities for Physical AI — effectively subsuming vision-language models, video generators, world simulators, and world-action models into a single framework.
