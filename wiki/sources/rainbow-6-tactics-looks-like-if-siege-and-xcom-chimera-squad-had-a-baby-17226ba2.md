## Excerpts

> During Gamescom Opening Night Live 2026, Ubisoft announced Rainbow Six Tactics, a turn-based single-player tactical game set in the Rainbow Six Siege universe. It is the first single-player Rainbow Six game in 19 years.

> Tom Clancy's Rainbow Six Tactics will feature 15 operators first seen in Rainbow Six Siege, including fan favorites like Doc and Ash. Players take on the role of Rainbow Six Vegas protagonist Paul Bishop across a series of missions spanning over 45 handcrafted maps and 90+ minutes of cutscenes.

> The game is being developed by Ubisoft Paris, the studio that previously brought us the Mario + Rabbids tactical games. Ubisoft confirmed there will be no in-game store or microtransactions. Rainbow Six Tactics will launch on PC, Xbox, and PS5 in 2027.
