## Excerpts

> Heart Machine, the studio behind the critically acclaimed action-RPG Hyper Light Drifter, is at risk of shutting down after a recent publishing deal fell through. Studio head Alx Preston announced on September 16 that the publisher decided to no longer move forward with an unannounced title they had been funding, and without this key income for the studio, nearly everyone at the studio had to be laid off.

> Preston said the studio will 'keep pushing to find a path,' but also warned that at this point, he's 'not sure yet what's next for Heart Machine to survive.'

> This recent collapse wasn't the first blow to the studio. Layoffs at Heart Machine began last fall, and a short-lived pivot to Patreon shut down earlier this year. The studio's recent projects—2024's Hyper Light Breaker, a loot-based roguelite action-RPG, and last year's Possessor(s), a side-scrolling Metroidvania—didn't perform well.
