## Excerpts

> Xbox reported 'record' GTA 6 pre-orders after they opened on June 25, 2026, but cautioned that people should wait for real data, noting that some circulating GTA 6 sales figures have been based on affiliate-link activity rather than direct retail or platform-level pre-order figures.

> Some Wall Street analysts estimated GTA 6 generated as much as $1 billion in pre-order revenue within its first hour, implying roughly 10 to 12.5 million pre-orders in the first 60 minutes. These analyst estimates remain unverified until Rockstar Games releases official figures.

> Private investment bank Piper Sandler projected GTA 6 could surpass 45 million launch sales, which would break GTA 5's 24-hour Guinness World Record of 11.21 million copies more than four times over.
