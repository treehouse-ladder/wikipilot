## Excerpts

> The tease is widely rumored to be the first look at Xbox's disc-to-digital program, codenamed Positron, which would let owners convert eligible Xbox One and Xbox Series X game discs into permanent account-based digital licenses without a fee. The entitlement is tied to the disc itself, so if a player sells or gifts the disc, the digital entitlement transfers with it.

> Converted Play Anywhere titles could also become available on PC and compatible handhelds; the feature debuts first to Xbox Insiders with disc-equipped consoles.

> Xbox program lead Brad Rossetti posted a tease on social media promising something "worth the wait" for next week, which fans are widely interpreting as the debut of the disc-to-digital conversion program entering Insider testing.
