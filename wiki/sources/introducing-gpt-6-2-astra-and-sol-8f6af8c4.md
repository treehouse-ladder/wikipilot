> We're introducing GPT-6.2 Astra, our new frontier model for your hardest projects, and GPT-6.2 Sol, our efficient, versatile model for everyday work.

> GPT-6.2 Astra is priced at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens. GPT-6.2 Sol keeps GPT-6.1 Sol's API token prices of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens.

> GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol are rolling out to Plus, Pro, Enterprise, and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex, and are available to developers through the OpenAI API as gpt-6.2-astra and gpt-6.2-sol, with Ultrafast available for both models at launch.
