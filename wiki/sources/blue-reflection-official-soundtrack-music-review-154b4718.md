## Excerpts

> The stellar music for Blue Reflection was composed by Hayato Asano, who is also known for his work on many of the recent Atelier games.

> Composer Hayato Asano brings an incredibly varied soundtrack in which he draws from decades of electronic music to create a collection of pieces that are thumping, atmospheric, and sometimes experimental. It's easily one of my favorite soundtracks of the year, and demands to be heard.

> The Blue Reflection OST represents the pinnacle of artcore—an experimental and eclectic subgenre of electronic music based on Japanese electronic hardcore. The Blue Reflection Official Soundtrack is split across three discs and contains remixes and unreleased demo versions.
