## Excerpts

> The soundtrack comprises 70 total tracks, with the first 16 being collectible records featuring lyrical ballads plus remixed Golden versions. The opener Feel is described as rain-soaked smooth jazz, properly setting the tone that marks Lies of P apart from its fellow action RPGs.

> As of 2025, 51 new tracks from the Overture DLC were freely added to digital copies of the original soundtrack.

> The collectible records in the hub world appear to be a major highlight of the soundtrack for the reviewer. The reviewer's favorite tracks include Quixotic, Proposal, Flower, Wolf Part 1, and Fascination.
