## Excerpts

> Obsidian Entertainment has canceled multiple projects, including a planned sequel to Avowed, and laid off a quarter of its staff as it starts work on a new game in the Fallout series.

> The new Fallout title will be led by Obsidian studio design director Josh Sawyer, who previously directed Fallout: New Vegas, the only entry in the Bethesda Fallout universe not developed by Bethesda itself.

> According to Bloomberg's Jason Schreier, the canceled Avowed sequel was going well and on track to be announced within the next year, but ultimately did not fit into Xbox CEO Asha Sharma's overall strategy. A WARN notice issued in California indicates 52 employees were let go from the Irvine-based developer.
