## Excerpts

> The game's lofi soundtrack from Minecraft composer C418 will be available as a 2LP set pressed on sunny yellow vinyl, while the animal-themed songs that make up Wanderstop's in-game radio are also being released on seed pink vinyl. The vinyl sets will ship August 28, 2026.

> Both vinyl sets are priced at $42.99 and feature new artwork from Nat F. The vinyl is being released through iam8bit in partnership with Annapurna Interactive and Ivy Road.

> The album displays both old C418 sensibilities and delicate moments that remind listeners of works from Brian Eno or Philip Glass.
