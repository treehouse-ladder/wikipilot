## Excerpts

> With Scott Pilgrim EX, the series returns to the beat 'em up genre and tags Anamanaguchi back in for their biggest, most sonically expansive project yet.

> The soundtrack features 71 tracks across an hour and a half, with each screen and shop having its own short looping track; most tracks loop in under a minute.

> 'One More Summer,' a bright track for the first main quest area, fills the void left since 'Another Winter' from Scott Pilgrim vs. the World: The Game, and 'United by Fate' is a triumphant culmination harkening to Anamanaguchi's joyful nostalgia.

> The soundtrack delivers a 90-minute medley of more diverse emotions and sound palettes than seen in their game soundtrack work before.
