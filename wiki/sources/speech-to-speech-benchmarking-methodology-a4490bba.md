## Excerpts

> The Artificial Analysis Speech to Speech Index is an equal-weighted score for native audio models combining results across Speech Reasoning, Agentic Performance, Arena Preference, and Task Success Rate.

> The Speech Agent Arena is a blind preference benchmark that evaluates which native audio model participants prefer in live voice conversations, complementing automated benchmarks by measuring the end-to-end conversational experience on realistic tasks.

> We released Big Bench Audio, a new dataset for benchmarking the performance of native audio models containing 1,000 audio files representing questions designed to test the intelligence of models, based on four categories of the Big Bench Hard dataset and generated using 23 synthetic voices.
