## Excerpts

> After a long period without clear answers, we have received confirmation that Spiders is being liquidated.

> GreedFall and The Technomancer developer Spiders has closed down. The Parisian studio and parent company Nacon filed for insolvency earlier this year after the latter missed a loan repayment.

> The developer has been in operation for 18 years but owner Nacon has been unable to find a buyer for the developer and so they are shutting down.
