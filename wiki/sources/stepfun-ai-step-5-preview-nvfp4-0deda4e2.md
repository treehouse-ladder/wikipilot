> Step 5 Preview is StepFun's flagship model for agentic work. It delivers frontier-level performance across software engineering and professional knowledge work, with particular strength in finance. Step 5 Preview is a sparse Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) model with 600B total parameters and 27B active parameters. It supports up to 1 million tokens of context and accepts text, images, and video, enabling sustained reasoning and tool use across complex tasks.
