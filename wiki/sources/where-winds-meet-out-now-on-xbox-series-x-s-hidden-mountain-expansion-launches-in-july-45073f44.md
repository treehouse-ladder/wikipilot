## Excerpts

> Where Winds Meet officially arrives on Xbox Series X|S, Xbox on PC, and Xbox Cloud on June 8, 2026, fully free-to-play via the Microsoft Store and supporting cross-progression with PC and PS5 through NetEase account linking. The Xbox release was shadow-dropped during the Xbox Games Showcase 2026. The game has already attracted more than 80 million players across other platforms since its debut on PC and PS5 in November 2025 before expanding to iOS and Android devices a month later. The studio also unveiled the upcoming third major expansion, 'Hidden Mountain,' planned to release in July 2026, introducing a new multi-layered region, the secretive Mohist Sect faction, and a new Hand Guard Discipline combat style.
