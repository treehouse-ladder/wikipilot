## Excerpts

> Ward announced the news to Savvy employees in a message stating that it is the right time for new leadership as the company embarks on its next period of transformational growth.

> He is being temporarily replaced by Turqi Alnowaiser, deputy governor of Savvy Game Group's parent organization (the Saudi Arabia Public Investment Fund), who also heads the PIF's international investments division.

> Ward became CEO of Savvy Games Group in 2021 and oversaw a $37.8 billion spree of international investments and acquisitions in game companies, including its $4.9 billion purchase of Scopely and $3.5 billion acquisition of Niantic.
