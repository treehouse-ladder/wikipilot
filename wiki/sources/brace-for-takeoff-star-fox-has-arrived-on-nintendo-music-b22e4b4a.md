## Excerpts

> The soundtrack to the 2026 Star Fox remake is now available on Nintendo Music, with an ambitious symphonic score led by composer Stephen Barton.

> The soundtrack includes all 62 tracks. The score was tracked at London's historic Abbey Road Studios and features reimagined versions of classic tracks from the original 1997 game.
