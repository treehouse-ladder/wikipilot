## Excerpts

> CD Projekt Red announced The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Songs of the Past is coming in 2027. This new Witcher 3 expansion is coming 12 years after the game's release.

> CDPR said that story content is being co-developed with fellow Polish developer Fool's Theory, which is the same studio that is currently working on a remake of The Witcher.

> Songs of the Past will arrive at some point in 2027 on PC, Xbox Series X|S and PS5. The new Songs of the Past DLC will reportedly bridge the story gap between The Wild Hunt and The Witcher 4.
