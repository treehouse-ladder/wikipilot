## Excerpts

> We're introducing Clef and Clef-flash, our open-source decision models hosted on Workers AI, and fully open-sourced on Hugging Face under an Apache 2.0 license.

> A decision model produces bounded, structured outputs cheaply, quickly, and consistently that can be added into a workflow when a decision is required. Clef is currently the leader on the Jev Decision Index.

> Clef has a 64k context window compared to Jev's 32k, and Clef has a vision encoder so it is able to take in images and classify visual content.

> Clef's median decision latency is 38.8 ms against Jev's 524.1 ms.

> Clef is Jev-API compatible. We are also offering fine-tuning services, first as a hands-on partner with our forward-deployed engineer team, and later as a self-serve fine-tuning platform for customers.
