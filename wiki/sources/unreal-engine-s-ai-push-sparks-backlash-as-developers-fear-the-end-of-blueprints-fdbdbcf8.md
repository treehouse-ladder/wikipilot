## Excerpts

> Verse is set to become the primary way to script gameplay in Unreal Engine 6, putting Blueprints — the node-based visual scripting that made Unreal accessible to designers, technical artists, and solo developers without C++ — on a deprecation path.

> Critics argue that embedding AI tools so deeply into Unreal Engine inevitably nudges studios toward adoption and the eventual dropping of Blueprints, especially when teams come under pressure to iterate faster and produce more content on lower budgets.

> Epic was explicit that Actors and Blueprints will still be present in early versions of Unreal Engine 6, and will only be deprecated once the new Verse-based framework is sufficiently mature.
