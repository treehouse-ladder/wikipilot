## Excerpts

> Laid-off Ubisoft workers in Halifax voted overwhelmingly in favour of a settlement with the company. The Game and Media Workers Guild of Canada (CWA Canada Local 30111) negotiated the deal for its members who lost their jobs when Ubisoft closed its Halifax studio in January, affecting 71 employees including 61 union members — a closure that came just weeks after those employees voted to unionize.

> Ubisoft maintained the closure was part of cost-cutting and not related to unionization.
