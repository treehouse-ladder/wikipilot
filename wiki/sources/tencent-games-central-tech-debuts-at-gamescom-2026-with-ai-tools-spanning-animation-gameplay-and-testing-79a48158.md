## Excerpts

> Motus is Tencent Games' end-to-end AI pipeline for 3D character animation, built around the company's proprietary generative animation foundation models, connecting production stages including character rigging, skinning, motion generation, motion refinement, and real-time character interaction.

> Tencent says its refinement tools can automatically identify and correct problems such as jitter, mesh penetration, joint anomalies, and foot sliding.

> Motus technologies have already been used across Game for Peace, PUBG MOBILE, Delta Force, Honor of Kings, Roco Kingdom, League of Legends, and Teamfight Tactics.

> Tencent Games Central Tech also presented TG-PlayTest, an autonomous AI playtesting system that simulates human player behaviors across diverse skill levels and playstyles, generating structured bug reports and gameplay quality metrics without requiring manual tester sessions.

> The company's GameGenius platform, a large language model fine-tuned for game logic and mechanics, powers in-game adaptive difficulty tuning and real-time narrative branching in several of Tencent's live-service titles.
