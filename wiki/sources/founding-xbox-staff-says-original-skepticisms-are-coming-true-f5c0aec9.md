## Excerpts

> Laura Fryer, one of the founding team members who helped build the original Xbox in the late 1990s, says that Xbox appears to be in a state of collapse and that her original skepticisms about Microsoft entering the hardware market are coming true 25 years later. Fryer believes that a company structured like Microsoft is bound to struggle under current conditions, including the AI-fueled hardware crunch, cannibalized game sales from Game Pass, and rapid studio expansion that has put more of the industry at jeopardy.
