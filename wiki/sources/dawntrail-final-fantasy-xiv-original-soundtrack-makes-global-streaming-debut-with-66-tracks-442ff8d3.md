## Excerpts

> Square Enix has officially released the complete 66-track Dawntrail: Final Fantasy XIV Original Soundtrack across all major digital streaming services worldwide, bringing the rich auditory world of the game's latest expansion to listeners everywhere.

> The soundtrack spans from the uplifting main theme 'Dawntrail' to vibrant area themes, pulse-pounding trial boss tracks, and the high-octane battle music from the raid dungeon The Arcadion: AAC Light Heavyweight, composed by Masayoshi Soken.

> Square Enix Music is hosting a Dawntrail Streaming Celebration: FFXIV OST Share Campaign on its official Discord server, running through September 10th.
