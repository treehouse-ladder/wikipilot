## Excerpts

> Metacore is planning a restructuring to 'better align with the core business, improve efficiency of operations, and respond to an increasingly competitive market environment.'

> If the plans are realized, the company would 'consider reducing up to 160 roles' in the country.

> As part of what Metacore calls 'its next phase,' Supercell will acquire 'the rest of Metacore.'

> The decision is based on the fact that Merge Mansion's growth has 'plateaued over the past few years' since its 2020 launch, while Metacore's new investments 'have not succeeded' in launching new games globally.
