## Excerpts

> Krafton announced PUBG Ally, an innovative CPC (Co-Playable Character) enabled by an on-device SLM (Small Language Model) built with NVIDIA ACE technology, an interactive PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS character equipped with the ability to communicate and cooperate with players.

> The system enables voice-based interaction, is designed for in-game dialogue, understands PUBG terminology and maps, and operates entirely on-device with extremely low latency. It supports English, Korean, and Chinese.

> Players can experience a test version of the AI teammate for the first time via PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS Arcade in the first half of 2026, initially aimed at English, Korean, and Chinese-speaking audiences to gather feedback and refine interaction mechanics.

> PUBG Ally can discuss strategies with players and adapt its playstyle accordingly, can bring requested items, assist in reviving teammates, and make independent decisions during looting, combat, and survival situations.
