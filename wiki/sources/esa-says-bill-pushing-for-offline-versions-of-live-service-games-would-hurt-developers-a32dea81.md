## Excerpts

> The Entertainment Software Association is opposing California Assembly Bill 1921, new legislation that would require developers of online games shutting down to give 60 days' notice, offer refunds, and make their games playable offline — stipulations that generally align with the Stop Killing Games movement. The ESA argues the bill would make games 'prohibitively expensive to create' and would hurt developers. AB 1921 has not yet been signed into law; it would take effect January 1, 2027.
