## Excerpts

> Ilari Kuittinen, co-founder and CEO of PlayStation-owned game studio Housemarque, has announced his exit from the company after 31 years of game development.

> One major stabilizing move was the appointment of Sami Haxu Hakala as Housemarque managing director. Sony purchased Housemarque in 2021, and it's since released Returnal and Saros under that banner.

> Kuittinen joins other studio heads and co-founders in retiring after decades-long runs, and is taking a rare and well-deserved breather.
