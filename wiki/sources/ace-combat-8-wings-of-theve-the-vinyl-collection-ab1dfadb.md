## Excerpts

> The collection includes two vinyl records with a selection of 15 tracks from the game, as well as a copy of Aces At War: A History 2029.

> The collection is included with all purchases of the Joker Flight Pack edition of Wings of Theve but can also be purchased standalone.

> 2LP on white colored vinyl housed in a gatefold jacket with game art within an individually numbered box set, including a felt slipmat and a 200-page artbook, priced at 80 EUR/USD and released on or around October 2, 2026.
