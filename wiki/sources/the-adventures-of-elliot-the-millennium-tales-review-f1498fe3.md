## Excerpts

> HD-2D graphics are compelling, soundtrack as good as the game looks, fun time-travelling escapades. Composers Tomohiro Nakamachi and Yuto Moritani deliver a score that captures the soul of each time period magnificently, from the despair of the Age of Reconstruction to the wonder of the Age of Magic, and the heroic grandeur of Elliot's travels overall.
