## Excerpts

> At its annual Roblox Developer Conference (RDC), the company announced several new features, including new game-creation tools, expanded NPC (non-player character) capabilities, and the ability to make games available across platforms, including the web.

> The feature called "Build" lets Roblox users create games using natural language prompts. Powered by a broad set of AI models, including both open-source and proprietary Roblox models, Build handles gameplay mechanics, environment, characters, visual style, sound, and more.

> Roblox also previewed Scene Generator, an AI tool expected to arrive later in 2026. Scene Generator will allow creators to provide a text prompt and a reference image.

> Creators can release games as standalone apps powered by its engine on mobile, PC, consoles and web.
