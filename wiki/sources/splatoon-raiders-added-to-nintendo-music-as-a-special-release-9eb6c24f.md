## Excerpts

> Nintendo has hosted nine previews of the Splatoon Raiders soundtrack on Nintendo Music, a 'special release' of 9 tracks with a combined runtime of around 30 minutes, timed to the game's Switch 2 launch on July 23, 2026.

> The Splatoon Raiders music carries the franchise's signature sound, with upbeat, funky tunes and that signature musical quirkiness. A Nintendo Switch Online subscription is required to stream them.
