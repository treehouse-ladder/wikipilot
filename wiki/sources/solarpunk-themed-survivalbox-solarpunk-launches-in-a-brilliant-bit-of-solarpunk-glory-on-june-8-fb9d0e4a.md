## Excerpts

> Solarpunk launches June 8, 2026 across PC (Steam/EGS/GOG), PS5, Xbox Series X|S and Nintendo Switch 2 — and launches day-one into Xbox Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass. The game surpassed 1 million Steam wishlists and saw over 500,000 demo downloads during Steam Next Fest. The chill sandbox places you on floating islands you tame with buildings, farms, and craft projects before sailing an airship to other frontiers; supports cooperative multiplayer for up to four players, each with their own inventory and airship.
