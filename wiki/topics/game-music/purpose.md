# Game music — topic charter

This file is **HUMAN-OWNED** (per the file ownership matrix in `CLAUDE.md`).
The synthesis page for this topic is [[game-music]].

## Cross-cutting bar (applies first)

See `CLAUDE.md` "Cross-cutting relevance criteria" for the meta-bar:
**highly relevant**, **highly innovative**, or **directly impacts/improves
agentic workflow OR video game development**. This is a low-natural-flow
topic — the safety cap of 20 sources/run will rarely engage. Bias toward
inclusion still holds: a single high-quality piece per week is the right
shape, but if the researcher finds 8 in a productive week, ingest all 8.

## What this topic is

The state of game music as a craft and as an industry: composers, scores,
adaptive-music technology, live performance, criticism. Distinct from
the general games industry coverage in `games-of-note` (a soundtrack
release belongs here; the game's gameplay belongs there).

## In scope

- Notable composers and their works (commissions, releases,
  retrospectives).
- Soundtrack releases (vinyl, streaming, OST drops paired with game
  launches).
- Adaptive / interactive music technology — Wwise, FMOD, proprietary
  systems used by major studios; horizontal resequencing, vertical
  remixing, stinger systems.
- Live concerts and performance series (Distant Worlds, Video Games
  Live, dedicated composer-led concerts).
- Notable music criticism (deep-dives on a score's structure, motif
  analysis, leitmotif usage).
- Notable music tooling/middleware in the game-audio pipeline.

## Out of scope

- General games coverage (gameplay, sales, awards) — that's
  `games-of-note`. A composer's name will appear here; the GOTY race
  appears there.
- General music industry coverage not games-related (pop releases, etc.).
- Live concerts of music not originally written for games.

## Counter-arguments to actively look for

The researcher MUST attempt to find at least one counter-argument or data
gap per non-trivial claim, OR add the divergence sentinel. Examples:

- "Composer X's score elevates the game" → look for player feedback that
  the music feels disconnected.
- "Adaptive music tech Y is the standard" → look for studios using
  alternatives.

## Source quality bar

Prefer composer interviews, GDC audio talks, label-published OST drops,
and dedicated game-music outlets (VGM Online, RPGFan music section,
GameMusic.tools). Reject general-news outlets when they cover game
music shallowly (those cover release-day; the deep coverage is in the
specialist outlets).

## Voice

Music-literate. Use composition vocabulary (motif, ostinato, ground
bass, etc.) without apology — the wiki is for someone who cares
enough to search.

## Citation discipline reminder

Every claim MUST be backed by a wikilink to a source page and a `>` quote.
