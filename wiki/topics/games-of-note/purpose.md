# Games of note — topic charter

This file is **HUMAN-OWNED** (per the file ownership matrix in `CLAUDE.md`).
The synthesis page for this topic is [[games-of-note]].

## Cross-cutting bar (applies first)

See `CLAUDE.md` "Cross-cutting relevance criteria" for the meta-bar:
**highly relevant**, **highly innovative**, or **directly impacts/improves
agentic workflow OR video game development**. Any one suffices. Game
development is one of the user's two anchor domains; "highly innovative"
is the most common path to inclusion here (a notable mechanical or
design innovation worth tracking).

## What this topic is

A curated record of **notable games and games-industry events** — what
shipped that's worth remembering, what's being argued about, where the
industry is moving. NOT a games-news aggregator (the allowlist is
deliberately broad to catch industry-specific outlets, but the
*inclusion bar* is "notable", not "released").

## In scope

- Notable AAA + indie releases (mechanic-, narrative-, or
  commercially-notable).
- Studio news: acquisitions, closures, notable hires/departures, layoffs.
- Awards: Game Awards, BAFTAs, IGF, Steam Awards — winners and
  nomination rationale.
- Genre revivals (immersive sims, RTS, CRPG, Metroidvania waves).
- Significant DLC/expansions (Phantom Liberty-tier, not cosmetic packs).
- Commercial-vs-critical disconnects worth analyzing.
- Postmortems (live-service shutdowns, GaaS failures, indie breakouts).

## Out of scope

- Game music — that's `game-music`. Mention soundtracks in passing here
  only when central to a release's identity.
- Routine patch notes and balance updates.
- Reviews of every release — only NOTABLE.
- Pre-release marketing and trailer drops without substance.
- Esports tournament results.

## Counter-arguments to actively look for

The researcher MUST attempt to find at least one counter-argument or data
gap per non-trivial claim, OR add the divergence sentinel. Examples:

- "Game X is a critical hit" → look for skeptical reviews; note any
  controversy.
- "Studio Y is in trouble" → look for counter-evidence (recent successful
  release, healthy bookings).
- "Live-service is dead" → look for live-service successes from the same
  quarter.

## Source quality bar

Allowlist is deliberately broad (this is a noisier domain than the
others). Prefer outlets known for analysis (Game Developer / Polygon
features / RPS deep-dives / Eurogamer Saturday pieces) over fast-news
outlets when the choice exists.

## Voice

Knowledgeable. Avoid hype. Note critical reception both for and against.
Cite reviewers (with handles or names) when summarizing critical
consensus.

## Citation discipline reminder

Every claim MUST be backed by a wikilink to a source page and a `>` quote.
