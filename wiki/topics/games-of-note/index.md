## Summary

**Industry contraction continues into 2026.** Sony shuttered Bluepoint Games in February 2026 — five years after acquiring the studio behind the Demon's Souls and Shadow of the Colossus remakes — with PlayStation boss Hermen Hulst blaming an 'increasingly challenging industry environment' and 70 employees facing layoffs [[playstation-boss-blames-bluepoint-cuts-on-budgets-and-players-f38a5da9]]. Ubisoft restructured into a 'Creative Houses' model with Tencent taking a 26.32% stake in new subsidiary Vantage Studios (the new home for Assassin's Creed, Rainbow Six, and Far Cry) via a 1.16 billion euro investment, alongside multiple cancellations, more studio closures, a five-day return-to-office mandate, and another 200 million euros in planned cuts over two years [[ubisoft-and-tencent-launch-vantage-studios-a43d07e1]].

> Sony is shutting Bluepoint Games down just five years after buying it... 70 employees are facing layoffs at the studio—which is known for modern remakes of games like Demon's Souls and Shadow of the Colossus.

> Tencent now holds a 26.32 percent economic interest in Vantage Studios—the new home of Ubisoft franchises including Assassin's Creed, Rainbow Six, and Far Cry.

**Live-service attrition is accelerating.** Highguard launched in late January 2026 and lived just 45 days before servers closed March 12, with studio head Chad Grenier citing insufficient revenue [[highguard-is-permanently-shutting-down-on-march-12-3599cd1e]]. Bungie's Marathon — the PvPvE extraction shooter launched March 5 at $40 — was reviewed as having 'incredible highs, painful lows' with promise but uncertain trajectory [[marathon-review-incredible-highs-painful-lows-4d8a2d55]].

> Highguard, which launched in late January 2026, had a strong initial player count but couldn't sustain momentum... When the servers closed on March 12, the game had lived for just 45 days.

> Bungie's slick extraction shooter is great at creating tense battles, incredible highs, and painful lows.

**May 2026 release slate is unusually strong.** Forza Horizon 6 (Xbox/PC May 15 early access, May 19 general) takes the racing series to Japan with the franchise's largest, most detailed world to date [[forza-horizon-6-review-the-best-racing-series-is-back-and-better-2679cc0e]]. Nioh 3 (February 6, 2026) introduces an 'open field' design — multiple curated maps rather than one interconnected world — that reviewers say works shockingly well but is undercut by a lack of challenge and an overlong story [[nioh-3-review-rise-of-the-shogun-95af04fb]]. Pokemon Pokopia, developed by Omega Force (Dragon Quest Builders), is the highest-rated Pokemon game on Metacritic at 90, an Animal Crossing-style life sim set in a Pokemon world where humanity has vanished [[pokopia-becomes-highest-rated-pokemon-game-on-metacritic-934cabcc]]. Subnautica 2 entered Early Access May 14 with an estimated 2–3 year EA window and peaked at 467,582 concurrents on Steam despite the prior legal dispute between Krafton and Unknown Worlds [[subnautica-2-s-early-access-impressions-shhhh-calm-down-5b8d0429]]. 007 First Light from IO Interactive (Hitman) launches May 27 across all platforms including Switch 2, with hands-on previews calling it a possible 2026 GOTY contender [[007-first-light-could-be-2026-s-game-of-the-year-and-the-best-bond-game-ever-5c7b8ae8]].

> Forza Horizon 6's digital recreation of the country is easily the franchise's most detailed and gorgeous world yet.

> Nioh 3 expands the possibility space of the series, and at its best moments on the early maps, playing it is surprisingly relaxing.

> Pokopia is sitting at a 90 on Metacritic, the highest aggregated score of any game in the series on the site.

> Despite the legal controversies surrounding its development, the game attracted hundreds of thousands of concurrent players on Steam, peaking at 467,582.

> Based on hands-on previews, the game could end up being one of the best games of the year, and also the best James Bond game ever created.

**Indie awards & ecosystem.** The 28th annual IGF Awards saw AP Thomson's Titanium Court win both the Seumas McNally Grand Prize and Excellence in Design; Baby Steps took Excellence in Audio [[titanium-court-honoured-with-grand-prize-at-igf-awards-0547801d]]. At the GDC Festival of Gaming, Innersloth's indie fund Outersloth disclosed it has rejected every single generative-AI pitch it has received — a notable stance as AI adoption spreads through the industry [[outersloth-has-rejected-every-generative-ai-pitch-it-has-received-b0b92b62]].

> Titanium Court, developed by AP Thomson, scooped up the prestigious Seumas McNally Grand Prize for Best Independent Game at the 28th annual Independent Game Festival (IGF) awards.

> Outersloth has rejected every single generative AI pitch that has been sent its way.
