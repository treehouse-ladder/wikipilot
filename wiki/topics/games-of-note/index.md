## Summary

Games-of-note in 2026 is defined by a single defining paradox: **record top-line revenue running alongside historic developer contraction**. The global games market cleared **$200B for the first time ever in 2025, closing at $201.6B (+9.1% YoY, Newzoo)** — PC was the standout segment at +12% ($43.6B, the strongest annual growth in Newzoo's historical dataset), mobile stayed largest at $113.3B, and console grew just 2.8% ($44.7B) [[global-games-revenue-breached-200b-in-2025-newzoo-bbccd35d]]. Yet the same window has produced the most severe wave of studio closures and layoffs the industry has tracked. Around that paradox sit consolidation among the major publishers, a bifurcating live-service market, an unusually strong release slate fronted by GTA 6, and sharpening labor/genAI/regulation fault lines. The full dated chronology lives in `## Recent updates` below; this is the current-state synthesis.

> The global games market passed $200 billion in revenue for the first time in 2025, closing at $201.6 billion according to Newzoo's latest quarterly update to the Global Games Market Report.

**Consolidation & contraction.** The major-publisher map is being redrawn: EA agreed to a record **$55B Saudi-PIF-led take-private**, the largest leveraged buyout ever [[ea-sets-a-new-8-billion-sales-record-just-months-after-laying-off-battlefield-6-developers-9ddae332]]; Ubisoft split its franchises into Tencent-backed Vantage Studios [[ubisoft-and-tencent-launch-vantage-studios-a43d07e1]]; Paramount Skydance is acquiring Warner Bros. Discovery [[paramount-acknowledged-the-existence-of-warner-bros-games-faa1d7b1]] and stood up a unified **Paramount Game Studios** even before the WB close [[paramount-unveils-new-unified-paramount-games-studio-3b154906]]; and Embracer is spinning off **Fellowship Entertainment** (Tomb Raider, Lord of the Rings, 11 studios) [[embracer-to-spin-off-major-video-game-franchises-and-studios-aa29c338]]. Ubisoft is structurally winding down its two-decade multi-studio model — closing Winnipeg and Belgrade for up to 380 cuts and refocusing Barcelona on Rainbow Six [[ubisoft-cutting-jobs-and-shutting-winnipeg-and-belgrade-studios-691886c0]]. Closures and layoffs span every tier — Sony shut Bluepoint and Dark Outlaw Games [[playstation-boss-blames-bluepoint-cuts-on-budgets-and-players-f38a5da9]] [[playstation-shuts-down-call-of-duty-leads-second-studio-4b2ca078]], EA cut Battlefield staff months after a record launch [[ea-has-laid-off-an-undisclosed-number-of-battlefield-developers-55eb4720]], Nacon's insolvency liquidated Spiders [[greedfall-developer-spiders-has-been-liquidated-c72bc9fe]], and Behaviour Interactive cut 95 more jobs in a second wave within two months [[behaviour-interactive-cuts-95-jobs-in-strategic-corporate-change-c6d65431]].

> Electronic Arts announced that it's sold itself in a $55 billion deal to an investor consortium made up of Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, Jared Kushner's Affinity Partners, and the private equity firm Silver Lake. The $55 billion take-private deal is the largest leveraged buyout (LBO) of all time.

> EA has laid off an undisclosed number of Battlefield developers just months after the record-breaking launch of Battlefield 6. There have been layoffs across all four studios within the division, which comprises internal studios Criterion, DICE, Ripple Effect, and Motive.
