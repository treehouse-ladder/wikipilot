## Today's brief

**agentic-coding** (6 sources, PR #79): OpenAI shipped its orchestration-as-platform answer to Anthropic Routines — Symphony, an open-source spec treating Linear as a state machine with devbox-resident unattended agent loops. Alongside it, the Harness Engineering post quantifies the thesis (3.5 merged PRs/engineer/day, ~1M LOC, ~10x speedup) and Unrolling the Codex Agent Loop opens the internals. Most novel: three AIDev GitHub-scale observational studies provide the first non-benchmark evidence axis — 22–29% OSS adoption rate, but larger agentic PRs fail CI at higher rates and bug-fix tasks have the worst merge success; security-related PRs cluster on hardening/testing rather than fixes.

**frontier-models** (5 sources, PR #80): The open-weights late-May wave continued below the closed frontier. Google re-entered with Gemma 4 (31B, AA Index 39, runs on MacBook); NVIDIA shipped Nemotron 3 Super (120B/12B-active hybrid Mamba-Transformer MoE, AA Index 36, fully open data+recipes); OpenBMB released MiniCPM5-1B (AA Index 17.9, highest ≤1B, 131K context, Apache-2.0 full stack). Key dispute resolved narrowly: GLM-5.1's independent AA Index (51) confirms it as a leading open-weights agentic model but well below the closed frontier, narrowing the prior vendor SWE-Bench Pro SOTA claim to a coding-axis-only result.

**ai-in-game-dev** (4 sources, PR #81): Two new deployment data points. Capcom disclosed 30,000+ autonomous playtesting hours/month via a 4-agent Google Cloud toolset on Monster Hunter Stories 3 — the largest concrete AI-QA number on record, a strong counterweight to the "developer genAI use declining" narrative in QA if not asset-gen. Krafton announced PUBG Ally, an on-device SLM Co-Playable Character (NVIDIA ACE) entering PUBG Arcade in H1 2026 — behavioral co-player, not just conversational NPC, supporting English/Korean/Chinese. Two pattern additions: NVIDIA NVIGI 1.5 code-agent (program-once NPC, no per-frame SLM calls) and Aura (proprietary UE AI agent, invite-only alternative to the MCP-server route).

**games-of-note** (6 sources, PR #82): Mina the Hollower shipped to ~93 Metacritic — 2026's highest-rated game so far (Forza Horizon 6 at 91). Witcher 3: Songs of the Past (2027) announced — a 12-years-later CDPR expansion as a legacy-IP-reinvestment counterpoint to the contraction narrative. Destruction AllStars shut down after 5 years. Three more layoff/closure data points: Piranha Games (30%, second round in 16 months), Hasbro/WotC's Atomic Arcade (Snake Eyes game), and Behaviour Interactive (~40 people, denied union/AI link).

**game-music** (3 sources, PR #78): Jake Kaufman interview adds craft precision to the Mina the Hollower score (Furnace Tracker/Game Boy+MSX-SCC, 100+ tracks, 2 Yuzo Koshiro guest tracks, "Cajunvania" Zydeco/folk areas). Doom OST inducted into the US National Recording Registry — only the third game-music entry ever after Super Mario Bros. theme and Minecraft: Volume Alpha. Elder Scrolls: Arena full-score remake (Ryan Zachariah Martin, fidelity-to-intent reconstruction).
