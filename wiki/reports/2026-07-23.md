## Key findings

### agentic-coding
Claude Code v2.1.212–218 (July 18–22, 2026) ships four runaway-loop caps: WebSearch per session (200), subagent spawns per session (200), concurrent subagents (default 20, `CLAUDE_CODE_MAX_CONCURRENT_SUBAGENTS`), and MCP server auto-background after 2 min idle (`CLAUDE_CODE_MCP_AUTO_BACKGROUND_MS`). New primitives: `EndConversation` tool for clean session termination, docker daemon-redirect flag permissions, `context:fork` now background-by-default, `/code-review` dispatched as background subagent. Cross-page sweep updated `parallel-subagents.md` (concurrent cap, fork background default) and `agent-sandboxing.md` (docker daemon-redirect permission hardening).

### frontier-models
BusinessCaseBench (arxiv 2607.16057): 238 real-world business cases, 615 questions, 18 disciplines. The cross-model oracle (best model per question) scores 4.5 points above the single best model — a strong signal for model routing on business knowledge work. Advisory tasks are the hardest category (<7% all-fail). No frontier-model leader or price changes; roster re-verified unchanged.

### ai-in-game-dev
SIGGRAPH 2026 (July 19, Los Angeles — first-ever SIGGRAPH Games Summit) dominates the run. DLSS 5 architecture revealed: three modular neural rendering models (A/B/C assignable per asset), 4K/60fps in under 16ms, fall 2026 ship date — a qualitative shift from DLSS 4.x upscaling (dispute filed). NVIDIA expanded its MCP ecosystem to Adobe, Blender, Houdini (APEX Script for procedural rigs), Affinity, and Unreal Engine. Cosmos 3 Edge (4B-param world foundation model) targets Jetson/RTX edge inference. MotionBricks: 15,000 FPS real-time motion generation from a 350,000-clip library, ships with GR00T integration. Summary regeneration triggered (DLSS 5 displaces DLSS 4.x framing; MotionBricks resets real-time motion bar).

### games-of-note

Two new open questions filed.
