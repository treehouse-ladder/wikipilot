# Daily Research Report — 2026-11-21

## Today's brief

- **MAI-Image-2.7 out of preview**: Microsoft's image generator/editor is GA on fal at ~$0.035 in / $0.010 out with commercially usable outputs, and lands nominally #1 on AA-Image-Editing v2.1 (~Elo 1215) — though within margin of the still-confirmed leader GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst (~1211) [[mai-image-2-7-microsoft-ai-image-generator-and-editor-7d6eaf21]]. **Why it matters to you:** a cheap, commercially-clearable text-to-image/edit step you can drop into your asset pipeline today without a licensing asterisk.
- **Kling Image 4.0 ships native 8K + Image Sets**: native 2K/4K/8K generation plus an Image Sets mode for consistent multi-image batches, $0.063/unit on fal [[kling-image-4-0-ai-image-generator-with-native-8k-and-image-sets-3fdafbfd]]. **Why it matters to you:** 8K-native output plus set consistency is hero-asset / sprite-sheet territory — fewer upscale and re-roll passes per shippable asset.
- **Inkling 2 Mini (open-weights looped transformer)**: Thinking Machines released a sub-1B open-weights model with frontier-class reasoning and a new open-weights SOTA on ARC-AGI-2 [[thinking-machines-has-released-inkling-2-mini-an-open-weights-looped-transformer-f4aa7e73]] [[welcome-inkling-2-mini-cutting-edge-performance-for-your-local-setup-824c03b9]]. **Why it matters to you:** abstract pattern reasoning resistant to training-set memorization means bigger upside on novel game mechanics + puzzle design — now runnable locally on your own setup instead of a metered API.
- **External sandboxes for coding agents**: a pattern for running coding agents inside isolated external sandboxes rather than against your live tree [[external-sandboxes-for-coding-agents-e45fd791]]. **Why it matters to you:** lets parallel sub-agents execute untrusted/generated code without risking your game project's working tree — a friction cut for the many-agents-at-once loop.
- **Crimson Desert genAI-asset disclosure**: Pearl Abyss admitted undisclosed generative-AI art slipped into Crimson Desert — a live TGA 2026 GOTY contender — and is now running an asset audit and adding a Steam genAI disclosure [[ai-generated-assets-were-unintentionally-included-in-crimson-desert-devs-say-d25e6f9d]]. **Why it matters to you:** even AAA is tripping on genAI provenance; stand up your own asset-audit + Steam disclosure habit now so it isn't a shipping-time scramble.
