## Today's brief

- **Frontier-model cost collapse quantified.** Artificial Analysis published a market-structure consolidation of the July 8–16 burst: six labs now field a model above AA Intelligence Index v4.1 = 50 (up from two in early June). The key new datapoint is per-task cost normalization — **GPT-5.6 Sol (max) costs $1.04/task** (within one point of Fable 5 at $2.75) and **Kimi K3 costs $0.94/task**, while GPT-5.6 Luna ($0.21), Muse Spark 1.1 ($0.26), and Grok 4.5 ($0.31) now cluster at or below the $0.32 GLM-5.2 set a week earlier. For agentic-coding fan-out loops this collapses the "only GLM-5.2 is cheap-near-frontier" framing from last week. No leader moved; Fable 5 = 60 re-confirmed as #1.
- **Bethesda doubles down on Starfield.** Amid the Xbox/ZeniMax Reset that gutted ZeniMax Online and id Software, Bethesda publicly reaffirmed that Starfield "remains an important part of our future" with new Starborn content pledged for 2027. The game has reached 17M players with nearly a billion hours played and >40% of players using fan-created Creations. Filed as an open question: can the studio deliver at reduced post-Reset headcount?

## Leader changes

No leader changes today. Fable 5 (AA Intelligence Index 60) remains public #1; the frontier-models re-verification confirms this unchanged. The cost-per-task landscape shifted materially but no aggregate-intelligence rank moved.
