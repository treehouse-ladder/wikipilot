# Daily Research Report — 2026-10-31

## Today's brief

- **Claude Code v2.1.316**: adds `CLAUDE_CODE_SUBAGENT_EFFORT` to set a default effort level for sub-agents and a `CLAUDE_CODE_DISABLE_ULTRATHINK` killswitch, plus a shell-cleanup-on-exit fix [[release-v2-1-316-11bfb616]]. **Why it matters to you:** a direct knob on your parallel-sub-agent workflow — dial effort down on cheap fan-out agents and reserve ultrathink budget for the orchestrator, cutting per-session token spend.
- **Claude Code v2.1.314**: new `permissions.blockWritesOutsideWorkingDirectories` enforces a write-path deny-all in every permission mode [[key-updates-in-claude-code-v2-1-314-denying-writes-outside-the-working-directory-and-fixing-gaps-in-sandbox-and-hook-enforcement-74166b1e]]. **Why it matters to you:** lets you run more autonomous agents against your game repo without them writing outside the project tree — fewer manual approval gates in the loop.
- **[[mistral-large-4]] ("Le Chonk")**: open weights released on HuggingFace under the Mistral Ecosystem Open-Weight License (modified MIT), with API pricing cut ~50% to $0.70/$2.10 per Mtoken (was $1.36/$4.18) [[mistral-large-4-le-chonk-is-now-open-weight-5a25565a]]. **Why it matters to you:** a sub-$1-in / ~$2-out open-weights option clears the >30% cost-cliff bar — worth benchmarking against your current daily driver for high-volume parallel-agent sessions before your next budget cycle.
- **Unreal Engine EULA Section 6e**: Epic removed its AI-training restriction — devs can now use UE source and MetaHuman outputs to train AI models; a "NoAI" opt-out tag must be actively applied, and the change applies to existing users [[epic-is-updating-its-eula-to-allow-ai-training-on-outputs-from-unreal-engine-and-metahuman-9502f826]]. **Why it matters to you:** unblocks training custom in-house content/animation models on MetaHuman outputs for your pipeline without licence risk — a shipping-economics shift for small studios.
- **[[seedance]] 2.5**: took #1 on the new AA-Video-I2V v2.1 board (Elo 1152), the first Artificial Analysis video board scored with audio output [[aa-video-i2v-v2-1-leaderboard-top-ai-video-models-7458c112]]. **Why it matters to you:** best-measured image-to-video-with-sound available today — a candidate for trailer/cutscene generation that no longer needs a separate audio pass.
- **[[minimax-music]] 2.6**: now live on fal at $0.15/audio [[minimax-music-2-6-text-to-audio-api-on-fal-ff196b6a]]. **Why it matters to you:** cheap, API-accessible music generation you can wire into an agentic asset pipeline for placeholder or shippable game tracks.
