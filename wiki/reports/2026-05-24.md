## Key findings

**frontier-models**: ZAYA1-8B from XGen-AI is the first frontier-class model trained entirely on AMD hardware (MI325X cluster), matching GPT-4o on several benchmarks at 8B scale via Compressed Convolutional Attention (CCA) — a third independent route to subquadratic attention alongside SubQ SSA and DeepSeek Sparse Attention. DeepSeek R2 confirmed as a 32B dense open-weight model (MIT, 92.7% AIME'25, single 24GB GPU) — distinct from V4-Pro's 1.6T MoE architecture. Gemini 3.2 Flash leaked via Google's own infrastructure, showing coding improvements over 3.1 Pro before official announcement.

**agentic-coding**: Google I/O 2026 delivered Antigravity 2.0 (updated desktop app + CLI, implicit competition with Cursor/Claude Code). Three new behavioral lenses on coding agents: SWE-Atlas (resolution rate is insufficient — behavioral/process metrics needed), scaffold taxonomy (4 architecture classes across 20+ open-source agents), behavioral drivers (what actually predicts success beyond passing the task). All three push the eval community toward process over outcome.

**ai-in-game-dev**: Two complementary findings on model scale for games: specialized 1.3M-parameter DOOM agent outperforms GPT-4V on real-time control (latency wins over capability breadth), while GameUIAgent shows LLM reasoning is essential for structured UI generation. Sprite formalizes the static-mockup-to-engine-ready UI pipeline. Emotional-arc procgen (All Stories Are One Story) adds narrative structure as a first-class level-generation constraint.

**games-of-note**: Industry contraction continues across multiple fronts simultaneously — Bungie plans significant layoffs with no Destiny 3 greenlit, Build a Rocket Boy (MindsEye) layoffs deepen, Eurogamer editorial staff cut by IGN parent, and EA stock trades below its $55B PIF buyout price as CFIUS review drags despite record FY26 results.

**game-music**: Grammy Awards for Best Score Soundtrack went to Austin Wintory (Sword of the Sea), not Lorien Testard's Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 — which topped Billboard Classical for 10 weeks and drew near-universal acclaim. MOUSE: P.I. For Hire (Patryk Scelina, big-band noir/electro-swing) and Ghost of Yotei (Toma Otowa, Milan Records gold-shimmer 2LP) each highlight the vinyl revival's continued momentum in game music.
