# Daily Research Report — 2026-06-07

## Today's brief

- **Harness engineering dominates agentic-coding**: Five new papers converge on a unified framing — RHO (self-preference over trajectory rollouts), HarnessForge (joint harness + policy co-evolution), Harness-Bench (multi-model harness effects), harness-updating vs AHE disentanglement, and HarnessAudit (mid-trajectory safety channels). For Claude Code-style workflows this week's batch is the most directly actionable set since the AHE paper: the non-monotonic benefit finding in HarnessForge directly targets the "should the evolver subagent be Haiku-class?" question [[harnessforge-joint-harness-and-policy-evolution-for-adaptive-agent-systems-0a4762a0]].
- **MiniMax M3 rewrites the cost table**: Open-weight model at $0.60/$2.40/Mtoken with vendor-reported 59.0% SWE-Bench Pro (eclipsing GPT-5.5 at 58.6%). Now the cheapest option in both cost columns; independent benchmark replication pending ~June 11 HuggingFace weights release [[minimax-m3-frontier-coding-1m-context-native-multimodality-all-in-one-model-d466ccc6]].
- **World-model wave in game dev**: Four new papers (Matrix-Game 3.0, Hunyuan-GameCraft-2, WorldPlay, WorldCam) advance real-time interactive world generation, but none yet export engine-importable assets — still video frames [[matrix-game-3-0-real-time-and-streaming-interactive-world-model-with-long-horizon-memory-e6f2a8e3]].
- **Industry: Panic bans generative AI on Playdate** and PlayerUnknown halts Go Wayback — two ends of the indie-vs-AAI spectrum both made news today [[panic-isn-t-keen-on-playdate-titles-using-generative-ai-209c7393]] [[playerunknown-productions-is-laying-off-staff-and-halting-development-on-go-wayback-5f4ffba7]].
- **Mr. Records (Day of the Devs)**: Glee-Cheese's next game inverts the composer-serves-game contract — 45 original Bardin & Ducloux compositions ARE the level geometry, Q1 2027 [[after-mixtape-cheerful-music-game-mr-records-is-a-day-of-the-devs-standout-7995adb8]].
