## Key stories

### agentic-coding (PR #358 — 2 sources, 4 pages)

**TRACEPROBE** (arXiv 2607.06184) proposes a deterministic rule-based complement to resolve-rate monoculture: nine-type action taxonomy, INSIGHT anti-patterns (e.g. excessive exploration, repetitive cycling), CONVERGE pairwise divergence classification — applied retrospectively to 2,500 SWE-Bench Verified trajectories from five leading agents. Distinct from SWE-Judge's LLM-based approach; the two share a failure-mode tension (TRACEPROBE: brittle to novel patterns; SWE-Judge: model-family bias). [[what-resolve-rate-hides-trajectory-structure-diagnostics-for-coding-agents-3704621c]]

**ChainSWE** (arXiv 2607.02606) is the first sequential, no-reset multi-bug benchmark: 304 issues across 54 real-world projects, each requiring ordered series of dependent fixes. Performance degrades up to 70% as chain length grows (compounding state corruption). Filed as new dispute with SWE-Bench single-bug reset methodology. [[chainswe-benchmarking-coding-agents-on-multi-bug-software-maintenance-50ac361b]]

Pages updated: `wiki/topics/agentic-coding/index.md`, `wiki/concepts/harness-engineering.md` + 2 source pages. `summary_affecting: false`.

### ai-in-game-dev (PR #359 — 1 source, 2 pages)

**GameEngineBench** (arXiv 2607.03525): scoped C++ implementation tasks inside real Unreal Engine 5 projects (9 game repos, 110 tasks). Strongest of 12 model/configuration combinations: 55.5% pass@1. 31 tasks unsolved by every configuration. Harness uses Play-in-Editor (PiE) automation + behavior-judge, not string-matching — making it the most engine-native eval to date. Adds a fourth agentic-game-dev capability data point, reinforcing the sub-55% ceiling cluster without changing recommended best practices.
