## Key findings (editorial)

1. **Harness co-training at scale.** Meta's Muse Spark 1.2 is the first production model co-trained with its own agentic harness (Muse Code) using rejection-sampled trajectories — a qualitative shift from "harness wraps a general model" to "harness shapes training." No independent benchmark yet corroborates Meta's co-training claims.

2. **Halo CE PS5 underperformance.** Analyst data shows ~452K copies and ~$28M in the first month for a 130M install-base platform — roughly 1/6 of what the simultaneous Xbox launch moved. The $50 price point for what critics call a "2001 graphical touch-up" appears to be the primary driver.

3. **Halo Studios structural turbulence.** Post-launch layoffs confirmed alongside reports of a troubled development (100% of Blam assets rebuilt for UE5, a canceled multiplayer game). Pattern echoes Xbox Reset period: shipping a game does not guarantee studio stability.

4. **LLM CLI content-addressable log.** Simon Willison's LLM 0.32 adds a Git-inspired content-addressable SQLite event log — useful for agentic-coding audit trails and debugging agent reasoning chains.
