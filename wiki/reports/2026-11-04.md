# Daily Research Report — 2026-11-04

## Today's brief

- **[[gemini-4-carbon]]**: Gemini 4 Carbon is the new top-measured generalist on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5 — Carbon (High) scores 59 (highest measured), edging Claude Opus 5.5 (Max) at 58, and Carbon (Medium) hits 58 at ~$6.59/task [[gemini-4-carbon-medium-intelligence-performance-price-analysis-022fb946]]. **Why it matters to you:** aggregate intelligence across many evals — a new #1 here is the strongest-generalist candidate to re-evaluate as your default daily-driver for agentic coding (note: not yet in the roster, so it hasn't moved the benchmark-leaders table — backfill pending).
- **[[pixellab]]**: PixelLab ships a Claude-compatible MCP server that generates sprites from text prompts via its API, from $0.001/image [[pixellab-sprite-generation-mcp-server-for-claude-code-1195a2d7]]. **Why it matters to you:** hits both anchors at once — drops a 2D sprite-generation step directly into your Claude Code agent loop over MCP, so a small-studio asset pipeline runs inside the same agentic session instead of a separate tool.
- **[[pika-h3-flex]]**: new open-weights video model (6-step LoRA on the MiniMax H3 33B base) launched #1 open-weights / #7 overall on DesignArena's text-to-video board (Elo 1293), producing a 15-second clip with synchronized audio in as little as ~7 seconds [[introducing-pika-h3-flex-0a7ab440]]. **Why it matters to you:** faster-than-realtime, locally-runnable (H100 80GB) cutscene/trailer prototyping with exportable audio stems — an open-weights option you can self-host for a small studio, though shipping/commercial-license terms are still unresolved on the entity page.
- **Genie 4** ([[genie-4-a-new-generation-of-world-simulation-e960e889]]): Google DeepMind's Genie 4 world-simulation model entered research preview, generating interactive simulated worlds anchored by Gemini Omni with other Gemini models maintaining coherence [[genie-4-a-new-generation-of-world-simulation-e960e889]]. **Why it matters to you:** a genuinely new ceiling-raiser for game dev (playable generated worlds), but gated to trusted testers and limited to a few minutes of continuous interaction with no measured game-dev uplift yet — watch the API rollout, don't plan around it.
- **Games-industry layoffs hit a record 10,977 in 2026**: the ASGC tracker counts 10,977 jobs lost (topping 2025's full-year 9,197), 35 studios closed, and 161 studios hit — with months left in the year [[games-industry-layoffs-hit-10977-in-2026-already-surpassing-all-of-2025-with-months-to-go-0c7ffab2]]. **Why it matters to you:** the competitive landscape for a small/solo studio keeps thinning — a market signal to factor into scope and timing decisions.
