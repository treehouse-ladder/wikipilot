# Daily Research Report — 2026-10-01

## Today's brief

The leading story is Google's return to the frontier-model top tier:

- **Gemini 4 Argon debuts at AA Intelligence Index v4.3 = 53**, creating a three-way #3 tie with Claude Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra — Google's first new frontier flagship since Gemini 3.1 Pro (46) in early 2026. Limited enterprise preview only; GA, Pro/Flash tier, and Deep Think mode all unconfirmed. Google headlines DeepSWE v1.1 at 77.9% (claiming a coding benchmark lead), but Terminal-Bench 4 at only 57% flags weak long-horizon agentic execution. Independent AA aggregate places it at #3, not #1. Priced at $4/$20 per Mtoken ($1.99/task at the introductory rate — lowest cost-per-task in the #3 tier cluster). New entity `wiki/entities/gemini-4-argon.md` filed.

- **GPT-6.1 Sol release confirmed** (from the 09-29 research window, landed in today's update): replaces GPT-6 Sol after 7 days at AA Index 50 (high effort), $2/$10 standard / $0.10 cached. Near-Astra intelligence at roughly 20% of Astra's token cost; extends the cost-efficiency floor.

- **Xbox "not for sale" / World's Edge AoE cancellation** extend the Xbox Reset narrative. Phil Spencer issued a flat denial of the June 2026 sale rumour while studio closures continue; World's Edge cancelled an unannounced Age of Empires project and cut ~50% of staff following the Activision transition — the most concrete data point yet that the Reset's org moves land as staff cuts.

- **Alexander Brandon displacement case** (game-music): Deus Ex/Unreal composer, 50 resumes / 1 interview after Frost Giant (Stormgate) layoff — the first named-veteran AI-displacement case study on the wiki. Corroborated by freelancer-market-deterioration coverage. September 2026 VGM: 99 OSTs, ~8% AI-generated (stable MoM vs August, but likely under-counts vs Deezer's 85% fraud-rate check).

- **Cache-aware routing** (agentic-coding): arXiv 2609.28919 claims 14–21% enterprise cost recovery; counterintuitively makes highest-priced model cheapest on long tool-heavy sessions. Directly actionable for agentic-workflow cost governance.

- **TGS 2026** (ai-in-game-dev): AI pavilion displaced indie showcase; 86% Japanese developer AI adoption (up from 51% at CESA 2025).
