# Daily Research — 2026-10-26

## Today's brief

- **[[flux]]**: Black Forest Labs shipped FLUX 3 Image, an open-weights (5.6B-param) image generation + editing model, with the distilled Klein variant on the BFL API from $0.009/1K images [[flux-3-image-klein-open-weights-5-6b-parameters-155c7b54]] [[flux-3-image-maximum-control-over-every-pixel-37948658]]. **Why it matters to you:** a self-hostable, per-pixel-controllable image+edit model at sub-cent-per-image API pricing is a candidate to own the 2D asset / texture-iteration step of your pipeline without per-call vendor lock-in.
- **[[kimodo]]**: NVIDIA Research released Kimodo, an open-weights controllable human/humanoid motion-diffusion model trained on 700 hours of optical mocap that accepts keyframes, sparse joints, 2D waypoints and dense 2D paths as constraints — the SOMA-SEED-v1.1 weights are commercial-ready [[kimodo-scaling-controllable-human-motion-generation-fe9ff887]] [[nvidia-kimodo-soma-rp-v1-1-hugging-face-3543d52b]]. **Why it matters to you:** constraint-conditioned (not blind text-to-motion) generation could replace a mocap/hand-keying step for character animation — but confirm its SOMA/SMPL-X/G1 skeleton output retargets cleanly to your Unity/Unreal rigs before committing (open question still open on the entity page).
- **[[gpt-image]]**: GPT Image 2.5 (Sunburst) took #1 on both Artificial Analysis image boards this run — AA-Image-T2I v2.1 at Elo 1196 and AA-Image-Editing v2.1 at Elo 1199 [[aa-image-t2i-v2-1-leaderboard-top-ai-image-models-634924f7]] [[gpt-image-2-5-flare-and-sunburst-released-by-openai-cf34266e]]. **Why it matters to you:** the editing-board #1 is the game-dev-relevant half — strongest measured option right now for iterating/retexturing an existing asset rather than regenerating from scratch.
- **[[sora]]**: OpenAI is discontinuing Sora [[what-to-know-about-the-sora-discontinuation-4d8dc7c1]]. **Why it matters to you:** if any part of your prototyping or marketing-clip workflow routes through Sora, migrate now — the active video leaders to evaluate are on the AA I2V boards (see watchlist).

## Watchlist

- **[[flux-3-max]]**: BFL's text/image-to-video model (fal-post-trained, $0.085/s via fal) is claimed joint #1 image-to-video on AA's *preliminary, vendor-reported* arena (Elo 1139, tied Seedance 2.5) [[flux-3-max-text-and-image-to-video-post-trained-by-fal-4991600a]] — but the live AA I2V boards show MiniMax H3 (Elo 1140) and Seedance 2.0 ahead [[aa-video-i2v-v1-1-leaderboard-top-ai-video-models-d50415cf]]; dispute is unresolved. Watch for the live-board confirmation before trusting the #1 claim.
- **[[seedance]]**: Seedance 2.5 announced — 30-second single-pass audio-video with multi-round extensions and up to 50 reference inputs [[introducing-seedance-2-5-78d1ddb7]]. Announcement-stage (no independent measurement yet); the flexible-referencing angle is worth tracking for shot-consistent cutscene generation.
- **Video-board pricing**: on the live AA I2V boards, Seedance 2.0 720p leads the audio board at Elo 1197 (AA-measured $9.07/min) and MiniMax H3 768p leads the no-audio board at Elo 1140 ($34.12/min) [[aa-video-i2v-v1-1-leaderboard-top-ai-video-models-d50415cf]]. The ~3.8x cost gap matters if you budget AI video for trailers/marketing.
- **[[ideogram]]**: licensing backfilled — API-generated outputs are free for commercial use (vendor FAQ), but the open-weights variant is non-commercial, with a self-serve commercial weights license at $300/10k images/mo; fal API runs $0.03–$0.09/image [[licensing-ideogram-bdab80bb]] [[ideogram-text-to-image-api-on-fal-7cd91f04]]. Gotcha for any indie plan to self-host the weights.
- **[[kling]]**: Kling 4.0 API is still "Coming Soon" — the listed rates (720p 1.0 unit/s, 1080p 1.5 units/s) are preview-only and can't serve live requests yet [[kling-api-pricing-fba30089]]. Don't design a pipeline against these numbers until launch.
- **Claude Code**: v2.1.310 ships idle compaction (summary computed while away, used only if your next message is 1h+ later) plus compaction/gateway reliability fixes [[release-v2-1-310-anthropics-claude-code-97c5df49]]. Minor, but idle compaction trims context cost on long-running agent sessions.
