# Daily Research Report — 2026-10-05

## Today's brief

- **agentic-coding**: Cross-benchmark RL transfer breakthrough — Kimi K2.7 Code (1T-param MoE, 32B active) improved across all 6 public benchmarks via a single epoch of GSPO on a LoRA adapter trained on 1,700 tasks [[cross-benchmark-transfer-from-rl-on-agentic-coding-tasks-388f3c12]]. SWE-Bench Pro 60.1→64.8, DeepSWE 31.0→43.4, Terminal-Bench 2.1 67.4→82.0. Key implication for agentic workflows: RL training on one closed task set generalizes to harnesses the model was never trained on.
- **games-of-note**: IGN layoffs hit multiple departments including long-time hosts Brian Altano (Beyond, since 2009), Seth Macy (Nintendo Voice Chat), and Max Scoville [[brutal-ign-layoffs-hit-multiple-departments-and-long-time-hosts-5b6c1faf]]. Gears of War: E-Day launched October 6 — reviews split: Kotaku calls it "best the series has ever looked or felt" but "far from perfect" [[the-gears-of-war-e-day-campaign-is-full-of-highs-and-lows-095316eb]]; GameSpot finds the "increased spectacle" impressive but insufficient [[gears-of-war-e-day-review-97468a09]].
- **ai-in-game-dev**: IGMBench formalizes executable world-editing as a benchmark axis — 110 tasks across Minecraft/Terraria at 4 intervention depths, reliability falls off sharply with depth [[world-editing-intervening-on-executable-worlds-at-increasing-depth-e2108494]]. Unity MCP v10.3.0 adds a Blender Bridge, extending the no-sandbox editor-control posture from the standalone BlenderMCP server into the Unity Editor [[unity-mcp-v10-3-0-blender-bridge-drives-blendermcp-from-the-unity-editor-d2642a3e]].
