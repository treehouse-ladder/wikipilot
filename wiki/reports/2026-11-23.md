# Daily Research Report — 2026-11-23

## Today's brief

- **GTA 6 is the biggest entertainment launch in history**: Take-Two reports 32.5M copies and $3.79B in day-one sales, superseding the earlier ~24.58M/$2.36B third-party estimate [[gta-6-s-astonishing-4-billion-day-one-includes-32-5-million-copies-sold-a109c5a2]] [[grand-theft-auto-vi-is-the-biggest-video-game-launch-of-all-time-34871891]]. **Why it matters to you:** resets the ceiling for open-world ambition and the commercial bar every premium title you benchmark against is now measured against.
- **New top coding agent — Antigravity CLI + Gemini 4 Neon (xhigh) scores 59 on the AA Coding Agent Index**, edging Claude Code + [[claude-fable-5]] 5.2 (58), Gemini 4 Carbon high (55) and Codex + GPT-6.2 Astra Max (53) [[introducing-gemini-4-neon-fd9bebbf]] [[antigravity-cli-coding-agent-index-score-performance-price-a0d47cb6]]. Caveat: this index was rebased to Terminal-Bench 4.1 and is NOT comparable to the prior 62-basis read. **Why it matters to you:** a new default candidate for agentic-coding sessions inside your game project — but the 1-point lead over Claude Code is within rebase noise, so choose on cost/latency, not rank.
- **Cost cliff for parallel-agent sessions**: Antigravity + Neon runs ~$6.86/task (median $5.26, ~30.5 min/task) vs Claude Code + Fable 5.2 at $15.47/task for near-identical index scores; Codex + GPT-6.2 Astra is cheapest at $5.12/task [[antigravity-cli-coding-agent-index-score-performance-price-a0d47cb6]]. **Why it matters to you:** at roughly 45% of Claude Code's per-task cost for the same tier, Neon or Codex materially cuts the bill when you fan out many agents at once.
