# Daily Research Report — 2026-07-29

## Today's brief

- **MCP 2026-07-28 is now FINAL — not RC.** The spec landed as a published release, replacing the 2025-11-25 version. Key changes: `Mcp-Session-Id` removed (stateless by default), `ttlMs`/`cacheScope` required on all list endpoints, `Mcp-Method` and `Mcp-Name` headers required, Enterprise-Managed Authorization stable, Roots/Sampling/Logging deprecated. This is a breaking change for any coding-agent tool server that relied on session state or skipped list-endpoint cache fields. [[the-2026-07-28-mcp-specification-1479f16f]]
- **Poolside Laguna S 2.1 is an open-weight coding model beating rivals 10× its size.** Positioned as the best open-weight model in its parameter class; relevant for local/cost-sensitive agentic coding deployments. [[introducing-laguna-s-2-1-131cc339]] [[poolside-drops-laguna-s-2-1-an-open-weight-coding-model-that-beats-rivals-10x-its-size-37368a6e]]
- **GTA 6 is tracking the strongest pre-order campaign ever recorded.** Newzoo projects 37–51M copies in week one; $180M pre-orders in US+EU5 alone; $3.25–5.2B week-one revenue projected. The "biggest entertainment launch in history" trajectory is now backed by firm pre-order data, not speculation. [[gta-6-has-the-strongest-pre-order-campaign-on-record-is-on-track-to-sell-37-51-million-in-week-one-analytics-firm-says-4bc7e6e7]]
- **Double Fine laid off 23 (~29%) of its ~80 staff** as it completes the Xbox divestiture and targets a "sustainable independent size." Signals that the spun-off studios face immediate restructuring pressure, not a soft landing. [[psychonauts-2-studio-double-fine-hit-with-layoffs-amid-divestiture-from-xbox-4da4374a]]
- **God of War Laufey dated February 16, 2027**, joining a crowded February window. Santa Monica Studio confirmed a second Kratos-led entry alongside the Laufey protagonist thread. [[god-of-war-laufey-gets-february-date-as-major-releases-pile-up-c3bfe97d]]
- **MAGIC generates navigable multi-scene game worlds** using a multi-agent LLM pipeline with transition awareness. Addresses the cross-scene continuity gap in prior single-scene procedural content generation work. [[magic-transition-aware-generation-of-navigable-multi-scene-game-worlds-with-large-language-models-c396d86d]]
- **Gordy Haab released the first Star Wars: Zero Company track** (self-titled, 1:17). Full soundtrack arrives August 27. Grammy winner (Jedi: Survivor) continuing the franchise — franchise-composer continuity rather than a handoff. [[award-winning-gordy-haab-drops-the-first-single-for-star-wars-zero-company-427d9a90]]
- **GitHub Copilot enterprise managed settings now govern the cloud agent.** `managed-settings.json` controls both the desktop Copilot app and the async autonomous cloud agent (background agent that analyzes issues and opens draft PRs). Central policy surface for enterprise agentic-coding governance. [[enterprise-managed-settings-in-the-github-copilot-app-and-copilot-cloud-agent-beab8f92]]

Any agentic harness that assumed session continuity or omitted cache fields will break against compliant servers. Watch for SDK update announcements. [[the-2026-07-28-mcp-specification-1479f16f]]

Open question: does Microsoft's runway funding hold them steady or do more cuts follow? [[psychonauts-2-studio-double-fine-hit-with-layoffs-amid-divestiture-from-xbox-4da4374a]]
