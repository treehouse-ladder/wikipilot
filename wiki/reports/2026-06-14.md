# Daily Research Report — 2026-06-14

## Today's brief

- **Agents supersede code review** (agentic-coding): Monperrus et al. (arxiv 2606.13175) argue human code review is structurally unnecessary — agents have crossed the capability threshold. Counter-evidence shows agentic PRs fail sabotage detection (94% miss rate) and have lower merge success on bug-fix tasks. The assurance-layer gap is the live open question.
- **Gemini 3.5 Live Translate** (frontier-models): Google announced a speech-to-speech live translation model that preserves voice character and emotional cadence. The model card confirms it is based on Gemini 3 Pro and explicitly "less capable than Gemini 3.1 Pro" — not a frontier jump, a specialised deployment.
- **3DCodeBench** (ai-in-game-dev): First benchmark for code-driven procedural 3D modeling (arxiv 2606.01057), covering 12 VLMs across 1,600 tasks. Relevant for pipeline tools in procedural content generation.
- **GTA 6 analyst split** (games-of-note): van Dreunen (NYU Stern) says GTA 6 won't rescue structural industry problems; Circana forecasts $62.8B record year. Behaviour Interactive laid off 95 more employees (second wave in two months), pivoting to horror/service/LBE.
- **Stellar Blade vinyl live** (game-music): Pre-orders opened June 11 for 4LP ($98) and 2LP ($42) editions; standard black editions now available at retail partners.

## Leader changes

No benchmark or cost leader changes today. Gemini 3.5 Live Translate is a specialised model positioned below Gemini 3.1 Pro; it does not affect the AA Intelligence Index standings.

## Watchlist

- **Code review replacement** — Monperrus thesis will be contested; watch for responses from security researchers on agent-as-reviewer sabotage resistance. The missing assurance layer is the most actionable gap for agentic workflow builders.
- **GTA 6 industry impact** — The van Dreunen / Circana split will resolve by December 2026. If layoffs continue post-November release despite a $62.8B headline, that validates the concentration-not-rescue argument.
- **3DCodeBench** — Procedural 3D by code is a natural fit for agentic content pipelines; watch for AAA studio adoption and follow-on fine-tuning work.
