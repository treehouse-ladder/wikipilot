## Today's brief

- **[[claude-code]] v2.1.326**: first release that makes **Code Mode composable with parallel agent teams** — control-flow collapse and multi-agent fan-out now stack in one session; the sandbox also stops hiding destructive Bash (`find -delete`, `cat a > b`, `sort -o` were showing as harmless reads) and fixes `cp` over-blocking [[release-v2-1-326-anthropics-claude-code-4e2b4f15]]. **Why it matters to you:** this is a direct upgrade to your daily-driver agentic loop — you can now orchestrate parallel sub-agents under collapsed control flow, and the transcript finally tells the truth about what the sandbox blocked.
- **[[hyper3d-rodin]]**: ToS review confirms paid plans grant **commercial-use rights** for generated 3D models in games/products/client work, with Rodin output not limited by the vendor (ChatAvatar output still needs a separately purchased licence) [[hyper3d-rodin-terms-of-service-fdae09c1]]. **Why it matters to you:** removes the licensing blocker on shipping Rodin-generated meshes in a commercial game — a text/image-to-3D step you can now legally bake into your asset pipeline.
- **[[unity-spark]] + Synty POLYGON**: Unity acquired Synty Studios and is folding the 760k+ POLYGON asset library into Unity Spark [[unity-acquires-synty-studios-library-coming-to-unity-spark-73d27350]]. **Why it matters to you:** POLYGON is the de-facto "stock art of game jams"; first-party integration lowers the prototyping-to-production asset cost for small-team projects you build in Unity.
- **[[kimi-k3.1-pro]]**: open weights confirmed live and now the top *released* open-weights model on the AA Intelligence Index v5 (score 46), but the Kimi K3.1 Pro License **restricts MaaS use**, unlike MIT-licensed [[glm-5.3]] [[kimi-k3-1-pro-launches-at-4th-on-the-intelligence-index-and-will-lead-open-weights-models-c9f225d9]]. **Why it matters to you:** a stronger open-weights option for self-hosted agentic work, but the licence caveat means it's not a drop-in replacement for GLM-5.3 if you serve it — read the terms before standing it up.
