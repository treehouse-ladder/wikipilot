# Daily Research 2026-11-10

## Today's brief

- **Unreal Engine 5.9 MegaJam Preview**: Unreal MCP now auto-launches with the editor and ships expanded agent toolsets (rendering, source control, profiling, debugging, gameplay testing, geometry, audio, UI, level creation) plus new Lore source-control and Fab integrations [[unreal-engine-5-9-megajam-preview-a37599a6]]. **Why it matters to you:** this hits both your anchors at once — an agentic-coding surface living inside the engine means your sub-agents can drive level/geometry/test work directly in UE; note the caveat that Toolset plugins are off by default on new 5.9-preview projects, so you must enable them.
- **[[claude-fable-5]]**: Fable 5.2 (`claude-fable-5-2`) is the new default model in Claude Code v2.1.321 — 1M context, $8/$40 per Mtok, and $0.20/Mtok cache reads — and it tops `aa_intelligence_index` (62) on the regenerated benchmark table [[release-v2-1-321-anthropics-claude-code-ace31f3b]]. **Why it matters to you:** aggregate intelligence (avg across many evals) — high = strong generalist and the right default daily-driver for agentic coding; the cheap cache-read tier makes long-codebase, cached-prompt parallel-agent sessions markedly cheaper to run. The release also lands multiple sandbox/permission-bypass security fixes.
- **[[stable-audio]]**: Stability AI Stable Audio 3.0 ships text-to-music over API with AudioSparx-licensed, game-use-cleared commercial rights [[stable-audio-vs-suno-udio-elevenlabs-mubert-and-lyria-licensing-export-rights-and-self-hosting-compared-3b88bdac]]. **Why it matters to you:** a drop-in text-to-music step whose licensing is explicitly cleared for shipping in a game — removes the export-rights ambiguity that blocks most AI-music tools from your soundtrack/ambience pipeline.
- **[[seedream]]**: ByteDance Seedream 5.0 Pro is a new text+image multimodal generator with strong Chinese/English text rendering at $0.0675/image on fal [[introducing-seedream-5-0-pro-it-understands-design-fbaad63d]] [[seedream-5-0-pro-api-multimodal-ai-image-model-12934420]]. **Why it matters to you:** reliable in-image text rendering is the usual failure point for AI-generated UI mockups, logos, and signage — a cheap API option that handles it is a usable concept/asset step for a small studio.

## Leader changes

- **[[elevenlabs]]**: Eleven v4 Turbo took #1 on the AA Controlled Voice TTS board (Elo 1155), with Eleven v4 at #2 (Elo 1154) — a move from no prior rank to the top [[controlled-voice-leaderboard-top-ai-voice-models-0313c536]]. **Why it matters to you:** this is now the top-ranked controlled-voice TTS for your voice-NPC / dialog pipeline; worth re-benchmarking against your current voice step.
- **[[gpt-image]]**: GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst retains #1 on both AA-Image-T2I v2.1 and AA-Image-Editing v2.1, but widened its lead sharply — T2I Elo 1196→1259 and Editing Elo 1199→1209 [[aa-image-editing-v2-1-leaderboard-top-ai-image-models-9069ca61]]. **Why it matters to you:** no swap, but the strongest text-to-image + edit model for your concept/asset pipeline just got materially better, so prior quality comparisons against it are now stale.

## Watchlist

- **[[cartesia]]**: Cartesia Sonic 3.6 debuts at #3 on AA Controlled Voice TTS (Elo 1148), API + web access [[introducing-sonic-3-6-1274d75a]]. **Why it matters to you:** a credible second-source voice option behind ElevenLabs for your NPC-dialog pipeline — watch, don't switch yet.
- **[[nano-banana-2-1]]**: pricing now confirmed (~$0.04/image @1K, ~$0.06 @2K, ~$0.13 @4K on fal); AA Elo still unconfirmed [[nano-banana-2-1-text-to-image-api-on-fal-76f8d675]]. **Why it matters to you:** closes yesterday's open pricing question for budgeting image-asset batches; the benchmark placement is the remaining gap.
- **Tencent Hy4**: new frontier entry scores 35 on AA Intelligence Index v5.0.1 (below the 37 median) and is verbose (~160M tokens/run vs 72.6M median) at $0.83/$2.50 per Mtok; no board impact [[tencent-hy4-intelligence-performance-price-analysis-65eda1d6]]. **Why it matters to you:** cheap and strong on structured extraction / Chinese tasks, but its token verbosity erodes the price advantage for agentic loops — not a daily-driver candidate yet.
- **Skydance / Paramount Games layoffs (2026-11-10)**: 30+ cut from the merged Skydance Interactive + New Media division a month after the WBD deal closed [[skydance-lays-off-game-developers-post-warner-bros-merger-a0c5eed2]]. **Why it matters to you:** publisher-consolidation fallout signal for the market you ship into; affects available talent and the competitive landscape for small studios.
- **GTA VI (launch Nov 19)**: worldwide preloading is live — code-in-a-box Nov 12, unlock Nov 19; datamined 2027 multiplayer and PS5 Pro patch; $100 digital-only Ultimate Edition [[gta-6-preloading-is-officially-live-files-mention-multiplayer-and-ps5-pro-features-f03efcee]]. **Why it matters to you:** the release-window gravity well for the whole market — carried from yesterday, now 9 days out and distribution-live.
