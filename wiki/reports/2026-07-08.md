## Today's brief

**Xbox Reset studio fate:** Double Fine Productions and Compulsion Games are spinning off as independent studios with full IP rights and financial runway. Ninja Theory and Undead Labs have been signed to undisclosed buyers, with Senua's Saga and State of Decay 3 secured. Arkane Lyon is in Works Council consultation, signaling a potential closure. DOOM: The Dark Ages — Revelations DLC launched the same day id Software confirmed it lost roughly half its staff (~50 jobs).

**Latent Programming Horizons (agentic-coding):** A KTH/Monperrus study shows that probing a coding agent's residual stream mid-trajectory predicts final correctness at AUC 0.83 — before execution finishes. Simon Willison's "Fable's judgement" post articulates a complementary pattern: use a frontier model only for judgment calls and route implementation tasks to cheaper subagents by default.

**Sakana Fugu (frontier-models):** Sakana AI's Fugu and Fugu-Ultra models were ingested; they claim SWE-bench Pro SOTA via a multi-agent orchestrator. Claims are vendor-reported and not yet independently verified. The existing leader picture holds: Fable 5 (AA Intelligence Index 60), Opus 4.8 (56), GPT-5.5 (55).

**AI Native Games survey (ai-in-game-dev):** arXiv:2607.00527 proposes a counterfactual criterion for "AI-native" games (would the core gameplay loop collapse without runtime AI?) and introduces the G/N taxonomy validated over 53 prototypes. Companion-play and multi-agent-simulation quadrants remain underrepresented in existing corpora.

**FF Resonance soundtrack (game-music):** Square Enix released soundtrack samples for FINAL FANTASY RESONANCE (Oct 22, 2026). Elements Garden/Noriyasu Agematsu are composing 33 new tracks alongside Brave Exvius carry-overs; the collector's edition includes a 120-track CD.
