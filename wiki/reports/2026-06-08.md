# Daily Research Report — 2026-06-08

## Today's brief

- **Dynamic workflows benchmark — Bun Zig→Rust in 11 days**: Jarred Sumner ported Bun from Zig to Rust using a Claude Code dynamic-workflow JS orchestration script spinning tens-to-hundreds of parallel subagents — 750k lines of Rust, 99.8% test suite pass [[a-harness-for-every-task-dynamic-workflows-in-claude-code-bbb7a8da]]. **Why it matters:** highest-leverage published exemplar yet for the parallel-subagent pattern — the measured throughput (full-language port in <2 weeks) is the most actionable signal of the month for any agentic workflow optimization.
- **Human oversight study (23 developers)**: Mixed-methods study across Claude Code / Cursor / Copilot / Aider finds verification consumes 60–70% of oversight effort; surfaces four modalities (pre-flight, in-flight, post-flight, trajectory replay) and three heuristics — 'shrink the diff', 'trust the test not the chat', 'reversibility first' [[human-oversight-of-agentic-systems-in-practice-ab5cc8f1]]. **Why it matters:** quantifies where the time actually goes and names three heuristics directly applicable to your subagent review step.
- **Anthropic skills taxonomy (9 categories)**: Anthropic publishes a nine-category skills taxonomy (file ops, shell, browser, analysis, comms, git, MCP, edit, tool-writing) framing skills as LLM-generated harness extensions [[lessons-from-building-claude-code-how-we-use-skills-0270e620]]. **Why it matters:** clean taxonomy to audit your own `.claude/skills/*` directory against, and vendor-blessed pattern for agent-authored skills.
- **NVIDIA Cosmos 3 open omnimodel**: NVIDIA released Cosmos 3, an open frontier omnimodel for physical AI handling vision + text + action jointly [[nvidia-launches-cosmos-3-the-open-frontier-foundation-model-for-physical-ai-4800a40c]] [[welcome-nvidia-cosmos-3-the-first-open-omni-model-for-physical-ai-reasoning-and-action-e13281b4]]. **Why it matters:** open-weights physical-AI foundation model is the missing piece for a self-hosted NPC behavior / animation-conditioning pipeline that doesn't depend on closed APIs.
- **SGF 2026 AI disclosure backlash wave**: Multiple games (including Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis) carry Steam AI disclosure flags and are drawing community pushback; yesterday's Panic Playdate ban reads as the leading edge of a broader storefront-policy shift [[summer-game-fest-is-here-so-get-ready-for-a-lot-of-ugh-that-game-with-the-cool-trailer-used-ai-57711bd5]] [[tomb-raider-legacy-of-atlantis-was-made-with-ai-steam-page-reveals-800eb98e]]. **Why it matters:** if you ship a small-team title with AI in the pipeline, the disclosure language and audience-trust playbook are being written in public this week — read both threads before your own announcement.
- **Apple WWDC: Gemini-powered Siri (1.2T params)**: Apple unveiled a Gemini-powered Siri at WWDC 2026 running a custom 1.2T-parameter Gemini model (8× their prior 150B) on Private Cloud Compute infrastructure [[what-to-expect-from-wwdc-2026-gemini-powered-siri-ios-27-macos-27-and-more-273ea775]]. **Why it matters:** signals Google has effectively won the on-device-LLM-on-iPhone tier for this generation — relevant if any project targets mobile companion or voice-assistant experiences.
- **Xbox Showcase 2026 wave**: Gears of War: E-Day (Xbox console exclusive), Halo: Campaign Evolved (July 28 day-and-date PS5), Persona 4 Revival, Spyro: A Realm Beyond (Tom Kenny), Stronghold 4 (Samantha Béart + Ben Starr), Valheim 1.0, Fields of Mistria 1.0; plus Future Games Show, PC Gaming Show, Wholesome Direct (53 games) [[xbox-games-showcase-2026-recap-the-return-of-exclusives-world-premieres-and-anniversary-hardware-9d1a38cb]]. **Why it matters:** Valheim 1.0 and Fields of Mistria 1.0 are small-team exemplars worth post-mortem reading once they ship.
