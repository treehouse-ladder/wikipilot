## Key findings

**games-of-note:** Blizzard/CWA ratified the first union contract covering 1,900 Microsoft game-studio employees, with three industry-first clauses: (1) AI guardrail requiring Microsoft to bargain before automating or replacing jobs; (2) 14-month layoff recall rights; (3) structural consolidation of multiple smaller unions into three bargaining units. This is the largest game-industry union contract on record and sets a potential template for CWA Local 6215 at id Software.

**frontier-models:** DeepSeek V4.1 Flash introduces a Causal Encoder-Decoder (CED) architecture — 40-layer Transformer split as 20-layer causal encoder + 20-layer decoder — with only 8B active parameters during prefill (16B during decode). At $0.30/$1.20 per million tokens (~83% cheaper than V4-Pro-0813) it is the lowest-cost entry in the DeepSeek V4 family. AA Intelligence Index 40 is well above the open-weights-of-similar-size median (18) but below V4-Pro (53); the HuggingFace model card's "outperforms V4-Pro" claim appears to refer to latency/cost-normalized metrics.

**agentic-coding:** Cursor Projects introduces a coordinator-agent model where one persistent Cursor Agent acts as the long-running coordinator, spinning up background Cursor Agents as parallel sub-tasks. This extends the parallel-subagents thread tracked since May 2026.

**ai-in-game-dev:** AnimalLift (arXiv) extends the image-to-3D research thread with the first single-image pipeline that outputs simulation-ready Blender curve-based fur with UV attributes and mesh bindings — not just mesh+texture as prior work.

**game-music:** RPGFan review of the Square Enix Jazz: Final Fantasy VII live album recorded at Billboard Live Tokyo (Feb 2020). Arrangers Eijiro Nakagawa and Ryu Kawamura; Ryoji Ihara tenor sax. Tracklist differs from the studio album: "Cosmo Canyon" absent, "Main Theme" and "Fanfare" added. This is the fourth Square Enix Jazz series entry tracked in the wiki.
