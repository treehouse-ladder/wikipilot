# Daily Research Report — 2026-07-27

## Today's brief

- **Harness-evolution methodological crisis**: Three independent July 2026 papers converge on a damaging finding for the agentic-coding community — sequential harness iteration without held-out evaluation is either a null result (35 releases, no gain) or outright overfitting. The constructive path (gated QD, +9–15.5 pp sealed-test) requires separating proposal from deterministic crediting and using a held-out benchmark, exactly the discipline missing from prior AHE/HarnessForge claims.
- **Kimi K3 open weights confirmed**: The 2.8T MXFP4 weights landed July 27 as promised. With native Blackwell/MI400 support, ~1.4 TB footprint, and leads on SWE Marathon + Program Bench, K3 is now the dominant open-weights frontier model — though hardware dependency limits community reach vs. GGUF Q4.
- **Roblox mobile AI creation**: Roblox Build enters New Zealand public alpha July 28, sharing back end with Studio. Mobile-vs-desktop capability parity is unresolved.
- **Halo Campaign Evolved (July 28)**: Already tracking as the lowest-rated Halo yet on pre-launch coverage — launches tomorrow. The key question is whether a commercial hit still validates the Xbox reset narrative regardless of review scores.
