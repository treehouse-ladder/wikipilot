## Summary

Spry Fox is an independent game development studio known for Cozy Grove, which was sold back to its co-founders Daniel Cook and David Edery in December 2025 after being acquired by Netflix in 2022 [[spry-fox-co-founder-on-the-new-normal-after-netflix-split-56697b7f]].

The buyback required significant personal sacrifices from both co-founders, who reduced their salaries to $20,000/year and spent their own cash to reacquire the studio [[spry-fox-co-founder-on-the-new-normal-after-netflix-split-56697b7f]]. Following the split, the studio shifted its mission from launching Spirit Crossing exclusively on Netflix to bringing the title to multiple platforms [[spry-fox-co-founder-on-the-new-normal-after-netflix-split-56697b7f]].

> Spry Fox joined Netflix in 2022 but was sold back to co-founders Daniel Cook and David Edery in December 2025.

> Co-founder David Edery explained that the team had to make sacrifices to regain their independence, with both Daniel Cook and Edery reducing their salaries to $20,000/year and spending their own cash to buy the studio back.
