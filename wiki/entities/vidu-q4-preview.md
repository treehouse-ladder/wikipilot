## Summary

**Vidu Q4 Preview** is Shengshu Technology's next-generation flagship video model, launched in public preview on `platform.vidu.com` (API model ID: `viduq4-preview`) in October 2026 [[shengshu-technology-launches-vidu-q4-preview-a-next-generation-ai-video-model-built-for-lifelike-performances-b956992b]]. It is focused on image-to-video and reference-to-video generation; text-only generation is not available in the preview.

> Vidu Q4 Preview is a public preview of Shengshu Technology's next-generation flagship video model. The full Q4 release date has not been announced. [[shengshu-technology-launches-vidu-q4-preview-a-next-generation-ai-video-model-built-for-lifelike-performances-b956992b]]

Key capabilities: up to 15 reference images and 3 reference audio clips per generation, synchronized audio-video output, and clips up to 16 seconds at 2K or 4K resolution [[shengshu-technology-launches-vidu-q4-preview-a-next-generation-ai-video-model-built-for-lifelike-performances-b956992b]].

> The model supports image-to-video and reference-to-video generation with up to 15 image references and up to 3 audio references per request. Text-only prompt-to-video generation is not available in this preview release. [[shengshu-technology-launches-vidu-q4-preview-a-next-generation-ai-video-model-built-for-lifelike-performances-b956992b]]

Pricing starts at **$0.045/s at 540p** and scales to **$0.39/s at 4K**; a 30% introductory discount on image-to-video and reference-to-video generation runs through November 30, 2026 [[shengshu-technology-launches-vidu-q4-preview-a-next-generation-ai-video-model-built-for-lifelike-performances-b956992b]].

> Pricing starts at $0.045 per second of video at 540P, scaling up to $0.39 per second at 4K resolution. A 30% promotional discount applies to image-to-video and reference-to-video generation through November 30, 2026. [[shengshu-technology-launches-vidu-q4-preview-a-next-generation-ai-video-model-built-for-lifelike-performances-b956992b]]

On debut, an aggregator source reported Vidu Q4 Preview at **#3 on AA-Video-I2V v1.0 (Elo 1,179)** [[vidu-q4-preview-aa-video-i2v-debut-38fa453c]]. The v1.0 Elos are not directly comparable to the v1.1 standings the wiki tracks; see Open questions.

> Vidu Q4 Preview entered the Artificial Analysis AA-Video-I2V v1.0 leaderboard at number three with an Elo score of 1,179 on its debut, making it among the highest-ranked commercial image-to-video models on its launch day. [[vidu-q4-preview-aa-video-i2v-debut-38fa453c]]
