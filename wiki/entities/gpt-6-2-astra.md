# GPT-6.2 Astra

## Summary

GPT-6.2 Astra is OpenAI's latest frontier model, released **2026-11-17** alongside GPT-6.2 Sol. It is available via the OpenAI API as `gpt-6.2-astra` with Ultrafast available at launch [[introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-and-sol-8f6af8c4]]. **GPT-6.2 Astra scores 62 on the AA Intelligence Index** (max), 5 points above GPT-6 Astra (57) and placing OpenAI at **#2 overall**, trailing only Claude Fable 5.2 (65) [[openai-has-the-2-model-in-the-intelligence-index-with-the-release-of-gpt-6-2-astra-and-gpt-6-2-sol-c19f50a3]]. Effort-level scores: **62 (max), 62 (xhigh), 60 (high), 57 (medium), and 46 (low)** [[gpt-6-2-astra-intelligence-performance-price-analysis-63f294bd]].

Pricing: **$8/$40 per Mtoken** (input/output), **20% cheaper than GPT-6 Astra** ($10/$50); cached input drops from $1.00 to **$0.40/Mtoken** [[introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-and-sol-8f6af8c4]] [[gpt-6-2-astra-intelligence-performance-price-analysis-63f294bd]]. At max effort GPT-6.2 Astra costs approximately **$4.00 per Intelligence Index task**, while at **low effort it is the cheapest option at $0.66/task** — cheaper than Claude Fable 5.2 at low effort ($3.10/task) [[gpt-6-2-astra-intelligence-performance-price-analysis-63f294bd]].

Safety: GPT-6.2 Astra meets the **"High" threshold for AI Self-Improvement capabilities** while GPT-6.2 Sol falls below it. Both models are classified as **"Critical" for cybersecurity** [[introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-and-sol-8f6af8c4]].

On the **Coding Agent Index**, GPT-6.2 Astra is flat with GPT-6 Astra (no gain); GPT-6.2 Sol loses 3 points relative to GPT-6.1 Sol [[openai-has-the-2-model-in-the-intelligence-index-with-the-release-of-gpt-6-2-astra-and-gpt-6-2-sol-c19f50a3]].

> GPT-6.2 Astra (max) scores 62 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, 5 points above GPT-6 Astra (57), behind Claude Fable 5.2 (max, 65). [[openai-has-the-2-model-in-the-intelligence-index-with-the-release-of-gpt-6-2-astra-and-gpt-6-2-sol-c19f50a3]]

> GPT-6.2 Astra is priced at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens. [[introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-and-sol-8f6af8c4]]

> GPT-6.2 Astra is priced at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, 20% cheaper than GPT-6 Astra, with cached input dropping to $0.40 from $1.00 per million tokens. [[gpt-6-2-astra-intelligence-performance-price-analysis-63f294bd]]

> Running the Intelligence Index on GPT-6.2 Astra (max) costs approximately $4.00 per task; at low effort it is the cheapest option at $0.66 per task versus $3.10 for Claude Fable 5.2 at low effort. [[gpt-6-2-astra-intelligence-performance-price-analysis-63f294bd]]

> GPT-6.2 Astra meets the High threshold for AI Self-Improvement capabilities, while GPT-6.2 Sol falls below it. Both models are treated as Critical for cybersecurity. [[introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-and-sol-8f6af8c4]]

> On the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index (Codex harness, max effort), GPT-6.2 Astra is flat with GPT-6 Astra, and GPT-6.2 Sol loses 3 points relative to GPT-6.1 Sol. [[openai-has-the-2-model-in-the-intelligence-index-with-the-release-of-gpt-6-2-astra-and-gpt-6-2-sol-c19f50a3]]
