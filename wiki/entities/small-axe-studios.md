# Small Axe Studios

## Summary

**Small Axe Studios** is a Vancouver-based AAA game development studio announced by Take-Two-owned 2K in August 2026, focused on building '2K's next blockbuster sports franchise' with the explicit aim to 'redefine the future of sports video games' [[2k-announces-new-vancouver-based-aaa-studio-led-by-ea-veteran-2511af2e]]. The studio is led by **Aaron McHardy**, an 18-year EA veteran who served as executive producer on Madden, College Football, and the FIFA team, now holding the titles of vice president, executive producer, and studio head [[2k-announces-new-vancouver-based-aaa-studio-led-by-ea-veteran-2511af2e]]. The leadership bench is heavily staffed by ex-EA Sports personnel — head of design Gary Paterson, head of operations Tom Pan, and head of engineering Mike Thompson — signaling a talent-exodus dynamic from EA to 2K at a moment when EA itself is conducting post-privatization cost cuts [[2k-announces-new-vancouver-based-aaa-studio-led-by-ea-veteran-2511af2e]].

Small Axe Studios represents a formal expansion of 2K's internal **'2K Sports Lab'** (created 2023), elevating the experimental unit to a full production studio [[2k-announces-new-vancouver-based-aaa-studio-led-by-ea-veteran-2511af2e]]. The studio's formation arrives as a counter-narrative to the dominant 2026 industry trend of studio closures and layoffs — it is the first major new AAA studio announcement amid that contraction wave, and the heavily ex-EA composition positions it as a nascent challenger to EA Sports FC's near-monopoly in football simulation games.

> Take-Two-owned 2K has announced a new AAA studio called Small Axe Studios, based in Vancouver, which aims to 'redefine the future of sports video games.' The studio is led by EA veteran Aaron McHardy, who is serving as vice president, executive producer, and studio head.

> McHardy was previously the executive producer of the Madden and College Football franchises, as well as the FIFA team, over a tenure of almost 18 years. McHardy is far from the only EA veteran in the current staff, with head of design Gary Paterson, head of operations Tom Pan, head of engineering Mike Thompson, and more rounding out the roster.
