# Claude Opus 5.5

## Summary

**Claude Opus 5.5** (shipped 2026-09-22) is Anthropic's new standard flagship model, achieving **58 on the AA Intelligence Index (max), the highest score measured by several points**, breaking the prior Fable 5.1 = GPT-6 Astra tie at 53 [[introducing-claude-opus-5-5-ec3fa700]] [[claude-opus-5-5-takes-the-top-spot-on-the-artificial-analysis-intelligence-index-4950ff35]]. It leads **six of the ten Index evaluations** (Humanity's Last Exam 61.4%, SciCode 66.9%, GDPval-AA v2.1, AA-Briefcase v1.1, AA-Omniscience and AutomationBench-AA) and reaches **AA-Briefcase Elo 1822, +143 over Fable 5.1** [[claude-opus-5-5-takes-the-top-spot-on-the-artificial-analysis-intelligence-index-4950ff35]].

Pricing is **$4/$20 per Mtoken (20% below Opus 5's $5/$25)** with **cache reads cut 60% to $0.20/Mtok**, making it ~40% cheaper to run than Opus 5 on typical workloads [[introducing-claude-opus-5-5-ec3fa700]]. It is **level with Opus 5 on cost per task despite 1.6x the output tokens**, and four of its five effort levels sit on the AA Intelligence-vs-Cost Pareto frontier (Low Effort at $0.55/task is the cheapest measured) [[claude-opus-5-5-takes-the-top-spot-on-the-artificial-analysis-intelligence-index-4950ff35]].

The model launches with the **"preserved thinking" anti-distillation safeguard** (first shipped with Fable 5.1) and is live on AWS, Google Cloud and Azure [[introducing-claude-opus-5-5-ec3fa700]].
