# Stable Audio 3.0

## Summary

Stable Audio 3.0 is Stability AI's music and SFX generation model, exclusively trained on licensed data from the AudioSparx music library [[stable-audio-vs-suno-udio-elevenlabs-mubert-and-lyria-licensing-export-rights-and-self-hosting-compared-3b88bdac]]. It does not generate vocals or lyrics. It is explicitly licensed for use in mobile apps, desktop software, and video games, making it a notable option for the game-dev asset pipeline.

> Stable Audio 3.0 was exclusively trained on licensed data from the AudioSparx music library. For games specifically, you can use Stable Audio-generated music and sound effects in mobile apps, desktop software, and video games. The model does not generate vocals or lyrics. [[stable-audio-vs-suno-udio-elevenlabs-mubert-and-lyria-licensing-export-rights-and-self-hosting-compared-3b88bdac]]

**Commercial-use terms.** The Community License covers organizations under $1M annual revenue. Organizations exceeding that threshold must request a separate license from Stability AI [[stable-audio-vs-suno-udio-elevenlabs-mubert-and-lyria-licensing-export-rights-and-self-hosting-compared-3b88bdac]].

> Stable Audio's Community License covers organizations under the revenue threshold. If you or your affiliates exceed 1 million USD in annual revenue, you can no longer rely on the Community License and must request a separate license from Stability AI.
