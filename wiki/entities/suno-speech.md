# Suno Speech

## Summary

**Suno Speech (beta)** is Suno's voice+music audio model, launched 2026-10-01 alongside the Suno v6 music models — described as the first audio model that generates voice and music together as one cohesive track [[introducing-speech-beta-2f956a47]]. The user types or pastes text and describes the desired voice and musical style; it is available on mobile and web, with no stated public API, pricing tier, or commercial-use terms (access recorded as web) [[introducing-speech-beta-2f956a47]]. It is the topic's first voice+music speech entity and a candidate narration-over-score tool for game cutscenes/trailers, pending an API.

> Speech is the first audio model that generates voice and music together as one cohesive track. Type an idea or paste existing text, then describe the voice and musical style you want. Speech is available on mobile and web. [[introducing-speech-beta-2f956a47]]
