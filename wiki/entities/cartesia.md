# Sonic 3.6

## Summary

Sonic 3.6 is Cartesia's TTS model, generally available via playground and API. The stable snapshot `sonic-3.6-2026-08-27` was released August 27, 2026, and is marked stable in Cartesia's docs [[introducing-sonic-3-6-1274d75a]].

> Sonic-3.6 is now generally available in the playground or via the API. The docs list a dated snapshot, sonic-3.6-2026-08-27, with a release date of August 27, 2026, marked stable. [[introducing-sonic-3-6-1274d75a]]

**Pricing.** Standard TTS costs approximately 1 credit per character, translating to 750–800 credits per minute of audio. A free tier is available; the Pro plan is $5/mo with approximately 133 TTS minutes [[introducing-sonic-3-6-1274d75a]].

> Standard TTS costs approximately 1 credit per character, about 750 to 800 credits per minute of audio. Plans start with a free tier, and the Pro plan is 5 USD per month with about 133 TTS minutes. [[introducing-sonic-3-6-1274d75a]]

**Arena placement.** On the Artificial Analysis Controlled Voice TTS leaderboard (2026-11-10), Sonic 3.6 ranks #3 at Elo 1148, behind ElevenLabs Eleven v4 Turbo (#1, Elo 1155) and Eleven v4 (#2, Elo 1154) [[controlled-voice-leaderboard-top-ai-voice-models-0313c536]]. Inworld Realtime TTS-2 is #4 at Elo 1143.
