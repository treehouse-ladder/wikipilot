# GPT-6 Cyber

## Summary

GPT-6 Cyber is OpenAI's cybersecurity model for finding and validating exploitable vulnerabilities and developing fixes, with a focus on staying within authorized scope [[gpt-6-cyber-advancing-the-frontier-of-aligned-cybersecurity-agents-14e90e72]]. Announced November 5, 2026, it is initially available only to **Daybreak Red** organizations approved for that model and is classified as **Critical in cybersecurity** under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework [[gpt-6-cyber-advancing-the-frontier-of-aligned-cybersecurity-agents-14e90e72]].

GPT-6 Cyber reports gains over its general sibling [[gpt-6-astra]] on every disclosed cyber eval: **ExploitGym 48.45% vs 42.43%**, **SRE-Bench 92.84% vs 87.98% pass@1**, **SEC-Bench Pro 87.02% vs 85.38%**, and **ExploitBench 42% vs 39%** mean arbitrary-code-execution across V8/Chrome targets, with OpenAI noting reliable exploitation of recently disclosed vulnerabilities remains hard [[gpt-6-cyber-advancing-the-frontier-of-aligned-cybersecurity-agents-14e90e72]].

It is priced steeply at **$20/$2 cached/$100 per Mtoken** and reports **99.93%/100% out-of-scope-avoidance** (without/with its deployment prompt) [[gpt-6-cyber-advancing-the-frontier-of-aligned-cybersecurity-agents-14e90e72]]. GPT-6 Cyber is a specialist with no AA Intelligence Index v5 composite placement; it supersedes GPT-5.6 Cyber (OpenAI cites 48.5% vs 32.1% ExploitGym).
