## Summary

Sackbird Studios is an independent, employee-owned game development studio formed in August 2026 by developers laid off from ZeniMax Online Studios during Microsoft's July 2026 Xbox reset [[laid-off-zenimax-developers-form-sackbird-studios-5ba7900f]]. The team had been working on an online looter-shooter codenamed Project Blackbird, which Microsoft cancelled despite the project reportedly impressing Xbox executives including Phil Spencer during an internal demonstration [[laid-off-zenimax-developers-form-sackbird-studios-5ba7900f]].

The studio is structured as employee-owned and plans to leverage internal funding to retain full creative control, aiming to build character-driven experiences free from corporate compromises [[laid-off-zenimax-developers-form-sackbird-studios-5ba7900f]].

> A group of ZeniMax Online Studios developers who were recently laid off by Microsoft have reunited to form Sackbird Studios. The cohort had been working on an online looter shooter codenamed Project Blackbird at ZOS.

> Sackbird is billed as an independent, employee-owned studio. The team intend to leverage internal funding to retain full creative control and plan to build bold, character-driven experiences free from corporate compromises.
