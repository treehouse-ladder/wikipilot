# Autodesk Wonder 3D

## Summary

Wonder 3D is a generative model inside Autodesk Flow Studio that turns text and reference images into editable 3D assets, with Text-to-3D, Image-to-3D and Text-to-Image modes and USD / STL / OBJ export [[autodesk-flow-studio-launches-wonder-3d-gen-ai-model-to-help-creators-make-3d-characters-and-objects-faster-and-easier-063ed32f]]. Autodesk pitches it at indie developers and small teams for prop creation, character ideation and scene building [[autodesk-flow-studio-launches-wonder-3d-gen-ai-model-to-help-creators-make-3d-characters-and-objects-faster-and-easier-063ed32f]].

> Wonder 3D supports USD, STL and OBJ exports, which means characters and props can move beyond the screen into 3D printing, physical prototyping, and real-world model creation. With Text to 3D, Image to 3D, and Text to Image capabilities, Wonder 3D enables creators to generate 3D assets from simple text or reference images, then refine, remix, and reuse them across projects.

The announcement [[autodesk-flow-studio-launches-wonder-3d-gen-ai-model-to-help-creators-make-3d-characters-and-objects-faster-and-easier-063ed32f]] does not say whether Wonder 3D is reachable outside the Flow Studio UI (API, desktop), nor its pricing or licence terms, so those fields are `unknown`.
