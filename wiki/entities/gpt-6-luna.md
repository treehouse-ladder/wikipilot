# GPT-6 Luna

## Summary

**GPT-6 Luna** (released 2026-09-22) is OpenAI's cost-efficient tier model that **halves pricing relative to GPT-5.6 Luna: from $0.20/$1.20 to $0.10/$0.50 per Mtoken** [[gpt-6-sol-and-luna-push-the-cost-efficiency-frontier-0c4a202e]] [[introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna-73461b3e]]. Like its sibling GPT-6 Sol, **Intelligence Index and Coding Agent Index scores remain level with GPT-5.6**, with savings driven by caching and inference improvements rather than new capabilities [[gpt-6-sol-and-luna-push-the-cost-efficiency-frontier-0c4a202e]].

Luna uses more output tokens per task (51k vs 41k for GPT-5.6 Luna), but per-task cost still drops: **GPT-6 Luna (max) costs $0.07/task** [[gpt-6-sol-and-luna-push-the-cost-efficiency-frontier-0c4a202e]]. The price cut positions GPT-6 Luna as a new cost-efficiency floor for high-volume workloads.

> GPT-6 Sol and Luna push the cost efficiency frontier by halving cost relative to GPT-5.6 Sol and Luna. Sol drops from $4/$20 to $2/$10 per million input/output tokens, and Luna from $0.20/$1.20 to $0.10/$0.50. Intelligence Index and Coding Agent Index scores remain level with GPT-5.6. [[gpt-6-sol-and-luna-push-the-cost-efficiency-frontier-0c4a202e]]

> We're passing those savings directly on to users and customers by reducing API prices for Sol and Luna by 50% compared with their GPT-5.6 promotional pricing. [[introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna-73461b3e]]
