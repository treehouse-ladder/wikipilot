# Seedance

## Summary

Seedance is ByteDance's video generation model, supporting image-to-video and text-to-video with synchronized audio output [[seedance-wins-artificial-analysis-image-to-video-with-audio-output-benchmark-1-a7869d54]]. As of October 25, 2026, it ranks first on the Artificial Analysis Image-to-Video (audio output) benchmark — the only video benchmark placement on record for this model.

> Seedance by ByteDance ranks first on the Artificial Analysis Image-to-Video (audio output) benchmark as of October 25, 2026. Available via the Seedance API. Supports image-to-video and text-to-video with synchronized audio output.
