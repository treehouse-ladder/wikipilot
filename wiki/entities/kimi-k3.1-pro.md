# Kimi K3.1 Pro

## Summary

Moonshot AI's **Kimi K3.1 Pro** (launched 2026-10-23) is a 2.8-trillion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model with **104B active parameters** that entered the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at **#4 with a score of 55**, trailing only Claude Opus 5.5 (max with fallback, 58), Claude Sonnet 5.5 (max with fallback, 56) and Muse Wonder Full (max, 56) — a +11-point gain over base Kimi K3 (max, 44) on the current index basis [[kimi-k3-1-pro-launches-at-4th-on-the-intelligence-index-and-will-lead-open-weights-models-c9f225d9]]. It is priced at **$2/$10 per Mtoken with $0.20 per Mtoken cache-hit input** at an estimated **$2.11 per Intelligence Index task** [[kimi-k3-1-pro-intelligence-performance-price-analysis-fb7b4f33]]. At launch its **weights were not yet published**; once released Artificial Analysis projects it will be the **highest-scoring open-weights model** [[kimi-k3-1-pro-launches-at-4th-on-the-intelligence-index-and-will-lead-open-weights-models-c9f225d9]].

> Kimi K3.1 Pro scores 55 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing fourth overall, trailing only Claude Opus 5.5 (max with fallback, 58), Claude Sonnet 5.5 (max with fallback, 56) and Muse Wonder Full (max, 56). This is an 11-point gain over Kimi K3 (max, 44). Kimi K3.1 Pro has 2.8 trillion total parameters with 104 billion active parameters. Weights are not yet published; Moonshot is expected to release them soon after launch. Once the weights are released, Kimi K3.1 Pro will be the highest-scoring open weights model. [[kimi-k3-1-pro-launches-at-4th-on-the-intelligence-index-and-will-lead-open-weights-models-c9f225d9]]

> Pricing for Kimi K3.1 Pro is $2 per 1M input tokens and $10 per 1M output tokens, with cache hits at $0.20 per 1M tokens. The estimated cost to run the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index is $2.11 per task. [[kimi-k3-1-pro-intelligence-performance-price-analysis-fb7b4f33]]
