## Summary

**Gemini 4 Carbon** is Google DeepMind's cybersecurity- and long-horizon-focused frontier model, announced 2026-10-30. It immediately ranks **#1 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5 at 59 (High)**, one point above Claude Opus 5.5 (Max, Default Fallback, 58), and **leads 4 of the 9 v5 evaluations: HLE-Diamond, Terminal-Bench-Science, AA-Omniscience, and AutomationBench-AA** [[gemini-4-carbon-is-the-new-1-on-the-artificial-analysis-intelligence-index-v5-53854855]]. It follows Gemini 4 Argon, which launched ~2 weeks earlier, and **outperforms Argon in most domains** [[gemini-4-carbon-our-next-frontier-model-a92d7b7e]].

> Gemini 4 Carbon (High) scores the highest on Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index with a score of 59, followed by Claude Opus 5.5 (Max, Default Fallback) with a score of 58. [[gemini-4-carbon-is-the-new-1-on-the-artificial-analysis-intelligence-index-v5-53854855]]

On the **Coding Agent Index it places #2 at 59**, 3 points behind Claude Code with Claude Opus 5.5 (max) at 62 [[gemini-4-carbon-is-the-new-1-on-the-artificial-analysis-intelligence-index-v5-53854855]]. Pricing is **$2/$10 per Mtoken introductory, rising to $4/$20 after the introductory period**, with an estimated **$7.76 per Intelligence Index task — ~23% less than Claude Opus 5.5 (max) at $10.04** [[gemini-4-carbon-is-the-new-1-on-the-artificial-analysis-intelligence-index-v5-53854855]] [[gemini-4-carbon-our-next-frontier-model-a92d7b7e]].

> The introductory price is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. After the introductory period, the price rises to $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens. [[gemini-4-carbon-our-next-frontier-model-a92d7b7e]]

> Its cost per task is $7.76 per Intelligence Index task, approximately 23% less than Claude Opus 5.5 (max) at $10.04. [[gemini-4-carbon-is-the-new-1-on-the-artificial-analysis-intelligence-index-v5-53854855]]

**Cybersecurity specialization** is Carbon's headline differentiator: it leads **SEC-bench Pro (88.2%)** for finding vulnerabilities, **ExploitGym (54.8%)** for exploitation, and **CWE-bench (71.0%)** for patching [[gemini-4-carbon-our-next-frontier-model-a92d7b7e]]. Google also reports **0.0% attack success on the Gray Swan prompt-injection benchmark** and **0.0% successful cheating on Terminal-Bench 4.0** as safety achievements [[gemini-4-carbon-our-next-frontier-model-a92d7b7e]].

> Gemini 4 Carbon lands a firm lead in SEC-bench Pro (88.2%) for finding vulnerabilities, ExploitGym (54.8%) for exploitation, and CWE-bench (71.0%) for patching. [[gemini-4-carbon-our-next-frontier-model-a92d7b7e]]

**Access** is currently restricted: Google is "first giving access to a subset of trusted cyber defenders" via the **Fairwind Program**, with broader availability to developers, businesses, and consumers described as "coming soon" [[gemini-4-carbon-our-next-frontier-model-a92d7b7e]]. All cybersecurity figures above are Google-reported under this limited release; independent corroboration is pending.

> We are first giving access to a subset of trusted cyber defenders, with more partners to follow through our Fairwind Program, and we are taking part in the U.S. government's voluntary pre-release testing. Broad availability to developers, businesses, and consumers is coming soon. [[gemini-4-carbon-our-next-frontier-model-a92d7b7e]]
