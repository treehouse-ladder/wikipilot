## Summary

MiMo-V2.5-Pro is Xiaomi's flagship open-weight reasoning model (released April 22, 2026), a Mixture-of-Experts with ~1.0 trillion total parameters and 42 billion active per forward pass [[mimo-v2-5-pro-intelligence-performance-price-analysis-51e3baae]]. It scores 54 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, tied with Moonshot's Kimi K2.6 as the joint top open-weights model and ahead of DeepSeek V4-Pro (Reasoning, Max Effort) at 52 [[mimo-v2-5-pro-intelligence-performance-price-analysis-51e3baae]]. Weights are publicly downloadable under the MIT license; the model is text-in/text-out with a 1M-token context window, priced at $1.00/$3.00 per Mtoken [[mimo-v2-5-pro-intelligence-performance-price-analysis-51e3baae]].

> Kimi K2.6 (54) and MiMo-V2.5-Pro (54) are tied as the top open weights models by Intelligence Index, followed by DeepSeek V4 Pro (Reasoning, Max Effort) (52).

> MiMo-V2.5-Pro is a Mixture of Experts (MoE) model with 1.0 trillion total parameters, but only 42 billion active parameters are used during inference.

> MiMo-V2.5-Pro is open weights, with model weights publicly available and downloadable for self-hosting. The model is released under the MIT license. The model supports text input, outputs text, and has a 1m tokens context window.

> Pricing for MiMo-V2.5-Pro is $1.00 per 1M input tokens and $3.00 per 1M output tokens.
