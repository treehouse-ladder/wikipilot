## Summary

Yoko Shimomura is a Japanese video game composer best known for her scores on Kingdom Hearts, Legend of Mana, and Final Fantasy XV. On June 21, 2026, she attended the "The Music of Square Enix: Magic, Memories and Melodies" concert at OVO Arena Wembley, where three of her compositions were performed by the Novello Orchestra and Coro Spezzato under conductor Arnie Roth [[the-music-of-square-enix-the-moments-that-will-undo-you-67ea1b78]].

> On Sunday 21 June, eleven pieces of Square Enix music filled OVO Arena Wembley for one night, performed by the Novello Orchestra and Coro Spezzato under conductor Arnie Roth, with Yoko Shimomura, who wrote three of them, watching from the audience.
