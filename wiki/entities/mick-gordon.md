# Mick Gordon

## Summary

**Mick Gordon** is the composer behind the industrial metal soundtracks for *DOOM* (2016) and *DOOM Eternal* (2020), known for his ferocious, high-intensity approach that helped redefine video game music in the FPS genre [[dooms-mick-gordon-to-perform-iconic-soundtrack-live-with-orchestra-a76d163c]]. His DOOM scores are characterized by aggressive guitar riffs, crushing bass, and his custom-built **Doom Machine** — a hybrid instrument that blends traditional metal instrumentation with experimental sonic manipulation.

In August 2026, Gordon was announced as the first headline act for **RADAR Festival 2027** in Manchester (July 30 – August 1, 2027), where he will perform the music of DOOM live for the first time ever [[dooms-mick-gordon-to-perform-iconic-soundtrack-live-with-orchestra-a76d163c]]. The performance is positioned as the actual composer performing the actual score at full volume — not a DJ set or tribute act — with Gordon bringing his custom Doom Machine alongside a full live band and an orchestra.

> Mick Gordon, the mastermind behind the ferocious industrial metal soundtrack for 2016's Doom reboot and the 2020 sequel Eternal, has been announced as the first act for next year's Radar Festival in Manchester, where he'll be performing the music of Doom.

> This isn't a DJ set, and it isn't a tribute act. It's the actual composer, the actual score – the riffs played properly loud in a room that can withstand all the ripping and tearing. Mick has never performed this music live like this [before].
