## Summary

**Gemini 4 Argon** is Google DeepMind's new frontier model, announced September 30, 2026 and released in limited enterprise preview. It places at **AA Intelligence Index v4.3 = 53**, tying GPT-6 Astra (max, 53) for the #3 aggregate position — behind Claude Opus 5.5 (58) and Claude Sonnet 5.5 (56), and equal to the prior Claude Fable 5.1 v4.3 placement [[gemini-4-argon-google-is-back-as-one-of-the-top-three-labs-in-intelligence-achieved-52050b56]].

> Google has released Gemini 4 Argon, its first new frontier-class model since Gemini 3.1 Pro in early 2026. Independent analysis puts it at Intelligence Index = 53 on the v4.3 basis — level with GPT-6 Astra and behind only the Anthropic top two. [[gemini-4-argon-google-is-back-as-one-of-the-top-three-labs-in-intelligence-achieved-52050b56]]

Google's first-party materials headline **DeepSWE v1.1 at 77.9%** (claiming a benchmark lead) and **MMMU 89%** as vision strengths [[gemini-4-argon-our-next-era-of-frontier-intelligence-8a18dee8]], but **Terminal-Bench 4 at only 57%** — the long-horizon agentic-terminal axis — is 16 points below the DeepSWE figure, suggesting Argon is strongest at structured code edits and multimodal reasoning but not at sustained autonomous agentic execution.

> Gemini 4 Argon leads on our internal coding benchmarks with 77.9% on DeepSWE v1.1, marking a return to leadership for Google on agentic software engineering. [[gemini-4-argon-our-next-era-of-frontier-intelligence-8a18dee8]]

Pricing is listed at **$4/$20 per Mtoken** (standard input/output) with an introductory offer reducing to $1.99/task on the AA per-Intelligence-Index-task metric — the lowest-cost per-task model in the current #3-tier cluster. The **"retaking the benchmark lead" headline** from secondary coverage [[google-unveils-gemini-4-argon-retaking-benchmark-lead-over-openai-and-anthropic-but-in-limited-release-37b6da44]] is contested by the independent v4.3 placement: at AA Index 53 it ties Astra but trails Anthropic's top two by 5 points, so the claim is benchmark-specific (DeepSWE v1.1) rather than aggregate-index.

**Key watch items:** Argon is in limited enterprise preview only — GA timeline, a potential Pro/Flash tier, Deep Think reasoning mode availability, and CyberGym placement are all unconfirmed. CyberGym and ARC-AGI-2 fields remain null pending independent evaluation.
