# Skydance

## Summary

Skydance Corporation (formerly Paramount Skydance Corporation) is an American entertainment company that became one of the largest video game publishers in the world on **October 6, 2026**, when it completed its **$111B acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery** [[warner-bros-games-now-belongs-to-skydance-but-company-makes-no-mention-of-that-in-big-announcement-ab54b942]]. Through that deal, Skydance owns **Warner Bros. Games**, whose studios include:

- **Rocksteady Studios** — Batman: Arkham series, Suicide Squad: Kill the Justice League
- **NetherRealm Studios** — Mortal Kombat series, Injustice series
- **Avalanche Software** — Hogwarts Legacy
- **TT Games** — LEGO video game series

Prior to the WBD acquisition, Skydance had existing game studios including Skydance Interactive (VR games) and Skydance New Media (narrative adventure games, founded by Amy Hennig). It also stood up **Paramount Game Studios** as a unified label in advance of the WBD close [[paramount-unveils-new-unified-paramount-games-studio-3b154906]].

Notably, **Skydance's official announcement of the deal closing contained no mention of its new video game holdings** — a studied silence given the combined revenue of Rocksteady, NetherRealm, Avalanche, and TT Games [[warner-bros-games-now-belongs-to-skydance-but-company-makes-no-mention-of-that-in-big-announcement-ab54b942]].
