# Skydance

## Summary

Skydance Corporation (formerly Paramount Skydance Corporation) is an American entertainment company that became one of the largest video game publishers in the world on **October 6, 2026**, when it completed its **$111B acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery** [[warner-bros-games-now-belongs-to-skydance-but-company-makes-no-mention-of-that-in-big-announcement-ab54b942]]. Through that deal, Skydance owns **Warner Bros. Games**, whose studios include:

- **Rocksteady Studios** — Batman: Arkham series, Suicide Squad: Kill the Justice League
- **NetherRealm Studios** — Mortal Kombat series, Injustice series
- **Avalanche Software** — Hogwarts Legacy
- **TT Games** — LEGO video game series

Prior to the WBD acquisition, Skydance had existing game studios including Skydance Interactive (VR games) and Skydance New Media (narrative adventure games, founded by Amy Hennig). It also stood up **Paramount Game Studios** as a unified label in advance of the WBD close [[paramount-unveils-new-unified-paramount-games-studio-3b154906]].

Notably, **Skydance's official announcement of the deal closing contained no mention of its new video game holdings** — a studied silence given the combined revenue of Rocksteady, NetherRealm, Avalanche, and TT Games [[warner-bros-games-now-belongs-to-skydance-but-company-makes-no-mention-of-that-in-big-announcement-ab54b942]].

> On October 6, 2026, Skydance Corporation completed its $111 billion acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery, making it the new owner of Warner Bros. Games. The company's announcement of the deal closing contained no mention of its new video game holdings. [[warner-bros-games-now-belongs-to-skydance-but-company-makes-no-mention-of-that-in-big-announcement-ab54b942]]

**Post-acquisition restructuring (October 9, 2026).** Skydance is merging the Paramount game label and all Warner Bros.' internal studios into a single **Games and Experiences division**, to be led by **Tony Driscoll** [[skydance-is-merging-paramount-and-warner-bros-game-studios-into-one-unit-3b2122db]]. The division spans Avalanche Software, NetherRealm, Rocksteady, TT Games, WB Montreal, and WB Boston — consolidating the largest single game-studio portfolio in the industry under one roof. Layoff exposure remains unclear. In a separate development, **former Activision Blizzard CEO Bobby Kotick has been named to Skydance's board** [[former-activision-ceo-bobby-kotick-named-to-the-board-of-post-merger-skydance-9d605d24]], drawing scrutiny given the labor and conduct controversies that marked his Activision tenure.

> Skydance is merging Paramount and Warner Bros.' game studios into one unit. The Warner Bros. studios that now sit in this unit include Hogwarts Legacy's developer and the Mortal Kombat studio, among others. It is still unclear what the division's plans are or how many layoffs it will face. Tony Driscoll will lead the combined division.
