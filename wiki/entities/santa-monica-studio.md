## Summary

Santa Monica Studio is a first-party PlayStation studio founded in 1999, based in Los Angeles, California. They are known primarily for the God of War franchise, including God of War (2018) and God of War Ragnarök (2022).

Their next mainline project, **God of War: Laufey**, was revealed at the PlayStation State of Play on June 3, 2026, and marks the first time the series has fully shifted its focus away from Kratos [[god-of-war-laufey-brings-back-gow-feature-not-seen-in-13-years-06e41341]].

> God of War Laufey was announced by Santa Monica Studio as the next mainline entry to the God of War series, with the game revealed on June 3, 2026, during the PlayStation State of Play broadcast.

God of War: Laufey stars Faye (Kratos's deceased wife from the Norse duology) as protagonist. Faye is "far more agile than Kratos, capable of double jumps and rapid aerial movement, with faster and more stylish combat that resembles character action games such as Devil May Cry" [[god-of-war-laufey-brings-back-gow-feature-not-seen-in-13-years-06e41341]]. The game also "brings back the platforming-focused level design not seen since God of War: Ascension in 2013" [[god-of-war-laufey-brings-back-gow-feature-not-seen-in-13-years-06e41341]].

> Faye is far more agile than Kratos, capable of double jumps and rapid aerial movement, with faster and more stylish combat that resembles character action games such as Devil May Cry.

> This marks the first time the series has fully shifted its focus away from Kratos in a mainline-style adventure. The game brings back the platforming-focused level design not seen since God of War: Ascension in 2013.
