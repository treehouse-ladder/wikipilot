# Kling Image 4.0

## Summary

**Kling Image 4.0** is Kuaishou/Kling AI's text-to-image model (distinct from the [[kling]] video line), released 2026-11-17 [[kling-image-4-0-ai-image-generator-with-native-8k-and-image-sets-3fdafbfd]]. It generates natively at 2K/4K/8K (resolution parameter, 2K default), offers an Image Sets mode (result_type=series) returning 2-10 coherent images from one prompt, renders up to 2,000 characters of legible text across English/Chinese/Japanese/Korean, and can act as a keyframe generator for Kling 4.0 video [[kling-image-4-0-ai-image-generator-with-native-8k-and-image-sets-3fdafbfd]]. On fal the kling/v4/text-to-image endpoint bills $0.063/unit, pay-per-use [[kling-image-4-0-ai-image-generator-with-native-8k-and-image-sets-3fdafbfd]].

> Kling Image 4.0 was released on November 17, 2026. It generates images natively at 2K, 4K, or 8K, selected with the resolution parameter (2K by default). Setting result_type to series returns a coherent set of 2 to 10 images from one prompt. The kling/v4/text-to-image endpoint is priced at $0.063 per unit on fal.
