# GPT-6 Sol

## Summary

**GPT-6 Sol** (released 2026-09-22) is OpenAI's cost-optimized flagship model that **halves pricing relative to GPT-5.6 Sol: from $4/$20 to $2/$10 per Mtoken** [[gpt-6-sol-and-luna-push-the-cost-efficiency-frontier-0c4a202e]] [[introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna-73461b3e]]. **Intelligence Index and Coding Agent Index scores remain level with GPT-5.6** (GPT-6 Sol max ~48 on the current AA Index basis), with savings driven by caching and inference improvements rather than capability gains [[gpt-6-sol-and-luna-push-the-cost-efficiency-frontier-0c4a202e]].

Both models use slightly more output tokens per task (Sol 31k vs 29k for GPT-5.6 Sol), but per-task cost still drops significantly: **GPT-6 Sol (max) costs $1.06/task, ~50% below GPT-5.6 Sol's $1.99** [[gpt-6-sol-and-luna-push-the-cost-efficiency-frontier-0c4a202e]]. This resets the cost-efficiency floor among frontier-lab models without changing the aggregate-intelligence leaderboard.

> GPT-6 Sol and Luna push the cost efficiency frontier by halving cost relative to GPT-5.6 Sol and Luna. Sol drops from $4/$20 to $2/$10 per million input/output tokens, and Luna from $0.20/$1.20 to $0.10/$0.50. Intelligence Index and Coding Agent Index scores remain level with GPT-5.6. [[gpt-6-sol-and-luna-push-the-cost-efficiency-frontier-0c4a202e]]

> We're passing those savings directly on to users and customers by reducing API prices for Sol and Luna by 50% compared with their GPT-5.6 promotional pricing. [[introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna-73461b3e]]
