# GPT-5.6 Sol

## Summary

GPT-5.6 Sol is OpenAI's flagship model in the GPT-5.6 family (Sol flagship / Terra balanced / Luna cost-efficient), released to GA on July 9, 2026 [[gpt-5-6-frontier-intelligence-that-scales-with-your-ambition-e19fbb75]]. Artificial Analysis places **GPT-5.6 Sol (max) at AA Intelligence Index 59 — 1 point below Claude Fable 5 (60) at ~1/3 the cost — the new publicly-accessible #2 aggregate model (ahead of Opus 4.8 at 56 and GPT-5.5 at 55)**, and Sol (max) **leads the AA Coding Agent Index at 80** [[gpt-5-6-benchmarks-across-intelligence-speed-and-cost-1b9cd635]]. Pricing: $5/$30 per Mtoken; max-effort ~$1.04 per Intelligence-Index task. OpenAI ships `ultra` mode coordinating four agents in parallel by default [[gpt-5-6-frontier-intelligence-that-scales-with-your-ambition-e19fbb75]]. Supersedes GPT-5.5 as OpenAI's flagship.

On 2026-08-10 OpenAI introduced **GPT-5.6-Cyber**, a cyber-specialized variant **built on GPT-5.6 Sol** and fine-tuned for zero-day discovery and exploit-chain development with reduced refusals on higher-risk dual-use cyber tasks [[expanding-daybreak-as-the-cyber-defense-window-narrows-081601c2]]. GPT-5.6-Cyber is gated behind the **Daybreak Red** applicant-vetted tier for advanced authorized cybersecurity work (proof-of-concept exploit development, exploit-chain validation, penetration testing, red teaming). OpenAI reports it **completes 95.0% of its internal Advanced Cybersecurity Completion Rate evaluation vs 1.5% for GPT-5.6 Sol** — a willingness-to-respond metric measuring reduced refusals, not correctness, so it is not comparable to aggregate-intelligence or CyberGym axes [[expanding-daybreak-as-the-cyber-defense-window-narrows-081601c2]]. No AA Intelligence Index or CyberGym placement for GPT-5.6-Cyber has been published.

> GPT-5.6 Sol (max) scores 1 point below Claude Fable 5 (max) in the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 59 points, at approximately one third of the cost. GPT-5.6 Sol (max) leads the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index at 80 points. [[gpt-5-6-benchmarks-across-intelligence-speed-and-cost-1b9cd635]]

> Built on GPT-5.6 Sol, it is trained to improve capabilities on several specialized cybersecurity tasks (e.g., finding zero-day vulnerabilities and developing exploit chains) and to reduce refusals for certain higher-risk, dual-use cyber tasks.
