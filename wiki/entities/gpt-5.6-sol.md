# GPT-5.6 Sol

## Summary

GPT-5.6 Sol is OpenAI's flagship model in the GPT-5.6 family (Sol flagship / Terra balanced / Luna cost-efficient), released to GA on July 9, 2026 [[gpt-5-6-frontier-intelligence-that-scales-with-your-ambition-e19fbb75]]. Artificial Analysis places **GPT-5.6 Sol (max) at AA Intelligence Index 59 — 1 point below Claude Fable 5 (60) at ~1/3 the cost — the new publicly-accessible #2 aggregate model (ahead of Opus 4.8 at 56 and GPT-5.5 at 55)**, and Sol (max) **leads the AA Coding Agent Index at 80** [[gpt-5-6-benchmarks-across-intelligence-speed-and-cost-1b9cd635]]. Pricing: $5/$30 per Mtoken; max-effort ~$1.04 per Intelligence-Index task. OpenAI ships `ultra` mode coordinating four agents in parallel by default [[gpt-5-6-frontier-intelligence-that-scales-with-your-ambition-e19fbb75]]. Supersedes GPT-5.5 as OpenAI's flagship; **superseded by GPT-6 Astra (Sep 3, 2026) as OpenAI's flagship** — Astra scores AA Index 61 (tied with Sol at v4.1.1) at $10/$50 per Mtoken (2.5× Sol's $4/$20) [[benchmarking-gpt-6-astra-b4150b17]].

On 2026-08-10 OpenAI introduced **GPT-5.6-Cyber**, a cyber-specialized variant **built on GPT-5.6 Sol** and fine-tuned for zero-day discovery and exploit-chain development with reduced refusals on higher-risk dual-use cyber tasks [[expanding-daybreak-as-the-cyber-defense-window-narrows-081601c2]]. GPT-5.6-Cyber is gated behind the **Daybreak Red** applicant-vetted tier for advanced authorized cybersecurity work (proof-of-concept exploit development, exploit-chain validation, penetration testing, red teaming). OpenAI reports it **completes 95.0% of its internal Advanced Cybersecurity Completion Rate evaluation vs 1.5% for GPT-5.6 Sol** — a willingness-to-respond metric measuring reduced refusals, not correctness, so it is not comparable to aggregate-intelligence or CyberGym axes [[expanding-daybreak-as-the-cyber-defense-window-narrows-081601c2]]. No AA Intelligence Index or CyberGym placement for GPT-5.6-Cyber has been published.

> GPT-5.6 Sol (max) scores 1 point below Claude Fable 5 (max) in the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 59 points, at approximately one third of the cost. GPT-5.6 Sol (max) leads the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index at 80 points. [[gpt-5-6-benchmarks-across-intelligence-speed-and-cost-1b9cd635]]

> Built on GPT-5.6 Sol, it is trained to improve capabilities on several specialized cybersecurity tasks (e.g., finding zero-day vulnerabilities and developing exploit chains) and to reduce refusals for certain higher-risk, dual-use cyber tasks. [[expanding-daybreak-as-the-cyber-defense-window-narrows-081601c2]]

On 2026-08-21 OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Sol's price by over 20% for at least three months: **$4/$20 per Mtoken** (down from the $5/$30 launch pricing), a 20% input and 33% output reduction, promotional through at least 2026-11-21 across the API and Codex credits (token-based USD usage only; legacy metering excluded) [[advancing-the-price-performance-frontier-with-gpt-5-6-dca7adc6]]. At the promo rate Sol's cost per Intelligence-Index task falls below the ~$1.04 measured at launch pricing, tightening its cost-efficiency standing against Grok 4.6 ($2/$6) and Claude Opus 5 ($5/$25).

> GPT-5.6 Sol now costs $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens, a 20% reduction in input pricing and a 33% reduction in output pricing. GPT-5.6 Sol's promotional pricing is available at least through November 21, 2026. [[advancing-the-price-performance-frontier-with-gpt-5-6-dca7adc6]]
