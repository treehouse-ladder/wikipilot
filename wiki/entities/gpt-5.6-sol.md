# GPT-5.6 Sol

## Summary

GPT-5.6 Sol is OpenAI's flagship model in the GPT-5.6 family (Sol flagship / Terra balanced / Luna cost-efficient), released to GA on July 9, 2026 [[gpt-5-6-frontier-intelligence-that-scales-with-your-ambition-e19fbb75]]. Artificial Analysis places **GPT-5.6 Sol (max) at AA Intelligence Index 59 — 1 point below Claude Fable 5 (60) at ~1/3 the cost — the new publicly-accessible #2 aggregate model (ahead of Opus 4.8 at 56 and GPT-5.5 at 55)**, and Sol (max) **leads the AA Coding Agent Index at 80** [[gpt-5-6-benchmarks-across-intelligence-speed-and-cost-1b9cd635]]. Pricing: $5/$30 per Mtoken; max-effort ~$1.04 per Intelligence-Index task. OpenAI ships `ultra` mode coordinating four agents in parallel by default [[gpt-5-6-frontier-intelligence-that-scales-with-your-ambition-e19fbb75]]. Supersedes GPT-5.5 as OpenAI's flagship.

> GPT-5.6 Sol (max) scores 1 point below Claude Fable 5 (max) in the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 59 points, at approximately one third of the cost. GPT-5.6 Sol (max) leads the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index at 80 points. [[gpt-5-6-benchmarks-across-intelligence-speed-and-cost-1b9cd635]]
