# Grok 4.7

## Summary

Grok 4.7 is SpaceXAI's (formerly xAI) flagship model as of 2026-09-21, **superseding [[grok-4.6]]**. It scores **46 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.3.2** — a +2 gain over Grok 4.6 (44→46) but trailing leaders Claude Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra (both 53) [[xai-launches-grok-4-7-at-bargain-prices-but-benchmarks-reveal-a-wide-gap-to-claude-and-gpt-6-70713dc1]]. On **AA-Briefcase**, Grok 4.7 scores **Elo 1657** (+111 over Grok 4.6); **Coding Agent Index 56** (+9 over Grok 4.6) [[benchmarking-grok-4-7-653374de]]. On **Terminal-Bench 4.0** it scores only **26%**, far behind GPT-6 Astra (60%) and Claude Fable 5.1 (55%) [[xai-launches-grok-4-7-at-bargain-prices-but-benchmarks-reveal-a-wide-gap-to-claude-and-gpt-6-70713dc1]]. Pricing is **$2/$6 per Mtoken input/output** — unchanged from Grok 4.6 — with a **500K-token context window** [[xai-launches-grok-4-7-at-bargain-prices-but-benchmarks-reveal-a-wide-gap-to-claude-and-gpt-6-70713dc1]]. A notable cost concern: Grok 4.7 uses approximately **81,000 output tokens per Intelligence Index task** (xHigh), versus 36,000 for Grok 4.6 High and 27,000 for GPT-6 Astra Max, yielding per-task costs of ~$3.74 (xHigh) and ~$2.73 (High) [[xai-launches-grok-4-7-at-bargain-prices-but-benchmarks-reveal-a-wide-gap-to-claude-and-gpt-6-70713dc1]].

> On the independent Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (v4.3.2), which combines ten benchmarks, Grok 4.7 scores 46 and lands mid-pack, while Claude Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 lead with 53 each. [[xai-launches-grok-4-7-at-bargain-prices-but-benchmarks-reveal-a-wide-gap-to-claude-and-gpt-6-70713dc1]]

> On Terminal-Bench 4.0, Grok 4.7 hits just 26 percent, versus 60 percent for GPT-6 Astra and 55 percent for Claude Fable 5.1. [[xai-launches-grok-4-7-at-bargain-prices-but-benchmarks-reveal-a-wide-gap-to-claude-and-gpt-6-70713dc1]]
