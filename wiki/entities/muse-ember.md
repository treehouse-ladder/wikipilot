# Muse Ember

## Summary

**Muse Ember** is Meta's low-cost frontier entry — currently the **cheapest model on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index to reach a score of 36 (max)** — priced at **$0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens** [[meta-s-muse-ember-is-the-cheapest-model-scoring-36-on-the-intelligence-index-545a831c]].

> Meta's Muse Ember (max) scores 36 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, and it is the cheapest model to reach that score. [[meta-s-muse-ember-is-the-cheapest-model-scoring-36-on-the-intelligence-index-545a831c]]

At the same AA Index score (36), Muse Ember (max) costs **approximately $0.14 per task** — roughly 3× cheaper than **[[claude-haiku-5-5]] (max) at ~$0.41** — while the xhigh variant scores 34 at ~$0.12 per task [[meta-s-muse-ember-is-the-cheapest-model-scoring-36-on-the-intelligence-index-545a831c]].

> Muse Ember (max) costs about $0.14 per Intelligence Index task, while Claude Haiku 5.5 (max) costs almost 3x more at $0.41 for the same score; the xhigh variant scores 34 at about $0.12 per task. [[meta-s-muse-ember-is-the-cheapest-model-scoring-36-on-the-intelligence-index-545a831c]]

Muse Ember has a notably high question-refusal rate — **64% of questions are declined** — keeping its hallucination rate at 17.4%. On specific benchmarks it beats [[claude-haiku-5-5]] on **Terminal-Bench 4.0 (40.9% vs 32.8%)** but trails it on **FrontierCode 2.0 (27.2% vs 34.9%)** [[meta-s-muse-ember-is-the-cheapest-model-scoring-36-on-the-intelligence-index-545a831c]].

> Muse Ember declines to answer 64% of questions, which keeps its hallucination rate at 17.4%. It beats Claude Haiku 5.5 on Terminal-Bench 4.0 (40.9% vs 32.8%) but trails it on FrontierCode 2.0 (27.2% vs 34.9%). [[meta-s-muse-ember-is-the-cheapest-model-scoring-36-on-the-intelligence-index-545a831c]]

Muse Ember is priced at a 58% discount to the input median ($0.24) and 37% below the output median ($0.80) among comparable models [[meta-s-muse-ember-is-the-cheapest-model-scoring-36-on-the-intelligence-index-545a831c]].

> Muse Ember (max) is priced at $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens, competitively priced against a median of $0.24 and $0.80. [[meta-s-muse-ember-is-the-cheapest-model-scoring-36-on-the-intelligence-index-545a831c]]
