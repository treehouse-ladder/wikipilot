# Black Pony Immersive

## Summary

**Black Pony Immersive** is an independent game studio founded in Austin, Texas by **Harvey Smith** (creative director of Dishonored 2 and Dishonored: Death of the Outsider) and **Ben Horne** (production director on Redfall), both former Arkane Austin veterans [[harvey-smith-and-fellow-arkane-austin-veterans-announce-a-new-studio-black-pony-immersive-1bc215a8]]. The studio was formed after Microsoft shut down Arkane Austin in May 2024 and has been operating for approximately a year as of August 2026.

> Black Pony Immersive is led by Harvey Smith, creative director of Dishonored 2 and Dishonored: Death of the Outsider, and Ben Horne, most recently the production director on Redfall. The duo worked together at Arkane Austin for years until parent company Microsoft shut down the studio in 2024. [[harvey-smith-and-fellow-arkane-austin-veterans-announce-a-new-studio-black-pony-immersive-1bc215a8]]

**Team and structure.** As of the August 2026 announcement, the studio employs 26 people drawn from veterans of Insomniac, Epic, Avalanche, and Volition. It is based in Austin, Texas with remote workers, and has secured funding from an unnamed publisher.

> The team is staffed with veterans from Insomniac, Epic, Avalanche and Volition studios, is based in Austin, Texas with remote workers, and has secured funding from an unnamed publisher. [[harvey-smith-and-fellow-arkane-austin-veterans-announce-a-new-studio-black-pony-immersive-1bc215a8]]

**Creative direction.** Black Pony Immersive is building single-player, first-person narrative games centered on exploration and creative problem-solving, with Deus Ex, Dishonored, Prey, and Thief named as the primary genre touchstones — placing it squarely in the immersive sim lineage that Harvey Smith helped define at Looking Glass, Ion Storm, and Arkane.

> The studio has been operating for about a year and already has 26 people on staff. Black Pony Immersive is building single-player, first-person narrative games centered on exploration and creative problem-solving, with Deus Ex, Dishonored, Prey, and Thief named as the main touchstones. [[harvey-smith-and-fellow-arkane-austin-veterans-announce-a-new-studio-black-pony-immersive-1bc215a8]]

**Context in the 2026 layoff wave.** The studio's founding is directly downstream of the Xbox Reset: Arkane Austin was closed by Microsoft in 2024 alongside Tango Gameworks; Smith and Horne's formation of Black Pony within 18 months is the first concrete data point on what happens to Arkane Austin's creative leadership after the closure. The August 2026 announcement coincides with Warren Spector's retirement [[deus-ex-director-warren-spector-is-retiring-after-nearly-45-years-in-game-dev-96a74153]], the exit of the last first-generation immersive sim founder — making Black Pony Immersive the primary carrier of that creative lineage going forward.
